# Playbook — buying an oversold bounce (OSB / ATHOSB)

The mechanical checklist for entering the setup Dr. Wish says he **prefers to buying a breakout**: a strong stock near a recent all-time high pulls back until it is oversold, turns up, and he buys the turn with a stop just under the bounce low. The doctrine, its history and the reasoning live on the [oversold-bounce concept page](../methodology/oversold-bounce.md); this page is only the procedure. Where the [GLB playbook](buying-glb.md) and this one overlap, the difference is *when* you buy the same stock — the GLB is the moment it clears its green line, the OSB is the first pullback-and-turn afterwards.

## Inputs

- Market state: [GMI](../methodology/gmi.md) colour, [QQQ short-term trend](../methodology/qqq-short-term-timing.md) day count → [market-state playbook](market-state.md)
- A watchlist of strong stocks — his is a ~700-name IBD/MarketSmith list ([WW 2022-06-15](../../raw/posts/2022-06-15-blog-post-one-of-my-favorite-set-ups-athosb-found-one-stock-sqm-check-it-out-and-see-the-tutorial.md))
- Daily chart with the 10.1 stochastic (and 10.4 / 10.4.4), the lower 15.2 Bollinger Band, and the green line from the monthly chart
- Optional: the TC2000 black-dot / green-dot / blue-dot indicators — the dot definitions are in the [glossary](../methodology/glossary.md)

---

## Step 1 — Confirm the market gate

Same gate as every long entry, and he says so specifically for this setup: "In a weak market most set-ups fail... In a strong up-trending market this is a powerful set-up." ([WW 2022-06-15](../../raw/posts/2022-06-15-blog-post-one-of-my-favorite-set-ups-athosb-found-one-stock-sqm-check-it-out-and-see-the-tutorial.md))

- GMI GREEN and a QQQ short-term **up**-trend is the normal condition. He does *publish* OSB candidates during down-trends, but with the caveat attached — the May 2021 lists were posted under a Red GMI with the note that it was "a very difficult time to be in the market profitably." ([WW 2021-05-06](../../raw/posts/2021-05-06-blog-post-day-2-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-but-378-new-highs-among-us-stocks-non-qqq-type-stocks-still-risi.md))
- If the gate is not met, the setup is a *watchlist* item, not a trade.

---

## Step 2 — Qualify the stock (the "ATH" in ATHOSB)

All of these before you look at the oscillator:

1. **Recent all-time high or recent yearly high.** "I am looking for a stock that has been strong enough to reach a high and then becomes oversold and starts to move up." ([WW 2023-07-09](../../raw/posts/2023-07-09-blog-post-day-48-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-my-favorite-set-up-oversold-bounce-black-dot-after-a-recent-yearl.md)) The blue-dot scan operationalises "recent" as within 3 months of a 250-bar monthly high. ([WW 2025-05-22](../../raw/posts/2025-05-22-blog-post-day-20-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-thank-you-for-attending-my-presentation-to-the-boston-ibd-meetup.md))
2. **Above its last green-line top.** No overhead supply — the same rule as the GLB. ([WW 2021-05-06](../../raw/posts/2021-05-06-blog-post-day-2-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-but-378-new-highs-among-us-stocks-non-qqq-type-stocks-still-risi.md))
3. **Still in an up-trend on the daily** — above its 30-day average or 21-day EMA (the black-dot definition), or in a daily RWB / yellowband pattern (the green-dot prerequisite). A bounce in a *declining* stock is not this setup: "green dots can occur in declining stocks but many of them will fail." ([WW 2021-06-13](../../raw/posts/2021-06-13-follow-on-to-traderlion-conference-this-wednesdays-long-island-talk-examples-of-black-dot-signals-gmi6-of-6.md), [WW 2018-03-11](../../raw/posts/2018-03-11-green-dot-strategy-defined.md))
4. **Price > $200** if you are running his scan as he runs it (a filter added August 2021; see the [$200 revision](../methodology/stock-selection.md#price-level-preference)). ([WW 2021-08-11](../../raw/posts/2021-08-11-blog-post-why-buying-stocks-over-200-works-better-revisited-examples-bntx-and-gs.md))
5. **Fundamentals and the earnings date.** IBD composite / RS / expected earnings growth checked, and the next earnings date known — "holding through earnings has been treacherous lately." ([WW 2023-08-03](../../raw/posts/2023-08-03-blog-post-day-67-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-115-new-us-highs-and-44-lows-11-stocks-have-osb-after-recent-ath.md))

---

## Step 3 — Wait for oversold, then the turn (the "OSB")

The oversold measure is "based on the daily 10.1, 10.4 and 10.4.4 stochastics indicators." ([WW 2022-06-15](../../raw/posts/2022-06-15-blog-post-one-of-my-favorite-set-ups-athosb-found-one-stock-sqm-check-it-out-and-see-the-tutorial.md)) He has used three concrete triggers over the years; any one of them is the "bounce day":

| Trigger | Oversold condition | Turn condition | Introduced |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Black dot** | daily 10.1 stochastic ≤ 25 in any of the past 3 days | close > prior close, stock above 30-day avg or 21-day EMA | 2021 ([WW 2021-06-13](../../raw/posts/2021-06-13-follow-on-to-traderlion-conference-this-wednesdays-long-island-talk-examples-of-black-dot-signals-gmi6-of-6.md)); refined 2023 to require a prior recent yearly high ([WW 2023-07-09](../../raw/posts/2023-07-09-blog-post-day-48-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-my-favorite-set-up-oversold-bounce-black-dot-after-a-recent-yearl.md)) |
| **Green dot** | 10.4.4 stochastic, ideally < 50 | fast 10.4 line crosses above the 10.4.4 signal line; dot drawn at the 5-day low | 2018 ([WW 2018-03-11](../../raw/posts/2018-03-11-green-dot-strategy-defined.md)) |
| **Blue dot** | 10.1 stochastic was < 20 yesterday | 10.1 crosses above 20 today; Stoc1.1 > 50 (closed in the top half of the day's range) | 2025 ([WW 2025-05-22](../../raw/posts/2025-05-22-blog-post-day-20-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-thank-you-for-attending-my-presentation-to-the-boston-ibd-meetup.md)) |

Supporting evidence he likes to see on the same chart: a touch of the **lower 15.2 Bollinger Band** during the pullback (the original 2016 BOS trigger, still cited on 2023 OSB charts) ([WW 2016-07-24](../../raw/posts/2016-07-24-how-i-buy-rocket-stocks-bouncing-up-off-of-support-bos-an-example-hii.md), [WW 2023-07-20](../../raw/posts/2023-07-20-blog-post-day-57-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-list-of-4-growth-stocks-with-black-dot-osb-set-up-see-daily-chart.md)), and a bounce off the 8-day EMA ([WW 2025-07-09](../../raw/posts/2025-07-09-blog-post-day-51-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-recent-glb-shak-has-retaken-green-line-after-a-recent-blue-dot-os.md)).

**Do not buy the oversold reading itself.** The entry is the *turn* — the day the dot prints. "Buy on strength" was the BOS rule in 2016 and it has not changed. ([WW 2016-07-24](../../raw/posts/2016-07-24-how-i-buy-rocket-stocks-bouncing-up-off-of-support-bos-an-example-hii.md))

---

## Step 4 — Rank, if the scan returns more than one

Sort by **close ÷ lowest close of the past 250 days** — strongest movers to the top (BELFB at 3.3× its low; NVDA topped the August 2023 list). ([WW 2023-07-09](../../raw/posts/2023-07-09-blog-post-day-48-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-my-favorite-set-up-oversold-bounce-black-dot-after-a-recent-yearl.md), [WW 2023-08-03](../../raw/posts/2023-08-03-blog-post-day-67-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-115-new-us-highs-and-44-lows-11-stocks-have-osb-after-recent-ath.md)) A cluster of hits in one industry is a second-order buy signal — "big buying interest." ([WW 2023-07-10 via the concept page](../methodology/oversold-bounce.md#reading-the-scan-output))

---

## Step 5 — Enter and place the stop, in that order, the same day

- **Entry:** on the bounce day or shortly after, once the dot has printed. He has bought in the after-market on the signal day. ([WW 2023-07-20](../../raw/posts/2023-07-20-blog-post-day-57-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-list-of-4-growth-stocks-with-black-dot-osb-set-up-see-daily-chart.md))
- **Stop:** immediately, just below the **low of the bounce day**. "If I buy any OSB, I place an immediate stop below the bounce." ([WW 2023-08-03](../../raw/posts/2023-08-03-blog-post-day-67-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-115-new-us-highs-and-44-lows-11-stocks-have-osb-after-recent-ath.md)) Worked numerically: WWE bought in the after-market, "will exit if it trades below Thursday's low (104.34)." ([WW 2023-07-20](../../raw/posts/2023-07-20-blog-post-day-57-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-list-of-4-growth-stocks-with-black-dot-osb-set-up-see-daily-chart.md)) For a green-dot entry the dot itself sits at the 5-day low and *is* the stop reference. ([WW 2018-03-11](../../raw/posts/2018-03-11-green-dot-strategy-defined.md))
- **Why this is the whole point.** The stop is the reason he prefers this to the breakout: "with the OSB set-up it is much easier to determine the price where the set-up has failed." ([WW 2023-07-13](../../raw/posts/2023-07-13-blog-post-cavas-break-out-fails-why-i-prefer-my-osb-set-up-over-break-outs-see-daily-charts-of-cava-and-mlm.md)) An entry without the stop is not this setup.

- **Stop variant (2024):** on his black-dot ATH scan he places the stop "just below the low of the **past 2 days**" rather than the bounce day alone — a slightly wider window on the same idea. ([WW 2024-03-12](../../raw/posts/2024-03-12-blog-post-day-44-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-qqq-has-black-dot-oversold-bounce-see-daily-chart-and-list-of-16.md))

---

## Step 6 — Manage

- **Failure is immediate and cheap.** "If I buy an OSB stock I sell immediately if the bounce fails." ([WW 2021-05-06](../../raw/posts/2021-05-06-blog-post-day-2-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-but-378-new-highs-among-us-stocks-non-qqq-type-stocks-still-risi.md)) A close below the bounce low, or (green dot) the fast stochastic closing back under its signal line, ends the trade. ([WW 2018-03-11](../../raw/posts/2018-03-11-green-dot-strategy-defined.md))
- **If it works,** it becomes an ordinary position and the [exits playbook](exits.md) takes over — trail with the daily RWB red-line rule or the weekly 10-week close rule depending on horizon.
- **Earnings:** either be out before the date or accept it as a separate, unhedged risk. ([WW 2023-08-03](../../raw/posts/2023-08-03-blog-post-day-67-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-115-new-us-highs-and-44-lows-11-stocks-have-osb-after-recent-ath.md))

---

## Where this playbook meets the GLB playbook

The two are one workflow, not two. "If I did not buy the day of the gap, I could wait until the stock has an oversold bounce (OSB)." ([WW 2023-01-16](../../raw/posts/2023-01-16-blog-post-gmi-turns-green-and-day-2-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-how-i-trade-a-glb-green-line-breakout-2-exampl.md)) VRT is the worked case — GLB on 2023-08-02, pullback, black-dot OSB on 2023-09-13, "if it holds Wednesday's low of 36.22 I would become interested" ([WW 2023-09-13](../../raw/posts/2023-09-13-blog-post-day-10-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-42-us-new-highs-and-63-lows-vrt-has-osb-oversold-bounce-and-a-rec.md)) — and SHAK repeats it with a blue dot in 2025 ([WW 2025-07-09](../../raw/posts/2025-07-09-blog-post-day-51-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-recent-glb-shak-has-retaken-green-line-after-a-recent-blue-dot-os.md)). So: use [buying-glb](buying-glb.md) to *find* the stock; if you missed the breakout day, or want the tighter stop, use this page to *enter* it.

## Notes / caveats

- The exact composite rule behind the black dot has never been published in full — he invites readers to "see if you can figure out the signals" ([WW 2022-06-15](../../raw/posts/2022-06-15-blog-post-one-of-my-favorite-set-ups-athosb-found-one-stock-sqm-check-it-out-and-see-the-tutorial.md)); the blue dot (2025) is the one trigger disclosed as a formula. The table above uses each dot's stated conditions.
- Nothing here is a hit-rate claim. His stated reason for the preference is stop *definability*, not win rate; see the concept page's ["why he prefers it"](../methodology/oversold-bounce.md#why-he-prefers-it-to-a-breakout).
