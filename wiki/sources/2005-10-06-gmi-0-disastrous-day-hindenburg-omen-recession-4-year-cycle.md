# WW 2005-10-06 — The Hindenburg Omen; failed break-outs as the reason component 1 exists

**Source:** [GMI: 0; Disastrous day; Hindenburg Omen; Recession?  4 year cycle](../../raw/posts/2005-10-06-gmi-0-disastrous-day-hindenburg-omen-recession-4-year-cycle.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/10/gmi-0-disastrous-day-hindenburg-omen-recession-4-year-cycle/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The day the GMI hit zero. Three things are documented here that appear nowhere earlier. First, **the rationale
behind GMI component 1**, attributed rather than asserted: "Many of the gurus cited in the books to the right
have observed that **failed break-outs are a sign of impending market weakness**." Only 54 stocks — 35% — of
those that hit a new high ten days earlier closed higher. Second, **the low-side mirror counted rather than
described**: "there were more (99) '**successful**' 10 day new lows, suggesting that **shorting stocks at new
lows has been profitable**." Third, he names the **Hindenburg Omen**: "I mentioned last post that having a lot
of new highs AND new lows suggested a troubled market. Well, I read today that this phenomenon is known as the
'Hindenburg Omen'" — that day, 41 new yearly highs against 148 new lows. Also here: only 36% of his universe
above the 10-week average, "the lowest number since I began tabulating this statistic on **June 17**," which
dates that series; D-1 of a new QQQQ down-trend; and the bounce-as-exit instruction — "**if we get a bounce
during earnings season the next few days, it might be a good time to unload losers and raise cash.**"

## Key claims

- **Failed break-outs are why component 1 exists** — he credits the observation to the trading literature, not to his own research.
- **The Hindenburg Omen**: simultaneous elevated new highs *and* new lows read as a sign of a market pulling apart. Named here, and the concept ('a lot of new highs AND new lows') was already his the post before.
- The successful-10-day-new-**low** count is in the table on 2005-10-06 — ten months earlier than the August 2006 instance previously documented.
- The % of his universe above the 10-week average has been tabulated since **June 17, 2005**.
- A bounce inside a confirmed down-trend is an opportunity to sell, not to buy: "unload losers and raise cash."
- Context he was reading at the time: cracking housing stocks, Jim Rogers forecasting recession, and the four-year cycle (1962, 66, 70, 74, 78, 82, 87, 90, 94, 98, 02 → 06).
