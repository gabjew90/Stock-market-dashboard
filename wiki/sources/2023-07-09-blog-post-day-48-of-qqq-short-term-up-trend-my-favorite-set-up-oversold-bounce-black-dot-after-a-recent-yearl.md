# WW 2023-07-09 — My favorite set-up: oversold bounce after a recent yearly high; OPRA BELFB

## What it covers

Documents a refinement to the black-dot indicator — the addition of a **prior recent yearly high** requirement — and restates the stop logic that makes the setup his favorite. Calls it "my favorite set-up" in the title, corroborating the ATHOSB framing from 2022.

## Key claims

- The refinement: "I have been refining my black dot oversold indicator by **requiring a prior recent yearly high**."
- The intent behind it: "I am looking for a stock that has been strong enough to reach a high and then becomes oversold and starts to move up."
- The stop rule, restated: "The beauty of such a set-up is that I can place my stop loss below the low of the bounce day. If the bounce fails I am sold out with a small loss."
- **Scan output is ranked by close ÷ minimum price of the last 250 days** — a "how far has this already run" sort. BELFB was 3.3× its low.
- The green-line filter still applies: both OPRA and BELFB are noted as above their last green line tops.
- Honest bookkeeping: "I missed this bounce and do not own it" (OPRA); "BELFB triggered this set-up on Friday and I bought a little."
