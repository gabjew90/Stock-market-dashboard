# WW 2005-09-11 — GMI 6; the new 'doubler' indicator; the truck (2005)

**Source:** [GMI: +6; New “doubler” indicator; GMI and QQQQ trends; WPM shows short AND longer term strength; Staying long](../../raw/posts/2005-09-11-gmi-6-new-doubler-indicator-gmi-and-qqqq-trends-wpm-shows-short-and-longer-term-strength-staying-long.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/09/gmi-6-new-doubler-indicator-gmi-and-qqqq-trends-wpm-shows-short-and-longer-term-strength-staying-long/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"By focusing on the GMI and ignoring everyone's opinions I have been able to remain with the market's trend. One must trade with 'what is' and not what I think or hope should be. As I have written before, if a truck is bearing down on me, it makes no sense to question whether it should be there or heading in my direction. I must react quickly to what is happening." A new indicator: the percentage of stocks that doubled in the past year that closed above their 30-day averages — leader health. 345 new highs; 63% above their 10-week; 48% in a short-term up-trend.

## Key claims

- The truck metaphor is on the blog by September 2005.
- The doubler indicator (doublers above their 30-day) introduced.
- "Trade with 'what is'."
