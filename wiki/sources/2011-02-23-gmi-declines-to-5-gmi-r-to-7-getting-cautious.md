# WW 2011-02-23 — GMI declines to 5; getting cautious — T2108 as a pendulum; what makes him cautious

**Source:** [GMI declines to 5, GMI-R to 7; getting cautious](../../raw/posts/2011-02-23-gmi-declines-to-5-gmi-r-to-7-getting-cautious.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2011/02/gmi-declines-to-5-gmi-r-to-7-getting-cautious/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

T2108 hit 75% then 60%: "acts like a pendulum of the market. Up-trends tend to end near 80% and down-trends below 20%" (chart at freestockcharts.com, symbol T2108). For UMD Stock Challenge readers: "most stocks follow the trend of the general market"; the GMI counts short- and long-term indicators focused on the Nasdaq-100. Day 64 of the up-trend; 24 weeks above the 10-week — "I tend to become cautious when the market indexes close below their 10 week averages or when the GMI declines to 3 or less. I also become concerned when the market leaders, like GOOG, AAPL, NFLX, weaken, as is now the case." "One can always get out of a position and re-enter it if the stock shows your buy signal" (no tax or wash-sale cost in a tax-deferred account). "The key to success in the stock market is to have a lot of little losses and a few very large gains."

## Key claims

- T2108 pendulum: up-trends end near 80%, down-trends below 20% (2011).
- Caution triggers: indexes under their 10-week; GMI ≤ 3; leaders weakening.
- Exit and re-enter freely in tax-deferred accounts.
- "A lot of little losses and a few very large gains."
