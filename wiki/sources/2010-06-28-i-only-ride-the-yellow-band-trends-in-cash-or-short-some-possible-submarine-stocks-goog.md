# WW 2010-06-28 — I only ride the yellow band trends; in cash or short; submarine stocks

**Source:** [I only ride the yellow band trends!  Down-trend, in cash or short; Some possible submarine stocks–GOOG????](../../raw/posts/2010-06-28-i-only-ride-the-yellow-band-trends-in-cash-or-short-some-possible-submarine-stocks-goog.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2010/06/i-only-ride-the-yellow-band-trends-in-cash-or-short-some-possible-submarine-stocks-goog/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"One of the toughest lessons I have learned over the past 40 years is that I should only stay in the market when there is a well defined trend" — the give-back pattern that taught it, and the yellow-band answer, here applied for the first time to a *daily* Guppy chart of the QQQQ: a yellow band can be drawn between certain short- and longer-term averages that are rising or falling together, and "it is during yellow band trends that I can usually make money trading consistent with the trend." GMI 0, D-1; QQQQ/SPY under their 10-week for 8 weeks; on a new down-trend he sometimes buys "a little QID or TYP… with the idea of selling it if the trend reverses up, or adding to my position if the market decline deepens. The key to trading success is to have many small losses and a few very large gains." A TC2007 submarine scan returned 95 names; ten listed, GOOG among them, as possible Stage 4 declines.

## Key claims

- "I should only stay in the market when there is a well defined trend."
- Yellow band applied to the Guppy daily chart of the index, not only to stocks.
- "The key to trading success is to have many small losses and a few very large gains."
- Small ultra-short (QID/TYP) on Day 1 of a down-trend; sell on reversal, add if it deepens.
- Submarine scan: 95 hits; GOOG on the list.
