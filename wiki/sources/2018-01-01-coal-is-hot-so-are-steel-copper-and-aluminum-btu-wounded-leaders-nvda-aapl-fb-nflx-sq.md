# WW 2018-01-01 — The red lines and the blue lines read as two different warnings

**Source:** [Coal is hot!! So are steel, copper and aluminum: $BTU; Wounded leaders: $NVDA $AAPL $FB $NFLX $SQ](../../raw/posts/2018-01-01-coal-is-hot-so-are-steel-copper-and-aluminum-btu-wounded-leaders-nvda-aapl-fb-nflx-sq.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2018/01/coal-is-hot-so-are-steel-copper-and-aluminum-btu-wounded-leaders-nvda-aapl-fb-nflx-sq/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The daily RWB chart read as two separate warning levels, not one.** With the QQQ closing below all six red
short-term averages for the first time since early December: "**As long as it closes above the blue line averages
I am not too concerned. However, the QQQ needs to close back above all of the red lines to resume the up-trend**,
as it did in October and December. The RWB pattern also needs to endure with all of the red lines above the blue
lines with a white space between them." Losing the red lines is a pause; losing the blue lines is the problem —
which is what makes the RLC a caution counter rather than an exit trigger at the index level. **The stock-level
exit stated crisply**: "**I don't wait around holding declining stocks. Buy and hope is not my style. Once a
stock closes 2 days below its red lines I like to exit.** As the great William O'Neil has written, all stocks are
bad, unless they are rising." **An industry-group ranking by monthly performance**: for December 2017, copper
(+19.4%), aluminium (+18.4%), coal (+11.2%), industrial metals and minerals (+9.8%), steel (+8.6%) — against
semiconductors at +0.12% and internet content at +2.9% — with the framing "**we growth stock investors are so
focused on the innovative tech stocks that we often overlook the strongest non-tech stocks.**" **And "wounded
leaders" as a top tell**: NVDA, AAPL, FB, NFLX and SQ charted together — "I become concerned when many of the
market leaders look wounded… **are the others forming their bull market tops?**" The GMI was at 6 while the GMI-2
had weakened to 4 of 8.

## Key claims

- **Two thresholds on one chart**: below the red lines is a pause, below the blue lines is a trend problem. The RLC counts the first, not the second.
- **"Once a stock closes 2 days below its red lines I like to exit"** — the stock-level rule at two days, with "buy and hope is not my style" as the rationale.
- **Industry groups ranked by monthly performance and new highs** as a rotation screen, run deliberately outside tech.
- **"Wounded leaders" as a plural tell** — the concern is several leaders damaged at once, not one.
- O'Neil's "all stocks are bad unless they are rising" quoted a third time across the corpus, always attributed.
- GMI 6 against GMI-2 at 4 of 8 — the sensitive index leading the slow one down again.
