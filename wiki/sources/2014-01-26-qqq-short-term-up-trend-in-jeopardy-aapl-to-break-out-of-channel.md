# WW 2014-01-26 — 52 short-term up-trends since 2006 — the duration table, five years before the 2019 tabulation

**Source:** [$QQQ short term up-trend in jeopardy; $AAPL to break out of channel?](../../raw/posts/2014-01-26-qqq-short-term-up-trend-in-jeopardy-aapl-to-break-out-of-channel.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2014/01/qqq-short-term-up-trend-in-jeopardy-aapl-to-break-out-of-channel/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The earliest published duration *table* in the corpus: "since 2006, there have been **52 short term up-trends in the QQQ**," with a chart of the ten longest; the up-trend then running was in its **72nd day and the fourth longest since 2006**, "the way I define them." The read is deliberately two-sided: "the end of a short term up-trend has not necessarily implied the beginning of an extended short term down-trend. The new down-trend could become only a short pause in this long term up-trend." But with T2108, the put/call ratio and his other indicators "not at extremely oversold levels," he concludes "we have more of a decline to come" — and is 90%+ cash in the trading accounts while fully invested in the pension.

## Key claims

- 52 QQQ short-term up-trends between 2006 and January 2014, with a published table of the ten longest.
- A 72-day up-trend was the fourth longest in that span.
- The end of an up-trend does not imply a sustained down-trend.
- Absence of oversold extremes as the argument that a decline has further to run.
