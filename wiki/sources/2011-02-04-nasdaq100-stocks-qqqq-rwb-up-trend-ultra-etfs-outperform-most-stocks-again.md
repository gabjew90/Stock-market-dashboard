# WW 2011-02-04 — Never average down, always average up — and each purchase higher than the last

**Source:** [Nasdaq100 stocks (QQQQ): RWB up-trend; Ultra ETF’s outperform most stocks again](../../raw/posts/2011-02-04-nasdaq100-stocks-qqqq-rwb-up-trend-ultra-etfs-outperform-most-stocks-again.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2011/02/nasdaq100-stocks-qqqq-rwb-up-trend-ultra-etfs-outperform-most-stocks-again/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A concrete position-management rule, stated while pointing out that the Nasdaq-100 *index itself* showed the weekly RWB pattern: "It may make sense to own some of this index by buying any of the bull ETF's or ultra ETF's that track it: QQQQ, QLD, TQQQ. **I usually wade into these in stages with each purchase having to be higher than the previous one. I never add to a long position that is moving down — I always average up.**" The arithmetic behind preferring the index: since November 30, QQQQ +9.5%, QLD +19.9%, TQQQ +30.8%, while "only 3% of the Nasdaq 100 component stocks have advanced 30% or more and 17% advanced 20% or more."

## Key claims

- **"I never add to a long position that is moving down — I always average up."**
- **Each successive purchase must be at a higher price than the last** — the one concrete sizing rule in the corpus.
- The RWB pattern read on an index, not just a stock, as the reason to own the index.
- Ultra-ETF arithmetic: TQQQ +30.8% vs 3% of Nasdaq-100 stocks up ≥30% over the same stretch.
