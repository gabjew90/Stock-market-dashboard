# WW 2018-08-26 — Nineteen recent IPOs at an ATH with a recent GLB — the barchart→TC2000 workflow

**Source:** [19 Recent IPOs at ATH with recent GLB: $MTCH $TWLO $SEND $SFIX $ROKU $CABO….](../../raw/posts/2018-08-26-19-recent-ipos-at-ath-with-recent-glb-mtch-twlo-send-sfix-roku-cabo.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2018/08/19-recent-ipos-at-ath-with-recent-glb-mtch-twlo-send-sfix-roku-cabo/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A reproducible workflow built from two tools. "**Every rocket stock has a long series of repeated ATH.**" He pulls the day's all-time-high list from **barchart.com**, copies it via Excel into a TC2000 watchlist, then isolates recent IPOs with a column formula — `c/c4` set to **yearly**, where a result of `**` means the stock did not trade before 2015 and so cannot be calculated. He then reads each monthly chart by hand for an ATH followed by a consolidation of at least three months, draws the green line at the pre-consolidation peak, and keeps those that have since closed above it, preferring above-average volume in the GLB week. The lineage: "**the great trader, Jesse Livermore, liked to buy the first GLB (he did not use my term!) of a recent IPO.**" Exit unchanged: "if a GLB stock *closes* back below its green line, I sell it immediately."

## Key claims

- A cross-tool screen: barchart.com ATH list → Excel → TC2000 watchlist → `c/c4` yearly column to isolate post-2015 IPOs.
- "Every rocket stock has a long series of repeated ATH."
- The green line is still drawn by hand on the monthly chart after the screen narrows the field.
- Livermore credited specifically with the first-GLB-of-a-recent-IPO idea.
- Above-average volume in the week of the GLB preferred.
