# WW 2021-05-23 — Revised OSB (oversold bounce) scan yields GS and BGFV

**Source:** [13th day of $QQQ short term down-trend; Revised OSB (oversold bounce) scan yields $GS and $BGFV](../../raw/posts/2021-05-23-blog-post-13th-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-revised-osb-oversold-bounce-scan-yields-gs-and-bgfv.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2021/05/blog-post-13th-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-revised-osb-oversold-bounce-scan-yields-gs-and-bgfv/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Gives the one-line functional definition of the revised OSB scan and pairs it with an explicit market-state caveat. Dr. Wish is publishing OSB candidates while the GMI is on a Red signal, and says plainly that this is a hard environment to trade — a useful corrective to reading the scan output as a buy list.

## Key claims

- Scan definition: "It detects stocks near a recent ATH that have bounced from an over-sold level."
- The signals are rendered as **black and green dots** on the daily chart.
- Candidates are conditional: "as long as the bounce lows hold" — the stop is the bounce low.
- Market-state caveat: with the GMI Red and QQQ weak while SPY and DIA held up, "This is a very difficult time to be in the market profitably. Much easier to trade growth stocks when the GMI is Green and consistently registering 5 or 6."
