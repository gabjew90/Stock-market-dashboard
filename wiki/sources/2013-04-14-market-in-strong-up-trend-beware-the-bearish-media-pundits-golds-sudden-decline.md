# WW 2013-04-14 — SPY and DIA break their green lines; react, don't anticipate; GLD's failed GLB

**Source:** [Market  in strong up-trend; Beware the bearish media pundits; Gold’s “sudden” decline](../../raw/posts/2013-04-14-market-in-strong-up-trend-beware-the-bearish-media-pundits-golds-sudden-decline.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2013/04/market-in-strong-up-trend-beware-the-bearish-media-pundits-golds-sudden-decline/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"It is amazing to me how many of the media pundits are saying the market is too high when the SPY and DIA have just broken through their green line tops to all-time highs. When a stock or index does this, it often is followed by a multi-month rise… the index or stock should stay above its green line level. If it fails, like GLD and AAPL did a while ago, then I would expect a major reversal." "The critical key is to not anticipate a change in trend, but to react *after* the signal is evident. Such is the goal of the trend-*follower*." DIA and SPY in weekly RWB; QQQ in RWB but far below its March-2000 green line (~119). The new-high + technical strength + earnings scan (7 names; SFLY, LNKD). GLD: under its 30-week in a Stage 4 since February, its July GLB failed and a new green line formed — "the time to buy a stock or ETF is when it first breaks *above* a green line top to an all-time high… That is how the great Nicolas Darvas made a fortune."

## Key claims

- Index GLBs (SPY, DIA, April 2013) as multi-month-rise signals; failure = major reversal.
- "Not anticipate a change in trend, but react after the signal is evident."
- GLD as the failed-GLB / Stage 4 example; a failed GLB establishes a new green line.
- QQQ's green line from March 2000 at ~119.
