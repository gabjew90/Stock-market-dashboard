# WW 2006-01-03 — What if? — the manifesto; trade with the trend

**Source:** [Happy New Year–What if?; GMI: +2; Trade with the trend](../../raw/posts/2006-01-03-happy-new-year-what-if-gmi-2-trade-with-the-trend.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2006/01/happy-new-year-what-if-gmi-2-trade-with-the-trend/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A New Year's credo in the form of six 'what ifs' — no one can predict the market, pundits predict the past, Cramer is a modern Elmer Gantry, the market is a casino à la Darvas, analysts promote an illusion of order, psychology beats fundamentals — with the conclusion: "isolate ourselves from outside opinions, use our charts to maximize the chances of a gain, place our bets, and quickly and ruthlessly cut our losses. In harmony with the trend and with thoughtful trial and error, each small loss brings us closer to the next big gain." Then the read: GMI +2, D-8, only 39% successful 10-day highs, QQQQ under its 10-week for the first time in nine weeks, 3% of the 176 doublers at a new high ("the leaders have stalled"), advisers 60.4% bullish vs 20.8% bearish (a contrary indicator), WPM deteriorating in all five indexes — "for me, this is a time to be short or in cash… I can turn on a dime, or dollar, along with the GMI."

## Key claims

- The six 'what ifs' and their consequence: isolate, chart, bet, cut — trade in harmony with the trend.
- "Each small loss brings us closer to the next big gain."
- Investors' newsletter sentiment (60.4% bulls / 20.8% bears) used as a contrary indicator in 2006.
- Doubler breadth: 3% of the 176 stocks that doubled in 250 days made a new high — leaders stalling as a read.
- "The key to conservation of capital is not to fight the current market down trend."
