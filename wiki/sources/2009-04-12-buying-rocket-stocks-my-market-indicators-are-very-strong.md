# WW 2009-04-12 — Rockets to the moon, not bargains at new lows

**Source:** [Buying Rocket Stocks; My market indicators are very strong.](../../raw/posts/2009-04-12-buying-rocket-stocks-my-market-indicators-are-very-strong.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2009/04/buying-rocket-stocks-my-market-indicators-are-very-strong/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The core selection principle in its most quotable form, written as the 2009 bottom turned: "Stocks on the 52 week new high lists that are also at or near all time highs is where I find potential rockets. Nicolas Darvas made a fortune trading such stocks. **While most of the world looks for bargain stocks at new lows, I seek rockets heading to the moon.** The greatest winning stocks are those heading up to ever higher levels. Think of how many times that YHOO or CSCO or MSFT hit all time new highs as they multiplied their prices many times during their great bull runs. **If you want to hop a rocket to the moon, it better be pointing up and gaining altitude.**" The GMI had registered 5 for the first time since August 2008 and the Successful 10-Day New High Index had finally turned positive; the IBD100 names near all-time highs included GMCR — the stock that became the 13-bagger.

## Key claims

- "While most of the world looks for bargain stocks at new lows, I seek rockets heading to the moon."
- "If you want to hop a rocket to the moon, it better be pointing up and gaining altitude."
- Serial new all-time highs (YHOO, CSCO, MSFT) as the evidence that strength compounds.
- The component-1 turn as the signal that buying new highs had become viable again.
