## What it covers

The most thoroughly measured statement of the leveraged-ETF case. From day one of the up-trend he called on 4/28 through 6/9, **TQQQ advanced 29.6%**; "only six of the Nasdaq 100 stocks did better… If you could have successfully picked these 6 stocks on 4/28, then just ignore this strategy and maybe tell the rest of us how you do it." It also beat all but five S&P 500 stocks — "**TQQQ outperformed 99% of the S&P 500 stocks**" — and, checked against his MarketSmith Growth 250 watchlist from 4/13, "**TQQQ even beat more than 90% of those promising stocks**." The moral and the caveat together: "buy TQQQ instead of trying to predict in advance the small percentage of stocks that will beat it… **keep in mind that TQQQ is triple leveraged and moves 3x as fast as QQQ, *up or down*. So one must have a valid indicat**"or of the trend.

## Key claims

- Measured three ways: vs the Nasdaq 100 (6 ahead), the S&P 500 (5 ahead, 99% beaten), and a curated 250-stock growth watchlist (>90% beaten).
- Testing against a *curated* growth list is the strongest version of the claim — the comparison set was pre-selected for quality.
- "Buy TQQQ instead of trying to predict in advance the small percentage of stocks that will beat it."
- The leverage caveat restated: 3× up or down, so a valid trend indicator is the precondition.
