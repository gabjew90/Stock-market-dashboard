# WW 2019-11-24 — Why I sold INMD at $57 — the 'too easy' voice; ideas from Twitter

**Source:** [Why I sold $INMD at $57; Following the tweets of some smart traders: @markminervini and @TMLTrader](../../raw/posts/2019-11-24-why-i-sold-inmd-at-57-following-the-tweets-of-some-smart-traders-markminervini-and-tmltrader.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2019/11/why-i-sold-inmd-at-57-following-the-tweets-of-some-smart-traders-markminervini-and-tmltrader/) · tier: trade_example

## What it covers

A discretionary sell rule that sits above the mechanical ones: when a position makes money too fast — Yahoo up $50 in a week around $400 pre-split in the 1990s; INMD up ~$9 pre-market on 11/18, vertical, outside its upper 15.2 daily Bollinger Band on very heavy volume — an "internal voice" says making money is too easy, and he sells. INMD: bought November 6 (a strong recent IPO at ATHs, tweeted by @markminervini) with the intent to sell below the prior day's low; sold ~$57 for a 40%+ gain in 9 days; it closed the week at $41.55. "Bulls make money, Bears make money and Pigs get slaughtered." He credits @markminervini and @TMLTrader as idea sources.

## Key claims

- "Whenever my voice cautions me that way, I sell. (It rarely happens!)" — the trigger is a parabolic move: vertical, outside the upper 15.2 daily BB, on very heavy volume.
- "When I make money too fast, I sell."
- Entry discipline on a tweeted idea: bought at ATH with a stop below the prior day's low.
- Twitter as an idea channel: @markminervini, @TMLTrader (met at Minervini's workshop).
