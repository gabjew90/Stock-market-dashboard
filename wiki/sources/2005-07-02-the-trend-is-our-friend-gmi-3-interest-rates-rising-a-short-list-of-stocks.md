# WW 2005-07-02 — The trend is our friend — the 'M' in CAN SLIM; the odds in a decline

**Source:** [The trend is our friend; GMI: +3; Interest rates rising; a short list of stocks](../../raw/posts/2005-07-02-the-trend-is-our-friend-gmi-3-interest-rates-rising-a-short-list-of-stocks.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/07/the-trend-is-our-friend-gmi-3-interest-rates-rising-a-short-list-of-stocks/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"Why do I continually talk about the GMI? It is because the majority of stocks follow the trend of the general market averages. It is exemplified by the 'M' in CANSLIM… about 70% of stocks follow the market trend… This is one of the hardest lessons to learn. In the past, I would lose all of my hard earned trading profits and more, when I kept buying break-out stocks in the inevitable decline that followed a bull move." D-6: 38% of Nasdaq-100 stocks rose — a long had a 38% chance, a short 62%.

## Key claims

- The 70% rule tied to O'Neil's 'M'.
- The autobiographical failure: buying breakouts into the decline that follows a bull move.
- D-6 odds: 38% long / 62% short.
