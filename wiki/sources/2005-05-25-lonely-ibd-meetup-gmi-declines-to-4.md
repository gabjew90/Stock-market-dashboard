# WW 2005-05-25 — An empty meetup room as a contrary indicator

**Source:** [Lonely IBD Meetup, GMI declines to +4](../../raw/posts/2005-05-25-lonely-ibd-meetup-gmi-declines-to-4.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/05/lonely-ibd-meetup-gmi-declines-to-4/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The sentiment gauge nobody else has: how many people turn up to the local IBD meetup. "Normally about 8-10
persons get together to share ideas about stock trading. **Tonight, just 3 persons showed up, including me.**"
The group leader — "who has lived through several market cycles" — was both unsurprised and bearish, predicting
"it would take a few months of an up market with people making money, for attendance to pick up again," and was
"skeptical about the longevity of the current market rise and was not interested in pursuing volatile growth
stocks." Dr. Wish's own indicators said the opposite, and he backed them: "**I have to conclude that the low
attendance and caution about the market constitute contrarian indicators, suggesting that this rally has more to
go.**" On a weak day inside an up-trend (U-14; only 21% of the Nasdaq-100 rising) his stance is unchanged, and
the two entry types are already stated as a pair in May 2005: "I plan to **increase my positions as my stocks
break to new highs or bounce off of their support levels**. Of course, I also have my sell stops in place in case
things suddenly turn ugly."

## Key claims

- **Meetup attendance as a sentiment series** — the room empties near lows and refills only after people have been making money for months, which makes it lagging as a gauge and contrarian as a signal.
- **A weak day inside an up-trend does not change the stance.** U-14 with 21% of the Nasdaq-100 advancing, and he adds on strength anyway.
- **Both entry types stated together in May 2005**: buy the breakout to a new high *or* the bounce off support — eleven years before BOS was named.
- He backs his indicators against an experienced peer's judgement and records the disagreement rather than deferring.
- Stops are mentioned in the same breath as adding — the position grows and the protection grows with it.
