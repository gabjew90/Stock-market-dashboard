# WW 2017-09-17 — The yellowband scan criteria; purple dots; the club channel

**Source:** [Yellowband scan picks up $UCTT, $NVDA, $FSLR, $HNH, $LOXO, $EXEL, $NVMI, $CORT](../../raw/posts/2017-09-17-yellowband-scan-picks-up-uctt-nvda-fslr-hnh-loxo-exel-nvmi-cort.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2017/09/yellowband-scan-picks-up-uctt-nvda-fslr-hnh-loxo-exel-nvmi-cort/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The yellowband pattern turned into a scan, with criteria — further confirmation (with
2017-06-25, ingested earlier today) that yellowband was an active 2017 framework, not a
superseded term. Also documents the **TC2000 club** distribution channel and the purple-dot
chart convention.

## Key claims

- **The scan criteria:** "up at least **70% from their 50 week low**, within **20% of their 20
  week high**, in a **yellowband pattern**, and have **regained their 10 week average last
  week**." A strength filter, a proximity filter, the structural pattern, and a fresh-bounce
  trigger — the same four-part shape as his other scans.
- **The exit attached at purchase:** "If I bought UCTT based on its yellowband pattern, I would
  consider selling or reducing my position **if it closed any week (or day) back below its
  rising 10 week average.**"
- **Purple dots decoded:** on his weekly yellowband charts "the purple dots indicate the weekly
  lows" — in a healthy yellowband the weekly *lows* ride above the 10-week average, a stricter
  read than closes alone.
- **The UCTT header read:** earnings +2850%, revenue +76%, already quadrupled in 12 months,
  above its last green line top, next earnings date noted — the standard fundamental overlay on
  a technical hit.
- **The club channel:** "You can run this scan yourself (09162017yellowband…) in TC2000 by
  **joining my club**... at wishingwealthblog.com/club" — a named off-blog distribution channel
  for his actual scan files, alongside Twitter (@wishingwealth) for intraday alerts.
- The standing humility line: "most successful traders are only right on about **50%** of their
  trades."
