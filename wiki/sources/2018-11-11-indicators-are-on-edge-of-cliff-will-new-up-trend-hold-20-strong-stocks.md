# WW 2018-11-11 — His students polled as a contrarian indicator

**Source:** [Indicators are on edge of cliff–will new up-trend hold? 20 strong stocks](../../raw/posts/2018-11-11-indicators-are-on-edge-of-cliff-will-new-up-trend-hold-20-strong-stocks.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2018/11/indicators-are-on-edge-of-cliff-will-new-up-trend-hold-20-strong-stocks/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**A sentiment gauge he built out of his own classroom.** "Perhaps because the GMI changed to Green last week,
**80% of my students were bullish on Friday, the highest reading this semester. In the past, my student poll has
performed well as a contrarian indicator. So watch out below!**" He polls the class, tracks the series across a
semester, and reads it against the position — which puts it alongside the
[meetup-attendance](../methodology/risk-and-cash.md) and reader-mail gauges as a third crowd he can observe
directly. What makes this one sharper is that he knows *why* the reading moved: the GMI had just turned Green, so
his own published signal was feeding the sentiment he then reads contrarily. That is a small, honest observation
about teaching — the students' bullishness is partly his own doing. Alongside it, a screen: stocks with "recent
**triple digit earnings gains**, are near all-time highs and in **daily and weekly RWB up-trends**," with columns
for last-quarter EPS change and one-year performance.

## Key claims

- **He polls his students each week and treats the series as a contrarian indicator** — a third observable crowd, after the meetup room and his readers.
- **80% bullish was the semester's high** and he read it as a warning.
- He notes the reading was probably caused by his own GMI turning Green — the sentiment he is fading is partly his own signal propagating.
- The accompanying screen requires *both* daily and weekly RWB plus triple-digit quarterly EPS growth and proximity to an all-time high.
