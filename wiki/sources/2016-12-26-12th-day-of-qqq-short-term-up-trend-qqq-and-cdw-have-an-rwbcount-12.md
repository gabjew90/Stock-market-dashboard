# WW 2016-12-26 — RWBCount introduced — 'I have created a new indicator'

**Source:** [12th day of $QQQ short term up-trend; $QQQ and $CDW have an RWBCount= 12](../../raw/posts/2016-12-26-12th-day-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-qqq-and-cdw-have-an-rwbcount-12.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2016/12/12th-day-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-qqq-and-cdw-have-an-rwbcount-12/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The indicator's introduction, with the full construction. His adapted weekly Guppy chart uses **13** averages: six short exponential (3, 5, 8, 10, 12, 15) in red, six long (30, 35, 40, 45, 50, 60) in blue, plus a 13th of length 1 as a dotted line for the weekly close. RWB is red rising above blue with white space between; but "**when all 13 averages line up with each shorter average rising above the next one (1>3>5>8……..60) this constitutes a really strong up-trend.** I have created a new indicator that counts the number of averages that are rising above each longer average. **The indicator, called the RWBCount, goes from 0-12.** This indicator can be applied to both ETFs and individual stocks and in the future I will often provide the RWBCount." QQQ, DIA and SPY all read 12 that week.

## Key claims

- **RWBCount is introduced 2016-12-26** — "I have created a new indicator" — a month before the January 2017 post the wiki cited for it.
- It counts strict ordering (1>3>5>…>60), not merely red-above-blue: a stricter test than the RWB pattern itself.
- Range 0–12; applies to ETFs and individual stocks alike.
- The full period list restated: red 3/5/8/10/12/15, blue 30/35/40/45/50/60, plus a length-1 close line.
