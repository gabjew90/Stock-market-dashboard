# WW 2024-01-08 — The blue dot defined; a trend call made on the duration statistic

**Source:** [Blog Post: Day 2 of the new $QQQ short term down-trend; it could end on Tuesday as we enter 4th quarter earnings release period; many blue dot signals, see chart of $AMD](../../raw/posts/2024-01-08-blog-post-day-2-of-the-new-qqq-short-term-down-trend-it-could-end-on-tuesday-as-we-enter-4th-quarter-earnings.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2024/01/blog-post-day-2-of-the-new-qqq-short-term-down-trend-it-could-end-on-tuesday-as-we-enter-4th-quarter-earnings-release-period-many-blue-dot-signals-see-chart-of-amd/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The blue dot, defined in a parenthesis**: "my scan for stocks having a **blue dot (daily 10.4 oversold
stochastic climbing back above 20)**" — the fast 10.4 stochastic crossing back *up through 20*, which
distinguishes it from the green dot (a fast/slow crossover below 50) and the black dot. **And the duration
statistic used to make a call, then acted on**: "I wrote yesterday that **40% of new QQQ short term down-trends
end in less than 5 days.** I subsequently **tweeted on Monday that it looked to me like the current down-trend
might end on Tuesday**… **I sold my small position in SQQQ and bought a little TQQQ.**" The base rate is not
merely reported here — it generates a forecast, the forecast is published on Twitter with a date, and the
position is reversed on it. That is the most direct instance in the corpus of a duration statistic driving a
trade. Corroborating evidence he cites: 91% of Nasdaq-100 stocks rose that Monday, and the blue-dot scan returned
79 names from his watchlist.

## Key claims

- **Blue dot = the daily 10.4 oversold stochastic climbing back above 20** — the definition, distinct from the green dot's crossover.
- **The 40%-under-5-days statistic used to generate a dated forecast**, published on Twitter, and acted on by reversing SQQQ into TQQQ.
- The clearest case of a base rate driving a position rather than merely describing one.
- "The market often declines before rising as earnings are released" — the seasonal overlay on the call.
- The blue-dot scan's yield that day: 79 names from the IBD/MarketSmith watchlist.
