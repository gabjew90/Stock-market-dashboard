# WW 2010-12-13 — The 95-year Dow cycle framework: monthly 5/30 MAs + 25.4.4 stochastic

**Source:** [Where are we in the market cycle? Riding the up-trend](../../raw/posts/2010-12-13-where-are-we-in-the-market-cycle.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2010/12/where-are-we-in-the-market-cycle/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

His slowest instrument: a *monthly*-chart cycle framework validated by eye against 95 years of
Dow data (back to 1915, the limit of TC2007). The wiki had daily/weekly/30-week tooling but
nothing at cycle scale. Also carries one of his best statements of the simple-over-complex
principle.

## Key claims

- **The framework:** monthly Dow with the **5-month and 30-month simple moving averages** plus a
  **25.4.4 monthly stochastic**. Up-cycle: price above a rising 30-month, 5-month above
  30-month, stochastic parked above 80 ("an area where it has stayed for years in some previous
  rising markets"). Top-warning: the stochastic begins a steady decline (it "foreshadowed" the
  2000 top from 1999) and the 5-month crosses below the 30-month.
- **The bottom signature:** "Major bottoms in the Dow have occurred with this stochastic below
  50% and the more severe ones, around 20%," with price below a declining 30-month.
- **The standing rule derived from it:** "I won't begin to suspect the end of the current
  up-trend until I see the stochastic turn down and the 5 month average decline below the 30
  month average."
- **Simple beats clever, stated at full strength:** "patterns are meant to be broken. **The road
  is littered with the carcasses of Ph.D.'s who wagered heavily and lost**, based upon
  complicated mathematical relationships that worked in the past. I can tell you that the
  *simple* patterns I have discussed... seem to me to have worked well over the past 95 years."
- Applied on the day: December 2010 read as "a strong up-trend with no sign of any weakening
  yet" — GMI 6/6, GMI-R 10/10, 15 straight weeks above the 10-week averages — so he was adding
  QLD/SPY/DIA in the conservative accounts.
