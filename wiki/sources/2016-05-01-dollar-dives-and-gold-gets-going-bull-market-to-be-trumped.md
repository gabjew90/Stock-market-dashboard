# WW 2016-05-01 — GMMA on the dollar and gold; the down-sloping neckline

**Source:** [Dollar Dives and Gold Gets Going–bull market to be Trumped?](../../raw/posts/2016-05-01-dollar-dives-and-gold-gets-going-bull-market-to-be-trumped.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2016/05/dollar-dives-and-gold-gets-going-bull-market-to-be-trumped/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The GMMA applied to a macro pair, and a chart-pattern rule stated precisely. **Dollar and gold read against
each other**: the weekly UUP chart "shows a developing BWR decline pattern. The six shorter term averages (red
lines) are now below the longer term averages (blue lines). **The white space separating them is yet to clearly
develop**" — a BWR in formation rather than confirmed — while GLD "now shows the opposite advancing RWB pattern
with a white space developing." His conclusion is instrument-first: "**I will leave it to others to opine about
why the dollar is declining**… Regardless, for now, **with GLD in an RWB up-trend, it may be advisable for me to
hop on the gold train**." He does lay out the macro chain — weak dollar, higher commodity prices, more inflation,
a Fed response, "**such a scenario of higher interest rates would eventually hurt the stock market. It always
has**" — but files it as context, not as the trigger. **The neckline rule**: on the QQQ's possible head-and-
shoulders top, "**the downward sloping neckline, caused by the most recent decline having ended lower than the
prior decline, is considered by technicians to be a major sign of weakness.**" And on the SPY, gyrating with the
2014–mid-2015 RWB "clearly over": "**at some point a persistent RWB or BWR pattern will develop and it will pay
to be fully invested with that trend**" — patience during the in-between. GMI 3 of 6, GMI2 1 of 8, QQQ below its
10-week average.

## Key claims

- **The RWB/BWR framework applied to currency and commodity ETFs**, and read as a *pair* — UUP forming BWR while GLD forms RWB.
- **A pattern in formation is distinguished from a confirmed one** by the white space: red below blue is not yet BWR until the gap opens.
- **A down-sloping neckline is the bearish variant** — it arises when the most recent decline bottoms below the prior one.
- The macro chain is written out and then explicitly set aside: "I will leave it to others to opine about why."
- "Higher interest rates would eventually hurt the stock market. **It always has**" — an unhedged historical claim, unusual for him, and offered without evidence.
- The in-between regime named: when neither pattern holds, wait rather than position.
