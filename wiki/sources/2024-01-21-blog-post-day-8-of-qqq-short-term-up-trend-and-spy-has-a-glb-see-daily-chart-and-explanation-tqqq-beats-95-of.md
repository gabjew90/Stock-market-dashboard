## What it covers

The leveraged-ETF claim re-measured on a live trend: since the January 8, 2024 up-trend signal, QQQ +4% and TQQQ +11.9%, and "only five of the Nasdaq 100 stocks and 6 of the S&P500 stocks have advanced more than TQQQ… **Thus, as I have shown repeatedly, in a QQQ up-trend TQQQ advances more than 95% of stocks. Why search for the rare needles in the hay stock (5% or less) that can beat TQQQ. I just ride TQQQ.**" (He still held smaller positions in three of the six.) The chart shows the dot sequence in order — a **blue dot** oversold bounce on January 8, then a **green dot**, with black dots marking other oversold bounces — followed by SPY's green-line breakout on above-average volume, its first above the January 2022 top. Tutorials for adding the dots were built by his late co-instructor David McCandlish.

## Key claims

- In a QQQ up-trend TQQQ beats >95% of Nasdaq-100 and S&P 500 stocks — measured on the January 2024 advance.
- Accumulate TQQQ 'as the short term up-trend developed' rather than all at once.
- The dot family read together on one chart: blue dot, then green dot, black dots for other bounces.
- SPY's January 2024 GLB above its January 2022 green line.
