# WW 2018-02-19 — The contrarian gauges are timelier than the GMI at bottoms

**Source:** [GMI may turn Green on Tuesday; $ITA moves up](../../raw/posts/2018-02-19-gmi-may-turn-green-on-tuesday-ita-moves-up.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2018/02/gmi-may-turn-green-on-tuesday-ita-moves-up/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**A clean statement of what each instrument is good for, and when.** "**Remember, trend followers *follow* the
trend and do not anticipate a turn. The GMI typically turns Green several days after a bottom is in.**" That is
the lag, owned. And then the exception, in the same breath: "**As I wrote, however, the put/call ratio and T2108
had extremely bearish readings that did signal the bottom on the day it was reached.**" So the contrarian gauges
*can* mark a bottom in real time where the trend indicator structurally cannot — which is why he watches both and
why the sentiment readings are treated as a bounce warning rather than as noise. It also explains the shape of
the whole system: the fast gauges tell you a turn is *possible*, the trend count tells you it has *happened*, and
he trades the second while reading the first. His caution about what a bottom does not guarantee: "whether this
short term bottom holds and the market regains all-time highs is yet to be seen. **If the market should stall out
before reaching new highs, the next decline could be swift.**"

## Key claims

- **"The GMI typically turns Green several days after a bottom is in"** — the lag stated as a structural property, not a flaw.
- **The put/call ratio and T2108 "did signal the bottom on the day it was reached"** — the contrarian gauges are timelier than the trend indicator, by construction.
- The division of labour: fast gauges say a turn is possible, the trend count says it happened, and he acts on the second.
- A bottom that stalls before new highs is flagged as the dangerous case — recovery without confirmation.
- He also sorts all ETFs by dollars gained over 30 days, which surfaced the volatility funds (VXX, VIXY) at the top.
