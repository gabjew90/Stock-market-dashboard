## What it covers

With the GMI at 0 and the longer trend down, the *hourly* adapted GMMA shows QQQ in a new RWB up-trend and 69% of Nasdaq-100 stocks in the same hourly pattern — "which suggests to me this rally has some legs." The rule beneath it: "a change in the longer term trend always begins with a change in the shorter term trend," and any short-term flip "needs to last 5 days for me to really trust it"; he might buy a little TQQQ with a close stop. Also a scan on his 780-stock IBD/MarketSmith watchlist — near an ATH *and* weekly relative strength vs SPY at a 50-week high — yielding 12 names sorted by one-year change (HRMY +76%); "any stock that can come through the recent market down-trend near an ATH is worth monitoring," while fallen leaders may never regain their ATH because trapped holders sell into every recovery. LLY (tirzepatide phase 3) as the example, with a successful GLB retest.

## Key claims

- "A change in the longer term trend always begins with a change in the shorter term trend."
- Hourly breadth: 69% of Nasdaq-100 components in an hourly RWB up-trend read as rally durability.
- "Any change in the short term trend needs to last 5 days for me to really trust it."
- Scan: watchlist stocks near an ATH with weekly RS vs SPY at a 50-week high; sorted by change from 250 days ago.
- Overhead supply stated for fallen leaders: holders "are grateful to sell as soon as they can get their original investments back."
