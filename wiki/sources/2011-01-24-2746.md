# WW 2011-01-24 — Still cautious; the muni-bond thesis; high-dividend ETFs; IBM breaks out

**Source:** [Still cautious, high dividend paying ETF’s, IBM breaks out](../../raw/posts/2011-01-24-2746.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2011/01/2746/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A rare thesis-driven defensive move: with the GMI at 5 and GMI-R at 8, he sold his tech stocks, bought inverse tech ETFs and puts on AGO (the muni-bond insurer) on the municipal-bond collapse he had written about — while conceding "these indicators… can only confirm a change in trend *after* it has occurred, so the long and short term trends remain up, for now." "I am a chicken and would rather get out quickly and get back in after the dust settles. I do not mind if I am wrong. Every loss brings me to the next gain." QQQQ in a short-term up-trend 94 of the past 96 days; QQQQ/SPY above their 10-week 20 straight weeks — "as long as these indexes stay above their 10 week averages, I am likely to make money by being long stocks." T2108 58%: "readings near 80% are typical at market tops, and readings below 20% tend to occur near bottoms." Watching high-dividend ETFs (FDD, LVL, DWX, IDV, PID, FGD, GULF, VIG) as the muni refugees' destination; IBM at an ATH out of a 12-month consolidation.

## Key claims

- The indicators confirm a change *after* it occurs — a limit he states while overriding them on a thesis.
- T2108 bands as stated in 2011: ~80% at tops, <20% near bottoms.
- 20 straight weeks above the 10-week; 94 of 96 days in an up-trend.
- "Every loss brings me to the next gain."
