# WW 2005-07-11 — A hot market — component 1's criterion changed to 'count ≥ 100 OR ≥ 50%'

**Source:** [A hot market; GMI: +6; more rockets; covered calls on GOOG](../../raw/posts/2005-07-11-a-hot-market-gmi-6-more-rockets-covered-calls-on-goog.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/07/a-hot-market-gmi-6-more-rockets-covered-calls-on-goog/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"I changed the criterion for the 10 day new high index to be positive if there were 100 successful stocks or at least 50% of the stocks that hit a new high 10 days ago closed higher today than 10 days ago. There were only 95 new highs 10 days ago, so the indicator would have had to be negative (less than 100) the way I originally defined the index. So today, 76/95 stocks or 80% qualified as successful 10 day new highs." 629 new highs (16% of the universe); 83% of stocks above their 10-week; the Coolcat scan noted.

## Key claims

- Component 1 redefined on July 11, 2005: positive if ≥ 100 successful new highs OR ≥ 50% of the stocks that made new highs 10 days ago closed higher — the origin of the percentage rule the table still uses.
- 629 new highs = 16% of the universe.
