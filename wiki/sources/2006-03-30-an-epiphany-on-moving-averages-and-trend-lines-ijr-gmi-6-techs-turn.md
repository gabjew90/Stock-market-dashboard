# WW 2006-03-30 — The epiphany: moving averages are useless in a trendless market

**Source:** [An epiphany on moving averages and trend lines; IJR; GMI: +6; tech’s turn?](../../raw/posts/2006-03-30-an-epiphany-on-moving-averages-and-trend-lines-ijr-gmi-6-techs-turn.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2006/03/an-epiphany-on-moving-averages-and-trend-lines-ijr-gmi-6-techs-turn/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Written after a break — "I have been burnt out on this market. **Nothing I have done worked and my account
has gone nowhere**" — and it produces the sharpest limitation he ever states about his own toolkit: "**I realized
that moving averages are useless for providing signals in a trendless market.**" The evidence is the QQQQ's flat
channel since the previous November: "the index has gone back and forth over the 30 day (red) and 50 day (green)
moving averages. Thus, **the averages and the GMI have been giving erratic, contradictory signals.**" His
question is the right one to ask of any losing stretch: "This index has gone nowhere since last November. **Why
should we expect to have made any money trading tech stocks in this period?**" The replacement tool in that
regime is the channel: he will believe the rally "only if the index closes above the upper channel line (around
42.10) **on higher volume**," and expects a decline back toward 40.19 if it bounces off. The contrast case is
IJR, in a clean trend since October — "traders in small cap stocks probably made money in this period." On why
trend lines work at all, he is frankly instrumentalist: "**I guess because so many people think they work. Why
should I care to understand the reasons, so long as the lines help me to predict probable levels of support and
resistance.**"

## Key claims

- **Moving averages — and therefore the GMI — degrade in a trendless market**, producing "erratic, contradictory signals." The clearest self-published limitation of his primary machinery, and the earliest.
- **A flat index is a sufficient explanation for a flat account.** He checks the regime before concluding his method is broken.
- **Trend lines and channels are the trendless-market substitute** — support/resistance levels with a volume condition on the breakout.
- Reflexivity accepted without embarrassment: the lines work because enough people act on them, and the mechanism does not need to be understood to be used.
- Regime dispersion noted in passing: QQQQ flat while IJR trended, so "traders in small cap stocks probably made money in this period."
- Burnout stated in the first person, with a four-person IBD meetup as the corroborating detail.
