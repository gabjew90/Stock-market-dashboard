# WW 2008-10-13 — Capitulation week: 2,832 new lows, T2108 at 1%; the education mission

**Source:** [GMI: 0; GMI-R: 0; 2,832 new lows on Friday; T2108: 1%; Our embarrassing failure to educate youth about the market](../../raw/posts/2008-10-13-gmi-0-gmi-r-0-2832-new-lows-on-friday-t2108-1-our-embarassing-failure-to-educate-youth-about-the-market.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2008/10/gmi-0-gmi-r-0-2832-new-lows-on-friday-t2108-1-our-embarassing-failure-to-educate-youth-about-the-market/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Written the Monday after the worst week of the 2008 crisis — the blog's real-time record of
capitulation. Fixes two extreme datapoints, restates the discipline at the moment it was
hardest, and states the education mission that later becomes the university course thread.

## Key claims

- **The breadth extreme, on record:** "On Friday [2008-10-10], there were 23 new highs and
  **2,832 new lows** in my universe of 4,000 stocks. This is the **largest number of daily new
  lows since I started this blog**." Nearly 71% of the universe at a 52-week low on one day.
- **T2108 at 1%** — "about as low as it gets" — on the same date the breadth reconstruction
  computes ~1.7% (NYSE). A direct calibration point for `ww breadth validate`.
- **The discipline at maximum stress:** "This is the time to be on the sidelines in cash. **No
  one knows when this down-trend will end.**" Day 29 of the QQQQ short-term down-trend.
- **The Cramer receipt:** "It is amazing how Jim Cramer is now claiming to have been urging
  people to be in cash. The truth is that he and the other market pundits have been urging
  people to buy stocks all the way down... Cramer has repeatedly ridiculed the use of charts.
  **But it is the chart patterns that warned me to get out of this market recently, and in
  2000.**"
- **The education mission, at its most explicit:** "It is a crime that we do not teach people
  how to think for themselves when they invest their hard earned money. High schools and
  universities should require students to attend classes in financial planning and
  investments." His honors TA course: students "report that my class... is the most practical
  course they have attended."
- The re-entry comfort, restated mid-crash: "There will be plenty of time to ride the next
  up-trend **once it has proven itself**."
