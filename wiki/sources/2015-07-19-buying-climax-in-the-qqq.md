# WW 2015-07-19 — Buying climax in the QQQ? Reading index strength against breadth

**Source:** [Buying climax in the $QQQ?](../../raw/posts/2015-07-19-buying-climax-in-the-qqq.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2015/07/buying-climax-in-the-qqq/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The QQQ closes up 5.5% on the week to an all-time high while SPY and DIA lag, only 34% of Nasdaq-100 stocks rise on the day, and — the striking finding — new yearly lows exceed new highs across his ~5,000-stock universe. Cap-weighting explains it: GOOG, FB, NFLX, CELG, AMZN carry the index. "It does not bode well for an army when most troops do not follow the generals." Two overbought reads (56% of QQQ stocks overbought; QQQ above its upper 15.2 daily Bollinger Band, as in the prior April) make the 7%-in-6-days run look like a buying climax; he holds a small SQQQ position and will revise if SPY and DIA confirm.

## Key claims

- The generals-and-troops divergence: index ATH on a handful of mega-caps while new lows > new highs.
- "It does not bode well for an army when most troops do not follow the generals."
- Overbought markers: >50% of index constituents overbought; index closing above its upper 15.2 daily Bollinger Band.
- GMI still 6 of 6 — the divergence read is a warning layered on top of a GREEN GMI, not a Sell.
