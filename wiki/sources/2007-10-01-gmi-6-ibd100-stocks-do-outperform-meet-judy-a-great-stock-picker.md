# WW 2007-10-01 — IBD100 outperformance in a rising market; buy-stop automation (PAY)

**Source:** [GMI: 6; IBD100 stocks do outperform; Meet Judy, a great stock-picker](../../raw/posts/2007-10-01-gmi-6-ibd100-stocks-do-outperform-meet-judy-a-great-stock-picker.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2007/10/gmi-6-ibd100-stocks-do-outperform-meet-judy-a-great-stock-picker/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Published two weeks before the October 2007 market top, with the GMI at 6 and the QQQQ on Day 21 of a short-term up-trend. Read against [WW 2007-09-17](2007-09-17-gmi-5-ibd100-stocks-not-good-for-buy-and-hold.md) two weeks earlier, the pair is a clean statement of his actual position on the IBD100: it outperforms *in a rising market* and decays into the index otherwise. Also documents the **buy-stop automation** technique and how he combines a fundamental researcher's ideas with his own technical screen.

## Key claims

- **IBD100 outperformance, measured (8/17 → 9/28):** Nasdaq 100 +11%, but the IBD100 median stock +15.6%, and **42 of 100** rose 20% or more — against 12% of Nasdaq 100 stocks and under 4% of S&P 500 stocks. "The IBD100 stocks list is a prime place to look for stocks that will outperform **in a rising market**."
- Directly rebuts the "list for suckers" claim, while conceding the constraint: "Like all stocks, the IBD100 move with the overall direction of the market."
- **Buy-stop automation, with the Darvas precedent:** unable to watch the market intraday, he placed a standing order to buy PAY above $40; it filled at ~$40.05 on the breakout while he worked. "remember Nicolas Darvas made a fortune trading while he danced around the world." Use buy-stop orders or price triggers depending on the broker.
- **The immediate-failure rule applied to a breakout:** "If PAY had closed back lower on the break-out day or showed little increase in volume, I would have sold out my position immediately." Volume confirmation is part of the entry, not optional.
- **Covered calls working in practice:** September account +5%; "Almost all (90%) of the stocks I wrote calls on were called away from me in my IRA. Writing short term (present month) calls on IBD100 stocks in up-trends has proved to be a highly successful and profitable strategy."
- **Combining sources:** he takes fundamental ideas from his friend Judy but "always subject Judy's picks to my own technical analysis before I buy" — the idea supplies the candidate, the chart supplies the timing.
