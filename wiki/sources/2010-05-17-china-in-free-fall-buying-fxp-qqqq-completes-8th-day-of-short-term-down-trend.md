# WW 2010-05-17 — China in free-fall; the pension moves only on the weekly QQQQ index / Stage 4

**Source:** [China in Free-fall, buying FXP; QQQQ completes 8th day of short term down-trend](../../raw/posts/2010-05-17-china-in-free-fall-buying-fxp-qqqq-completes-8th-day-of-short-term-down-trend.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2010/05/china-in-free-fall-buying-fxp-qqqq-completes-8th-day-of-short-term-down-trend/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The Shanghai Composite's failed rallies and breakdown as a scare; the GMI and GMI-R at one, cash/hedged/short in the trading IRA. The pension rule as stated in May 2010: the one positive component is the weekly QQQQ index — "if that should turn negative, making the GMI and GMI-R register zero, I will consider moving my university pension funds from mutual funds to money market funds. I typically, however, like to see a Stage 4 down-trend begin before I do that." D-8: 90% of Nasdaq-100 and 81% of S&P 500 stocks down — "so much for trying to find the few winners to go long with."

## Key claims

- Pension trigger 2010: weekly QQQQ index negative → consider; Stage 4 preferred before acting.
- D-8 breadth: 90% / 81% down.
