# WW 2011-02-28 — Why a breakout level becomes support — the second-chance buyers

**Source:** [Market indexes hold; Many stocks bounce off of support; IBD50 stock, LULU; Cup-with handle–MSTR.](../../raw/posts/2011-02-28-market-indexes-hold-many-stocks-bounce-off-of-support-ibd50-stock-lulu-cup-with-handle-mstr.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2011/02/market-indexes-hold-many-stocks-bounce-off-of-support-ibd50-stock-lulu-cup-with-handle-mstr/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The mechanism behind every 'bounce off support' entry, stated explicitly on LULU: "**When a stock breaks out of a trading range, the level where it has been turned back and stops advancing, once broken, becomes a support level. (This occurs because people who missed the break-out get a second chance to buy the stock near that level.)**" LULU had retraced to its breakout point during the week's weakness and held. The entry rule that follows: of the watchlist stocks that found support that week, "**I might buy one of these companies and place my sell stop below the low of the recent bounce**." And why he starts from the IBD 50: "I like to buy stocks on the IBD 50 list because they meet IBD's stringent fundamental and technical criteria."

## Key claims

- **Resistance becomes support because the buyers who missed the breakout are waiting there** — the demand mechanism, named.
- A retest of the breakout level that holds is a buy setup, not a warning.
- Stop below the low of the recent bounce — the same rule the OSB later formalises.
- The IBD 50 as the starting universe, on fundamental *and* technical grounds.
