## What it covers

**The two moving averages given distinct jobs, as compactly as he ever states it.** "**When the market closes
(gray line) below its 10 week average I become defensive. When it closes below its 30 week average I begin to
exit.**" Two levels, two different responses — caution then exit — which is the same graded structure as the
[daily RWB exit](../methodology/gmma-charts.md) (red lines then blue lines) and the
[monthly top template](../methodology/gmma-charts.md), one timeframe up. It also puts the pension trigger in
context: the 30-week is where *exiting* starts, and the further conditions (the average turning down) govern how
far it goes. Alongside it, the record claim in its standard form — "**my indicators have alerted me to get out of
the markets *early* in all major declines since 2000**" — and the reason he publishes at all: "**I post what I am
doing in order to teach other traders and investors.**" Position: day 6 of the up-trend, slowly adding TQQQ plus
individual biotechs and a biotech ETF.

## Key claims

- **Two levels, two responses**: a weekly close below the 10-week means *defensive*; below the 30-week means *begin to exit*.
- The same graded structure as the daily RWB exit and the monthly top template, one timeframe up.
- The pension trigger sits at the second level, with its extra condition (the average turning down) governing how far the exit goes.
- "I post what I am doing in order to teach other traders and investors" — the blog's purpose restated in 2025 as in 2010.
