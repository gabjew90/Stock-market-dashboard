# WW 2012-09-30 — 3rd day of a down-trend — the 5-day confirmation and how he scales into inverse ETFs

**Source:** [3rd day of QQQ short term down-trend; Teach MarketWatch Contest Value of TA](../../raw/posts/2012-09-30-3rd-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-teach-marketwatch-contest-value-of-ta.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2012/09/3rd-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-teach-marketwatch-contest-value-of-ta/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The add rule stated plainly on day 3 of a new down-trend: "I have noted many times that I am more sure of a change of trend once it lasts for 5 days. I have a small position in QID and TECS, two leveraged ETF's that rise as tech stocks decline. **If the down-trend continues Monday I will start to add to these positions.**" The account split in the same breath: "I am still fully long in my university pension, given the strength in the longer term trends, as evidenced by the GMI still being in a buy range." T2108 at 67% out of overbought; only 30% of Nasdaq-100 with MACD above signal.

## Key claims

- Day 1 small, Day 5 add — the inverse-ETF scaling rule, matching the long-side Day-1/Day-5 pattern.
- Pension stays long on the weekly trend while the trading account shorts the daily — the two speeds in one post.
- MACD breadth (30% above signal) as a short-term weakness confirmation.
