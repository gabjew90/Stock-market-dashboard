# WW 2009-01-12 — GMI component 6 named and sourced: the IBD growth mutual fund index (0muti)

**Source:** [GMI error: 3 since December 30; GMI-R: 4; QQQQ back near support](../../raw/posts/2009-01-12-gmi-error-3-since-december-30-gmi-r-4-qqqq-back-near-support.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2009/01/gmi-error-3-since-december-30-gmi-r-4-qqqq-back-near-support/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Resolves what the wiki had recorded as an open question about **GMI component 6**. Dr. Wish names the component, gives its rationale, and — because IBD stopped publishing the figure he had been reading — discloses the **ticker symbol he switched to**. Also a candid post: he publishes a correction to his own indicator after missing a crossover for two weeks.

## Key claims

- **Component 6 is confirmed and belongs to both composites:** "One of the GMI and GMI-R components counts whether the IBD growth mutual fund index is above its 50 day average."
- The rationale: "when these mutual funds are doing well, so will I be likely to, when I buy similar growth stocks."
- **The data source is disclosed:** "IBD no longer publishes the 50 day average for the mutual fund index, but the chart with the average is available on their website's stock charting application (enter symbol: **0muti**)."
- Self-correction: he missed the 2008-12-30 cross above the 50-day, so "the corrected GMI has been 3 since then" — he restates history rather than quietly revising it.
- A secondary read on the same series: its 50-day average being "far below its 200 day average" is treated as a caution even when the component itself is positive.
- The **GMI-R is characterised as "more sensitive"** than the GMI — a detail the wiki's GMI-R section did not have.
