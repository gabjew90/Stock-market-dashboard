Indexes are weak, but some promising IBD100 stocks appear](../../raw/posts/2009-02-15-jim-cramer-finds-ta-religion-tsys-cup-with-handle-breakout-indexes-are-weak-but-some-promising-ibd100-stocks.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2009/02/jim-cramer-finds-ta-religion-tsys-cup-with-handle-breakout-indexes-are-weak-but-some-promising-ibd100-stocks-appear/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Since the GMI last read 4 (late August 2008) QQQQ and SPY fell 35%, DIA 31%; 95% of Nasdaq-100 stocks declined, 36% by more than 40%; every Dow stock fell (C −80%, BAC −81%) — "do you see why it does not make sense to fight the general market's trend." "I prefer the GMI to be at least 4 before I commit many IRA funds, and especially my university pension, to the long side." IBD replaced a third of the IBD100 in three weeks — "that is why it is misleading to take IBD's claims for the outperformance… at face value"; still "I confine almost all of my buys to IBD 100 stocks because they do so well in up markets. In a bear market, however, they can do poorly" (80% of the January list below its publication price). TSYS as a cup-with-handle: a prior up-trend (tripled) is "a necessary component", a ~5-month base, a handle on falling volume, a break above the ~$8.91 pivot on heavy volume. Cramer's TA conversion mocked.

## Key claims

- GMI < 4 from August 2008: −35% QQQQ/SPY, 95% of Nasdaq-100 down.
- GMI ≥ 4 as the precondition for committing IRA — and especially pension — money.
- IBD100 turnover (a third replaced in three weeks) as the survivorship mechanism.
- Cup-with-handle anatomy: prior up-trend, multi-month base, handle on falling volume, pivot break on volume.
