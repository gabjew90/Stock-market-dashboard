## What it covers

From his TraderLion Day 6 talk, which covered "GLB, Blue Dot of Happiness and daily 8 EMA": a TC2000 scan for **stocks with a recent ATH that had bounced off the daily 8 EMA they had been tracking** — 26 of 6,200 stocks, sorted by Friday's close ÷ the close 250 days earlier. Three of the hits were IPOs from the past year with no 250-day figure. He notes the industry spread and earnings dates in the table, and that the scan would be rebuilt in Deepvue for a forthcoming Masterclass. "These stocks are for educational purposes only, to learn the setups. Plot them daily with their 8 EMA and explore them."

## Key claims

- The 8-EMA bounce scan: recent ATH + tracking and bouncing off the daily 8 EMA (26 of 6,200).
- Ranked by 250-day price change, as with his other ATH scans.
- Three months before he revised the trailing line from the 8 EMA to the 5 EMA.
