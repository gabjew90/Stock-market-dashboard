# WW 2009-05-31 — Rally gaining strength; the first weekly Guppy chart; rely on the weekly

**Source:** [Rally gaining strength; Guppy chart reveals major turn](../../raw/posts/2009-05-31-rally-gaining-strength-guppy-chart-reveals-major-turn.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2009/05/rally-gaining-strength-guppy-chart-reveals-major-turn/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

After a two-day head-fake in the daily count, he states a conclusion that shapes everything after: "the more that I analyze the market, the more I become convinced that I should rely on the longer term weekly trends to determine the market trend." He presents his first weekly Guppy multiple-moving-average chart of the QQQQ (12 averages; bull moves = shorter averages consistently above the longer with white space between) — the 2007 decline has bottomed, the long averages have stopped declining and are curving — and decides to "wade slowly back into this market with my most conservative money," willing to leave again if the short averages turn down. Also: 50% of Nasdaq-100 stocks with MACD above signal (an early MACD-breadth read); T2108 at 82%, "a high level at which the market can remain for months"; "I cannot trade successfully when I am stressed by other concerns"; and the self-description, "a trend following swing trader, trying to catch the multi-month up-swings."

## Key claims

- "I should rely on the longer term weekly trends to determine the market trend."
- First weekly Guppy chart of QQQQ; the pattern later named RWB is described here in black/red before the colours were fixed.
- "I am a trend following swing trader, trying to catch the multi-month up-swings… The key to success for me is to be out of the market (and lose little $$) when the black lines are below the red lines."
- "I probably could trade more profitably if I just follow my GMI and go long when it is 4 or above."
- "I cannot trade successfully when I am stressed by other concerns."
- T2108 82%: the market "can remain for months" at high readings; 50% of Nasdaq-100 with MACD above signal.
