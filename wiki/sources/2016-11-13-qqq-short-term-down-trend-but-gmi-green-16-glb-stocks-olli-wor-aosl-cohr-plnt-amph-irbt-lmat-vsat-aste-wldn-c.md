## What it covers

The post-election rotation: FANG sold, banks, industrials, defense and construction soared; 540 new highs to 51 lows; SPY +7.9% and DIA +5.5% on the week vs QQQ +1.9%; "one must never fight the tape, and many stocks are breaking out, just not the ones I normally focus on." Sixteen GLBs on rising volume kept on a special watchlist; OLLI bought on Friday out of a 7-month consolidation after doubling — "I will exit if the break out above the green line fails. *I always know in advance the price action that will tell me that my purchase is wrong and where I must exit. When a stock fails to behave as I anticipated I get out with a small loss and move on to the next winner. I do not marry a stock. As William O'Neil has written, all stocks are bad, unless they are going up!*" HQY: support at the 30-week on unusual volume, already doubled. QQQ D-11 while the GMI stays Green; he tweets intraday on trend reversals and on GLBs (@wishingwealth).
