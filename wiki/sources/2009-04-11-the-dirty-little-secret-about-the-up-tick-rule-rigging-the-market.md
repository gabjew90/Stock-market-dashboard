# WW 2009-04-11 — The dirty little secret about the up-tick rule

**Source:** [The dirty little secret about the up-tick rule: rigging the market](../../raw/posts/2009-04-11-the-dirty-little-secret-about-the-up-tick-rule-rigging-the-market.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2009/04/the-dirty-little-secret-about-the-up-tick-rule-rigging-the-market/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

An explainer written for students during the 2009 blame-the-shorts season: what a short sale is, why the uptick rule (1938, repealed 1997) exists, and why short selling can *slow* a decline — every short must be covered, so heavy short interest "often times rebound[s] quickly after a decline as the short sellers buy back their shares" (the mechanism behind his later 'short interest as fuel' filter). "If we need an uptick rule to protect us from frenzied short selling, then we need a downtick rule to protect us from frenzied buying." The real abuse: naked shorting.

## Key claims

- Short interest as latent buying — the mechanism stated.
- Symmetry argument: uptick vs 'downtick' rules.
- Naked shorting as the actual manipulation to police.
