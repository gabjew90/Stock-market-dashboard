# WW 2010-07-30 — The blog's statement of purpose, and the method in one paragraph

**Source:** [GMI back to 5; QQQQ short term up-trend completes 5th day; UNG](../../raw/posts/2010-07-30-gmi-back-to-5-qqqq-short-term-up-trend-completes-5th-day-ung.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2010/07/gmi-back-to-5-qqqq-short-term-up-trend-completes-5th-day-ung/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A welcome note for new readers, and the fullest single statement of who he is and what the method is for.
**Why he writes:** "I write this blog to share my experiences gained from trading over more than 40 years. I also
write this blog to **educate my students at the University of Maryland**, who are enrolled in my classes on
technical analysis of stocks. I am passionately committed to educating people about trading because **I believe
that our education system has failed to prepare its citizens for their financial survival.** My students learn to
greet assertions and advice from the pundits in the financial media with a heavy dose of skepticism and their own
critical analysis." **Who he is:** "As a **part-time trader**, I have educated myself through extensive reading of
the works of successful traders and **through analysis of my own trading performance**. **I bring my training as
a research psychologist to the subject of trading.**" **The record claimed:** "I have been able to **multiply my
IRA 14x since 1995** and have been able to **keep my university pension safely in cash during all of the major
market declines since 1998**, and to reinvest the funds in the subsequent advances." **The method in one
paragraph:** "I rely heavily upon my General Market Index (GMI) to keep me on the same side as the major market
trend… **I exit to cash when my indicators signal a major down-trend, usually when the GMI is below 3. I wade
back into the market slowly when the GMI recovers. I do not predict the length of trends, I try only to ride them
until they end.**" **And the publishing routine**, which explains a puzzle about the corpus: "I compose each daily
post after the market close, and publish it on Monday through Friday around 7:00 AM EST. **While I publish the GMI
reading every post, I present the GMI's full components only on Monday morning.**" The trade of the day is a
small UNG position on a two-part signal — an oversold stochastic followed by a close back above the 30-day
average, with the exit at a close back below it, and the 30-week (8.26) named as what would confirm a longer-term
turn.

## Key claims

- **"I do not predict the length of trends, I try only to ride them until they end"** — the method in one sentence, from the man himself.
- **Defensive below 3, and re-entry is slow**: "I wade back into the market slowly when the GMI recovers."
- **He is a research psychologist and a part-time trader**, and names both as inputs to the method — including "analysis of my own trading performance" as a study method.
- **The full GMI component table is published only on Mondays**; every other post carries the score alone. That is why the corpus's component detail is concentrated on Mondays.
- The educational mission is stated as a civic one: "our education system has failed to prepare its citizens for their financial survival."
- Track record as of July 2010: IRA up 14× since 1995; pension in cash through every major decline since 1998, including 2000–2002 and 2008.
- A commodity ETF gets a different risk frame: "since it is a valued commodity, I do not see much chance that it will fall to zero."
