# WW 2008-01-28 — Where 'exits matter more than entries' came from — Covel on the Turtles

**Source:** [GMI: 0; GMI-R: 0; 16th day of the down-trend; A great new book on the Turtles](../../raw/posts/2008-01-28-gmi-0-gmi-r-0-16th-day-of-the-down-trend-a-great-new-book-on-the-turtles.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2008/01/gmi-0-gmi-r-0-16th-day-of-the-down-trend-a-great-new-book-on-the-turtles/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The source of the proposition the wiki treats as his central one. Reviewing Michael Covel's *The Complete
Turtle Trader* in January 2008 — "over the past 40 years, I have rarely found a book on trading that made a
significant difference to my approach to trading" — he names what he took from it: "**The most important thing I
learned from this book was the mentors' primary emphasis on exits from a trade rather than entry. Dennis is
quoted as basically saying one might profit by selecting positions by trial and error as long as one had a good
exit strategy. Go into every trade as if you will lose money and you will be prepared in advance for that
possibility and limit your losses.**" That is, almost word for word, proposition five of the trading philosophy
he would publish twenty-two months later ("one could probably select stocks at random as long as losses are kept
at a minimum") — with the attribution attached here and dropped there. He also notes the Turtles' rules were
built for futures "which I think can be adapted for trading equities," and discloses that he earns nothing from
the books he recommends. Market context: GMI and GMI-R at zero, D-16, only two of the 509 stocks across a year of
IBD 100 lists at a new high. Two disciplines stated: "**while my most sensitive daily indicators are off of the
extreme low values they hit early last week, *none* of my indicators have turned positive**" — off the lows is
not positive — and a duration prior for bears: "**many of the bear markets I have witnessed back to the 70's
continued for nine months or more. We are only a little more than two months into the current decline.**"

## Key claims

- **"Exits matter more than entries" is Richard Dennis's argument, read in Covel's book in January 2008** — the wiki's 2009 statement of it is a restatement, and the attribution had been lost.
- "**Go into every trade as if you will lose money** and you will be prepared in advance for that possibility and limit your losses."
- **Off the extreme lows is not the same as positive.** A recovering indicator that has not crossed is still negative.
- **A duration prior for bear markets**: nine months or more, from the 1970s onward — used to argue that two months in was too early to look for a bottom.
- Downtime has a use: "a good time to be out of the market, **to analyze one's prior trading performance**, and to, perhaps, read a great book."
- He discloses that he earns nothing from book recommendations or from the blog.
