# WW 2024-03-12 — A black-dot scan with its own stop rule — 'just below the low of the past 2 days'

**Source:** [Blog Post: Day 44 of $QQQ short term up-trend; $QQQ has black dot, oversold bounce, see daily chart and list of 16 strong stocks that had a black dot on Tuesday](../../raw/posts/2024-03-12-blog-post-day-44-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-qqq-has-black-dot-oversold-bounce-see-daily-chart-and-list-of-16.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2024/03/blog-post-day-44-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-qqq-has-black-dot-oversold-bounce-see-daily-chart-and-list-of-16-strong-stocks-that-had-a-black-dot-on-tuesday/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A compact scan spec with an entry stop that differs from the usual bounce-day low: the stocks "met my criteria for technically strong stocks that are **up at least 50% the past year**, **hit an ATH in the last 10 weeks** and **had a black dot, or oversold bounce**. **If I buy such a stock I place my sell stop just below the low of the past 2 days.**" He had bought TQQQ on the Tuesday, and points readers to his 2024 Meetup presentation for the black-dot indicator itself.

## Key claims

- Scan: up ≥ 50% over the past year + ATH within the last 10 weeks + a black dot.
- **Stop placed below the low of the past *two* days** — a two-day window rather than the bounce-day low alone.
- The ATH recency window is 10 weeks here, against 3 months in the blue-dot scan and 'recent' elsewhere.
- TQQQ bought on the same signal day.
