# WW 2005-12-08 — Failed break-outs as a topping sign; the 10-day average of SPY and DIA

**Source:** [GMI: +6; Short term weakening; Failed break-outs: CMTL](../../raw/posts/2005-12-08-gmi-6-short-term-weakening-failed-break-outs-cmtl.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/12/gmi-6-short-term-weakening-failed-break-outs-cmtl/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

GMI still +6, but two short-term cracks. "For the second time since this rally began the SPY and the DIA each closed below their **10 day averages**, a sign of short term weakness in these indexes. The QQQQ is stronger and remains above its 10 day average." And the component-1 read used diagnostically: "only 49% of the 351 stocks that hit a new high 10 days ago closed higher on Wednesday than they closed ten days earlier. **This is a sign of failed break-outs.**" The percentage of his universe in a short-term up-trend receded from 65% to 53%, while 84% of doublers stayed above their 30-day and 69% of all stocks above their 10-week — "in the face of the short term weakening, the longer term up-trends remain in place, especially for the leading stocks."

## Key claims

- A sub-50% successful-10-day-new-high reading is read as failed break-outs, the topping tell.
- The 10-day average as the short-term line for SPY/DIA in 2005; index divergence noted.
- Short-term breadth receding while leadership breadth holds = weakening, not topping.
