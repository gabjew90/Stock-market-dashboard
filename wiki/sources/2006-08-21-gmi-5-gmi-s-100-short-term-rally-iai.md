# WW 2006-08-21 — GMI 5?; GMI-S 100; a tradeable rally; the market's reaction to news; bet on the brokers

**Source:** [GMI: 5?; GMI-S: 100; Short-term rally?;  IAI](../../raw/posts/2006-08-21-gmi-5-gmi-s-100-short-term-rally-iai.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2006/08/gmi-5-gmi-s-100-short-term-rally-iai/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

He had missed "another major sign of strength given off by the market — its failure to decline after the terrorist plot in Britain was announced. The market glass is always half full or empty and the way that the market responds to major events provides valuable clues as to how the masses are feeling towards stocks. (The same is true about how the market responds to news regarding individual stocks.)" GMI "5?" — 97 new highs against the 100 needed; GMI-S 100, GMI-L 63, weekly QQQQ index still negative; QQQQ above its 10-week but under its 30-week; SPY/DIA above both — "a tradeable rally… I closed out all of my short positions and have been slowly wading into the market." Also the brokerage thesis from his youth: each new bull rises on record volume, so "bet on the house" (IAI).

## Key claims

- Failure to decline on bad news as a strength signal.
- GMI marked "5?" at 97 vs 100 new highs — the margin graded openly.
- GMI-S 100 / GMI-L 63: short-term all positive, long-term not — a tradeable rally, not a new bull.
- Wading in long once shorts are closed.
