# WW 2021-08-22 — What a set-up *is* — objective conditions plus a stop price

**Source:** [Blog post: Stocks rally and 2 day old $QQQ short term down-trend may end Monday; $NKE–an example of how I use my latest oversold bounce (OSB) scan to find promising stocks; $QQQ retakes all shorter daily RWB averages](../../raw/posts/2021-08-22-blog-post-stocks-rally-and-2-day-old-qqq-short-term-down-trend-may-end-monday-nke-an-example-of-how-i-use-my.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2021/08/blog-post-stocks-rally-and-2-day-old-qqq-short-term-down-trend-may-end-monday-nke-an-example-of-how-i-use-my-latest-oversold-bounce-osb-scan-to-find-promising-stocks-qqq-retakes-all-shorter-d/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The most compact definition of his method in the corpus: "**The key to prospering in the market is to have developed a set-up, defined by objective conditions which leads one to expect that a stock will rise almost immediately. The set-up must also suggest a price at which to set a stop loss to sell out with a small loss if the set-up fails. If the set-up is good, one will likely end up with a lot of small losses and a few big winners that more than make up for the losses.**" Worked on NKE — a recent GLB to an ATH, bouncing Friday from oversold, with a black dot: "if I bought this bounce I would immediately place a GTC stop order to sell, just below the bottom of the bounce, at 164.67. If the stock trades below this price then the set-up has likely failed and I would quickly exit with a minimal loss." The scan behind it requires the stock to already be in an up-trend and to have recently reached an ATH.

## Key claims

- A set-up = objective conditions predicting an immediate rise **plus** a stop price implied by those conditions.
- Expected distribution: many small losses, a few big winners.
- A GTC sell-stop just below the bounce low, placed immediately on entry (NKE at 164.67).
- Failure is defined by the set-up itself, not by a percentage.
