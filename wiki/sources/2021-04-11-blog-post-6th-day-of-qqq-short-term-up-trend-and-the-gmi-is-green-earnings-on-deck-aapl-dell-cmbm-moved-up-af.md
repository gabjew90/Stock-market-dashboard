# WW 2021-04-11 — The black dot defined — and why he prefers bounces to breakouts

**Source:** [Blog post: 6th day of $QQQ short term up-trend and the GMI is Green; earnings on deck; $AAPL, $DELL, $CMBM moved up after black dot signals](../../raw/posts/2021-04-11-blog-post-6th-day-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-and-the-gmi-is-green-earnings-on-deck-aapl-dell-cmbm-moved-up-af.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2021/04/blog-post-6th-day-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-and-the-gmi-is-green-earnings-on-deck-aapl-dell-cmbm-moved-up-after-black-dot-signals/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The black dot's definition, and the clearest statement of the preference underneath his whole entry
philosophy.** "**My black dot signal occurs when a stock has been short term oversold and then closes above its
30 day or exp21 day averages. Buying an oversold bounce is minimal risk for me. If the bounce fails I can quickly
exit with a small loss. I prefer this strategy to buying break-outs. It works best for me with up-trending stocks
near all-time high[s].**" Four things in four sentences: the **definition** (oversold, then a close back above the
30-day or 21-day EMA), the **reason** (the invalidation is close, so the loss is small), the **ranking**
(bounces beat breakouts), and the **precondition** (an up-trending stock near an all-time high — the setup does
not transfer to beaten-down names). Note that the ranking is argued entirely from risk rather than from hit rate:
he does not claim bounces work more often, only that being wrong costs less. That is the same argument as the
[BOS](../methodology/entry-signals.md), the [green-line retest](../methodology/glb-in-practice.md), and the
[bounce off the 30-day](../methodology/entry-signals.md) — one principle, several signals.

## Key claims

- **Black dot = short-term oversold, then a close above the 30-day *or* 21-day exponential average.**
- **"I prefer this strategy to buying break-outs"** — the ranking stated outright, and argued from risk rather than from hit rate.
- "If the bounce fails I can quickly exit with a small loss" — the invalidation is nearby, which is the entire case.
- **The precondition matters**: it works "with up-trending stocks near all-time highs," not with beaten-down names.
- One principle behind several signals — BOS, the green-line retest, the 30-day bounce and the dots all buy proximity to support.
