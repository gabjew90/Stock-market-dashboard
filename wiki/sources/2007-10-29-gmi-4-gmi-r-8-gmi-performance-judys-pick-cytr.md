# WW 2007-10-29 — At the October 2007 top: GMI-L at 94%, QLD over stock-picking, a looser threshold

**Source:** [GMI: 4; GMI-R: 8; GMI performance; Judy's pick: CYTR](../../raw/posts/2007-10-29-gmi-4-gmi-r-8-gmi-performance-judys-pick-cytr.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2007/10/gmi-4-gmi-r-8-gmi-performance-judys-pick-cytr/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Written days after the **October 2007 market top**, and valuable precisely because it does not call it. Every long-term reading was still strong — GMI-L at 94%, QQQQ on Day 41 of a short-term up-trend, seven weeks of closes above the 10-week average — while the GMI had already ticked down to 4 on a deteriorating breadth component. A good record of what the indicators actually showed at a major top, rather than what hindsight suggests they should have.

## Key claims

- **The 10-Day Successful New High component explained in his own words:** "This index measures the number of stocks that hit a new high 10 days ago that closed higher than they did 10 days earlier. (In a strong market, stocks should rise after hitting new highs.)" It turning negative "barely" is what cost the GMI its point.
- **Composites reported as percentages:** GMI-R at 80% (8 of 10) and **GMI-L at 94%** — the long-term measure at a near-maximum reading days after the top, and one that would fall to 31% within seven weeks ([WW 2007-12-17](2007-12-17-gmi-0-gmi-r-0-gmi-l-31-why-fight-the-odds.md)).
- **A looser threshold than he states elsewhere:** "I will trade long in the market as long as the GMI is **greater than 2**." This conflicts with "exit when the GMI falls below 4" from [WW 2007-08-20](2007-08-20-gmi-1-more-new-highs-than-lows-performance-of-gmi-strongest-ibd100-stocks.md) two months earlier and with the ≥4 / ≤3 rule of 2011. Recorded as a variation, not a correction.
- **The 10-week average as his working condition:** "I typically make money trading growth stocks when the QQQQ is consistently above its 10 week average" — QQQQ and SPY had been above theirs for seven weeks.
- **QLD over stock-picking, quantified again:** since the GMI turned 3 on August 31, QQQQ +10.35% and QLD +20.07%. Over the same span only 19% of Nasdaq 100 stocks, 7% of S&P 500 stocks and 34% of the August 18 IBD100 list rose more than 20%. "We would have had to have been pretty lucky to select an entire portfolio of individual stocks that would have outperformed just sitting in the QLD!"
- **The behavioural trap, named:** "So many traders are interested in being right (proving their strategy or that they are smart), instead of just making money. I continually have to resist falling into this trap."
- **CYTR as a technical checklist applied to someone else's idea:** weekly close above the 30-week average ("a critical buy signal for me"), a nine-month base after a large rise, already up 250% on the year ("I like to buy stocks that have already doubled"), and sector confirmation from other RNAi names. Plus the pyramiding intent: "I will have plenty of time to average up as the stock climbs."
- T2108 back to 55% from a recent low of 44%.
