# WW 2025-03-06 — Value is a myth — going to cash in both accounts

**Source:** [Blog Post: Day 8 of $QQQ short term down-trend; 28 US new highs, 81 lows and 5 at ATH; I will resist the inevitable sudden bear market bounces and remain safely in cash.](../../raw/posts/2025-03-06-blog-post-day-8-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-28-us-new-highs-81-lows-and-5-at-ath-i-will-resist-the-inevitabl.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2025/03/blog-post-day-8-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-28-us-new-highs-81-lows-and-5-at-ath-i-will-resist-the-inevitable-sudden-bear-market-bounces-and-remain-safely-in-cash/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A blunt statement of what he thinks prices are. "As several great traders have opined, **all stocks are bad unless they are going up. Stocks are only worth what someone will pay for them. Value is a myth.** When the psychology turns and the masses become scared, they sell and all stocks decline. **I prefer to sell early and not get caught up in the panic selling near the bottom.**" Both accounts move: cash in the trading accounts, equity exposure reduced in the university retirement accounts, "and will sell the rest when we get a good bounce" — selling into strength rather than at the low. The bottom indicator restated: "one indicator that has helped me to detect a bottom is when T2108, now at 26%, declines to below 10%." And the perspective: "I have lived through many weak markets the past 60 years… **too many people have made money the past two years and have no idea what a major decline can do to one's portfolio. It took 25 years for the market (the Dow) to exceed the 1929 top.** So, yes, the market always comes back."

## Key claims

- "All stocks are bad unless they are going up. Stocks are only worth what someone will pay for them. **Value is a myth.**"
- "I prefer to sell early and not get caught up in the panic selling near the bottom."
- Sell the remaining pension exposure **into a bounce**, not at the low.
- T2108 below 10% restated as the bottom indicator (26% at the time).
- The 1929 recovery given here as **25 years** — matching April 2025 and differing from the 26 he gave in April 2022.
