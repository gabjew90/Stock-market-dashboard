## What it covers

An unusually un-Wish-like trade, and he says so. Judy had researched QS (solid-state batteries) more than a year earlier; on the news day she called to say it was "rocketing higher on news with enormous trading volume" and noted "**QS had a high short interest ratio signifying that many speculators will need to rush to buy shares to cover their short positions**." He bought a little at $8.02; it ran to $10.03 the same day. "I am holding the shares long term because… the company's tests of their battery have exceeded expectations and it may eventually become a major breakthrough product for EVs" — with Volkswagen's stake as corroboration. The chart also carries his blue and black oversold-bounce dots.

## Key claims

- Short interest ratio treated as latent buying — the squeeze mechanism as an accelerant on good news.
- A concept-driven long-term hold, explicitly outside his usual trend-following discipline (Judy's style, which he elsewhere says he lacks the confidence for).
- Enormous volume on the news day as the confirmation.
