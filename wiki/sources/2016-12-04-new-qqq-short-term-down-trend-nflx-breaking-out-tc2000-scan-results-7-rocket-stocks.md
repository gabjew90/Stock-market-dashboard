# WW 2016-12-04 — New QQQ down-trend; ~40% end in under 5 days; SPY vs QQQ weekly divergence

**Source:** [New $QQQ short term down-trend; $NFLX breaking out? TC2000 scan results: 7 rocket stocks](../../raw/posts/2016-12-04-new-qqq-short-term-down-trend-nflx-breaking-out-tc2000-scan-results-7-rocket-stocks.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2016/12/new-qqq-short-term-down-trend-nflx-breaking-out-tc2000-scan-results-7-rocket-stocks/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Day 1 of a new short-term down-trend, with the base rate attached: "many (about 40%) QQQ short term down-trends end in under 5 days." Both indexes remain in longer-term Stage II up-trends, but the QQQ has closed back below its 10-week. The weekly charts differ: SPY is climbing its 4-week average, which sits above its 10-week and 30-week, after breaking a multi-week counter-trend decline; QQQ is below its 4-week and 10-week with a possible head-and-shoulders forming, and "a *close* below its 30 week average (near 114) would be a sign of major weakness." The divergence is attributed to the post-election rotation into infrastructure and financials. The seasonal tactic: mutual funds will "spruce up their portfolios for end of quarter/year reports… such a rally may be a great time to exit positions or raise stops."

## Key claims

- ~40% of QQQ short-term down-trends end in under 5 days (2016 figure).
- The 4wk > 10wk > 30wk weekly stack read comparatively across two indexes to locate the weakness.
- Window-dressing rallies as exit/stop-raising opportunities, not entries.
