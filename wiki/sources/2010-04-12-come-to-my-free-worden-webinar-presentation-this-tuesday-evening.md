# WW 2010-04-12 — The GMI's 2008–2010 signal record, with dates

**Source:** [Come to my free Worden webinar presentation this Tuesday evening](../../raw/posts/2010-04-12-come-to-my-free-worden-webinar-presentation-this-tuesday-evening.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2010/04/come-to-my-free-worden-webinar-presentation-this-tuesday-evening/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Written while preparing a Worden webinar, and it contains something the wiki did not have: **the GMI's signal
record through the 2008 bear, with dates**. "On **September 2, 2008**, at the *beginning* of the steep drop in
the QQQQ, the GMI went from 3 to 1 and remained at mainly 0 **until December 30**. **I tend to get defensive when
the GMI is less than 3.** During that steep decline, **the QQQQ fell 35%**. During the next 8 days the market
rebounded and the GMI registered 3. But on **January 12, 2009**, the decline resumed and the GMI stayed below 3
again (mainly 0) **until March 23**, when the market successfully retested its bottom. Since the bottom, with the
exception of 7 days in July and 17 days during the decline at the end of January and early February, 2010, the
GMI has been 3 or higher." His own verdict is measured rather than promotional: "**While not perfect, the GMI has
kept me out of the market in down-trends and got me back in during up-trends.** (Of course, past performance does
not guarantee future success.)" Two other rules stated in passing: "**when these indexes** [QQQQ and SPY] **are
above their 10 week averages I usually can trade growth stocks (on the long side) profitably**," and T2108 at 83%
being "in overbought territory, but **below the extreme levels (around 90%) often seen at market tops**." He also
names himself: "I rely on TC2007 for my own trading (**I am Sir Silent Knight**)" and uses it with his university
classes.

## Key claims

- **A dated GMI signal record across the 2008 bear**: defensive from 2008-09-02 through 2008-12-30, an 8-day head-fake to 3, back below 3 from 2009-01-12 to 2009-03-23. The QQQQ fell 35% inside the first stretch.
- **"I tend to get defensive when the GMI is less than 3"** — the threshold stated plainly, a year before the two-day persistence rule was added.
- **The self-assessment is hedged, not sold**: "while not perfect… past performance does not guarantee future success."
- **The 10-week average as the go/no-go for trading growth stocks long**, stated as a general condition rather than about one position.
- T2108's top band given as ~90% — the same figure as two weeks later, and above the 80% he uses elsewhere.
- The pseudonym confirmed in the first person; TC2007 used both personally and in his university classes.
