# WW 2013-11-08 — The MACD histogram shows what price hides

**Source:** [20th day of QQQ short term up-trend; How daily MACD histogram predicted decline](../../raw/posts/2013-11-08-20th-day-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-how-daily-macd-histogram-predicted-decline.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2013/11/20th-day-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-how-daily-macd-histogram-predicted-decline/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**A worked demonstration of why he watches a derived indicator at all.** "I want to show you **how the MACD
histogram can illustrate something not seen in the price itself**. This daily chart of the QQQ shows the MACD
histogram below it. Note that in a 9 day period before Thursday's decline, **it looks to the naked eye like the
price of QQQ remained level. At the same time, the MACD histogram**" was steadily shrinking. Flat price with a
decaying histogram means the *rate* of the advance was falling even though its level was not — momentum draining
out of a move that still looks intact. That is information price cannot show, which is the entire case for the
indicator. He acted on it: "I had already closed out my position in TQQQ earlier in the week. Why? I posted last
Sunday night that I saw technical weakening in the QQQ, **based on its diminishing stochastics and MACD
histogram**." Two oscillators agreeing, published before the decline rather than after it — which is what makes
this a demonstration rather than a rationalisation.

## Key claims

- **The MACD histogram measures the rate of a move, not its level** — so flat price with a shrinking histogram is momentum draining out of an intact-looking advance.
- **Published before the decline**, with the reasoning, then shown afterwards — a forecast, not a post-hoc explanation.
- Two oscillators agreeing (stochastics and MACD histogram) is what he acted on; neither alone.
- He exited TQQQ on the divergence while the trend count and the GMI were still positive — a fast-indicator override, disclosed.
