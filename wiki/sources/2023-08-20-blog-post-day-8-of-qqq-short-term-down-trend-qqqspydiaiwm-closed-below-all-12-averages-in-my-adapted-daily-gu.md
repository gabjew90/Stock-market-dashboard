# WW 2023-08-20 — RWB up-trends over — 'be prepared to react once a trend is in'

## What it covers

The daily modified Guppy charts show the major indexes closing below all 12 averages, with BWR down-trends possible. The principle stated in one line: "**The key in technical analysis is to be prepared to react once a trend is in.**" And the stance: "Being long right now is fighting the tide. **It is so much easier to profit on the long side when the GMI is Green and the short term trends are up.**" On the calendar: "we are in the post earnings release period when the market tends to wander or decline. When the third quarter earnings are released in October, we might see a new up-trend. **The post earnings decline sets up the next rise. But rather than predict, I prefer to wait and then react.**" He is in cash and SQQQ with a small ACHR position.

## Key claims

- "The key in technical analysis is to be prepared to react once a trend is in."
- "Being long right now is fighting the tide."
- The post-earnings lull as a recurring seasonal that "sets up the next rise" — noticed but not traded on.
- Indexes closing below all 12 daily Guppy averages as the end of the RWB up-trend.
