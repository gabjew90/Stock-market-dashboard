# WW 2019-03-24 — Inverted yield curve or Mueller report? The declining 30-week average as the Stage 4 tell

## What it covers

Ignoring the media's causal story for a one-day sell-off, he names what actually troubles him: the QQQ and SPY 30-week averages are *still declining* even with the GMI at 6 — "a major sign of weakness for me, as it could indicate a Stage 4 decline." In prior large down-trends he waited for the 30-week to curve up before re-entering. He lightened up, will look for entries on a rebound, watches the 4-week average (~175.62) for support, and flags that a *close* back below the 30-week "could be ominous for the longs."

## Key claims

- "Most troubling to me is that for QQQ and SPY the 30 week average is still declining. This is a major sign of weakness for me, as it could indicate a Stage 4 decline."
- "In prior large down-trends I would wait for the 30 week average to curve up before tiptoeing back into the market."
- "A *close* back below the 30 week average could be ominous for the longs."
- GMI 6 of 6 and GMI2 barely weakened — the weekly-stage read governs how much to hold, not the daily count.
