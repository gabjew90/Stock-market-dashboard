# WW 2006-08-07 — QQQQ masks underlying strength in IBD-100; the new-low mirror statistic in 2006

**Source:** [GMI: +3; QQQQ masks underlying strength in IBD-100; Stocks to watch](../../raw/posts/2006-08-07-gmi-3-qqqq-masks-underlying-strength-in-ibd-100-stocks-to-watch.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2006/08/gmi-3-qqqq-masks-underlying-strength-in-ibd-100-stocks-to-watch/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Back from vacation with the QQQQ at D-22 and under its 10-week for 12 weeks, yet the GMI at 3 and the IBD Mutual Fund Index back near its 50-day ("when these funds start rising, I can make money trading IBD type growth stocks"). The mirror of component 1 is already in use: 70% of the 21 stocks that hit a new high 10 days earlier closed higher, but "only 25% of the 260 stocks that hit a new low 10 days ago closed lower. This suggests that shorting new lows has been unlikely to have been profitable recently" — he had closed his shorts. "My earliest indicator of a possible up-trend — stocks trading above their MACD signal line, was 69%" for both his universe and the IBD100. On CNBC's bearishness: the IBD-type trader Roppel "used to keep CNBC on all day with the volume muted." "If the GMI continues to climb I will wade slowly into this market. There is no need to hurry."

## Key claims

- Successful-10-day-new-low percentage in use in August 2006 (25% of 260) — thirteen years before the 2019 post that names it.
- % of stocks above their MACD signal line called "my earliest indicator of a possible up-trend" (2006).
- IBD MF index turning up as the permission to trade growth stocks.
- Wade slowly; "there is plenty of time to jump on board the train."
