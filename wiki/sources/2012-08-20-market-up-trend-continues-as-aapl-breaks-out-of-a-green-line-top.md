# WW 2012-08-20 — 'I never have the courage to do it right away'

**Source:** [Market up-trend continues as AAPL breaks out of a green line top](../../raw/posts/2012-08-20-market-up-trend-continues-as-aapl-breaks-out-of-a-green-line-top.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2012/08/market-up-trend-continues-as-aapl-breaks-out-of-a-green-line-top/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The leveraged-ETF measurement, and an admission that qualifies every other statement of it. Since the 30 July
buy signal: "**the QQQ has advanced +5.4%, the QLD by +10.9% and the TQQQ by +16.9%. As I have said before, the
easiest way to ride an up market is to buy one of these leveraged ETF's when the GMI flashes a buy signal.**"
Then, immediately: "**Of course, I never have the courage to do it right away when it is most scary. I tend to
slowly accumulate one of the leveraged ETF's as the up-trend persists.**" So the strategy he recommends and the
strategy he executes differ, and he says which is which. That gap is worth carrying whenever the ultra-ETF
studies are read — the returns quoted assume a day-1 entry he admits he does not make, and his actual results
come from scaling in later at worse prices. It is the same admission as the 2025 SQQQ post ("Day 1, by
definition, is a change in trend and most people do not believe it") and the 2023 TQQQ confession, stated here
first.

## Key claims

- **The recommended entry and the executed entry differ, and he says so**: "I never have the courage to do it right away when it is most scary."
- **The ultra-ETF return figures assume a day-1 entry he admits he does not make** — a caveat that belongs with every such measurement on this wiki.
- His actual method is scaling in as the trend persists, which necessarily earns less than the quoted figures.
- QQQ +5.4%, QLD +10.9%, TQQQ +16.9% from the buy signal — roughly 2× and 3× as designed.
