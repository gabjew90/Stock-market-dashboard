# WW 2006-05-30 — The IBD 100 comparison introduced — growth stocks hold longer-term trends

**Source:** [GMI: 0; IBD 100 stocks stronger; Some shorts; Ocean City had light traffic;](../../raw/posts/2006-05-30-gmi-0-ibd-100-stocks-stronger-some-shorts-ocean-city-had-light-traffic.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2006/05/gmi-0-ibd-100-stocks-stronger-some-shorts-ocean-city-had-light-traffic/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The day he starts a measurement he would run for years: "**I expanded the comparison of my indicators to
include the IBD 100 stocks. I think it will provide valuable insights into the market to see how the highly
rated IBD100 stocks' performance compares with my universe of 4,000 stocks.**" The first result is the one that
reconciles his later, harsher audits. On the *short-term* measures the two groups were indistinguishable —
similar percentages above their 10-day and 30-day averages, 8–11% in a short-term up-trend. The difference was
all in the long term: **31% of his universe above their 10-week averages against 51% of the IBD 100; 52% against
90% above their 30-week averages**; and "twice as many IBD 100 stocks bounced above their 10 week averages last
week (29% vs. 13%)." His reading: "these differences are important and signify that the **IBD 100 type of growth
stocks are more likely to remain in longer term up trends**, and almost one third found support last week at
their 10 week averages." Also here, the short screen written out as criteria rather than as a scan: candidates
for puts "have had some **high volume down weeks** since reaching peaks several months ago. All are **below
their 10 week averages** and their **declining 10 week averages are below their 30 week averages**." And an
off-chart observation — Ocean City empty on a holiday weekend, a realtor reporting unsold inventory — read as
early evidence of what high gas prices and rates were doing.

## Key claims

- **The IBD-100-versus-universe comparison begins 2006-05-30**, and it begins as a market-diagnostic instrument rather than as a scorecard for IBD.
- **Growth-list stocks hold long-term trends much better than the universe (90% vs 52% above the 30-week) while matching it short-term.** This is the finding his later negative audits sit alongside, not against: the lists persist in up-trends and underperform in declines.
- Support at the 10-week average is measured as a *rate* — 29% of the IBD 100 bounced there against 13% of the universe.
- The put-candidate screen in words: high-volume down weeks off a months-old peak, price below a declining 10-week, and that 10-week below the 30-week.
- GMI-S at 0 — none of the 16 short-term indicators across SPY, DIA, QQQQ and IJR positive — against a GMI-L of 63, the two-speed split at its widest.
