# WW 2007-07-08 — IBD 100 lists decay with age — the quantitative finding

**Source:** [GMI: 6; Rally has more to go; IBD100 stock performance](../../raw/posts/2007-07-08-gmi-6-rally-has-more-to-go-ibd100-stock-performance.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2007/07/gmi-6-rally-has-more-to-go-ibd100-stock-performance/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The IBD 100's decay measured by list age** — the most quantitative of his audits, and the one with an
actionable conclusion. Having restated the survivorship objection ("IBD's continual substitution of good
performers in the list for poor performers biases the performance of the IBD 100 index over time"), he tabulates
every original list by publication date: "only about **50-60% of the stocks in the lists from 2006** closed on
Friday above their price when the lists were first published." Then the new column: "**62% of the stocks on the
IBD 100 list published on May 14 and 71% on the list from June 18, closed within 5% of their 52 week highs. In
contrast, only about one third or less of the stocks on the lists from February 5 and before, are near their
yearly highs.**" And the new-high rate: "approximately one fourth to one third of the stocks on the lists from
April, May and June hit new highs last Friday, compared with only **10-14% of the Nasdaq 100 or S&P 500
stocks**." The conclusion: "**these statistics suggest to me that one should not marry the IBD 100 stocks, but
stay mainly with those on the more recent lists published by IBD.**" Also: T2108 at 56% off a 34% bottom, with
"the pendulum… typically does not top out until around 80%. **So this rally probably has more to go**"; and a
sentiment caution drawn from his own circle — "**I am a little cautious about this market because many traders
have told me they are making money.**"

## Key claims

- **Growth-list membership decays with age**: recent lists ran 62–71% within 5% of their highs, lists four months older about a third.
- **But a fresh list still beats the indexes**: a quarter to a third of April–June names hit new highs against 10–14% of the Nasdaq-100 and S&P 500.
- The actionable rule: "**one should not marry the IBD 100 stocks, but stay mainly with those on the more recent lists.**"
- **Peers reporting profits as a caution signal** — a sentiment sample taken from the people around him.
- T2108's upper band used to justify staying long: 56% off a 34% low, with tops "typically… around 80%."
