# WW 2020-07-13 — Ninety-five up-trends since 2006 — and the record-holder ended on the COVID top

**Source:** [Blog post: Day 67 of $QQQ short term up-trend, since 2006 only 7 of 95 up-trends lasted longer](../../raw/posts/2020-07-13-blog-post-day-67-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-since-2006-only-7-of-95-up-trends-lasted-longer.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2020/07/blog-post-day-67-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-since-2006-only-7-of-95-up-trends-lasted-longer/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The population size, stated**: "as my readers know, I compute each evening the number of days that the QQQ
has been in a short term up-trend or down-trend. (**I have a proprietary objective method for designating changes
in the short term trend, so don't ask for it.**) **Since 2006 there have been 95 short term up-trends.** The
current up-trend has now reached 67 days." That is the denominator behind every duration percentage on this wiki
— n ≈ 95 over fourteen years — and the refusal to publish the method is restated in the same breath. **And the
record-holder, with its ending**: "**The longest short term up-trend since 2006 lasted 91 days, from October 14,
2019 – February 24, 2020. This was followed by a 30 day short term down-trend that included the steep March
decline.**" The longest advance in his series terminated on the COVID top, and the down-trend that followed ran
30 days. Worth holding beside the [Feb 21 crossover](../sources/) that flagged the same top from a different
instrument — two independent series marking the same week.

## Key claims

- **95 short-term up-trends since 2006** — the denominator behind the duration percentages.
- **The longest ran 91 days and ended 24 February 2020**, on the COVID top; the following down-trend lasted 30 days.
- The trend-definition refusal restated: "a proprietary objective method… so don't ask for it."
- The count is maintained nightly, which is why the durations are exact rather than estimated.
- A very old up-trend is a reason for caution and not for exit — day 67 of 95 historical cases.
