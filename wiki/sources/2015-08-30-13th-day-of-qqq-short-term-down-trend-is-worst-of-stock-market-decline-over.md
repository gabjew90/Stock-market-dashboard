# WW 2015-08-30 — The August 2015 flash crash: depth comparison, Boomer thesis, pension exit criteria

**Source:** [Is worst of stock market decline over? I'm not betting on it](../../raw/posts/2015-08-30-13th-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-is-worst-of-stock-market-decline-over.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2015/08/13th-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-is-worst-of-stock-market-decline-over/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

His fullest response to the **August 24, 2015 flash crash**, written from 100% cash. 2015 was
the wiki's second-worst year-hole (one source page); this post carries the year's defining
market event, a comparative method for judging decline severity, and the clearest single
sentence he has written on the pension risk trade-off.

## Key claims

- **He was out before it hit:** "as my readers should know, I have been exiting the market for
  weeks. I have been 90%+ in cash and am now **100% in cash in my trading accounts**." The GMI
  had been 0 of 6 for five consecutive days.
- **Decline severity is judged by comparison with the last analogous episode.** He sets August
  2015 against October 2014 — the last GMI=0 stretch of similar length — and concludes the
  cases differ: "compare the depth of the QQQ's decline last October to the debacle that just
  occurred. **The technical break-down is far greater**... I therefore would not expect a quick
  resumption of the up-trend."
- **The recovery test is the 30-week retake:** "If the QQQ fails to retake its 30 week moving
  average like it did quickly last October, the recent decline could be just the *beginning* of
  something nasty."
- **The pension trade-off in one sentence:** "**I would rather miss a further 5-10% rise than
  sit through a possible 20-40% decline.**" Even the slow accounts were heading to money market.
- **A demographic thesis the wiki has nowhere else:** the Boomer cohort as a market-structure
  force — his generation drove the 80s–90s boom at peak earning power, and in drawdowns "we
  will see Boomers exiting the market, especially if the indexes retrace a little more... and
  they can get out near their recent account balance highs." Note the mechanism: *retracement
  toward break-even triggers supply.*
- **Weekend risk mechanics:** "When stocks have a bad week and end on a down note on Friday,
  people get scared over the weekend and sell on Monday morning." He was unsurprised by the
  Monday open.
- **Market-structure criticism:** "It is interesting to me how few commentators are stressing
  the **utter failure of the exchanges to have an orderly market open** on Monday morning!"
- **Pundit scepticism taught with 1929 and Enron:** the "permanent high plateau" prognosticators
  and Wall Street's buy ratings on Enron all the way to zero — "their broker will make them
  broker." And the context reads: seasonally weak September/October, Fed hikes likely — "I am a
  chicken when it comes to my investments."
