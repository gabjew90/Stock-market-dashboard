# WW 2020-06-09 — Green dot signals on FB and AMD — the definition, and the one critical requirement

**Source:** [$FB and $AMD had green dot signals Tuesday; go to my TC2000 club to view the green dot tutorial](../../raw/posts/2020-06-09-fb-and-amd-had-green-dot-signals-tuesday-go-to-my-tc2000-club-to-view-the-green-dot-tutorial.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2020/06/fb-and-amd-had-green-dot-signals-tuesday-go-to-my-tc2000-club-to-view-the-green-dot-tutorial/) · tier: trade_example

## What it covers

"A green dot signal occurs when the **fast 10.4 daily stochastic crosses above the slow daily 10.4.4 stochastic**." Both FB and AMD showed one, and both cleared what he calls the gate: "note that both of these stocks are **above their last green line tops, a critical requirement for me**. They also had recent green line breakouts (GLB). Note the above average volume on their green dot signals." The free tutorial for adding the dot to a TC2000 chart is in his club; he tweets the signals intraday at @wishingwealth.

## Key claims

- Green dot = fast 10.4 stochastic crossing above the slow 10.4.4.
- Above the last green-line top is "a critical requirement" — the dot alone is not the signal.
- Above-average volume on the dot day counts in its favour.
- Twitter used to publish dot signals the day they occur.
