# WW 2006-02-13 — A new breadth statistic introduced; small caps break ranks first

**Source:** [GMI: +4, GMI-S: 25; Small caps weaken; on side lines](../../raw/posts/2006-02-13-gmi-4-gmi-s-25-small-caps-weaken-on-side-lines.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2006/02/gmi-4-gmi-s-25-small-caps-weaken-on-side-lines/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**A new breadth statistic introduced and dated**: "I added a new indicator to the table tonight. **Stocks that
are in a strong advance tend to close above their 10 day moving average for some time. This new indicator shows
the percentage of stocks that have closed above their 10 day average for the past four days.** 19% of the stocks
in my universe did so on Friday. **We will watch to see whether this turns out to be a sensitive indicator of the
strength of the market**" — the "% in a strong rise" row that appears in later tables, defined here as *four
consecutive closes above the 10-day average*, and introduced provisionally rather than asserted. **Component 1
with a large denominator**, which is when the percentage form is most informative: "only **30% of the 630 stocks**
that hit a new high ten days ago closed higher Friday than they closed ten days before. **Buying new highs has
not been profitable.**" **And a WPM divergence he calls startling**: the small and mid caps (MDY, IJR) weakening
on both the 30-day and 30-week measures "even as the DIA, QQQQ and SPY remained stable or strengthened. **This
weakening in the strength of the mid and small cap stocks is a startling departure from the recent past and
warrants further attention.**" Component 6 approaching its 50-day from above, "suggesting that growth fund
managers are having trouble making money in this market environment."

## Key claims

- **The "% in a strong rise" statistic defined**: the share of the universe closing above its 10-day average for four consecutive days. Introduced 2006-02-13, explicitly on trial.
- **Small and mid caps breaking ranks before the large-cap indexes** is flagged as a leading divergence — the WPM's designed purpose working.
- Component 1's percentage form carries most information when the denominator is large: 30% of 630 is a verdict, 60% of 15 is not.
- "Buying new highs has not been profitable" — the component read as a statement about *strategy* viability, not just market health.
- Component 6 read on approach rather than on crossing — the fund index nearing its 50-day is already information.
