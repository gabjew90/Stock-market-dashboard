# WW 2015-07-26 — The warning four weeks before the flash crash: Stage IV spreading beneath a strong QQQ

**Source:** [Big concerns — too many indexes in Stage IV declines; GMI near Sell](../../raw/posts/2015-07-26-big-concerns-too-many-indexes-in-stage-iv-declines-gmi-near-sell.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2015/07/big-concerns-too-many-indexes-in-stage-iv-declines-gmi-near-sell/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Written **four weeks before the August 24, 2015 flash crash**, and it reads as the setup to the
[2015-08-30 aftermath post](2015-08-30-13th-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-is-worst-of-stock-market-decline-over.md)
already ingested: the narrow-leadership diagnosis, the spreading Stage IV, and the positioning
(SQQQ, a China put) that explains why he met the crash from cash. 2015 now has both the warning
and the aftermath from primary sources.

## Key claims

- **The narrow-leadership diagnosis:** "I have been writing all week about the fact that **the
  QQQ looked strong because of a few big name stocks, but that most stocks were breaking
  down.**" Transports and utilities "are already in solid Stage IV declines."
- **The stage picture:** DIA and SPY back below their 30-week averages after a failed retake —
  "a critical sign of weakness" — and QQQ's recent breakout flagged as a possible "head fake."
- **The pension trigger restated precisely:** "I begin to exit the market in my most
  conservative pension accounts **when the 30 week average starts to curve down**. It has not
  done so yet, but it is looking ominous."
- **The credential he cites for the method:** "The primary indicator that made me flee the
  market both times [2000, 2008] is based on Stan Weinstein's Stage Analysis. **When the market
  indexes enter a Stage IV decline I retreat.**"
- **Positioning before the event:** small SQQQ (3X inverse) and a put on GXC (China) — plus the
  macro read: China's weakening economy behind the worldwide commodity declines.
- **Candour about a bad call:** he'd stepped aside from fatigue and "missed the recent bounce
  and served as a wonderful contrary indicator" — self-deprecation published in the same post
  as the warning that proved right.
