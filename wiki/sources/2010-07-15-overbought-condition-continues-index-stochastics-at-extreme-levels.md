# WW 2010-07-15 — The stochastic rule — declines begin above 80, advances below 20

**Source:** [Overbought condition continues; index stochastics at extreme levels](../../raw/posts/2010-07-15-overbought-condition-continues-index-stochastics-at-extreme-levels.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2010/07/overbought-condition-continues-index-stochastics-at-extreme-levels/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The stochastic overlay stated as a rule rather than a reading. With the daily 10,4 stochastic at 85 (QQQQ), 95 (SPY) and 97 (DIA): "**Stocks and index ETF's can stay overbought for a long time. The important point to remember, though, is that declines tend to begin from a stochastic reading above 80 and advances begin from a level below 20.**" So an extreme reading is a *precondition* for a turn, not a trigger for one — "at some point we will get the decline and the key to the trend will be where it bottoms out." Day 1 of a new QQQQ up-trend; GMI 2 but the more sensitive GMI-R at 6, "reflecting the strength of the short term up-trend."

## Key claims

- "Declines tend to begin from a stochastic reading above 80 and advances begin from a level below 20."
- "Stocks and index ETF's can stay overbought for a long time" — the extreme is a precondition, not a trigger.
- The GMI-R moving ahead of the GMI as the short-term trend turns — the sensitivity difference, visible.
- What matters after an overbought decline is *where it bottoms*, not that it happens.
