# WW 2015-01-25 — Fly by my gut or follow my instruments? AAPL

**Source:** [Fly by my gut or follow my instruments? (fasten seat-belts!) $AAPL looks strong](../../raw/posts/2015-01-25-fly-by-my-gut-or-follow-my-instruments-fasten-seat-belts-aapl-looks-strong.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2015/01/fly-by-my-gut-or-follow-my-instruments-fasten-seat-belts-aapl-looks-strong/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A GMI Buy signal and QQQ Day U-1 arrive while every rational argument (Greece, oil, the dollar, index divergence with SPY still under its 10-week) and his own gut say the bounce will fail like the last several — each of which reversed in 5–7 days. He states the discipline: the instruments have the better record and "the market tends to move in a direction opposite to the way my emotions point." Also: AAPL regaining its 10-week average — "I have always profited by owning AAPL when it is above this average."

## Key claims

- "Every once in a while my technical indicators point one way and my gut to another. While I have never systematically analysed how often which way was correct, I suspect it was my instruments."
- "The market tends to move in a direction opposite to the way my emotions point."
- Recent GMI Buy signals had "occurred within a short bounce only to have each end and reverse in about 5-7 days" — the whipsaw pattern he formalised four weeks later in [WW 2015-02-22](2015-02-22-an-important-limitation-of-the-gmi-signals.md).
- Index divergence noted: SPY still below its 10-week average while QQQ climbs above it.
