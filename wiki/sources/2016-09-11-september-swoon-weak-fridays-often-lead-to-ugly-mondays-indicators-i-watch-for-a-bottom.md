# WW 2016-09-11 — September swoon — band expansion as the start of a major move

**Source:** [September swoon? Weak Fridays often lead to Ugly Mondays—Indicators I watch for a bottom](../../raw/posts/2016-09-11-september-swoon-weak-fridays-often-lead-to-ugly-mondays-indicators-i-watch-for-a-bottom.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2016/09/september-swoon-weak-fridays-often-lead-to-ugly-mondays-indicators-i-watch-for-a-bottom/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A Bollinger-band reading that complements the pinch: the indexes had been "consolidating within their narrowing upper and lower Bollinger Bands," and then "the market finally tipped its hand on Friday and broke out into a downward direction… a high volume decline out of the recent channel formed by the 15.2 daily Bollinger Bands. **The expansion (widening) of the bands often signals the beginning of a major move.**" Two seasonal observations: "**large declines on Fridays often lead to ugly Mondays when the public gets a chance to sell after pondering their portfolio losses over the weekend**"; and "**bottoms often come in October** when it may take 3rd quarter earnings release season to revive stocks. We are now in the post 2nd quarter earnings release lull and **a decline typically sets up the next earnings propelled rise**." September named as historically the weakest month.

## Key claims

- **Band expansion after a narrowing channel signals the start of a major move** — direction unknown until the break.
- The narrowing-then-widening sequence is the pinch and its resolution, described as one pattern.
- Friday declines → ugly Mondays: the weekend gives the public time to decide to sell.
- The post-earnings lull sets up the next earnings-driven rise; bottoms often in October.
- September as historically the weakest month.
