# WW 2014-04-20 — Market at critical juncture; Guppy charts show relative weakness in QQQ vs SPY

**Source:** [Market at critical juncture. Guppy charts show relative weakness in $QQQ vs. $SPY](../../raw/posts/2014-04-20-market-at-critical-juncture-guppy-charts-show-relative-weakness-in-qqq-vs-spy.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2014/04/market-at-critical-juncture-guppy-charts-show-relative-weakness-in-qqq-vs-spy/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The modified GMMA stated exactly — 12 EMAs, short 3/5/8/10/12/15 in red, long 30/35/40/45/50/60 in blue, plus a length-1 average for the close as a grey dotted line, plotted daily and weekly — with the reading rules: RWB = red rising well above blue; white space disappearing = the advance weakening; BWR = considerable weakness; "it pays to be invested long in the market only when an RWB pattern is present." April 2014's split market: QQQ in a daily BWR, SPY merely converged. GMI 3; QQQ under its 10-week for four weeks while SPY is back above; D-19; 69% of Nasdaq-100 down. Leaders decimated — CMG opening +$32 and closing −$33 on good earnings, AMZN, TSLA — read as "they shoot the leaders first, followed by the rest of the market… why would I hold stocks now?" Hourly indicators used to expect a Monday retreat; "why fight the tide?"

## Key claims

- Modified GMMA periods: red 3,5,8,10,12,15; blue 30,35,40,45,50,60; +1 for the close.
- "It pays to be invested long in the market only when an RWB pattern is present."
- Split-market read: QQQ BWR vs SPY convergence.
- Leaders breaking on good news (CMG) as a market-weakness tell: "they shoot the leaders first."
