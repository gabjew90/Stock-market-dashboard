## What it covers

The re-entry after the September 2020 pullback, with the psychology attached: "I have learned to follow the market's direction and to ignore the media or my personal opinions. My indicators have turned up and I must heed their message." QQQ, SPY and DIA all retook their 10-week averages; he bought TQQQ and would add if the up-trend continued. "A new up-trend is always met with skepticism because most people remain intimidated by the recent down-trend. **If the up-trend should falter, I can exit with a small loss — without emotion and no ego involvement.**" The claim restated: "I have found many times that **TQQQ will rise in a sustained up-trend more than 90%+ of individual stocks**." And TSM as the stock case: a GLB at 60.64 in early July, +31% in five weeks to $84, a nine-week consolidation, then a breakout with a **WGB** — "the 3 weekly averages of price were lined up properly with 4wk>10wk>30wk and the stock bounced up off of" the 4-week.

## Key claims

- "If the up-trend should falter, I can exit with a small loss — without emotion and no ego involvement" — the stop as the thing that removes ego.
- Skepticism at the start of an up-trend is expected and is not information.
- TQQQ beats 90%+ of individual stocks in a sustained up-trend (2020 statement).
- TSM: GLB → +31% → nine-week consolidation → WGB breakout, the full shape in one chart.
