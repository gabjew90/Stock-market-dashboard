# WW 2017-08-19 — A setup's hit rate measured in a bad tape — 8%

**Source:** [New $QQQ short term down-trend; in cash; Come back at Halloween?](../../raw/posts/2017-08-19-new-qqq-short-term-down-trend-in-cash-come-back-at-halloween.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2017/08/new-qqq-short-term-down-trend-in-cash-come-back-at-halloween/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**A rare quantification of how badly a setup performs in the wrong regime.** "With the GMI at 1, 6x more new 52
week lows than new highs, and **only 8% of recent bounce on support set-ups working**, I am on the side-lines in
my trading accounts." That is his own BOS entry, measured, and failing 92% of the time in a weak tape. It is the
number the [model-book critique](../methodology/trading-philosophy.md) says the pattern literature never
supplies, produced about his own primary bounce entry — and it makes the regime-dependence claim concrete rather
than rhetorical. He also tracks the setup's hit rate as a *market* indicator, not just as a performance measure:
a collapse in how often bounces hold is itself evidence about the tape. The seasonal frame around it: "I had
written that **declines often materialize after quarterly earnings are mainly out. We are now in the post
earnings lull during the weakest market months of the year.**"

## Key claims

- **8% of recent bounce-off-support setups were working** — his own entry, measured, failing more than nine times in ten.
- **The hit rate doubles as a market reading**: when setups stop working, that is information about the tape, not only about the setups.
- This is the missing denominator the model-book critique demands, supplied about his own method.
- The post-earnings lull in the seasonally weakest months given as the context.
