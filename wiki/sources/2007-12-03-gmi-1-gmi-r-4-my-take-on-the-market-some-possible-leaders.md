# WW 2007-12-03 — A two-sided evidence ledger, and how turnarounds must unfold

**Source:** [GMI: 1+; GMI-R: 4+; My take on the market; some possible leaders](../../raw/posts/2007-12-03-gmi-1-gmi-r-4-my-take-on-the-market-some-possible-leaders.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2007/12/gmi-1-gmi-r-4-my-take-on-the-market-some-possible-leaders/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**A two-sided evidence ledger, written out as one** — the clearest example of how he resolves conflicting
indicators. "Evidence on the side of a possible end to the current decline in the QQQQ are: 1) MACD for the daily
QQQQ has had a bullish cross of its signal line; 2) T2108 indicator is rising out of extreme lows; 3) GMI-R is
4+; GMI-S is now 63%; and 4) leading growth stocks (AAPL, RIMM, ISRG, BIDU) are holding up. **On the side of a
continuing decline** are: 1) GMI is 1+, reflecting a small number of new highs, a weak daily QQQQ trend, a weak
IBD growth mutual fund trend; 2) the QQQQ remains below its 10 week average; and 3) the QQQQ has just bounced
down off of resistance at the 30 day average." Then the synthesis, which is the durable part: "**it looks like the
most sensitive indicators are starting to turn up, but the relatively longer term trend indicators are still
declining. Statistically, this is how turnarounds must unfold. The prudent thing to do is to wait a little longer
to see whether the longer term indicators follow suit and start to rise. The jury is still out for me.**" Fast
indicators turning first is *necessary but not sufficient* — it is equally what a failed bounce looks like.
**GMI-R's design purpose, stated**: "the GMI-R is stronger than the GMI because it **has a number of shorter term
indicators that can detect a change in trend earlier**." And the leaders scan he would run if the turn held:
strong technical patterns plus "**recent quarterly earnings increases of at least 100% and revenue growth of
20+%**," six of eleven names drawn from the past year's IBD 100 lists.

## Key claims

- **The conflict is resolved by timeframe, not by vote count**: the fast indicators had turned, the slow ones had not, and the slow ones govern.
- **"Statistically, this is how turnarounds must unfold"** — every real bottom starts this way, and so does every failed bounce, so the pattern alone carries no signal.
- **GMI-R exists to turn earlier than the GMI** — its extra components are shorter-term by design, which is why it can read 4+ while the GMI reads 1+.
- He publishes the bear case against his own developing view, itemised and numbered.
- The leaders scan's fundamental gate: ≥100% quarterly EPS growth *and* ≥20% revenue growth on top of the technical pattern.
- GMI-S at 63% quoted as "63% of the 16 short term indicators for four indexes (DIA, QQQQ, SPY, IJR)" — the construction confirmed again.
