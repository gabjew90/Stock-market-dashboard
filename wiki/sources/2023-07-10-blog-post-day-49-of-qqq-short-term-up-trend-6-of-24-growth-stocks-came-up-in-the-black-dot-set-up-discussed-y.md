## What it covers

A short but methodologically distinct post: it establishes **industry clustering in the OSB scan output as a second-order signal**. When a quarter of the day's candidates came from one industry, Dr. Wish read the concentration itself — not any individual chart — as evidence of institutional accumulation.

## Key claims

- "The fact that so many stocks in the same industry had oversold bounces (OSB) after a recent yearly high suggests to me **big buying interest**."
- The cluster: DHI, KBH, LEN, PHM, MHO, CCS — 6 of the 24 growth stocks passing the setup that day, all residential construction.
- LEN is given as the typical example, noted for its recent climb above its green line top (a GLB), reinforcing the GLB + OSB pairing.
