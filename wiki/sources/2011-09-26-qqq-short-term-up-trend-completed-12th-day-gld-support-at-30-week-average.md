# WW 2011-09-26 — Why QQQ and SPY diverge — a structural reason

**Source:** [QQQ short  term up-trend completed 12th day; GLD support at 30 week average](../../raw/posts/2011-09-26-qqq-short-term-up-trend-completed-12th-day-gld-support-at-30-week-average.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2011/09/qqq-short-term-up-trend-completed-12th-day-gld-support-at-30-week-average/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**A structural explanation for index divergence, which the wiki has treated only as an observation.** "The SPY
has closed below its 10 week average for 9 weeks but the QQQ for just one week. **This reflects the fact that the
Nasdaq 100 Index (QQQ), unlike the S&P500 Index (SPY), contains no financial stocks. And as in 2008, the major
weakness is showing up in the financial stocks.**" So a QQQ/SPY split is not always a tech-versus-everything
signal — sometimes it is a **composition** fact, and the diagnostic question is which sector is missing from
which index. That reframes the [QQQ-centricity blind spot](../methodology/gmi.md) as well: the GMI's tech weighting means it can
be structurally blind to a financial-sector collapse. **GLD's support, answered as a chart question**: asked by
Judy where gold would find support, "note how well GLD has found support at the **30 week average** since the
current up-trend began in early 2009. Since then GLD has been in **a perfect Stage II advance**. Support should
therefore be around **\$154**." A support level derived from a moving average rather than from prior lows.
T2108 at 20%, "in low, neutral territory," with the note that "**the T2108 indicator reached 7%, very oversold,
at the August market bottom**." Position: partially invested, "**but will exit all positions if the short term
up-trend ends**."

## Key claims

- **Index divergence can be compositional**: the QQQ holds no financial stocks, so a financials-led decline shows up in the SPY first and says nothing about tech.
- That gives the GMI's tech weighting a second failure mode — structural blindness to a sector the QQQ does not contain.
- **Support projected from the 30-week average** rather than from prior lows, on an instrument in a clean Stage 2.
- "A perfect Stage II advance" — the stage framework applied to gold across two and a half years.
- T2108 at 7% recorded as the August 2011 bottom reading.
- Partial exposure with a stated full-exit condition: the short-term up-trend ending.
