# WW 2020-03-22 — The nightly spreadsheet, and the crossover that flagged the COVID top

**Source:** [My Successful New High and New Low indicators flagged the market top](../../raw/posts/2020-03-22-my-successful-new-high-and-new-low-indicators-flagged-the-market-top.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2020/03/my-successful-new-high-and-new-low-indicators-flagged-the-market-top/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The instrument itself, shown**: "below is a segment of **the spreadsheet I update every day after the market
closes**" — the two mirror indicators are its first two columns. The rationale is Darvas again, and stated as the
reason the calculation exists at all: "**Nicolas Darvas had said that when the stocks that he was buying at new
52 week highs started to falter it told him to exit the market. That is why I calculate the Successful New High
indicator each market day.**" **And the signal that mattered**: "**note that on February 21, the two indicators
were equal at 55%**. Almost all of the prior days there were [more successful new highs than new lows]." So the
top was flagged not by either indicator's level but by the **crossover** — the high-side follow-through falling
to meet the low-side follow-through, on the last trading day before the fastest bear market on record. That is a
use of the pair the wiki has documented only as two separate readings, and it is the sharpest instance of the
mirror statistic doing work.

## Key claims

- **The two mirror indicators are read as a pair, and their convergence is the signal** — equal at 55% on 21 February 2020, the last session before the crash.
- **The nightly spreadsheet is the primary instrument**, updated after every close, with these two as its first columns.
- Darvas is named again as the reason the follow-through calculation exists: leaders failing at new highs told him to exit.
- A crossover of the two is a different reading from either level — the wiki had them only as separate series.
- Documented *after* the fact, with the date and the numbers, rather than claimed as a call.
