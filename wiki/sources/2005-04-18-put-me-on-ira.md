# WW 2005-04-18 — Put me on, IRA — the case for the short side, 2005

**Source:** [Put me on, IRA](../../raw/posts/2005-04-18-put-me-on-ira.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/04/put-me-on-ira/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"The market always goes further than everyone thinks is justified — up or down." The founding statement of his two-sidedness: "Experienced traders do not care which way the market goes, as long as it moves in a trend… A good bear lasts 10 or more months and prices decline quicker than they go up — fear is quite a motivator. Why play only one side of the game." A short-selling explainer for readers who fear it, and the observation that the short-term interest-rate index he follows jumped as it had when the rate rise began in January.

## Key claims

- "Experienced traders do not care which way the market goes, as long as it moves in a trend."
- Bears last 10+ months and fall faster than markets rise.
- Short-selling mechanics for the fearful reader.
