# WW 2005-12-11 — Appel's new book on TA — 'three consecutive successful trades… that's when they get us'

**Source:** [Appel’s new book on TA; GMI declines to +5; WPM shows short term weakness;  Long bonds to weaken; Some weak sectors: Wash sale rule](../../raw/posts/2005-12-11-appels-new-book-on-ta-gmi-declines-to-5-wpm-shows-short-term-weakness-long-bonds-to-weaken-some-weak-sectors.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/12/appels-new-book-on-ta-gmi-declines-to-5-wpm-shows-short-term-weakness-long-bonds-to-weaken-some-weak-sectors-wash-sale-rule/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Gerald Appel (2005): "One successful trade makes us feel good. Two successful trades in a row make us feel pretty smart. Three consecutive successful trades makes us feel like a genius. That's when they get us." Appel's *Technical Analysis: Power Tools for Active Investors* recommended as the step after Weinstein — "the most refreshing and clearly written discussion of a select number of technical indicators" by the MACD's inventor. Component 1 under 50% ("failed breakouts in growth stocks is often a sign of a topping market") — one sign, up-trend intact.

## Key claims

- Overconfidence after a streak as the trap (Appel).
- Failed breakouts as a topping sign, restated.
- Reading order: Weinstein → Appel.
