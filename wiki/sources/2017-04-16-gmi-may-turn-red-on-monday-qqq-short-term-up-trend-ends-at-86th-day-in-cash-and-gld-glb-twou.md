# WW 2017-04-16 — 0/0 to 6/6 as the re-entry rule; the dwindling new-high list as an advantage

**Source:** [GMI may turn RED on Monday;  $QQQ short term up-trend ends at 86th day; In cash and $GLD; GLB:  $TWOU](../../raw/posts/2017-04-16-gmi-may-turn-red-on-monday-qqq-short-term-up-trend-ends-at-86th-day-in-cash-and-gld-glb-twou.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2017/04/gmi-may-turn-red-on-monday-qqq-short-term-up-trend-ends-at-86th-day-in-cash-and-gld-glb-twou/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Three procedural specifics. **The RWB count notation read out, and turned into a re-entry rule**: the daily SPY
shows "the **0/0 pattern**, reflecting that the SPY has now closed below all 12 daily moving averages. **A return
to 6/6, signifying a close above all 12 averages, would be a new buy signal for me.**" The weekly chart of the
same index reads 10/5/0/0 — "the 10 shows that 10 of the 12 *weekly* averages line up properly with each above
the next longer average." **The two speeds disagreeing, and what that means**: daily RWB gone while weekly RWB
holds is "a **short term pause within a longer term up-trend**," and "it is so much more safe and profitable to
trade on the long side when the daily and weekly charts both form RWB patterns." The pension trigger is stated
in GMMA terms: "**Should the *weekly* chart turn BWR I would consider transferring my university pension from
equity mutual funds into money market funds, something I do very rarely.**" And **the decline as a screening
advantage**: "**I like market declines because it is easier for me to find potential leaders in a dwindling new
high list.**" There were 60 new 52-week highs in a 4,800-stock universe that Thursday — few enough to read every
one looking for green line breakouts, which is how he found TWOU (public at \$13 in 2014, since tripled, daily
RWB 11/5/6, "above average green volume spikes on up days"). Also here, a clean corroboration of the Sell rule:
"The GMI is now at 1 (of 6) and **still on a GREEN signal. One more day below 3 will change it to RED.**"

## Key claims

- **6/6 — a close back above all 12 daily averages — is a stated buy signal**, and 0/0 its opposite. The notation is a trigger, not just a description.
- **Daily BWR inside weekly RWB = a pause, not a top.** The two speeds are read as a pair, and the slower one governs the pension.
- **The pension exit trigger in GMMA terms**: the *weekly* chart turning BWR, an action taken "very rarely."
- **A short new-high list is easier to work, not worse** — 60 names in a 4,800-stock universe can be read in full.
- The GMI Sell rule corroborated in practice: a score of 1 still carries a GREEN signal until a second day below 3.
- The 5-day SQQQ rule restated with its base rate: ~40% of new QQQ down-trends since 2006 lasted under 5 days.
