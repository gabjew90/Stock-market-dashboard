## What it covers

A stated revision to his own trailing rule. After the Blue Dot of Happiness on GLD (2025-08-20), the stock climbed above its **daily 5 EMA** two days later "and has only closed below it once. One could have bought GLD on any of the bounces up off of the daily 5 EMA and profited greatly." The revision, explicit: "During my recent TraderLion Conference talk… I described the Blue Dot of Happiness signal and how I was using the 8 EMA. **I have since found the 5 EMA to work even better for many rising stocks.** Add the 5 EMA to your daily charts and see how many times advancing stocks bounce up off of this EMA." The channel it defines: GLD "traded between the 5 EMA and its upper 2.15 Bollinger Band for the entire time." The exit: "if I owned GLD I would become defensive the first day it **closes** below the 5 EMA." And the epistemics: "Why this can work, I do not know, or care." IREN given as a second instance; he scans for stocks reaching ATHs and tracking along their 5 EMA.

## Key claims

- **The 8-day EMA trail is superseded by the 5-day EMA** for post-Blue-Dot rising stocks (October 2025).
- The pattern: price rides between the 5 EMA and the upper 2.15 Bollinger Band; buy the bounces off the 5 EMA.
- Exit: become defensive on the first *close* below the 5 EMA.
- "Why this can work, I do not know, or care" — the orthodox-agnostic stance applied to his newest rule.
