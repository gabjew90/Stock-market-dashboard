# WW 2008-03-27 — The record claimed in one line; the covered-call scanner named

**Source:** [GMI: 3; GMI-R: 6; Day 2 of QQQQ up-trend; CPST–a Judy’s pick](../../raw/posts/2008-03-27-gmi-3-gmi-r-6-day-2-of-qqqq-up-trend-cpst-a-judys-pick.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2008/03/gmi-3-gmi-r-6-day-2-of-qqqq-up-trend-cpst-a-judys-pick/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**A compact track-record claim with a number the wiki does not have.** "The indicators in the GMI have **kept
me out of all major market declines since 1995**, including the bad markets of 2001-2002. **I then got back in
the market in 2003 and my account was up over 50% that year.** I also avoided the decline that began last
January, **which lasted 55 days**." The 2003 figure (+50%) is the only annual return he states for that year. On
the day: day 2 of a new QQQQ up-trend, "and I am **beginning to wade into this market slowly by buying QLD and
writing some covered calls. If this up-trend should end quickly, I will get out and go immediately to cash
again. There is always time to catch a real up-trend that can last months.**" **And the tool named**: "the
service that I use to scan stocks for writing covered calls is at **www.poweropt.com**. I like this service
because **I can narrow down my scan to include only IBD100 stocks**" — the covered-call overlay documented
elsewhere runs on a screener filtered to the growth list, not on the whole option universe.

## Key claims

- **+50% in 2003** — the only stated annual return for the re-entry year, alongside "kept me out of all major market declines since 1995."
- **PowerOptions (poweropt.com) is the covered-call scanner**, filtered to IBD 100 names.
- Re-entry is a *combination*: QLD plus written calls, entered slowly on day 2 with an immediate-exit condition.
- "There is always time to catch a real up-trend that can last months" — the same durations argument as November 2007.
- The January 2008 down-trend is dated at 55 days.
