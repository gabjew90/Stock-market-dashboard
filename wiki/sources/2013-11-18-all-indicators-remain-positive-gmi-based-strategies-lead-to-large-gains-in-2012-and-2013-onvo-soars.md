# WW 2013-11-18 — A third party backtests the GMI — and he declines to verify it

**Source:** [All indicators remain positive; GMI based strategies lead to large gains in 2012 and 2013; ONVO soars](../../raw/posts/2013-11-18-all-indicators-remain-positive-gmi-based-strategies-lead-to-large-gains-in-2012-and-2013-onvo-soars.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2013/11/all-indicators-remain-positive-gmi-based-strategies-lead-to-large-gains-in-2012-and-2013-onvo-soars/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**Independent verification, reported with a caveat.** "The **Dark Liquidity site** has been comparing the GMI
based trading strategy versus a number of other strategies. For each of the last two years **a strategy that
bought the QLD whenever the GMI signaled buy, and went to cash every time it signaled sell, beat all other
strategies followed. In 2012 the GMI based QLD strategy was up about +30% and thus far in 2013 it is up +42%.**"
Then the qualification, which is the part worth keeping: "**I do not check that site's computations**, but my
experience is that repeatedly, buying QLD when the GMI signals buy has usually worked." He reports a favourable
independent result *and* discloses that he has not audited it. The in-period numbers he can check himself: since
the 4 September buy signal, QQQ +9.3%, SPY +8.5%, **QLD +19.5%, TQQQ +29.9%**. And the standing disclaimer:
"**while past performance does not guarantee future results**, it is striking to me that I have found a"
[strategy that keeps working]. This is the only third-party test of the GMI documented anywhere in the corpus.

## Key claims

- **A third party backtested GMI-signalled QLD and reported +30% (2012) and +42% (2013)**, beating every other strategy it tracked.
- **He discloses that he has not verified the computations** — "I do not check that site's computations" — rather than adopting the result.
- The only independent test of the GMI in the corpus; every other audit on this wiki is his own.
- In-period figures he could check: QLD +19.5% and TQQQ +29.9% against QQQ +9.3% since the signal.
- Note what the tested strategy is: leveraged long on Buy, **cash** on Sell — not short.
