# WW 2014-10-26 — QQQ short-term trend turns up, retakes the 30-week; a Day-1 TQQQ nibble; GLB stocks shine

**Source:** [$QQQ short term trend turns up, retakes 30 week average; GLB stocks shine: $AAPL,$BABY,$AGIO,$RGLS,$REGN](../../raw/posts/2014-10-26-qqq-short-term-trend-turns-up-retakes-30-week-average-glb-stocks-shine-aaplbabyagiorglsregn.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2014/10/qqq-short-term-trend-turns-up-retakes-30-week-average-glb-stocks-shine-aaplbabyagiorglsregn/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"A new QQQ short term up-trend has begun. However, it has to last 5 days for me to heavily accumulate TQQQ. I did take a small position in TQQQ at the end of the day on Friday. Buying right after a technical buy signal is actually low risk to me, because if the technical buy signal fails, I can quickly sell out with a relatively small loss." GMI above 3, one more day to a Buy; QQQ back above its 30-week — "the Stage 2 advance is still intact." Q4 seasonality and window dressing ("why buy mutual funds when one can buy index ETFs"). AAPL above its rising 10-week and back over its green line — "I have repeatedly found it profitable to own AAPL as long as it stayed above its rising 10 week average"; GLBs "can sometimes consolidate… especially if the market becomes weak" (BABY hugging its 10-week; AGIO; RGLS; REGN). On Judy's buy-and-hold: "I do not have the confidence to hold such stocks for a long time through thick and thin." SPY still under its 10-week — "not out of the woods yet."

## Key claims

- Day-1 nibble, heavy accumulation only after 5 days; the signal defines the stop, so early entry is low risk.
- 30-week retaken = Stage 2 intact.
- GLBs consolidate in weak markets; hugging the rising 10-week is healthy.
- His horizon vs Judy's, stated.
