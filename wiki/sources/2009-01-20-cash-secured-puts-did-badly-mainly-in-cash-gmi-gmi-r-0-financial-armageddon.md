# WW 2009-01-20 — A loss taken, and the rule it produced about selling options

**Source:** [Cash-secured puts did badly; Mainly in cash; GMI, GMI-R: 0; Financial Armageddon?](../../raw/posts/2009-01-20-cash-secured-puts-did-badly-mainly-in-cash-gmi-gmi-r-0-financial-armageddon.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2009/01/cash-secured-puts-did-badly-mainly-in-cash-gmi-gmi-r-0-financial-armageddon/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A loss reported and converted into a rule, which is the pattern worth noting as much as the rule itself. "This
month **I lost money on many of my cash-secured puts**. Too many stocks declined below the put option strike
price and I had to buy the puts I sold back at higher prices." Then the reasoning: "**Selling puts is equivalent
to buying stocks and selling call options on the shares. I learned again the lesson not to sell puts or covered
calls during a weak or declining market.**" That is the crucial qualifier on both income overlays documented
elsewhere on this wiki — a cash-secured put and a covered call are **long-delta positions**, so the premium does
not protect against a decline, it merely reduces the loss slightly while capping the recovery. "Learned **again**"
is his own word. On the trend: "once the QQQQ fell into a down-trend, **I closed all positions and bought a very
little of QID**… if the QQQQ down-trend deepens I will buy more" — the standard scaling. MACD breadth had
collapsed from 88% of the Nasdaq-100 on 2 January to 27%. And a read of the banks: C, BAC and JPM all showing the
same weekly pattern on "huge weekly volume, indicating massive selling… **I do not see a bottom, more like a
black hole.**"

## Key claims

- **Selling puts and writing covered calls are long-delta trades** — "selling puts is equivalent to buying stocks and selling call options on the shares" — so both fail in a declining market.
- **The rule stated as a loss, not a caution**: "I learned again the lesson not to sell puts or covered calls during a weak or declining market."
- "Learned **again**" — he flags the repetition rather than presenting it as a first discovery.
- The income overlays documented in 2007 and 2014 carry this regime condition; without it they read as unconditional.
- MACD breadth as a fast deterioration gauge: 88% to 27% in under three weeks.
