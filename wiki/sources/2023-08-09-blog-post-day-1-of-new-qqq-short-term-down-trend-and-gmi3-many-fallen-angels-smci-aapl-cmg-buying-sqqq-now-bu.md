## What it covers

The short-side accumulation rule stated with a live position: "I have found that about **40% of new QQQ short term down-trends, as I define them, end in 5 days or less. I bought a little SQQQ on Wednesday and will accumulate more as the down-trend progresses, especially if it can pass a fifth day.**" The GMI component watch is explicit: "the GMI could fall to 2 and trigger a Red signal if the IBD Mutual Fund Index (0muti) closes below its 50 day average. **It is right on it now.**" Context: darling stocks hitting air pockets (SMCI, CMG, AAPL), NVDA closing below its 10-week for the first time since January 6, sell-offs even after earnings beats, and the weak post-earnings period — "many down markets end in October after a wash out — 'come back at Halloween'." Accounts split as always: mutual funds in the retirement accounts, cash plus a little SQQQ in the trading IRA.

## Key claims

- Down-side accumulation mirrors the up-side: a little on Day 1, more as it progresses, especially past Day 5.
- The ~40% figure attributed to his own definition of a down-trend.
- Component 6 (`0muti` vs its 50-day) watched as the swing vote that would flip the GMI to Red.
- Seasonal: "many down markets end in October after a wash out."
- Leaders breaking on earnings beats as the tell that the tape has changed.
