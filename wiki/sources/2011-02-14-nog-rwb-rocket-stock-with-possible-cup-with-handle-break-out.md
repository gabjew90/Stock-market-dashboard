# WW 2011-02-14 — NOG: RWB rocket with a cup-with-handle; the ultra-ETF conclusion again; the IBD 50 bounce scan

**Source:** [NOG: RWB rocket stock with possible cup-with-handle break-out](../../raw/posts/2011-02-14-nog-rwb-rocket-stock-with-possible-cup-with-handle-break-out.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2011/02/nog-rwb-rocket-stock-with-possible-cup-with-handle-break-out/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"Half the battle for profits can be achieved if we track the market averages" — Darvas, Livermore, O'Neil all say so. GMI/GMI-R at maximum, 23 weeks above the 10-week; he had exited "a few weeks ago" on the muni news, too early — "the GMI only tells me the current trend. It will signal a down-trend only after it has begun. So, the proper course for me is to remain on the long side, with one eye always on the exits." The 58-day up-trend: QQQQ +11.4% vs QLD +23.9%, TYH +39.9%, TQQQ +37.2%, while only 20% of Nasdaq-100 stocks rose ≥ 20% — "the best strategy may be to slowly buy into one of these ultra long index ETFs when the short term trend turns up and to slowly wade into the inverse ultra ETF's (QID, TYP, SQQQ) when the trend turns down." A scan of the IBD 50 for stocks **bouncing off support** (NOG, MELI, COH, EZPW, LYB); NOG's cup-with-handle pivot ~$28.15 → O'Neil's 8% stop as a GTC order at ~$25.89 (David Ryan closer); NOG an RWB rocket at its ATH.

## Key claims

- "Remain on the long side, with one eye always on the exits" — the answer to exiting too early on news.
- Ultra-ETF arithmetic (58 days): TQQQ +37.2% vs 20% of Nasdaq-100 up ≥ 20%; wade into ultras with the short-term trend.
- An IBD 50 bounce-off-support scan in February 2011 — the BOS idea five years before the name.
- O'Neil's 8% below the pivot as a GTC stop; Ryan sets it closer.
