# WW 2009-07-20 — The turn is here — 'simplistic and naive, BUT IT WORKS'

**Source:** [Up-trend is here; 50% invested; some rocket stocks](../../raw/posts/2009-07-20-turn-is-here-50-invested.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2009/07/turn-is-here-50-invested/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The 30-week rule stated as a complete system, at the 2009 re-entry: "the 30 week averages for the SPY, QQQQ and DIA are turning up. This is a classic Weinstein Stage 2 pattern. **I get out of the market when the 30 week averages turn down and back in when they turn up. I know this seems simplistic and naive, BUT IT WORKS!! It keeps me on the right side of the market's primary trend. And 70-80% of stocks follow the general market's trend — end of story.**" The discipline it requires: "to be successful in the market, I have to block out all other opinions and look at what the market is telling me." He is 50% invested. Breadth snapped back — T2108 to 70%, MACD breadth from 10% to 79% in a week, 72% of Nasdaq-100 above their 30-day — and the watchlist of stocks near all-time highs includes GMCR and NTES.

## Key claims

- The 30-week rule as an entire system: out when it turns down, in when it turns up.
- "I know this seems simplistic and naive, BUT IT WORKS!!"
- The correlation figure given as **70-80%** here (usually 70%).
- Re-entry is partial — 50% invested, not all at once.
- MACD breadth moving 10% → 79% in a week as the snap-back measure.
