# WW 2006-01-23 — Clues to this rout? Biggest losers among the big names; WPM large/small divergence; chickens survive

Chickens survive](../../raw/posts/2006-01-23-gmi-4-clues-to-this-rout-wpm-shows-smallmid-caps-stronger-bounce-coming-chickens-survive.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2006/01/gmi-4-clues-to-this-rout-wpm-shows-smallmid-caps-stronger-bounce-coming-chickens-survive/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

After a Friday rout took the GMI from +6 to +4: "The GMI cannot predict changes in trend, it only provides me with a reasonably certain indication that the trend has changed." The clue in hindsight — the largest Nasdaq-100 decliners of the prior three days were YHOO, INTC, AAPL, GOOG: "when the biggest losers are the major big names, it may signify that the market is going to crumble"; and negative reactions to good earnings. Daily components "too close to call" were counted negative. WPM: the rout was concentrated in large caps — DIA/SPY/QQQQ under their 30-day, MDY/IJR not. "A chicken lives to play another day, while hogs get slaughtered and ostriches get run over."

## Key claims

- The GMI confirms, it does not predict (2006 statement).
- Big-name leaders as the biggest losers = a crumble tell; bad reactions to good earnings.
- WPM used to localise weakness by capitalisation.
- "A chicken lives to play another day."
