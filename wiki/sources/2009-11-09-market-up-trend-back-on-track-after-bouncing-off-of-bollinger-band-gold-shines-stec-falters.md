# WW 2009-11-09 — The fifth bounce off the lower Bollinger Band — and the patience he lacked

**Source:** [Market up-trend back on track after bouncing off of Bollinger band; Gold shines, STEC falters.](../../raw/posts/2009-11-09-market-up-trend-back-on-track-after-bouncing-off-of-bollinger-band-gold-shines-stec-falters.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2009/11/market-up-trend-back-on-track-after-bouncing-off-of-bollinger-band-gold-shines-stec-falters/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

He annotates a running chart: the QQQQ's **fifth** bounce off its lower Bollinger Band, added in red to the chart he had posted three weeks earlier after four. The confession attached: "**If only I had the patience to wait for the bounce to enter my long positions! Maybe next time.**" The tape had whipsawed him and the instruments now said long: "as much as I hate being whipsawed by the recent decline, **if I flew solely by instrument, I would have to be long again**" — QQQQ back above its 10-week, GMI 5, GMI-R 9, T2108 41% out of oversold. The caveat: a possible right shoulder — if QQQQ falters before surpassing the 10/21 peak, and especially on a close below the neckline near 40.6, expect a large drop; so "close sell stops" on the new longs.

## Key claims

- A repeated, numbered pattern tracked across weeks on one chart — the lower-band bounce as a recurring entry.
- "If only I had the patience to wait for the bounce to enter my long positions!" — the bounce doctrine as a lesson he keeps relearning.
- "If I flew solely by instrument, I would have to be long again" — the pilot metaphor again.
- A named neckline level and close stops as the hedge against the head-and-shoulders risk.
