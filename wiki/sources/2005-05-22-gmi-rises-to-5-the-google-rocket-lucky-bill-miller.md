# WW 2005-05-22 — GMI rises to +5 — go with your instruments (2005); new highs beat new lows

**Source:** [GMI rises to +5, The Google Rocket, Lucky Bill Miller](../../raw/posts/2005-05-22-gmi-rises-to-5-the-google-rocket-lucky-bill-miller.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/05/gmi-rises-to-5-the-google-rocket-lucky-bill-miller/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

GMI +5 with only the Successful New High component short: 65 of 104 stocks that hit new highs 10 days ago closed higher (65%), while only 15 of 44 new lows were lower — "the market is telling us that buying new highs has been a better strategy than shorting new lows." "Remember a few days ago I said that my gut was telling me the market should go down but my instruments were telling me to buy. **One must always go with his/her instruments — the market action.** I do not waste a lot of time trying to divine the market's direction by focusing on economic news or current events. Don't get attached to a scenario."

## Key claims

- The instruments-over-gut rule stated in May 2005 — ten years before the 2015 post.
- "Don't get attached to a scenario" (2005 form).
- New-high vs new-low success rates as a read of which side to play.
