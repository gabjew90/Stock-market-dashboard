# WW 2010-06-04 — IBD calls a follow-through day; he stays in cash

**Source:** [IBD says market in new up-trend, I am not yet convinced; some possible rocket stocks](../../raw/posts/2010-06-04-ibd-says-market-in-new-up-trend-i-am-not-yet-convinced-some-possible-rocket-stocks.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2010/06/ibd-says-market-in-new-up-trend-i-am-not-yet-convinced-some-possible-rocket-stocks/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The follow-through-day disagreement, worked in real time: IBD declares a confirmed up-trend on
its FTD rule while his own instruments say the down-trend is intact — and he defers to his
instruments. The second review flagged his FTD scepticism as undocumented; this is the earliest
clean instance.

## Key claims

- **The disagreement, respectfully:** "IBD has reported that the Nasdaq Composite has now had a
  follow through day... and that the market is now in a confirmed up-trend. **They may be right,
  but I need more of an advance** before I would identify a new short term up-trend." His state:
  Day 21 of the QQQQ down-trend, GMI at 1, GMI-R at 4 of 10, T2108 at 23%. "I remain mainly in
  cash in my IRA."
- The method difference in a sentence: IBD's FTD keys on one high-volume up day; his trend flip
  requires the persistent price structure his day-count and averages measure. (He was right this
  time — the June 2010 rally failed; his July 2010 pension-exit post, already ingested, is the
  sequel.)
- **Watch-list mining while unconvinced:** "if a new up-trend is imminent, one place to look for
  new leaders is in the **small list of new daily highs**" — 24 names near multi-year peaks
  published, with triple-digit-earnings names starred (DGIT, DTV, SAM). "I suspect that within
  this list are some new market leaders, ***if* the market trend turns up**." Preparation without
  participation.
