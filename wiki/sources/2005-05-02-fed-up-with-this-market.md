# WW 2005-05-02 — Fed up with this market — the GMI is not a leading indicator

**Source:** [Fed Up With This Market?](../../raw/posts/2005-05-02-fed-up-with-this-market.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/05/fed-up-with-this-market/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The disclaimer that governs every reading of the index, written a week after it launched: "**The WW-GMI is not a leading indicator. It is a summary of the indicators I watch to tell me the current trend. By definition, the index will not register a strong market until after the turn has come.**" With the GMI at zero he names the exact prices that would change his mind — "the QQQQ would have to **close** above 35.84 to get me interested… The SPY would have to **close** above 116.70" — and immediately qualifies it: "note that I said, to get me interested, not to get me confident to buy." Twelve successful 10-day new highs; 88 of 4,000 stocks at a 52-week high. Opens with Mark Twain on October being a peculiarly dangerous month "and the others."

## Key claims

- "The WW-GMI is not a leading indicator… by definition, the index will not register a strong market until after the turn has come."
- Pre-committing to price levels that would change his stance, before they arrive.
- 'Interested' and 'confident to buy' are separate thresholds.
