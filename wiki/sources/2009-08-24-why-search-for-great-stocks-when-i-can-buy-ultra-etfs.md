# WW 2009-08-24 — Why search for great stocks when I can buy ultra ETFs?

**Source:** [Why search for great stocks when I can buy ultra ETF’s?](../../raw/posts/2009-08-24-why-search-for-great-stocks-when-i-can-buy-ultra-etfs.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2009/08/why-search-for-great-stocks-when-i-can-buy-ultra-etfs/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The full argument for the index over the stock, made in August 2009 before TQQQ existed. "I spend so much time trying to find the right growth stock that will outperform the market. **Now that we have the extreme ultra (3x) ETF's it may be preferable to trade them.**" Two structural reasons for the QQQQ specifically: the Nasdaq-100 "escaped some of the carnage last year because **it contains no financial stocks**"; and "lately, stocks tend to explode or implode when earnings are announced. **Because the QQQQ represents 100 stocks, there is less impact from individual earnings announcements**" — the basket as earnings-risk diversification. The vehicles available then: QQQQ itself, options on it, QLD at 2×, and — with no 3× QQQQ yet — the bullish 3× tech ETF **TYH**.

## Key claims

- Written before TQQQ existed: the 3× exposure to tech had to come via TYH.
- The Nasdaq-100 preferred partly because it holds **no financial stocks** — the 2008 lesson.
- A 100-stock basket blunts single-name earnings risk, which he names as a specific hazard.
- The title itself is the thesis he repeats for the next fifteen years.
