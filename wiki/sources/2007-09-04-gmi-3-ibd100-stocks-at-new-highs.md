# WW 2007-09-04 — Component 1 clarified to ≥; and why he looks forward to declines

**Source:** [GMI: 3++; IBD100 stocks at new highs](../../raw/posts/2007-09-04-gmi-3-ibd100-stocks-at-new-highs.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2007/09/gmi-3-ibd100-stocks-at-new-highs/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A small but exact clarification to the component: "**I clarified in the table that the 10 Day Successful New High Index is positive if the number is *greater than or equal* to 100 or 50%**" — the thresholds are inclusive, which is how the published table has read ever since. And the habit that recurs through the whole corpus, stated as a preference: "**I look forward to market declines when it is much easier to see emerging leaders. Stocks that hit new highs quickly after a decline are often likely to lead if the market turns.**" He scanned 400+ stocks from a year of IBD100 lists and found 20 at new highs — "impressive, given that only 66 stocks in my entire universe of 4,000 stocks hit a new high on Friday." T2108 at 39%, "having marked the bottom in August with an extreme reading below 8%."

## Key claims

- Component 1's thresholds are **inclusive** (≥ 100 or ≥ 50%) — clarified on the table in September 2007.
- "I look forward to market declines when it is much easier to see emerging leaders."
- A relative-strength read: 20 of 400+ IBD100 names at new highs against 66 in a 4,000-stock universe.
- T2108 below 8% marked the August 2007 bottom.
