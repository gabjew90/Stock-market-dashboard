# WW 2005-07-28 — Another strong day; the GMI's track record; the 10-day line; benefits of naked charts

## What it covers

The GMI-changes chart since April; the QQQQ "consistently closed above its rising 10 day moving average" through the rally — "Hint: When is the market weakening? Wait for a close below the dotted line." And the sentence that becomes the index-ETF default: "To make $$$ in this rise one merely could have bought the QQQQ on July 8 or 9 and held on… **Why mess around with individual stocks when one can merely trade this index?** Until the 10 day average tops out, I see no reason not to be long Nasdaq 100 type stocks." Also the case for 'naked' charts (price and averages, nothing else).

## Key claims

- The 10-day moving average of the QQQQ as the 2005 short-term trend line; a close below it = weakening.
- "Why mess around with individual stocks when one can merely trade this index?" — 2005.
- Naked charts.
