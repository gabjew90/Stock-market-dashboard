# WW 2014-12-25 — More on bonds and rising rates — the thesis that would end the bull

**Source:** [More on bonds and rising rates; Buy and hold index funds?](../../raw/posts/2014-12-25-more-on-bonds-and-rising-rates-buy-and-hold-index-funds.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2014/12/more-on-bonds-and-rising-rates-buy-and-hold-index-funds/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A macro thesis he watches for, stated as a mechanism rather than a forecast. Bondholders enriched by a decade of falling rates "will likely flee their bonds if (when?) rates start to reverse up. **People who became accustomed to merely holding bonds and watching them rise in value may be in for a large shock when they plummet.**" Then the transmission to equities: "after the turn when rates rise enough to be attractive, people will exit risky stocks in favor of more conservative bonds or CDs, **precipitating a bear market in stocks**." Made concrete and personal: "**if the day ever returns that I could nail down 6% annual interest in insured CDs, I and many other boomers would presumably be happy to get out of stocks and park our money in interest bearing instruments after retirement. It is for this reason that I need to be very vigilant for the first signs that interest rates are reversing up.**" And the framing: "Mr Market tends to seduce people into the stock market so that the most persons get hurt at some point."

## Key claims

- Rising rates as the mechanism that would end an equity bull — competing yield, not valuation.
- A concrete personal threshold: 6% on insured CDs would move him and "many other boomers" out of stocks.
- Why he watches TLT and the bond ETFs at all — this is the thesis behind the monthly RWB on bond ETFs.
- "Mr Market tends to seduce people into the stock market so that the most persons get hurt at some point."
