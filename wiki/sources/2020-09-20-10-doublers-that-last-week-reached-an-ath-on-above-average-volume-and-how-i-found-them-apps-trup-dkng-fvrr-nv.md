# WW 2020-09-20 — The weekly doubler-ATH scan, full TC2000 syntax

**Source:** [10 doublers that last week reached an ATH on above average volume and how I found them](../../raw/posts/2020-09-20-10-doublers-that-last-week-reached-an-ath-on-above-average-volume-and-how-i-found-them-apps-trup-dkng-fvrr-nv.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2020/09/10-doublers-that-last-week-reached-an-ath-on-above-average-volume-and-how-i-found-them-apps-trup-dkng-fvrr-nvcr-grvy-nvta-penn-swav-wkhs/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A complete weekly scan with **verbatim TC2000 syntax** — the doubler-plus-ATH-plus-volume
combination that operationalises Darvas's criterion on the weekly timeframe — plus the
shaken-out rule in its most human form: a physical note taped to the monitor.

## Key claims

- **The syntax, quoted exactly:** "High this week was the highest in 50 weeks: `H=maxH50`; High
  this week was at least 200% the high 50 weeks ago or greater than 150% of the lowest low the
  past year: `H>2*H50 or H>1.5*MinL50`; Volume this week is greater than 130% of average volume
  the past 50 weeks: `V>1.3*AvgV50`; Closed this week greater than $20: `C>20`."
- **Yield:** 74 of 5,096 US stocks; 25 of his 948-stock IBD/MarketSmith watchlist, including
  APPS, TRUP, DKNG, FVRR, NVCR, PENN, SWAV, WKHS.
- **The scan feeds the GLB step, not replaces it:** after the column scan, "I then look at each
  **monthly** chart... and draw in the appropriate green line" — machine screen, manual line.
- **The doubling logic with its best example:** "Taser went up 7x, consolidated, and then went
  up 7x again." Doubling accepted from either the year-ago high or 1.5× off the yearly low; $20
  minimum because "I don't buy cheap stocks."
- **The note on the monitor:** "Many times I look back at a strong stock that I was shaken out
  of because of its daily volatility only to find that its weekly chart could have kept me from
  selling. **I have created a note on my monitor that says I must look at the weekly chart
  before I sell.**" The 2019 rule turned into a physical checklist item.
- **The part-time framing again:** "most of us who earn a living working... need a less eyes on
  the market and part-time approach" — weekly charts as the accommodation.
- The lineage list now includes **Minervini** alongside Darvas, Livermore, Ryan and O'Neil.
- The thesis sentence: "Stocks that have doubled already and **can come through a declining
  market to all-time-highs on above average trading volume** may prove to be true market
  leaders."
