# WW 2005-05-03 — CNBC: forever bullish — the letter to Cramer (70% follow the indexes)

**Source:** [CNBC:  Forever Bullish](../../raw/posts/2005-05-03-cnbc-forever-bullish.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/05/cnbc-forever-bullish/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

His April 15, 2005 letter to Cramer, printed: "Mutual funds must stay invested, individuals do not. About 70% of stocks go with the market indexes. When the indexes are declining as they have been for weeks, it is your duty to tell people not to fight the trend. Get out of the way, safely in cash, or go short. Why try to find the few exceptions that can fight the trend. Go with the odds, as Livermore, Baruch and O'Neil did." Then the complaint: Cramer tells his audience to avoid 80% of stocks and proceeds to list buys — "why does a talented hedge fund manager who clearly sold short and traded options, never advise his listeners to do what he did?"

## Key claims

- The 70% rule stated to Cramer in April 2005 — mutual funds must stay invested, individuals need not.
- Pundits' structural long bias named.
