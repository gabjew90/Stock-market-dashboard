# WW 2009-03-01 — No bottom in sight — how bear markets end: the volume signature

**Source:** [No bottom in sight for this bear market–it’s just the beginning?](../../raw/posts/2009-03-01-no-bottom-in-sight-for-this-bear-market-its-just-the-beginning.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2009/03/no-bottom-in-sight-for-this-bear-market-its-just-the-beginning/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"When you are crossing the street and a truck comes bearing down on you… you should not argue with the fact that it is there." Then a study of how the 1929–32 and 1974 bears ended, on weekly charts with a 50-week volume average: down-week volume shrinking below the average, then "a huge green spike soon after the bottom, followed by a number of green spikes several months later, after a re-test" — up-volume larger than anything in months. February 2009 showed the opposite ("the red spikes are unceasing… the green spikes tend to be lower than the red") — "this is not a market set to bottom like the prior bears. In fact, it looks to me like it is just getting started." 560 new lows vs the 2,185 of November 2008; GMI 0, D-9; mainly cash with some IRA puts. "The key is to conserve cash so as to be able to go long when those days return — and they will."

## Key claims

- Bear-market bottom signature: shrinking down-volume → the largest up-volume weeks in months → a re-test with even larger up-volume.
- "No one can detect a bottom until sometime after it has occurred."
- "I have been in cash for all of the major declines since 1995. (I also avoided the 1987 debacle.)"
- The truck metaphor: don't argue with the tape.
