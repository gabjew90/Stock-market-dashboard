# WW 2007-08-20 — GMI performance through the 2007 declines; the exit/re-entry thresholds

**Source:** [GMI: 1; More new highs than lows; Performance of GMI; Strongest IBD100 stocks](../../raw/posts/2007-08-20-gmi-1-more-new-highs-than-lows-performance-of-gmi-strongest-ibd100-stocks.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2007/08/gmi-1-more-new-highs-than-lows-performance-of-gmi-strongest-ibd100-stocks/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The clearest 2007 statement of what the GMI is *for*, with the year's two declines traced date by date as evidence. Contains the earliest explicit statement in the corpus of the exit/re-entry thresholds — four years before the 2011 post the wiki previously cited for them — plus the Livermore position on refusing to label markets bull or bear.

## Key claims

- **The thresholds, stated as practice:** "I typically start to exit the long side of the market when the GMI falls below 4 and to buy once it climbs back above 3." Predates the [2011-03-07](2011-03-07-introducing-the-gmi2-tc2000-com-ibd50-stock-performance-put-options-on-lulu.md) statement of the same rule.
- **What the GMI is:** "There is no magic to the GMI. It is merely a count of the six market characteristics that I find useful for tracking the current trend. These indicators have helped me to survive in the market and have kept me out of **all major market declines since 1994**."
- **The 2007 record, dated:** at the close of the sudden February drop the GMI registered 1, down from 6, and stayed below 4 until March 21. From March 21 to July 24 it ran 4–6, mostly 5–6, while the market rose ~11%. It fell to 3 on July 25 and stayed below 4 — "So, I have escaped the latest carnage."
- **Livermore on labels:** he "refused to label a market bear or bull... these terms have too much baggage regarding longevity and size of a move." Only the *current* trend is characterisable. "The market appears to me to be in a down-trend and it remains so until the GMI rises to 4."
- **The hardest lesson, stated as autobiography:** "It took me a very long time to learn to respect the trend of the market and not to fight it. (I used to profit in the up-trend and give it all back and more in the subsequent decline.) This is THE KEY to successful trading."
- **Relative strength during declines** as a leadership screen: "declines offer the best opportunity to find the next winners." 80% of the 440 IBD100 stocks he monitored had declined since July 19; he lists the ten biggest gainers (ISRG +33%, HMSY +32%, MORN +26%, ANSS +24%...) as candidates "when the market turns."
- **GMI-S construction partially disclosed:** "Only one of the 16 short term indicators for the IJR, DIA, SPY and QQQQ is positive (GMI-S: 6%)" — 1/16 ≈ 6%, so the GMI-S is the percentage of 16 short-term indicators across four index ETFs that are positive.
- Market data: T2108 rebounded from an extreme low of 8% to 16%; SPY and DIA closed below their 10-week averages for four consecutive weeks; Day 15 of the QQQQ short-term down-trend.
