# WW 2010-09-07 — This rally may have legs; Investors Intelligence bears > bulls; IBD100 top ten out-shine

**Source:** [This rally may have legs–IBD100 top ten out-shine again!](../../raw/posts/2010-09-07-this-rally-may-have-legs-ibd100-top-ten-out-shine-again.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2010/09/this-rally-may-have-legs-ibd100-top-ten-out-shine-again/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"When everyone is so bearish and expecting the worst, it is time to expect the opposite": the Investors Intelligence survey showed more bears than bulls (38% vs 29%) — "an exceedingly rare phenomenon"; over 50% bulls is when to look for a decline. GMI 4, GMI-R 8: "time for me to close out my few short positions and start going long. There are just too many stocks breaking out" (226 new highs). SPY and DIA above their 10-week, "the level at which I can begin to trade profitably on the long side"; 76% of Nasdaq-100 with MACD above signal; T2108 at 76% — "can remain around 80% for months… behaves like a pendulum." The strategy in one sentence: "select stocks from the IBD 100 list… I then time the entry according to my own trading rules." Top ten of the July 12 IBD100 list through September 3: 90% rose, 70% up ≥20%, vs 17% of the Nasdaq-100 and 11% of the S&P 500 up ≥20%, QQQQ +8%.

## Key claims

- Investors Intelligence bears > bulls as a rare buy tell (2010); >50% bulls as a sell tell.
- "Select stocks from the IBD 100 list… time the entry according to my own trading rules."
- IBD100 top-10 (7/12): 90% up, 70% ≥ +20% vs 17% / 11% for the indexes.
- T2108 as a pendulum; ~80% can persist for months.
