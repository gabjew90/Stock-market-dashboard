# WW 2011-03-11 — Gains attributed to the tide, not the trader

**Source:** [GMI dives to 1; in cash and inverse ETF’s](../../raw/posts/2011-03-11-gmi-dives-to-1-in-cash-and-inverse-etfs.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2011/03/gmi-dives-to-1-in-cash-and-inverse-etfs/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The most direct statement in the corpus about where returns actually come from, written as the market turned:
"**All of us look smart when the trend is up and our trades are profitable. Now we must accept that our trading
gains the past 2 years have been largely the result of a rising market. 'A rising tide raises all ships.' Well,
the tide is going out now and our job is to conserve capital.**" Attributing two years of gains to beta rather
than to skill — about himself and his readers together — is the honest counterpart to every performance figure
elsewhere on this wiki. And the symmetry that follows: "**I should not be long now, anymore than it made sense to
be short during the market's rise.**" The market gate stated as a matter of consistency rather than of
forecasting. He also notes the pundits waiting for a correction "so they can buy more stocks at lower prices" —
sentiment he treats as a warning rather than as an opportunity. Context: GMI down to 1 with only the Weekly QQQQ
Index still positive, the IBD Mutual Fund Index now below its 50-day, D-3.

## Key claims

- **Two years of trading gains attributed to a rising market**, explicitly and about himself: "a rising tide raises all ships."
- **The gate as symmetry, not prediction**: being long now would be as wrong as being short during the rise.
- "Our job is to conserve capital" — stated as the job changing with the regime, not as a temporary defensive move.
- Component 6 turning negative (the IBD fund index below its 50-day) noted as the professionals losing their edge too.
