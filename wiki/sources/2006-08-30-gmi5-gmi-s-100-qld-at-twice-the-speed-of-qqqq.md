# WW 2006-08-30 — The ultra ETFs arrive — August 2006

**Source:** [GMI:5; GMI-S: 100; QLD at twice the speed of QQQQ](../../raw/posts/2006-08-30-gmi5-gmi-s-100-qld-at-twice-the-speed-of-qqqq.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2006/08/gmi5-gmi-s-100-qld-at-twice-the-speed-of-qqqq/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The instrument's first appearance on the blog.** "**Did you know there are relatively new ETF's that allow
one to bet on twice the percentage move of major indexes and that these ProShares**" funds exist — introduced to
readers as a novelty, two months before the October 2006 post in which he first quantifies the case for them.
This dates the whole leveraged-ETF thread: the 2× ProShares funds are new in August 2006, he is explaining what
they are; by late October he has measured QLD against the distribution of Nasdaq-100 components and drawn the
conclusion he would repeat for the next two decades. Everything else — the 3× funds in 2009, the TQQQ default in
2011, the day-1 entry problem, the "falls 3× faster" caveat — descends from here. Market context: GMI 5, GMI-S at
100 (all sixteen short-term indicators across SPY, DIA, QQQQ and IJR positive), 183 new highs to 21 new lows, and
59% of his universe in an up-trend "up from 15% on July 14."

## Key claims

- **The 2× ProShares funds are introduced to readers as new in August 2006** — the start of the leveraged-ETF thread that runs to the present.
- Two months later he quantifies the case; here he is still explaining what the instruments are.
- GMI-S at 100 — all sixteen short-term indicators positive, the maximum reading of that sub-index.
- Breadth recovery measured against a recent trough: 59% in an up-trend against 15% six weeks earlier.
