# WW 2021-07-12 — The black dot formula published; 'almost every significant rise begins from an oversold level'

**Source:** [Blog post: 32nd day of $QQQ short term up-trend; Key to success in market is to have an objective, defined set-up that implies an immediate move up, then if it fails to move rapidly as predicted exit quickly with a small loss; I teach my students several set-ups that I like; one of them is the black dot signal, defined below](../../raw/posts/2021-07-12-blog-post-32nd-day-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-key-to-success-in-market-is-to-have-an-objective-defined-set-up.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2021/07/blog-post-32nd-day-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-key-to-success-in-market-is-to-have-an-objective-defined-set-up-that-implies-an-immediate-move-up-then-if-it-fails-to-move-rapidly-as-predicted-exit/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The black dot's TC2000 logic, given so readers can build it: "I want the 10.1 daily stochastic in any of the last three days to be ≤ 25 and for the stock to be higher today than yesterday and to be above its 30 day simple average or its 21 day exponential moving average. **The dot is placed at the 5 day low price.**" It is the black-dot variant of the green-dot tutorial slide from his TraderLion and Long Island Meetup presentations. The claim underneath the whole setup: "**I have discovered that almost every significant rise in a stock begins from an oversold level. But not every oversold level leads to a big rise.**" The filter that turns the second sentence into a strategy: "find a launched rocket near an all-time-high (ATH) that has become oversold and begins to move up. I can then buy it and place my stop loss below the low of the bounce." And: "I love a black dot signal soon after a green line break-out."

## Key claims

- Black dot logic in full: 10.1 stochastic ≤ 25 within 3 days; close > prior close; price above the 30-day SMA *or* the 21-day EMA; dot drawn at the 5-day low.
- "Almost every significant rise in a stock begins from an oversold level. But not every oversold level leads to a big rise" — the reason the ATH prerequisite exists.
- The dot's placement at the 5-day low doubles as the stop reference.
- A black dot soon after a GLB is the preferred combination.
