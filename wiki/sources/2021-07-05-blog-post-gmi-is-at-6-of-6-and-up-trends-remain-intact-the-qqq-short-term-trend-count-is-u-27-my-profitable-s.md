# WW 2021-07-05 — TQQQ from U-1: +23.3% while 94%+ of stocks lagged — 'the needle in the haystack' question

**Source:** [Blog post: GMI is at 6 (of 6) and up-trends remain intact; the $QQQ short term trend count is U-27; my profitable strategy of holding $TQQQ during a $QQQ short term up-trend;](../../raw/posts/2021-07-05-blog-post-gmi-is-at-6-of-6-and-up-trends-remain-intact-the-qqq-short-term-trend-count-is-u-27-my-profitable-s.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2021/07/blog-post-gmi-is-at-6-of-6-and-up-trends-remain-intact-the-qqq-short-term-trend-count-is-u-27-my-profitable-strategy-of-holding-tqqq-during-a-qqq-short-term-up-trend/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The default stated as a policy choice. "A profitable strategy for me is to make an initial purchase of TQQQ… on **U-1**. I then slowly accumulate TQQQ as the trend persists." Measured on the trend then running: since U-1 on 5/26, TQQQ +23.3%, while "only 4% of the Nasdaq100 stocks, 1% of the S&P500 stocks, none of the Dow 30 stocks, and 6% of all US stocks out performed TQQQ. In other words, holding TQQQ during an up-trend beats more than 94% of individual stocks." And the question he puts to the reader: "**Are you trying to find the needle in the haystack rare stock that will out perform TQQQ, or are you interested in a higher probability approach to making money in the market?**"

## Key claims

- U-1 entry, then slow accumulation while the trend persists.
- Measured: TQQQ +23.3% from U-1; beaten by 4% of Nasdaq-100, 1% of S&P 500, 0% of Dow 30, 6% of all US stocks.
- Framed as a probability choice, not a preference.
