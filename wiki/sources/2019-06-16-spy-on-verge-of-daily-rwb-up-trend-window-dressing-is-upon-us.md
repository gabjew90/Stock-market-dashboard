# WW 2019-06-16 — How an RWB up-trend *begins* — the onset sequence defined

**Source:** [$SPY on verge of daily RWB up-trend–window dressing is upon us](../../raw/posts/2019-06-16-spy-on-verge-of-daily-rwb-up-trend-window-dressing-is-upon-us.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2019/06/spy-on-verge-of-daily-rwb-up-trend-window-dressing-is-upon-us/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**How an RWB up-trend begins — the onset sequence, and the exact mirror of the top sequence.** "**I define an
RWB up-trend using 12 exponential moving averages and the close.** A daily RWB up-trend is when the 6 red shorter
daily averages are rising above the 6 blue longer daily averages so that there is a white space separating them.
**The beginning of a daily RWB up-trend starts with the closing price (dotted line) rising above all 12 averages.
Next the red lines climb above the blue lines.**" Two ordered stages, and the same structure as the 2018
description of a top read backwards. He also gives the pattern's *maintenance* signature and its failure
condition: "**during most of an RWB rise, the closing price leads all of the averages higher. A failure of the
RWB pattern would occur if the SPY were to close back below all 12 averages and the white separation
disappears.**" The chart carried arrows marking the start of four such up-trends — the pattern identified
repeatedly rather than asserted once. Applied that day: the SPY beginning one "but there is no visible white
separation yet," the DIA similar, the QQQ "a little further behind." Quarter-end window dressing was expected to
help. And the discipline in one sentence: "**it is important to study the market's behavior, ignore the news and
media pundits, and to pull the trigger only after a signal has occurred, not before.**"

## Key claims

- **The onset sequence**: (1) the close rises above all 12 averages, (2) the red lines climb above the blue, (3) white space opens. The top sequence is the same steps reversed.
- **The close leads during a healthy RWB advance** — price above the fan, not inside it, is what a working up-trend looks like.
- **The failure condition is symmetric with the onset**: a close back below all 12 averages with the white space gone.
- The definition is explicitly *12 exponential averages plus the close* — the close is a component of the pattern, not a separate observation.
- "Pull the trigger only after a signal has occurred, not before" — the anticipation prohibition, stated with the pattern that tempts it.
