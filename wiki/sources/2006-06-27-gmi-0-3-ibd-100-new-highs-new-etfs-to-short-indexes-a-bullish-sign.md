# WW 2006-06-27 — Inverse ETFs arrive; new products as a contrary indicator

**Source:** [GMI: 0; 3 IBD 100 new highs; New ETF’s to short indexes–a bullish sign?](../../raw/posts/2006-06-27-gmi-0-3-ibd-100-new-highs-new-etfs-to-short-indexes-a-bullish-sign.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2006/06/gmi-0-3-ibd-100-new-highs-new-etfs-to-short-indexes-a-bullish-sign/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Three things. **Component 1's small-denominator problem being handled by discretion**, twenty months before it
was formalised: "there were almost enough successful new highs from 10 days ago (9/15 = 60%) to turn this
indicator positive. While I typically call this index positive when 50% or more of the stocks that hit a new high
10 days ago close higher than they did 10 days ago, **the numbers here are too small to do so**. If it is above
50% again tomorrow, I will make the indicator positive." So the February 2008 revision (denominator ≥ 20)
codified a judgement he was already making by hand. **The first generation of inverse ETFs**: "There are now new
ways for an investor/trader to **short the major market indexes in an IRA without buying put options**. Inverse
ETF's have been born" — **PSQ** (QQQQ), **SH** (S&P 500), **DOG** (Dow 30), **MYY** (S&P 400 midcap) — with a
liquidity caveat: "these ETF's are thinly traded and should be researched carefully before buying." **And a
contrary indicator built out of product launches**: "Maybe the dawn of a new set of inverse ETF's is a bullish
sign. **Typically, not long after investors are so interested in a sector that a relevant new ETF is issued, that
sector is close to a top** — check out XHB, GLD and SLV for recent examples. Too many people may be interested in
shorting the major indexes??!! A new contrary indicator?" Also, the post-decline leadership screen restated:
"**any of the IBD 100 stocks that could survive the decline of the past month and go on quickly to hit a new high
is a stock worth watching.**"

## Key claims

- **The denominator floor began as discretion, not as a rule** — he declined to call component 1 positive on 9 of 15 because the sample was too small, in June 2006.
- **Inverse index ETFs arrive June 2006** (PSQ, SH, DOG, MYY), and their significance to him is IRA access: shorting without options.
- **New-product issuance as a crowding signal**: an ETF launched for a hot sector often arrives near that sector's top, with XHB, GLD and SLV cited as recent instances.
- He applies it to the launch he has just welcomed — if inverse ETFs are being issued, perhaps too many people want to be short.
- Thin liquidity flagged on the new instruments before recommending research.
- The growth-list pattern again: IBD 100 stocks matched the universe on the bounce after badly underperforming during the decline, because "traders and funds were clearly unloading the premier growth stocks."
