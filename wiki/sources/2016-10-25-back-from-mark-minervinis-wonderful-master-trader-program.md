# WW 2016-10-25 — Back from Mark Minervini's Master Trader Program

**Source:** [Back from Mark Minervini’s Wonderful Master Trader Program](../../raw/posts/2016-10-25-back-from-mark-minervinis-wonderful-master-trader-program.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2016/10/back-from-mark-minervinis-wonderful-master-trader-program/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Three days with Mark Minervini and David Ryan (whose Market Wizards chapter he assigns to every student): 600+ slides on "how to select stocks, the price patterns that are most profitable, the way to set stops, pyramid up, and close out positions… the best information on the value of cutting losses that I have ever seen"; a live-trading day; and a three-hour session on trading psychology. "He has put into a 3 day session much of what it took me over 50 years to learn." Both agreed to lecture his students. Documents the influences behind the 2019–2025 references to @markminervini, MarketSmith, and VCP.

## Key claims

- Minervini's method "draws upon stage analysis and his own brand of stock trading"; David Ryan = O'Neil's protégé.
- Named takeaways: stock selection, profitable patterns, stops, pyramiding, closing positions, cutting losses, psychology.
- Origin of the Minervini/Ryan lecture channel to his students and of the Twitter idea sources he later cites.
