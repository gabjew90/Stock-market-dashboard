# WW 2021-01-31 — The Investors Intelligence threshold, and the Twitter channel in use

**Source:** [Blog post: $QQQ short term up-trend may end Monday; Is GME debacle decoying attention from impact of virus? Failed GLB: $NFLX, $AMD](../../raw/posts/2021-01-31-blog-post-qqq-short-term-up-trend-may-end-monday-is-gme-debacle-decoying-attention-from-impact-of-virus-faile.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2021/01/blog-post-qqq-short-term-up-trend-may-end-monday-is-gme-debacle-decoying-attention-from-impact-of-virus-failed-glb-nflx-amd/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**A specific number on a sentiment gauge the wiki has only qualitatively**: "the Investors Intelligence poll of
investment newsletter writers now registers **61% bullish. A contrarian indicator, market tops occur when this
poll shows 60% or more bulls.**" A stated threshold, in the same form as the put/call 1.2 and the T2108 bands.
**And the Twitter channel doing real work** — one of the wiki's documented coverage gaps: "I told you I went to
cash more than a week ago when I did not like the action of my stocks and **I saw that David Ryan and Mark
Minervini tweeted that they expected a decline**." Two named traders' tweets as a corroborating input to an exit
decision, alongside his own indicators. **The insurance framing again, sharpened**: "being in cash in my IRA
trading account is comfortable because **I can always reenter when the dust settles. I prefer to risk missing out
on some new gains in order to avoid the greater risk of giving up my gains from the past year.**" And the two
speeds held apart as usual: "**I wait to transfer my university pension out of mutual funds to money market funds
when my longer term indicators turn down. We are not there yet, but I am watching closely.**"

## Key claims

- **II at 60%+ bulls is his stated top threshold** — the number the wiki was missing for that gauge.
- **The Twitter channel used as an input**, not just as an output: Ryan's and Minervini's tweets contributed to an exit decision.
- "I prefer to risk missing out on some new gains in order to avoid the greater risk of giving up my gains from the past year" — the asymmetry stated as a preference between two risks.
- He notes the GME saga was a plausible distraction from a virus-driven decline — and declines to choose between causes, because the action is the same.
- The pension trigger restated as pending rather than met.
