## What it covers

PANW kept surfacing in his ATH scans; it made a GLB to a new ATH on May 26 and closed above its green line at $213.61 on June 1; he bought on the Friday. That evening it was announced PANW would join the S&P 500 — index funds now forced buyers — and the stock jumped after hours. The lesson: "**a stock's strong behavior often occurs *before* the news is out.** Insiders may have been buying PANW prior to the announcement and their buying caused the stock to have a green line breakout." With the volume corollary: "when an earnings release is followed by a large gap up it can be the beginning of a significant move up. **Nicolas Darvas wrote years ago that the atypical volume in a rising stock often enabled him to get on board with the insiders who knew good news was coming.**"

## Key claims

- Technical strength as the observable trace of information you do not have.
- Darvas on atypical volume as the tell of informed buying.
- A large gap up after earnings can start a significant move.
- The GLB entry was made before the news, on the chart alone.
