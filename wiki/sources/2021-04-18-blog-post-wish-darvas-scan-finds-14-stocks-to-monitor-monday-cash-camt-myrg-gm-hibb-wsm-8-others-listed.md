# WW 2021-04-18 — The Wish-Darvas scan — the criteria, in full

**Source:** [Blog post: Wish-Darvas scan finds 14 stocks to monitor Monday: $CASH $CAMT $MYRG $GM $HIBB $WSM + 8 others listed](../../raw/posts/2021-04-18-blog-post-wish-darvas-scan-finds-14-stocks-to-monitor-monday-cash-camt-myrg-gm-hibb-wsm-8-others-listed.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2021/04/blog-post-wish-darvas-scan-finds-14-stocks-to-monitor-monday-cash-camt-myrg-gm-hibb-wsm-8-others-listed/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"This weekend I created a new scan column in TC2000 that puts together most of the characteristics of winning stocks that I have embraced over the years. Some were suggested by the great Nicolas Darvas. Most, I created based on my trading experience and reading the past 50+ years." The criteria as published: **> $20; near an all-time high; in a yellowband pattern for 5+ weeks; reached a new high within the last 25 days; closed up today; up > 80% from a year ago ("Darvas liked +100%"); and bounced up off its 21-day exponential or 30-day simple average today.** Fourteen of 6,081 US stocks qualified; he copies them to a watchlist with projected earnings dates and a column showing whether Monday's high exceeds Friday's — "another sign of strength." Six of the fourteen carried his IBD/MarketSmith flag. The exit travels with the entry: "if I buy a stock after a bounce up off of one of these moving averages, I must sell immediately if it ***closes*** below" it.

## Key claims

- Wish-Darvas scan criteria in full — price, ATH proximity, yellowband duration, recency of the high, up day, 80%+ year gain, bounce off the 21-EMA or 30-SMA.
- Darvas's doubling criterion relaxed to +80% for the scan.
- Follow-through test: does Monday's high exceed Friday's?
- Exit defined at entry: a close below the bounce average.
