## What it covers

Written explicitly as a teaching example: "a way to evaluate a company that comes to one's attention because of strong technical action." INSW, one of four US stocks at an ATH that Monday, public since 2016: a GLB on above-average volume 9/6, a close below the green line the next day, a retake on 9/8, then a new ATH on the highest daily volume since April. Technical support "at the 8 day exp average, which has been climbing above the 21 day exp avg since mid-July" — the x8/x21 alignment in use. Price doubled from a year ago. Then the fundamental pass on MarketSmith: composite 98, Acc/Dis A+, group RS 98, no profit yet but triple-digit sales growth two quarters running, rising fund ownership, projected EPS $5.03.

## Key claims

- The order of work: technical action surfaces the stock, fundamentals are checked afterwards.
- A failed GLB retaken the next day is not disqualifying.
- The 8-EMA above the 21-EMA with repeated support at the 8 as the trend-health read.
- Fundamental checklist: composite rating, Acc/Dis, group RS, sales growth, fund ownership, projected EPS.
