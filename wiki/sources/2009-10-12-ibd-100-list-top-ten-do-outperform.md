# WW 2009-10-12 — The IBD 100's top ten audited; why he trusts an empirical screen

**Source:** [IBD 100 list top ten stocks do outperform!](../../raw/posts/2009-10-12-ibd-100-list-top-ten-do-outperform.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2009/10/ibd-100-list-top-ten-do-outperform/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**An audit built to test a criticism, and the clearest statement of why he trusts a screen at all.** The
criticism: "one of the exclamations that I have heard from self-proclaimed experts is that **by the time that a
stock gets to the top of the IBD 100 list, it is too late to trade it for a profit**." The test: take the top ten
of a specific published list and measure them against the indexes. Result for the 3 August 2009 list — "**70% of
these ten stocks that were at the top of the IBD 100 list outperformed the QQQQ and SPY**" over the following ~50
days — replicated on the 27 July list, which "**also showed that 70% beat these two averages**." He notes the
path dependency honestly: "it is true that some of these 10 stocks declined before they recovered." **And the
epistemology**, which is the durable part: "IBD's stock selection criteria are based on analyses of their
comprehensive database of the best stocks over the past 100 years. **Imagine that — they actually analyzed the
characteristics of past market winners to design an empirically based system for selecting winners BEFORE they
take off. Because human trading psychology is pretty stable, one can discern lasting technical patterns (which
are really trading decisions) that are characteristic of winning stocks.**" That last clause is the whole
justification for technical analysis in nine words. Also: the ultra ETF beat the stock picks again (TYH +25% over
the period), and a sentiment note — "**it never ceases to amaze me how the pundits focus on questioning the
viability of the up-trend instead of riding it until it ends. When they all do jump on the bandwagon, it will
probably be time to get defensive.**"

## Key claims

- **The "too late by the time it's on the list" criticism tested and rejected**: 70% of the top ten beat both QQQQ and SPY, on two separately published lists.
- **Replication is what makes it evidence** — he runs the same test on a second list before drawing the conclusion.
- **Why a screen can work at all**: "technical patterns… are really trading decisions," and human trading psychology is stable enough for those decisions to repeat.
- The empirical pedigree matters to him: IBD's criteria come from analysing a century of winners *before* they ran, not from theory.
- "People just like to denigrate things without examining the evidence and **these untruths tend to get passed on unchallenged**."
- The leveraged ETF outperformed the hand-picked ten over the same window (TYH +25%) — noted against his own stock-picking.
