## What it covers

The green dot presented as a teachable setup, with a tutorial his co-instructor David McCandlish built for their students in the TC2000 club, and the blog glossary indexing the scans stored there. The honesty about hit rate is the substance: "**note that all green dot signals do not work and if it were possible to predict in advance which ones will, I would be living on my own island.**" And why that is survivable: "**the nice thing about the green dot set-up is that if the bounce fails, I can get out quickly with a small loss — the crucial sine qua non of trading success.**" The setup's precondition is unchanged — stocks or ETFs "in a nice up-trend (Stage 2)" that then print a green dot.

## Key claims

- "All green dot signals do not work and if it were possible to predict in advance which ones will, I would be living on my own island."
- The setup's value is the *cheap failure*, not the hit rate — "the crucial sine qua non of trading success."
- Stage 2 remains the precondition; the dot alone is never the signal.
- Tutorials and scans distributed through the TC2000 club, indexed in the blog glossary.
