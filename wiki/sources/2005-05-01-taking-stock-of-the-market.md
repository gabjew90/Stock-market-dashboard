# WW 2005-05-01 — Taking stock of the market — Darvas: the only sound reason for buying a stock

**Source:** [Taking Stock of the Market](../../raw/posts/2005-05-01-taking-stock-of-the-market.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/05/taking-stock-of-the-market/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Opens with Darvas: "MY ONLY SOUND REASON FOR BUYING A STOCK IS THAT IT IS RISING IN PRICE. IF THAT IS HAPPENING, NO OTHER REASON IS REQUIRED. IF THAT IS NOT HAPPENING NO OTHER REASON IS WORTH CONSIDERING." Darvas also liked visionary industries with earnings; the AMEX suspended stop orders after his book — "for many years I could only use stops on the NYSE"; the book was blackballed for calling the NYSE a casino. "The strategy of buying strong stocks at new highs in a bull market is also advocated by great traders like Livermore, Loeb and O'Neil"; Boik's *Lessons from the Greatest Stock Traders* recommended.

## Key claims

- Darvas's one reason to buy: the stock is rising.
- Stops were once unavailable on the AMEX — a piece of his stop-order history.
- The new-highs strategy attributed to Darvas, Livermore, Loeb, O'Neil.
