# WW 2010-07-12 — New up-trend or dead cat bounce? — the both-trends rule; the contrarian trio in hindsight

**Source:** [New up-trend or dead cat bounce?](../../raw/posts/2010-07-12-new-up-trend-or-dead-cat-bounce.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2010/07/new-up-trend-or-dead-cat-bounce/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"My rules tell me that when we are in short *and* long term down-trends that I do not go long until *both* trends have signaled a new up-trend. This means that if this rally is the start of a new up-trend, I will miss some of the gains, but such is the fate of the trend follower." In hindsight the bounce was signalled by extreme bearishness: seven down days in a row, put/call ~1.2, Investors Intelligence bears ≈ bulls, and the daily 10.4.4 stochastic near zero — now waiting to see whether the rally ends when the stochastic reaches the highs of prior tops. GMI 0, GMI-R 2; T2108 52%; 49% of Nasdaq-100 with MACD above signal; QQQQ/SPY under their 10-week ten weeks — "I only trade growth stocks profitably on the long side when the QQQQ closes above its 10 week average."

## Key claims

- The both-trends rule: no longs until short *and* long trends have turned up.
- "Such is the fate of the trend follower" — accepting missed early gains.
- Contrarian trio + stochastic near zero as bounce tells (recognised after the fact).
- 10-week close as the permission for long growth-stock trades.
