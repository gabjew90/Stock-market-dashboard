# WW 2015-10-25 — A GMI of 6 refused, and named as psychology

**Source:** [15th day of $QQQ short term up-trend; still a chicken](../../raw/posts/2015-10-25-15th-day-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-still-a-chicken.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2015/10/15th-day-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-still-a-chicken/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The clearest case of him declining to act on his own maximum signal — and, unusually, naming the reason as
psychological rather than analytical. "**Meanwhile, the GMI remains a positive 6 (of 6). If I was eager to trade
I would have just followed the GMI's signals. But after the August flash crash, I am much more of a chicken right
now than usual.**" He also flags the risk that this is exactly the wrong instinct: "(Maybe I will turn out to be
a great contrary indicator!?)" The technical case he assembles alongside it is genuine but secondary: the QQQ
"closed outside of its upper 15.2 daily Bollinger Band on Friday and **a major index rarely does so without a
reversion to the mean** (here the 15 day average)"; the daily 10.4 stochastic overbought at 87 with the fast line
below the slow; and a pattern analogy — "the last time the QQQ had a large weekly gain (5.9%) like last week's
(4.3%) was **the week before the July top**." Two macro worries are named (a debt-limit default, a government
shutdown), which is rare for him. **The re-entry condition is explicit and slow**: "I am concerned by the fact
that the 10 week averages for the QQQ, SPY and DIA **remain below their 30 week averages. I will slowly return to
the market on the long side when the 10 week averages climb back above the 30 week averages.** Who knows when
that will happen." And the divergence: tech strong, "the major nontech industrial stocks are much weaker."

## Key claims

- **A GMI of 6 does not compel him to buy.** The signal says one thing, he does another, and he says which is which: "if I was eager to trade I would have just followed the GMI's signals."
- **The stated reason is his own recent experience, not the data** — "after the August flash crash, I am much more of a chicken right now than usual" — with the self-aware hedge that he may be a contrary indicator.
- **Re-entry keyed to the 10-week crossing back above the 30-week**, and explicitly gradual: "I will slowly return."
- **A close outside the upper Bollinger Band as a mean-reversion expectation** for a major index — "rarely does so without a reversion to the mean."
- A pattern analogy used as a warning: the previous comparable weekly gain preceded the July top.
- Seasonality (Sell in May / Halloween, via Hulbert) acknowledged and declined.
