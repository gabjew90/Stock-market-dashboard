# WW 2018-02-04 — Put/call 1.2 and stochastic .20; the monthly top signature in three stages

**Source:** [Bloody Monday open likely–In cash during this mini-decline](../../raw/posts/2018-02-04-bloody-monday-open-likely-in-cash-during-this-mini-decline.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2018/02/bloody-monday-open-likely-in-cash-during-this-mini-decline/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Written the Sunday before a gap-down open, and unusually specific about thresholds. **The put/call rule with a
number**: "The put/call ratio =.97, showing that **option players are not scared enough yet. When the ratio
reaches 1.2 or higher we will be near a bounce.**" **The stochastic rule with a number**: "the daily 10.4
stochastics indicator I watch for the QQQ is at .47. **The market indexes usually bounce around .20 or less.**"
Both are used to argue *against* buying the dip yet — "the market indexes I follow have not become oversold…
So I expect more of a decline." **The weekend mechanism**: "bloody Fridays often lead to bloody Mondays when
many people decide to exit after pondering their falling balances over the weekend. **These people will likely
sell to the eager professionals at lower prices.**" **And the monthly top signature broken into ordered
stages**, read off SPY's 2000 and 2007 tops: "a major decline begins with the index… **closing below all of the
red line shorter averages. Then the red lines turn down and converge with the blue lines. This pattern takes
several months to develop.** The RWB pattern… then dissolves and the red lines move below the blue lines
(forming the opposite BWR pattern)." Against that template, February 2018 was nowhere close. The pension rule is
stated with both conditions: "**I tend to exit the market in my pension accounts when the index closes below its
30 week average *and the average begins to turn down***. Such a decline last happened in 2016." Position:
100% cash in the trading IRA after two weeks of reducing, 100% invested in the pension.

## Key claims

- **Put/call ≥ 1.2 is the bounce threshold**, stated as a rule rather than observed in passing.
- **A daily 10.4 stochastic of ~0.20 or less is where indexes usually bounce** — 0.47 is explicitly not oversold.
- **The monthly GMMA top has three ordered stages**: price closes below all six red averages → the red averages turn down and converge with the blue → red crosses below blue (BWR). It "takes several months to develop," which is the exit-latency argument in mechanical form.
- **The pension exit needs two conditions, not one**: a close below the 30-week average *and* that average turning down.
- The bloody-Friday-to-bloody-Monday mechanism named — weekend statement-watching produces Monday sellers, who sell to professionals.
- The two accounts moving in opposite directions on the same day, which is the two-speed design working as designed.
- The two-day GMI rule restated in 2018: "Two consecutive days with the GMI less than 3 turns its signal to Red."
