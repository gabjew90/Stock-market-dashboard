# WW 2011-03-13 — QQQQ closes below its 10-week after 26 weeks — the odds change, not the forecast

**Source:** [QQQQ short term down-trend reaches 4th day; GMI: 1, mainly in cash; 3X inverse ETF’s surge](../../raw/posts/2011-03-13-qqqq-short-term-down-trend-reaches-4th-day-gmi-1-mainly-in-cash-3x-inverse-etfs-surge.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2011/03/qqqq-short-term-down-trend-reaches-4th-day-gmi-1-mainly-in-cash-3x-inverse-etfs-surge/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"The key to profiting in the stock market is to be in long positions during up-trends and on the sidelines or short during down-trends." The pension stays invested on the longer-term trend while the trading accounts go flat. Then the reading that matters: the QQQQ "closed below its 10 week average on Friday, after staying above it for **26 straight weeks**. This is a significant breakdown in the up-trend that began in September" — with SPY still sitting on its own 10-week at week 27. The rule, carefully hedged: "**I have found that when the QQQQ closes below its 10 week average, I am unlikely to make money buying tech stocks. It does not mean that the market will enter a long down-trend, only that the odds are** " against him. The 5-day confirmation is invoked again for the down-trend.

## Key claims

- A 26-week run above the 10-week ending is a breakdown, not a forecast.
- "When the QQQQ closes below its 10 week average, I am unlikely to make money buying tech stocks… only that the odds are" against you.
- SPY and QQQQ can break at different weeks — the divergence is data.
- Two speeds again: pension long on the weekly trend, trading accounts out.
