# WW 2025-01-09 — Wading into SQQQ with one foot out the door

**Source:** [Blog Post: Day 6 of $QQQ short term down-trend; 144 US stocks at new lows and 42 at highs; $QQQ,$SPY,$DIA and $IWM now below 10 week averages; Moving to $SQQQ, $TBIL and cash in trading accounts; See 2 charts](../../raw/posts/2025-01-09-blog-post-day-6-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-144-us-stocks-at-new-lows-and-42-at-highs-qqqspydia-and-iwm-now.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2025/01/blog-post-day-6-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-144-us-stocks-at-new-lows-and-42-at-highs-qqqspydia-and-iwm-now-below-10-week-averages-moving-to-sqqq-tbil-and-cash-in-trading-accounts-see/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The current form of the pension trigger, stated as a conditional: "the market remains above its rising 30 week average. **If it should close below that, my GMI will turn to 0 and I will start to transfer university retirement accounts to cash.**" On the short side, sizing and caution together: "given that the QQQ short term down-trend has lasted 6 days, **I will *slowly* wade into SQQQ**, the inverse ETF that rises 3x as much as QQQ declines. **It also falls 3x as much as QQQ advances, so I always have one foot out the door.**" The weakness he reads: more 52-week lows than highs ("a signal of weakness"), no rebound from a high-volume decline three weeks earlier, and the 4-week average about to cross below the 10-week. But the weekly adapted GMMA still shows QQQ in an RWB up-trend — "the longer term trends remain up." And the stance: "**I never try to predict the market. I only look at what Mr. Market is telling me now.**"

## Key claims

- Pension trigger in its 2025 form: a close below the rising 30-week → GMI 0 → start transferring retirement accounts to cash.
- "I always have one foot out the door" — leverage cuts both ways, so the inverse position is provisional.
- Slow accumulation into SQQQ only after the down-trend passed day 5–6.
- More new lows than highs as a weakness signal in its own right.
- Short-term weakness and an intact weekly RWB up-trend held simultaneously without contradiction.
