# WW 2022-01-30 — End-of-decline signs — the 30-week must curve up; p/c > 1.10 and T2108 < 10%

**Source:** [Blog post: Day 16 of $QQQ short term down-trend; Some end of decline signs I am looking for; Promising stock scan finds 2 shipping stocks at ATHs: $ZIM and $MATX, see chartss](../../raw/posts/2022-01-30-blog-post-day-16-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-some-end-of-decline-signs-i-am-looking-for-promising-stock-scan.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2022/01/blog-post-day-16-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-some-end-of-decline-signs-i-am-looking-for-promising-stock-scan-finds-2-shipping-stocks-at-aths-zim-and-matx-see-chartss/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A checklist for the bottom he will not try to predict. "Now that the QQQ is below its 30 week average, the first thing I will look for is a close above that average. But… the index can whip-saw above and below that average. **I therefore want to see the 30 week average curving up again. That is the definitive signal to get me back in.**" Two early indicators: "the p/c ratio > 1.10 and T2108 < 10%" — T2108 had touched 12% and was back to 21%, the put/call 1.08 at its extreme. The buying method if T2108 breaks 10%: "I will buy a little SPY and buy more only at *higher* levels after a bottom is in." The daily adapted GMMA returning to RWB would signal a short-term bounce. "By definition, a trend follower follows the trend. I will get back in long after a bottom is formed, not before. Going in early in anticipation of a change in trend often leads to losses." And the mirror: "if the 30 week average curves down it will signify to me the *beginning* of a major decline."

## Key claims

- Re-entry signal hierarchy: close above the 30-week (weak, whipsaws) → **30-week curving up (definitive)**.
- Bottom-watch pair: put/call > 1.10 and T2108 < 10%.
- Accumulate SPY only at higher levels after a bottom forms — never at the low.
- "Going in early in anticipation of a change in trend often leads to losses."
