# WW 2019-04-21 — GMI signals applied to the QQQ, 2016–2019

**Source:** [GMI signals applied to $QQQ, January, 2016-April 18, 2019](../../raw/posts/2019-04-21-gmi-signals-applied-to-qqq-january-2016-april-18-2019.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2019/04/gmi-signals-applied-to-qqq-january-2016-april-18-2019/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A chart of the QQQ marked with every Red and Green GMI period from January 2016 to April 2019, with the colour-change criteria printed beneath it — the same self-audit exercise as November 2011 and February 2016, extended over three years. The routine behind it: "**Every day after the market closes I compute the GMI total (out of 6) and post the current Red or Green signal.**" And the purpose in one line: "**The GMI helps me to trade consistent with the longer term trend of the market.**" GMI at 5 and Green at the time.

## Key claims

- A three-year visual signal record (Jan 2016 – Apr 2019) published with its criteria.
- "Every day after the market closes I compute the GMI total (out of 6) and post the current Red or Green signal."
- "The GMI helps me to trade consistent with the longer term trend of the market."
- The third published self-audit of the signal, after 2011 and 2016.
