# WW 2016-12-11 — New TC2000 scan for bounces: CELG — the 2016 precursor of the green dot

**Source:** [All GMI components positive; $QQQ near all-time peak; New TC2000 scan for bounces: $CELG](../../raw/posts/2016-12-11-all-gmi-components-positive-qqq-near-all-time-peak-new-tc2000-scan-for-bounces-celg.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2016/12/all-gmi-components-positive-qqq-near-all-time-peak-new-tc2000-scan-for-bounces-celg/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A fully specified bounce scan added to his TC2000 club: (1) fast daily 10.4 stochastic above the slow 10.4.4, fast < 20 in any of the past 2 days (rising from oversold), fast ≤ 50 (not extended), close above the 50-day average; (2) at least a 15% rise from the 50-day low at some point in the past 50 days; (3) volume > 100,000. Five hits (CELG, MMS, HELE, AVXS, FTV); HELE dropped for a Stage II decline. The trade as taught: buy CELG Monday, stop below the recent bounce just under $110, and sell when the fast stochastic closes back below the slow — the same exit the 2018 green-dot definition would carry. CELG also finding support at its lower 15.2 daily Bollinger Band. QQQ at 119.50 approaching its March 2000 ATH of 120.50 — no vertical rise this time. Intraday ideas tweeted at @wishingwealth. GMI all six positive; T2108 nearing 80%.

## Key claims

- Scan spec: 10.4 > 10.4.4; 10.4 < 20 within 2 days; 10.4 ≤ 50; close > 50-day; ≥15% off the 50-day low within 50 days; volume > 100k.
- Exit pair: stop under the bounce low; sell on the fast stochastic closing back under the slow.
- Lower 15.2 BB support as a bonus, "not required by this scan."
- "I like to buy stocks that consolidate after a large gap up and then move up."
- QQQ vs its 2000 top: 119.50 vs 120.50, without the 2000 vertical rise.
