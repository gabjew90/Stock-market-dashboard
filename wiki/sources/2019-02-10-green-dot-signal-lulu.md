# WW 2019-02-10 — Green dot signal: LULU — the procedure, and why bounces over breakouts

**Source:** [Green dot signal:  $LULU](../../raw/posts/2019-02-10-green-dot-signal-lulu.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2019/02/green-dot-signal-lulu/) · tier: trade_example

## What it covers

A complete green-dot walk-through on LULU: the oversold fast stochastic crosses the slow one; check the weekly for trend and position versus the last green-line top (LULU is below it, but in a possible cup-with-handle); stop below the low of the bounce day (143.06 → stop 142.99); a recent bounce off the lower 15.2 daily Bollinger Band is a plus; IBD would wait for the pivot (~153) on volume. The market caveat is explicit — many GLBs failing, few new highs, so the setup may not work — and the preference for bounces over breakouts is restated on stop-placement grounds.

## Key claims

- "A green dot signal indicates that a stock is bouncing from an oversold level. This signal is not often useful for a declining stock."
- "Before I act on a green dot signal I look at the weekly chart to see the trend and the stock's position regarding its last green-line top."
- "I prefer to buy bounces from support rather than the actual break-out because I can set my stop loss at a technically meaningful place where the possible loss is also minimal. It is much harder for me to know where to set a stop after a break-out."
- "In a rising market with many successful break-outs to ATHs, I have found the green dot signal to work best for me."
- Idea sourced from @TMLTrader on Twitter.
