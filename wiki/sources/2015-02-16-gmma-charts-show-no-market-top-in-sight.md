# WW 2015-02-16 — GMMA charts show no market top in sight

**Source:** [GMMA charts show no market top in sight](../../raw/posts/2015-02-16-gmma-charts-show-no-market-top-in-sight.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2015/02/gmma-charts-show-no-market-top-in-sight/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Against pundits calling a top, he shows what a top actually looks like on his modified weekly GMMA (12 EMAs + a 13th of length 1 for the close): the red lines converge, then fall below the blue with white space between — BWR — illustrated on the 2000 and 2007 QQQ tops, with RWB re-emerging in 2003. February 2015 shows a continuing RWB advance since early 2013. He begins moving pension money back into mutual funds, having resisted the January 23 Buy signal for three weeks: "Everyone has to sell down to their own comfort level."

## Key claims

- "We must always guard against marrying a scenario and be ready to change direction to be consistent with the market's main trend."
- The top signature: "The red lines converge, demonstrating little price movement over the different average lengths, and finally you get the red lines below the blue lines with a white area separating them." "Anytime the white space disappears and the red lines fall below the blue lines it is appropriate for me to become defensive."
- "I have begun to transfer some of my university pension funds back into mutual funds. The GMI has been on a Buy signal since January 23rd, and I have resisted it until now."
- GMMA used on individual stocks too (AAPL RWB, TSLA "no more RWB", GLD's full bull-to-bear cycle).
