# WW 2009-12-21 — The rocket criteria, and two stop levels per position

**Source:** [Some new rocket stocks to watch](../../raw/posts/2009-12-21-some-new-rocket-stocks-to-watch.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2009/12/some-new-rocket-stocks-to-watch/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The rocket screen at its strictest, and the stop rule that goes with it.** "I used TC2007 to scan the market
for stocks that meet **my most stringent fundamental and technical criteria for rockets**. These stocks have great
fundamentals and technicals, **have already doubled their price a year ago and are near 5 year or all-time
highs**… **11 stocks out of 4,000**… All of these stocks had **last quarterly earnings up at least 100%.**"
Seven of the eleven had appeared on an IBD 100 or New America list that year. **Two stops, chosen by where you
bought**: "I have also noted in this table where I might place **long term or short term stop losses**… **the most
conservative stop loss is the short term support level. With a growth stock I rarely retain a long position if
the stock *closes* below its short term support level. However, if I bought near long term support I might use
the LT support level as my exit strategy.**" And the insurance is immediate: "**that is why I immediately enter a
sell stop or buy a put option for insurance, after buying one of these high momentum stocks**" — because "these
stocks have already proven themselves as being in strong up-trends, but **one never knows when an up-trend will
end**." **A statistic on trial**: "37% of the Nasdaq100 stocks had their **fast stochastic line above their slow
stochastic line, a new measure of the short term up-trend that I am trying out**" — December 2009, and the
ancestor of the stochastic components that later sit inside the GMI2. On media: "I listened a little to CNBC last
week and realized **how anxious I became about the market and how much my judgment was impaired**." 

## Key claims

- **The stop level is a function of the entry**: bought at short-term support, exit on a close below it; bought near long-term support, the LT level can be the exit instead.
- **The sell stop or protective put goes on immediately after the buy**, not later — the position is never unprotected.
- The strict rocket screen in full: already doubled year-on-year, near a 5-year or all-time high, and ≥100% growth in the most recent quarter's earnings. Yield: 11 of 4,000.
- **A stochastic-cross breadth statistic being trialled in December 2009** — the same oscillator family that later became GMI2 components.
- Media consumption named as a measurable impairment: "how anxious I became… and how much my judgment was impaired."
