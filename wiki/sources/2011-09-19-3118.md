# WW 2011-09-19 — Behavior over words; adviser sentiment as contrarian fuel

**Source:** [GMI rises to 3; Market may be turning, some promising growth stocks at new highs](../../raw/posts/2011-09-19-3118.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2011/09/3118/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A re-entry read after the August 2011 decline (one of the UMDSMC Education Posts category),
opening with the psychologist's version of his epistemics and containing the cleanest arithmetic
for the dollar-move preference: a point move priced through an option contract.

## Key claims

- **The behaviorist epistemics:** "To understand human beings, one must concentrate on their
  **behavior rather than their words**. The same goes for the markets. Ignore all of the verbal
  noise of the media pundits and concentrate on the markets' actions." (He is, professionally, a
  behavioral researcher — this is the method transferring.)
- **Leader durability as the tell:** "The fact that a leader like AAPL can come through the
  volatility of the past few months **within 1% of its all-time high** bodes well for tech and
  growth stocks." Leaders surviving the decline = the same survive-the-turmoil logic as the ATH
  watchlist.
- **Adviser sentiment as contrarian fuel:** the Investors Intelligence poll showed "about 5%
  **more bears than bulls**. This rarely happens and the market often goes up when so many
  professionals have given up." A *rare inversion* in professional sentiment, not retail mood,
  is the trigger.
- **Seasonality noted both ways:** October dread vs "sell in May... says to go back into the
  market around Halloween. I have seen many markets bottom out in October."
- **The point-move arithmetic:** AZO up $17.42 — "I buy expensive stocks and trade on the point
  move, not the percentage move, and **a 17 point move on one call option = $1,700**." The
  dollar-move doctrine priced through the option multiplier.
- **Sparse new-high lists are a feature:** "When there are only a few stocks hitting 52 week
  highs, it is easy to go through the list" — thin lists concentrate attention on survivors
  (JAZZ, ATHN, HITK with its 8-year base breakout and 98 composite).
- Positioning: "I am **wading** into this market" — staged, again, with AAPL first.
