# WW 2025-12-10 — The Blue Dot of Happiness setup, and why he likes it

**Source:** [Blog Post: Day 9 of $QQQ short term up-trend; $JNJ had a Blue Dot of Happiness setup early Wednesday, I tweeted about it on X in the morning; see daily chart.](../../raw/posts/2025-12-10-blog-post-day-9-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-jnj-had-a-blue-dot-of-happiness-setup-early-wednesday-i-tweeted-ab.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2025/12/blog-post-day-9-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-jnj-had-a-blue-dot-of-happiness-setup-early-wednesday-i-tweeted-about-it-on-x-in-the-morning-see-daily-chart/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The setup stated with the reason he prefers it**: "**I look for a stock that recently traded at an ATH and
then bounced from oversold. I like this setup because I know if the bounce fails I can exit immediately with a
small loss.**" Two conditions — recent all-time high (so it is a leader) and an oversold bounce (so the entry is
near support) — and the preference is justified by the *exit* rather than by the expected gain, which is how
almost every entry on this wiki is argued. **An intraday caveat worth having**: "**sometimes blue dots occur in
the morning but the stock fails the setup by closing.**" The dot is defined on a daily close, so a morning
crossing can un-cross by 4pm — the same reason the [3:45 PM rule](../methodology/scans.md) exists, applied to a
signal rather than to an order. JNJ is the worked case, "a successful break out… **note the high volume on this
bounce from oversold**." The setup is explained in full in his TraderLion presentation, which he links.

## Key claims

- **Blue Dot of Happiness = recently at an ATH, then a bounce from oversold** — leader plus proximity to support.
- **The preference is argued from the exit**: "if the bounce fails I can exit immediately with a small loss."
- **A morning blue dot can fail by the close** — the signal is defined on the daily close, so intraday sightings are provisional.
- Volume on the bounce day is the confirming statistic, as on a breakout.
- Documented in his TraderLion presentation, which is the off-blog teaching channel.
