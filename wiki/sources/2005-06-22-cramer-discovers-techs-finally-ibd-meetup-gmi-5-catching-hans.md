# WW 2005-06-22 — The IBD Meetup room as a sentiment gauge

**Source:** [Cramer discovers techs–finally!  IBD MeetUp; GMI: +5: Catching HANS](../../raw/posts/2005-06-22-cramer-discovers-techs-finally-ibd-meetup-gmi-5-catching-hans.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/06/cramer-discovers-techs-finally-ibd-meetup-gmi-5-catching-hans/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Sentiment measured in a room rather than a survey. Thirty-three days into a rally: "**What a difference a rally makes!** There were about a dozen people there, all discussing the market and their holdings. **Most members had not wanted to attend last month's meeting.** Some people had made money the past month, and some were still timid about owning stocks. **I asked people about GOOG — most were afraid of it — a good sign.**" Attendance itself, and fear of the leading stock, read as contrarian evidence that the rally had further to run. On Cramer endorsing tech 33 days into the move: "it is clear to me that Cramer is not a believer in charts — he attempts to discern the mythical value of stocks."

## Key claims

- Meetup attendance as a crowd-sentiment reading — low turnout at the lows, full room after a rally.
- Fear of the leading stock (GOOG) among experienced traders read as bullish.
- Pundits arriving late to a move (33 days in) as its own signal.
- "The mythical value of stocks" — the same anti-valuation stance as "value is a myth" twenty years later.
