# WW 2016-10-16 — TLT as the rate proxy; the jigsaw that fits until it doesn't

**Source:** [Watching for Halloween rally; $AAPL supporting $QQQ; Interest rates $TLT and the dollar $UUP rise and gold $GLD falls](../../raw/posts/2016-10-16-watching-for-halloween-rally-aapl-supporting-qqq-interest-rates-tlt-and-the-dollar-uup-rise-and-gold-gld-fall.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2016/10/watching-for-halloween-rally-aapl-supporting-qqq-interest-rates-tlt-and-the-dollar-uup-rise-and-gold-gld-falls/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The bond ETF named as the rate instrument**: "I follow the **20+ year government bond ETF, TLT, as an
indicator of how bond traders feel about long term interest rates**. TLT is in a swoon, leading to a higher
dollar and lower gold prices." He then lays the three charts side by side — TLT falling, UUP rising, GLD falling
— and closes with the caution that makes the passage worth keeping: "**It all fits together like a jig saw
puzzle — until it doesn't…**" A coherent macro story is a description, not a forecast, and he says so at the
moment it is most tempting to believe. **A limitation of his own signal, contemporaneously**: "**the GMI signal
recently flashed Red, but this signal has recently coincided with short term bottoms rather than tops**" — the
whipsaw problem he would formalise, noticed while it was happening. **A capitulation tell on the index's largest
component**: "AAPL is defying gravity and may be the last component to decline before the current weakness in the
QQQ ends? (**When the bulls give up on AAPL, the end of the decline may be near.**)" **And the seasonal
reasoning**, via Hulbert: coming up is the seasonally strongest stretch, and current weakness "may be setting us
up for it. **Scare everyone into selling out to stronger hands who will buy low and profit from the subsequent
move up. Most advances begin after a decline. So I will reverse and go long if this market shows any signs of
strength in the coming days.**" Position: mainly cash with a small SQQQ, "if the short term down-trend continues
a few more days I will add more." Readings: T2108 31% ("would have to fall closer to 10%"), 10.4 stochastic 25,
a failing bounce off the lower 15.2 Bollinger Band, MACD 12/26/9 histograms negative and declining.

## Key claims

- **TLT is the instrument he reads long rates through** — bond traders' opinion rather than the Fed's statements.
- **"It all fits together like a jig saw puzzle — until it doesn't"** — narrative coherence across markets is a warning sign, not confirmation.
- **He notices the GMI's whipsaw problem as it happens**: a Red signal "has recently coincided with short term bottoms rather than tops."
- **A capitulation tell keyed to the heaviest index component**: the decline ends when the bulls give up on AAPL, not before.
- Seasonality reasoned mechanically rather than statistically — weakness transfers stock from weak to strong hands ahead of the strongest season.
- **Reversal is pre-committed**: "I will reverse and go long if this market shows any signs of strength," while holding a small short.
