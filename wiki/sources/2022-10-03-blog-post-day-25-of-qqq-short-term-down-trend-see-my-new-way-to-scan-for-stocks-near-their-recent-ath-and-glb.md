# WW 2022-10-03 — A new way to scan for stocks near their recent ATH — the <5% column, in full

**Source:** [Blog Post: Day 25 of $QQQ short term down-trend; See my new way to scan for stocks near their recent ATH and GLB: $DGII](../../raw/posts/2022-10-03-blog-post-day-25-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-see-my-new-way-to-scan-for-stocks-near-their-recent-ath-and-glb.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2022/10/blog-post-day-25-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-see-my-new-way-to-scan-for-stocks-near-their-recent-ath-and-glb-dgii/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The TraderLion argument first: "most of the stocks that Bill O'Neil graphed as among his greatest winners were stocks that had bases at or approaching their all-time-highs… If you want to ride a stock to the moon one needs to get on a launched rocket that is hitting a series of ATHs." Then a reproducible TC2000 column scan for stocks within 5% of a recent ATH — catching both those that have already had a GLB and those still just below one: **Price New High** set to *monthly, 500-bar high within the last 3 bars* ("I am content to look over the past 41 years"); a daily condition `h > .95*maxh60` (today's high within 5% of the highest high of the last 60 days); plus `C > 30` and `V > 10000` "to get rid of junk." He then draws the green lines on the survivors by hand and looks for setups.

## Key claims

- Scan spec: monthly Price New High (500-bar high in the last 3 monthly bars) + daily `h > .95*maxh60` + close > $30 + volume > 10,000.
- 500 monthly bars ≈ 41 years — an all-time-high proxy stated in bars.
- The scan deliberately catches pre-breakout candidates as well as completed GLBs.
- O'Neil's greatest winners based at or near their ATHs — the rationale for the whole ATH orientation.
