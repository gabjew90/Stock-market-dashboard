# WW 2025-12-14 — GMI component 6 (0MUTI) still live; FFTY in a daily BWR

## What it covers

The most recent post in the corpus to describe a GMI component in words, and the one that closes the wiki's open question: **component 6 was never replaced.** Twenty years after the GMI's launch it is still the IBD Growth Mutual Fund index above its 50-day average. Also documents his use of FFTY's daily Guppy pattern as a growth-stock breadth read that runs ahead of the QQQ.

## Key claims

- **Component 6, in present tense, in 2025:** "The component in my GMI table below that is positive if the IBD Growth Mutual Fund index (**0MUTI**) has closed above its 50 day average is now negative." Same component, same 50-day rule, same symbol as [WW 2009-01-12](2009-01-12-gmi-error-3-since-december-30-gmi-r-4-qqqq-back-near-support.md).
- **FFTY (the IBD 50 ETF) as a growth-leadership proxy:** it entered a daily BWR during the April decline, recovered to RWB, and re-entered BWR in November — "perhaps signifying that many growth stocks are weakening."
- The component turning negative drives a stance change: "I am therefore becoming bearish on the growth stocks in the Nasdaq 100 ETF (QQQ)... I am therefore mainly in cash in my trading accounts. I am a chicken."
- Holding two opposing readings at once: QQQ was very oversold (stoc10.1 = 13), "a level where QQQ has bounced before," while the daily RWB up-trend looked to be faltering — "I must be defensive AND nimble."
- A daily-RWB deterioration sequence is described precisely: QQQ "closed (dotted line) below all of the red lines and has entered the blue lines."
