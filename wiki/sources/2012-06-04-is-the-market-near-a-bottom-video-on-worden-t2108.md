# WW 2012-06-04 — T2108 from its 1986 inception — a pendulum that cannot call a top or bottom

**Source:** [Is the market  near a bottom?](../../raw/posts/2012-06-04-is-the-market-near-a-bottom-video-on-worden-t2108.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2012/06/is-the-market-near-a-bottom-video-on-worden-t2108/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A monthly chart of T2108 "available from its inception, in **September, 1986**", with the framing he uses ever after: it "serves as a **pendulum of the market**, ranging from extremely overbought to oversold… **While it cannot call a top or bottom, the more extreme it becomes, the greater the likelihood that the market is near a top (>80%) or bottom (<10%).**" Definition restated: the percentage of NYSE stocks closing above their simple 40-day moving average. Current reading 19% — "not yet at a very extreme level. **If T2108 gets into single digits, I will look to slowly buy an index ETF.**" All GMI and GMI2 components negative; day 34 of the down-trend; QQQ and SPY below their 10-week.

## Key claims

- T2108 data begins September 1986 — the historical range he reads extremes against.
- "While it cannot call a top or bottom, the more extreme it becomes, the greater the likelihood" — probabilistic, not a signal.
- 19% is explicitly *not* extreme; single digits is the threshold for slow index-ETF accumulation.
- The monthly chart used to place a current reading in decades of history.
