# WW 2010-05-24 — The submarine scan measured — 56% of hits fell ≥15% vs 17–19% of index components

**Source:** [Submarine scan worked–PWRD dives; Market treacherous](../../raw/posts/2010-05-24-submarine-scan-worked-pwrd-dives-market-treacherous.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2010/05/submarine-scan-worked-pwrd-dives-market-treacherous/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A rare *measured* follow-up on one of his own scans. The nine stocks his submarine scan produced on 2010-04-29, tracked with TC2007's watchlist tracking report: all nine declined, and **five of the nine (56%) fell 15% or more**, against 17% of Nasdaq-100 and 19% of S&P 500 components over the same stretch (QQQQ −11%, SPY −10%). "Thus, my submarine scan detected stocks that were more likely to have taken big dives than would be expected in the components of the Nasdaq100 and S&P500 Indexes." PWRD, flagged in that post from its 4/30 bar, subsequently gapped down hard on 5/17.

## Key claims

- Submarine-scan hit rate measured against index base rates: 56% down ≥15% vs 17–19%.
- The method: a TC2007 watchlist tracking report showing each stock's change since it was added.
- The scan is directional edge, not certainty — all nine fell in a market that fell.
