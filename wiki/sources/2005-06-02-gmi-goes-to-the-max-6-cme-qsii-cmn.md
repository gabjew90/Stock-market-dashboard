# WW 2005-06-02 — GMI goes to the max, +6 — the first day of >100 successful new highs

**Source:** [GMI goes to the max: +6, CME, QSII, CMN](../../raw/posts/2005-06-02-gmi-goes-to-the-max-6-cme-qsii-cmn.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/06/gmi-goes-to-the-max-6-cme-qsii-cmn/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"For the first time since we began to post the GMI, there were more than 100 successful 10 day new highs… This result assures us that buying stocks at new highs has become profitable and relatively common. In contrast, there were only 10 'successful' 10 day new lows. (Shorting new lows is not the way to go now.)" 194 highs to 15 lows; U-19; "the last time the GMI indicators looked like this, the QQQQ up trend lasted about 92 trading days."

## Key claims

- Component 1 first turns positive on the >100 count, June 2, 2005.
- The prior comparable up-trend ran ~92 days — his first duration base rate.
