## What it covers

Two scan hits sharing one anatomy: "both had a recent GLB after which they consolidated for several weeks and then broke out again. All had **4wk>10wk>30wk averages, my prime pattern for advancing stocks**. They also had a weekly green bar, indicating a bounce up off of the 4 week average." And the timing principle: "**stocks at new ATHs after a market decline are often the new leaders.**"

## Key claims

- "4wk>10wk>30wk averages, my prime pattern for advancing stocks" — the alignment named as the primary weekly pattern.
- The weekly green bar = a bounce up off the 4-week average.
- GLB → multi-week consolidation → second breakout as a recurring shape.
- Stocks at new ATHs after a decline are often the next cycle's leaders.
