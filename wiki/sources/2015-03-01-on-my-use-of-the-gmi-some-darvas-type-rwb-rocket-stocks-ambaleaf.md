# WW 2015-03-01 — On my use of the GMI; a self-correction on the pension; the Darvas scan; AMBA

**Source:** [On my use of the GMI, A Darvas type RWB rocket stock: $AMBA](../../raw/posts/2015-03-01-on-my-use-of-the-gmi-some-darvas-type-rwb-rocket-stocks-ambaleaf.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2015/03/on-my-use-of-the-gmi-some-darvas-type-rwb-rocket-stocks-ambaleaf/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A rare explicit self-correction. His long-standing rule for the *pension* is that the QQQ's 30-week average must turn down before he pulls that money out; in 2014 it never did, yet he moved most of the pension to money market anyway — "the huge increase in my pension account since 2009 together with my fear of losing the gains caused me to ignore my primary rule and to prematurely go to cash." He is back in. He also describes his TC2000 Darvas EasyScan (46 hits out of ~6,000) and shows AMBA above its green-line top in a weekly RWB pattern.

## Key claims

- "Every time [the GMI] turns to a Sell does not signify to me that a major decline is beginning. In the past, the 30 week average of the QQQ had to turn down to get me to pull my most conservative pension money out of the market."
- "I mistakenly (in hindsight) took most of my university pension out of mutual funds… caused me to ignore my primary rule and to prematurely go to cash. I am back in the market now."
- "As one gets closer to retirement, s/he must deal with the fear of losing needed savings versus staying invested."
- Darvas EasyScan: ~6,000 stocks → 46; described in his 2012 Houston TC2000 webinar.
