# WW 2016-03-20 — Buying before the green line — with a different stop

**Source:** [GMI at 6, going back in, great week! $MXL and $SQ; 11 strong stocks, Get my posts as tweets:  @WishingWealth](../../raw/posts/2016-03-20-gmi-at-6-going-back-in-great-week-mxl-and-sq-11-strong-stocks.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2016/03/gmi-at-6-going-back-in-great-week-mxl-and-sq-11-strong-stocks/) · tier: trade_example

## What it covers

Two positions taken *before* the green line breakout, with the deviation from his own rule named and a
different stop attached. **MXL**: a cup-and-handle breakout "on above average trading volume," with the
all-time top at 19.50 from 2010 still overhead. "I started a position and **will go in much more if MXL can
close above its all-time top at 19.50**. **I usually wait for the GLB, but its fundamental and technical
strength tempted me to move early. If MXL closes below its pivot break-out line, around 18.18, I will begin to
exit the trade.**" The pre-GLB entry does not borrow the GLB's stop — it gets the pivot line, which is much
tighter. **SQ**: a November 2015 IPO bought on relative strength — "I bought SQ after I noticed that the stock
**held up at the bottom line in the chart even when the market had a few large down days in February**" — with
the same escalation attached: "**if SQ can close above its green line top at 14.78 I will aggressively
accumulate it.**" (Visa's 10% stake was announced about a week after he bought, which he calls "a very nice
surprise" rather than foresight.) Also here: a **monthly**-timeframe variant of the doubler scan — "my TC2000
scan for stocks that have doubled the past year, are near their yearly highs and have a strong long term
*monthly* RWB pattern," cross-marked against his IBD lists — and the pension being moved back into an S&P 500
fund as the GMI reached 6. The sentiment note is his standard one: "**I am glad there are so many media pundits
skeptical of this advance. The market climbs a wall of worry.**"

## Key claims

- **Position size is a function of confirmation level.** A starter position before the green line; "much more" or "aggressively accumulate" on the close above it.
- **A pre-GLB entry takes a different stop** — the cup-and-handle pivot at 18.18, not the green line at 19.50. Entering early means accepting a tighter invalidation, not a looser one.
- **He names the rule he is bending** ("I usually wait for the GLB") and what tempted him, rather than presenting the early entry as the method.
- **Relative strength during a market decline as the buy reason** on SQ — holding support while the market had large down days.
- A **monthly** RWB variant of the doubler-near-highs scan, cross-referenced against his IBD lists.
- Pension re-entry and stock buying happen together on a GMI of 6, with dividend payers bought alongside growth names.
