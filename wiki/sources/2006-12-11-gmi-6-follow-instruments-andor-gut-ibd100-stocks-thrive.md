# WW 2006-12-11 — Readers challenge him for overriding his own indicators — and he concedes the point

**Source:** [GMI: 6; Follow instruments and/or gut? IBD100 stocks thrive](../../raw/posts/2006-12-11-gmi-6-follow-instruments-andor-gut-ibd100-stocks-thrive.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2006/12/gmi-6-follow-instruments-andor-gut-ibd100-stocks-thrive/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A rare piece of the blog: readers catching an inconsistency and getting a straight answer. "With my market
indicators so positive, **some of you have written to me asking me why I have been raising cash and even taking
on a position in QID**, the ultra inverse ETF for the QQQQ. **You raise an important question that I have been
struggling with. Why not stay with the up-trend signaled by the GMI and not try to anticipate a change in trend?
You may be right — maybe I should fly on instruments. But doing so subjects me to a possible large cut in my
profits because my indicators will only call the peak after the indexes have fallen some.**" That is the
lag trade-off argued in the open, with the concession attached: he does not claim the discretionary exit is
correct, only that the alternative has a stated cost. It sits directly against the
[November 2008 measurement](../sources/) showing that a two-week delay captured almost all of an avoidable
decline — evidence that arrived later and cuts against the instinct he is defending here. Market context: GMI 6,
195 new highs, day 79 of the QQQQ up-trend.

## Key claims

- **Readers challenged the inconsistency and he published the challenge**, then conceded it might be right: "you may be right — maybe I should fly on instruments."
- **The stated cost of pure instrument-following is the lag**: "my indicators will only call the peak after the indexes have fallen some."
- He is raising cash and holding QID with the GMI at 6 and the up-trend 79 days old — the discretionary override at its most extreme.
- The 2008 arithmetic later measured this cost and found it small, which is worth reading against this post rather than separately.
