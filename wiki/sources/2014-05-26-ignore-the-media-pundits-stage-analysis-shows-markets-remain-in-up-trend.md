# WW 2014-05-26 — Stage Analysis as the pundit filter

**Source:** [Ignore the media pundits — Stage Analysis shows markets remain in up-trend](../../raw/posts/2014-05-26-ignore-the-media-pundits-stage-analysis-shows-markets-remain-in-up-trend.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2014/05/ignore-the-media-pundits-stage-analysis-shows-markets-remain-in-up-trend/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The best self-contained tutorial on Stage Analysis in the corpus, written against a backdrop of
bearish punditry in a flat-but-rising 2014 market. Two things the wiki lacked: the explicit
framing of **timeframe choice as a lifestyle decision**, and the observation that flat
consolidations inside Stage 2 are what health looks like.

## Key claims

- **Timeframe is chosen by life, not by edge:** "Day traders need to look at intraday trends
  using hourly or even 5 minute bars... I am too old to attempt such tactics, which are to me
  **the equivalent of flying with the Blue Angels.** I cannot react fast enough and I sure do
  not want to spend every second of the trading day watching my stocks... I want to enjoy other
  things in my life... and manage my investments **only part-time**." Weekly charts follow from
  that, not from a claim that weekly beats daily.
- **The Stage-2 mechanics, spelled out for beginners:** weekly bars plus the 30-week average of
  Friday closes; "as long as prices remain above the 30 week average **and the average is
  rising**, the market is in a Stage 2 up-trend." O'Neil cited as also using weekly charts.
- **The pension rule as a pundit filter:** "As long as the major index ETF's remain in a Stage 2
  advance I leave my conservative university pension in mutual funds. **I do not care what the
  media pundits are saying**, or try to out guess Mr. Market." With the two-speed caveat in
  parentheses: "(This does not stop me from trying to beat the market in my trading accounts,
  by exiting and entering even during a Stage 2 advance.)"
- **Flatness is not weakness:** the pundits "seem to think that they should always be climbing
  at this super rate! No!!! **A healthy market rises and consolidates over and over again.**"
- **Epistemics restated:** "*no one* knows what the market will do in the future... Trends tend
  to continue along — **until they don't!**"
- **The Forbes line on the advice industry:** "it is much easier to make money from the market
  by **selling advice**. Hence the plethora of writers and market prognosticators who have
  rarely walked their talk."
- Context note: the GMI had been on a Buy since 4/22 while **IBD still called the market in a
  correction** awaiting a follow-through day — a live example of his disagreement with IBD's
  timing framework.
