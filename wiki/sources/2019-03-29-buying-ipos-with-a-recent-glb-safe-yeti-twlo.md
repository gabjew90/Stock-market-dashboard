# WW 2019-03-29 — Buying IPOs with a recent GLB: SAFE, YETI, TWLO

**Source:** [Buying  IPOs with a recent GLB: $SAFE $YETI $TWLO](../../raw/posts/2019-03-29-buying-ipos-with-a-recent-glb-safe-yeti-twlo.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2019/03/buying-ipos-with-a-recent-glb-safe-yeti-twlo/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The IPO variant of the GLB stated compactly: recent IPOs (within the last 4–5 years) that form a multi-month top and break through on above-average volume to an all-time high — "a lot of room to gain as traders see the new highs and jump on board." Two rules attached: never hold a GLB that *closes* back below its green line, and often repurchase it if it closes back above.

## Key claims

- "I like to buy recent IPOs (within last 4-5 years) that form a multi-month top and then break through on above average volume to an all-time high."
- "I never hold a GLB that *closes* back below its green line, but I often will repurchase it if it closes back above its green line."
- Examples: SAFE, YETI, TWLO.
