# WW 2014-10-19 — Taking pension out into strength — 'if the market rises to likely resistance levels'

**Source:** [16th day of $QQQ short term down-trend; Bottom or dead cat bounce? Keeping an eye on the $BABY](../../raw/posts/2014-10-19-16th-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-bottom-or-dead-cat-bounce-keeping-an-eye-on-the-baby.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2014/10/16th-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-bottom-or-dead-cat-bounce-keeping-an-eye-on-the-baby/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The pension exit paced against resistance rather than dumped: with the GMI at 0 after a sharp oversold bounce, "**I therefore took a very small amount of my university pension out of the market on Friday. If the market rises to likely resistance levels, I will take more of my pension out of mutual funds, just in case.**" The reasoning is symmetric with his entries — scale out into strength as he scales in on confirmation — and the self-description is unchanged: "**I have repeatedly written that I trade like a chicken. If I am wrong, and the GMI returns to a Buy signal, I can always go back in.**" The technical picture: a weekly SPY up-trend support line "that lasted for more than one year has been broken," the 30-week penetrated, and large red volume spikes. Seasonality noted on the other side — "the market often is strong towards the end of the year."

## Key claims

- Pension sold **into strength**, in increments, at resistance — the same pacing as the 2025 "sell the rest when we get a good bounce."
- A very small first tranche while the bounce was still running.
- "If I am wrong… I can always go back in" — reversibility as what makes early exits affordable.
- A broken multi-year trendline plus a penetrated 30-week plus heavy down-volume read together.
