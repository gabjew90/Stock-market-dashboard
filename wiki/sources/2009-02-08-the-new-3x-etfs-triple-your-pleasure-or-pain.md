# WW 2009-02-08 — The new 3X ETFs — triple your pleasure or pain

**Source:** [The new 3x ETF’s–triple your pleasure — or pain](../../raw/posts/2009-02-08-the-new-3x-etfs-triple-your-pleasure-or-pain.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2009/02/the-new-3x-etfs-triple-your-pleasure-or-pain/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The arrival of 3× leveraged ETFs, catalogued as they appeared: "when I try to trade the trend of the QQQQ, I buy QLD (ultra long) or QID (ultra short)… these ultra ETF's are designed to move twice as much as the underlying index. Well, less well known is that there now exist **3x ETF's**." He lists sixteen Direxion funds — bulls FAS, BGU, TNA, ERX, EDC, MWJ, TYH, DZK; bears FAZ, BGZ, TZA, ERY, EDZ, MWN, TYP, DPK — notes eight already traded over a million shares a day, and that options trade on most. The warning is in the title and repeated: "**Remember, the leverage works both ways**, they aim to go up or down at 3x the speed of the relevant index. Still, if I have a good idea of the trend, these ETF's may prove better than going to the casino."

## Key claims

- February 2009: the 3× ETFs catalogued at their emergence, two years before TQQQ becomes his default.
- The prior vehicles were the 2× QLD / QID pair.
- "The leverage works both ways" — stated as the standing caveat.
- Leverage is justified only by confidence in the trend, never by the instrument.
