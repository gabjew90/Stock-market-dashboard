# WW 2022-04-06 — Livermore's day of reckoning — and a change to the pension rule

**Source:** [Blog post: Jesse Livermore said: “Finally there came the awful day of reckoning for the bulls and the optimists and the wishful thinkers and those vast hordes that, dreading the pain of a small loss at the beginning, were now about to suffer total amputation—without anesthetics.”](../../raw/posts/2022-04-06-blog-post-jesse-livermore-said-finally-there-came-the-awful-day-of-reckoning-for-the-bulls-and-the-optimists.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2022/04/blog-post-jesse-livermore-said-finally-there-came-the-awful-day-of-reckoning-for-the-bulls-and-the-optimists-and-the-wishful-thinkers-and-those-vast-hordes-that-dreading-the-pain-of-a-smal/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A documented revision to his own pension trigger. "**In the past when I took more chances I would wait for the 30 week average to curve down before taking my conservative university pension money out of the market.** We have reached that point now with QQQ, DIA, IWM, and maybe SPY. **However, as I wrote last November, I began to transfer my university pension money out then because of the technical weakness I saw in the market**" — that is, in November 2021 he acted on breadth divergence *before* the 30-week rolled over, five months ahead of the formal trigger. "In my opinion, a major decline is just beginning. While younger persons can wait out this cycle, I will not. It is much better for me to be in cash until the major trends turn up. Remember, after the 1929 debacle, **it took 26 years for the DOW to come back to its pre-crash level**." Re-entry condition stated: "**I will reenter when the 30 week averages start rising again.**" Closes with the Livermore amputation passage.

## Key claims

- **The pension rule changed in practice:** the 30-week curving down is what he "would" wait for "in the past"; in November 2021 he exited early on technical weakness instead.
- Re-entry remains the 30-week turning back up.
- Age determines whether a decline can be ridden — stated as a personal, not universal, rule.
- The 1929 recovery given as **26 years** here (he says 25 in 2025-04-20 — his own figure varies).
