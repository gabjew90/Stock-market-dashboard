# WW 2008-01-22 — GMI 0 for 12 days; sucker rally near?; IBD100 holds the winners

**Source:** [GMI: 0; GMI-R: 0; 12th day of down-trend, sucker rally near?; IBD100 holds winners](../../raw/posts/2008-01-22-gmi-0-gmi-r-0-12th-day-of-down-trend-sucker-rally-near-ibd100-holds-winners.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2008/01/gmi-0-gmi-r-0-12th-day-of-down-trend-sucker-rally-near-ibd100-holds-winners/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

January 2008 in numbers: 844 new lows to 10 new highs; QQQQ/SPY under their 10-week for ten weeks, and since the QQQQ first closed under it (Nov 8) it fell 12% with 82% of its components lower — "can you see why it makes no sense to fight such odds?" T2108 at 19% is "possible bottom territory" and a bounce is possible, but the 10-week sits ~10% above price, so "a rally would have to exceed about +10% for me to call a change in trend. There is clearly a lot of room on the up-side for failed, sucker rallies." Meanwhile the IBD100 lists had carried the 2007 leaders early (ISRG, AAPL, GOOG, BIDU, GRMN, with entry prices and later highs), so he lets IBD do the ranking and keeps a TC2007 watchlist of the 26 stocks resisting the decline (HMSY, VIVO, SRCL) — "the key is to wait for the turn and not to be seduced into them now when false moves are very likely."

## Key claims

- Below-the-10-week odds: 82% of Nasdaq-100 lower over the ten weeks; "I rarely make money going long."
- T2108 19% = possible bottom, but distance to the 10-week defines how big a rally must be to matter (~+10%).
- IBD100 as a starting point, not a verdict; leaders appeared on lists early (ISRG 8/2007 at 199 → 340; AAPL 10/2006 at 54 → 203; GOOG 10/2005 at 311 → 747).
- Build the resisters watchlist during the decline; buy only after the turn.
