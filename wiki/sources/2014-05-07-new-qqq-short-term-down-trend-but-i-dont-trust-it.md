# WW 2014-05-07 — Distrusting his own signal; the lower band as better support

**Source:** [New $QQQ short term down-trend–but I don’t trust it](../../raw/posts/2014-05-07-new-qqq-short-term-down-trend-but-i-dont-trust-it.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2014/05/new-qqq-short-term-down-trend-but-i-dont-trust-it/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**Distrusting his own signal, and saying so plainly**: "**While my objective criteria for a short term
down-trend have been met, I do not trust it.**" The reason is a competing read of support, and it produces a
ranking of two support instruments: "each time the QQQ bounced from its **lower channel trend line**, it was
**also** bouncing up from the **lower Bollinger Band**… **To me, the lower BB is a better indicator of potential
support.**" A hand-drawn trend line and a computed band coincided; he prefers the computed one. The projection
follows: "so on Wednesday the QQQ bounced off of its lower BB, suggesting that we will now get a rise in the QQQ,
**but only to the top BB**." **The pinch**: "**we are headed for a BB pinch** (thanks \$Bill). **At some point
the BB will break up or down with a new move**" — direction unspecified, which is the honest form of that
pattern. The resolution of signal-versus-doubt is the Day-5 rule doing its job: "I tend to trust a new short term
trend **only when it reaches day 5**. Since 2006, **41% of short term down-trends lasted 5 days or less**. So,
even though the GMI remains on a Buy signal, **I am waiting mainly on the sidelines until Mr. Market reveals his
true direction.**"

## Key claims

- **The lower Bollinger Band beats a drawn trend line as a support instrument**, on his own comparison, when the two coincide.
- **A bounce off the lower band projects to the upper band**, not beyond — a bounded expectation rather than a trend call.
- **The BB pinch is directionless by construction**: "at some point the BB will break up or down with a new move."
- "My objective criteria… have been met, **I do not trust it**" — the signal and the confidence in it are tracked separately.
- The Day-5 rule resolves the conflict without overriding either instrument: wait, and let duration decide.
- The pinch idea credited to a reader ($Bill).
