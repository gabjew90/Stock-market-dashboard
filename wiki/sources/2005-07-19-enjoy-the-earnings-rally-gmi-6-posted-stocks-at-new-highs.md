# WW 2005-07-19 — The earnings rally, and why he uses sell stops

**Source:** [Enjoy the earnings rally; GMI: +6; posted stocks at new highs](../../raw/posts/2005-07-19-enjoy-the-earnings-rally-gmi-6-posted-stocks-at-new-highs.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/07/enjoy-the-earnings-rally-gmi-6-posted-stocks-at-new-highs/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Two durable items inside a routine strong-tape note (GMI +6, 259 new highs to 11 new lows, U-8). **The
earnings-cycle pattern, stated generally**: "A lot of stocks are rising on earnings expectations and falling on
the release. **It is not unusual to get an earnings rally in the general market and then to get a post-release
decline.**" He declines to trade the forecast — "I would not be surprised to see a weak August through October
period, **but will let the GMI guide me**." **And the reason stops exist**, which is about his life rather than
about markets: "I have sell stops in on all of my holdings and am letting things ride for now. **Sell stops
provide me the ability to focus on my job during the day and to sleep peacefully at night.**"

## Key claims

- **The earnings rally and the post-release decline** as a recurring market-level pattern, not just a single-stock one.
- A seasonal expectation is stated and then subordinated to the indicator — "but will let the GMI guide me."
- **Stops are what make the method compatible with a full-time job** — they delegate the watching, which is why he can trade while employed.
- "Sleep peacefully at night" ties the stop to the [sleeping point](../methodology/trading-psychology.md) doctrine rather than to expected value.
