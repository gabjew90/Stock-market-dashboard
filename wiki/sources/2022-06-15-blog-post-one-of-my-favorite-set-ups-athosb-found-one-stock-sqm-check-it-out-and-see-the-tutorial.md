# WW 2022-06-15 — One of my favorite set-ups, ATHOSB; SQM

**Source:** [One of my favorite set-ups, ATHOSB, found one stock, $SQM, check it out and see the tutorial](../../raw/posts/2022-06-15-blog-post-one-of-my-favorite-set-ups-athosb-found-one-stock-sqm-check-it-out-and-see-the-tutorial.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2022/06/blog-post-one-of-my-favorite-set-ups-athosb-found-one-stock-sqm-check-it-out-and-see-the-tutorial/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Names the setup **ATHOSB** (all-time high → oversold → bounce), calls it "one of my favorite set-ups," and gives the fullest disclosure of what drives the dots. Also the clearest statement of the setup's market-state conditionality, written during the 2022 bear market when the scan returned exactly one candidate out of 700+.

## Key claims

- Setup definition: "It scans for stocks that recently reached an all time high (ATH), then became oversold and now looks like it could bounce (OSB)."
- **The oversold measure is disclosed in outline:** "My measure of oversold is based on the daily 10.1, 10.4 and 10.4.4 stochastics indicators." The exact composite rule is deliberately withheld — "See if you can figure out the signals" — and rendered as the black and green dots.
- The risk rationale: "The beauty of buying a strong stock that may be bouncing is that if the bounce fails it is immediately apparent and I can exit promptly with a small loss."
- **Market-state conditionality, stated explicitly:** "In a weak market most set-ups fail... In a strong up-trending market this is a powerful set-up."
- Scan universe: "over 700 stocks on my IBD/MS watchlist" — one candidate found (SQM), which is itself a comment on the June 2022 environment.
- Fundamentals are checked after the technical screen: SQM had expected earnings growth +353%, composite rating 99, ROE 24%, sales +282%. "This is a CAN SLIM type stock."
- Bollinger Band context used to frame the trade's room: just above the lower 2.15 band and well below the upper, so "a bounce could therefore extend to $111. My stop loss would be around $90.29."
- Attribution: a charting tutorial for adding the dots was made by his late co-instructor, **David McCandlish**.
