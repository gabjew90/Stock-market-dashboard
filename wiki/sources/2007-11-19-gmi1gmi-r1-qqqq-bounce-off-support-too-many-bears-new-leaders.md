# WW 2007-11-19 — QQQQ tests its 30-week average; the 2000 top attributed; contrarian sentiment

**Source:** [GMI:1; GMI-R:1; QQQQ bounce off support?; Too many bears?; New leaders?](../../raw/posts/2007-11-19-gmi1gmi-r1-qqqq-bounce-off-support-too-many-bears-new-leaders.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2007/11/gmi1gmi-r1-qqqq-bounce-off-support-too-many-bears-new-leaders/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Written six weeks after the October 2007 top, with the GMI at 1 and the Nasdaq 100 having just fallen vertically. The most valuable post of the 2007 decline for methodology: it identifies the **Weekly QQQQ Index as his primary long-term indicator**, ties it explicitly to the 30-week average, and attributes his October 2000 exit to that same signal — the earliest such attribution in the corpus.

## Key claims

- **The primary long-term signal, named:** "The reason that the GMI and GMI-R still register 1 is that the Weekly QQQQ Index remains positive. **This index is my primary indicator of a longer term up or down move.**" The accompanying chart is the QQQQ weekly against its 30-week average — corroborating GMI component 5.
- **The 2000 exit attributed:** "It was **the reversal of the 30 week average in Oct 2000** that caused me to exit the markets near the top." Credit given to Stan Weinstein for "alerting me to the importance of the 30 week average."
- **A third test of support, marked as unresolved:** he annotates the chart with a "?" — "it remains to be seen whether this support will hold." It did not; the 2008 decline followed.
- **The house rule, in capitals:** "DISCERN THE TREND FROM WHAT THE MARKET IS DOING, NOT FROM WHAT THE PUNDITS ARE SAYING."
- **Four contrarian readings stacked:** put/call ratio 1.19 (readings above 1.00 "a major contrary indicator suggestive of at least a short term bounce up"); media pundits "openly bearish" with depression talk; bearish bloggers in his sentiment poll tripled to over 60%; and the strongest seasonal period beginning.
- **Position despite the contrarian signals:** "I remain mainly in cash and short the QQQQ in the QID. But I am ready to reverse my stance immediately if this market starts up... it does not pay to marry a particular market scenario." Contrarian sentiment is a bounce warning, not a re-entry trigger.
- **Selling-climax reading:** the November 7–12 fall was "a vertical free fall more characteristic of selling climaxes," from which he expects back-and-forth rather than a clean resolution.
- **Breadth detail:** 268 new lows vs 33 new highs, down from 519 new lows on November 8; T2108 at 22% off a 19% low; only 12% of his universe in an up-trend, against 7% at the August bottom.
- **Leaders from the wreckage:** a TC2007 new-high scan cross-checked against fundamentals — BITI and ATRO (quarterly earnings +100%), CSIQ, FCN, EHTH, VMSI, ACO (revenue +25%). "Clearly, others (insiders?) are already accumulating them."
