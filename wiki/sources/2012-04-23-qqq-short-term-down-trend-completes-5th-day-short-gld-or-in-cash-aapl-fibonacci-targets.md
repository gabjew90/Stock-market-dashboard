# WW 2012-04-23 — Why counter-trend bets feel worst; the AAPL 10-week rule

**Source:** [QQQ short term down-trend completes 5th day; short QQQ and GLD or in cash; AAPL Fibonacci targets](../../raw/posts/2012-04-23-qqq-short-term-down-trend-completes-5th-day-short-gld-or-in-cash-aapl-fibonacci-targets.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2012/04/qqq-short-term-down-trend-completes-5th-day-short-gld-or-in-cash-aapl-fibonacci-targets/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The psychology of acting on a turn, named precisely**: "**I have learned that it is most anxiety producing
to bet on a trend reversal because, by definition, it differs from the most recent market trend which I have
become accustomed to.**" That is the mechanism behind every "day 1 is hard" statement in the corpus — the
discomfort is not irrational, it is the definition of a reversal doing its work — and his answer is to substitute
the instrument for the feeling: "**however, I will trust my instrument, the GMI, and stay short or in cash in my
trading accounts until the next buy signal.**" **A per-stock rule derived from his own results**: "**I have
learned that I can make money trading AAPL by being long only when it is above its 10 week average.**" AAPL was
at \$572.98 against a 10-week average of \$570.79 — the rule quoted with both numbers, so it is falsifiable
rather than impressionistic. And the leader-as-tell restated ahead of the event: "AAPL, the market leader,
reports earnings on Tuesday and **the stock's reaction will tell me a lot about the market's inclinations. If
good earnings are met with a decline or flat response, then I will expect more weakness.**" Position: QID taken
on day 5 with more to follow if the decline continues; pension still invested.

## Key claims

- **Counter-trend entries feel worst by construction** — a reversal is *defined* as differing from the trend you have adapted to.
- The remedy is substitution, not willpower: "I will trust my instrument, the GMI."
- **A single-stock version of the market gate**: long AAPL only above its 10-week average, quoted with both prices.
- The leader-on-good-news test stated *before* the earnings release, as a conditional — which is what makes it a test rather than a rationalisation.
- Day 5 as the point at which the inverse ETF position is taken.
