# WW 2005-05-23 — Google rockets on; funds that let you time the market; the 401(k) goes back in

**Source:** [Google rockets on; Funds to time the market; BOOM-cup with handle; GMI–+5](../../raw/posts/2005-05-23-google-rockets-on-funds-to-time-the-market-boom-cup-with-handle-gmi-5.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/05/google-rockets-on-funds-to-time-the-market-boom-cup-with-handle-gmi-5/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

GMI +5: "this reading does not mean that we won't have declines — only that the current trend is up. These indicators have gotten me out of every major market decline since 98." 171 new highs to 17 lows; some backing and filling welcomed. "I moved my 401(k) funds from the money market fund to equities about a week ago. Most mutual fund families allow a limited amount of transfers between funds" — and "there are at least 2 mutual fund families that are designed to allow you to time the market" for those who can trade the indexes.

## Key claims

- The GMI reads the current trend, not the absence of declines.
- First documented 401(k) re-entry on the blog (mid-May 2005).
- Fund families built for timing named as the workaround to switching limits.
