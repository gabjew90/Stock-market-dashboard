# WW 2012-08-26 — How to buy an IPO — wait for the first base

**Source:** [KORS and FRAN break out; my Worden Work-Shop video](../../raw/posts/2012-08-26-kors-breaks-out.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2012/08/kors-breaks-out/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The IPO rule in one sentence**: "**One of the best ways to buy an IPO is to wait for it to form its first
base and to then buy it on the break-out to an all-time high.**" That is what he did with KORS, and it is the
general form of the IPO-GLB material documented elsewhere — a recent listing has no green line until it has made
a high and held below it, so the "first base" *is* the green line's construction. The rule solves the problem
IPOs pose to his method: there is no all-time-high history to break out from, so you wait for the stock to
manufacture one. IBD picked up the same breakout, which he notes with evident satisfaction. Also here, the
standing discipline: "**I find it most important to let the market dictate the trend and to ignore all news and
media pundits' advice.**"

## Key claims

- **Wait for an IPO's first base, then buy the breakout to an all-time high** — the general rule behind the IPO-GLB material.
- The first base *is* the green line's construction: a recent listing has no ATH history until it makes one and holds below it.
- KORS as the worked case, with IBD flagging the same breakout independently.
- Solves the specific problem IPOs pose — no prior high to break out from.
