# WW 2011-01-06 — Judy, and what a concept-stock collaborator contributes

**Source:** [Judy’s concept stocks rocket up– ARMH, CAVM, APKT, MCP](../../raw/posts/2011-01-06-judys-concept-stocks-rocket-up-armh-cavm-apky-mcp.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2011/01/judys-concept-stocks-rocket-up-armh-cavm-apky-mcp/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The fullest description of the outside stock-picker who appears throughout the corpus. "I really need to set up
a '**Judy's Picks**' column. So many of the stocks that she told me about over a year ago have taken off. Check
out **ARMH, CAVM, APKT, PANL and FFIV**. Judy tells me that years ago **she bought ISRG for well under \$10**.
And I watched several years ago as a number of her picks were bought out by other companies. **She was the first
person to alert me to the concepts of cloud computing and LEDs, long before I heard about them from the media.**"
What Judy supplies is the half his method deliberately lacks: **thesis**. His screens find stocks already moving;
she identifies technologies before they move, and he has repeatedly bought names on her research and then applied
his own timing to them (INGN, TDSC, GNRC, CPST, ALTI, RIMM, AMZN elsewhere in the corpus). The division is
explicit — she picks "concept" stocks, he decides when. It is also the one input he takes from a person rather
than from a chart, and he credits it consistently, including when she was earlier than him.

## Key claims

- **Judy supplies thesis; he supplies timing** — the division of labour is consistent across a decade of posts.
- Named early calls: ARMH, CAVM, APKT, PANL, FFIV, and ISRG "for well under $10."
- **"She was the first person to alert me to the concepts of cloud computing and LEDs"** — ahead of the media, which is the point of the collaboration.
- The only recurring *human* input to a method otherwise built on charts, and always credited.
- He notes she is frequently earlier than he is, without softening it.
