## What it covers

The post that surfaced in the coverage audit's random sample of "untouched" posts and proved
the untouched bucket held real methodology. A bounce scan requiring **three simultaneous
support indicators**, a textbook confluence candidate, and the earnings-date hazard handled
with a hedge idea.

## Key claims

- **The scan:** "stocks that have bounced up off of **3 daily indicators of support/over-sold**
  within the past few days," plus closed ≥50% above the 250-day low and above $15 (and
  unstated extras). Yield: **8 of 4,800**. Requiring three support signals at once is the
  strictest form of his bounce family — confluence as the filter.
- **The PETS confluence stack:** high-volume GLB to an ATH in May → rested/consolidated →
  doubled in the past year → EPS +37%, Composite 96, RS 98 → a "powerful rising yellowband"
  on the weekly. Scan hit + GLB + yellowband + fundamentals, all on one candidate.
- **The earnings-date hazard, honestly:** "Unfortunately, PETS reports earnings on Monday
  morning. Should have run this scan Friday afternoon. What to do? **Buy PETS a collar?!**" —
  an options hedge floated as the way to hold a setup through an earnings print.
- Portfolio discipline in passing: ALNY qualified but "I have too many bio-techs already" —
  sector concentration limits applied to his own buying.
