# WW 2025-05-05 — How he works an all-time-high list

## What it covers

**How he actually works an all-time-high list** — the procedure, not just the principle. "Lists of stocks
reaching an ATH often contain huge leaders. **It is worth researching their fundamentals and stories.** For
example, in 2008, after the horrendous market decline, **GMCR**, the makers of the Keurig coffee machines, rose
quickly to an ATH and **turned out to become a 13 bagger**." The mechanics: "the list below is **sorted by each
stock's close Friday / close 250 days ago**… all of these stocks are **up at least 50% from last year**, a good
sign of a potential leader. Thus, **RGC is 17.4 times its close a year ago**." Then two reads layered on top.
**Industry clustering:** "note also **the industries represented and any multiple stocks in the same industry.
Education & training, and insurance appear twice, a sign of group strength.**" **And the practical caveat:**
"now look for any GLBs and have fun studying them for a low risk setup. **Also beware of projected earnings
dates, listed in the table.**" On the signal itself, a note of restraint: the GMI at 4 "now meets my criteria for
a change to Green. **However, I will be much more confident of this change when the GMI=5 or 6, indicating there
are sufficient numbers of stocks at new highs.**"

## Key claims

- **The ATH list is sorted by one-year relative performance** (close / close 250 days ago), with a ≥50% floor.
- **Two stocks from the same industry on the list is read as group strength** — the sector signal falls out of the sort for free.
- **Earnings dates are checked before entry**, and carried in the table itself.
- GMCR after 2008 given as the archetype: a stock reaching an ATH straight out of a bear market became a 13-bagger.
- A GMI of 4 technically qualifies as Green but he wants 5 or 6 — because the higher readings mean *new highs are being made*, which is what the leadership screen needs.
- The list is a research starting point ("researching their fundamentals and stories"), not a buy list.
