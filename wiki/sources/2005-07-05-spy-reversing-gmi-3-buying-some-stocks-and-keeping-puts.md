# WW 2005-07-05 — Turning on a dime — a losing hedge closed and the book reversed the same day

**Source:** [SPY reversing?  GMI: +3;  Buying some stocks and keeping puts](../../raw/posts/2005-07-05-spy-reversing-gmi-3-buying-some-stocks-and-keeping-puts.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/07/spy-reversing-gmi-3-buying-some-stocks-and-keeping-puts/) · tier: trade_example

## What it covers

The don't-marry-a-scenario rule executed rather than stated. Holding SPY puts into a divided tape — his QQQQ
daily and weekly indicators negative, the SPY daily close to turning positive, and over 400 new highs, "the most
since I began this blog" — he reverses in one session: "**I sold out my puts on the SPY at a loss, and then I
immediately went long stocks. (I told you yesterday that if I find myself on the wrong side of the trend I have
no problem turning on a dime. It is suicide to marry a scenario that is at odds with the market's action.)**"
The new longs are bought with the protection attached — "I found so many rockets rising that I could not resist
nibbling at such powerful stocks as FTO, BBY, HITK, LCAV, WFMI, HANS and MW. **Of course, I put stops in on all
of them in case this rally fades**" — and the old thesis is not abandoned entirely: "I would still be much more
confident if the QQQQ and SPY were in synch, but sometimes different market sectors behave in an uncorrelated
fashion. **Just in case, I have kept my put options on some of the weakest stocks.**" The GMI stood at +3 and the
QQQQ was on D-7.

## Key claims

- **The reversal costs a realised loss and he takes it the same day** — "sold out my puts… at a loss, and then I immediately went long."
- "**It is suicide to marry a scenario that is at odds with the market's action**" — the rule stated at the moment it is being obeyed.
- **Reversing does not mean going flat on the other side**: new longs get stops, and puts stay on the weakest names as the residual hedge.
- A divided market (SPY turning up while QQQQ is still in a down-trend) is traded rather than resolved — he says he would prefer them in sync and acts anyway.
- Calibration datum: over 400 new highs, the most since the blog began in April 2005.
