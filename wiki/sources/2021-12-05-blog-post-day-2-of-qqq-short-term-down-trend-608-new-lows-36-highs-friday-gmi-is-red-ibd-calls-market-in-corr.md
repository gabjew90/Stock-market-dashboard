## What it covers

Written two weeks after the November 2021 top, and it names the pension trigger **prospectively** — the clearest
statement of which single condition he is waiting on: "The GMI is 1, of 6. **The last GMI component to turn
negative will be for the weekly QQQ to close below its 30 week average. When that happens I will begin to go to
cash in my primary pension accounts.**" The divergence he is reading: "the DIA is **already** below its 30 week
average. The SPY remains above it and the QQQ remains far above." And the onset rule stated generally: "**Once
these indexes close below their 30 week averages and the averages begin to curve down, it is usually the
BEGINNING of a major decline.**" The exit is planned into strength, as always: "**If we get a good year-end rally
I will likely sell some of my mutual funds in my university pension accounts.**" The macro judgement is unusually
flat: "With the Fed ready to tighten, this market's next big move next year will likely be down. **NEVER trade
against the Fed. Higher rates will suck the money out of equities.**" Other readings: 608 new lows to 36 new
highs; T2108 at 25%, "low, but not as low as is typical at a market bottom"; the GMI-2 *rising* from 2 to 4
purely because two daily stochastics (10.1 and 10.4) hit very oversold levels; a put/call of 1.00 — "**a ratio
above 1 usually leads to a bounce**"; IWM "entering Stage IV decline?"; and the re-entry rule restated —
"**plenty of time to be long when the GMI is Green again. That will require 2 consecutive days with the GMI above
3.**"

## Key claims

- **The pension trigger identified in advance with a specific component**: the weekly QQQ closing below its 30-week average, named as the last GMI component still positive.
- **"Once these indexes close below their 30 week averages and the averages begin to curve down, it is usually the BEGINNING of a major decline"** — the conjunction again, and the claim that it marks a start rather than a middle.
- **Sell into the year-end rally**, not into the decline — the same pacing as 2014 and 2025.
- "**NEVER trade against the Fed. Higher rates will suck the money out of equities**" — his flattest macro statement, and it is a stance rather than a signal.
- The GMI-2 can *rise* on oversold oscillator components while the market deteriorates — a construction artefact worth knowing when reading the pair.
- Put/call at 1.00 read as bounce territory — consistent with the 2012 statement of the threshold, against 1.2 in 2018.
- Index dispersion tracked one by one: DIA already under its 30-week, SPY above, QQQ far above, IWM possibly Stage 4.
