# WW 2018-08-05 — A green-dot scan over $70 — 'I have more success with expensive stocks'

**Source:** [16 Stocks with a Green Dot Bounce from Oversold](../../raw/posts/2018-08-05-16-stocks-with-a-green-dot-bounce-from-oversold.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2018/08/16-stocks-with-a-green-dot-bounce-from-oversold/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"I prefer to buy up-trending stocks that have bounced from oversold levels and have resumed their advance." The scan run that weekend: green-dot stocks **over $70** — "(**I have more success with expensive stocks**)" — that were recently very oversold; 16 hits. CRM is the worked case: a recent green dot, a bounce off the **rising 50-day average** *and* off the **lower Bollinger Band**, earnings on 8/28 flagged. The honest framing: "**Four prior green dot signals have worked for CRM but the signal can fail at any time.** A decline back below a green dot would be the spot for my stop loss or an exit point." And the disclaimer he attaches to every list: "I have not researched these stocks. This list is for educational purposes only and is provided to help others understand and evaluate this set-up."

## Key claims

- A $70 price floor on the green-dot scan — between the 2018 $100 preference and the 2021 $200 rule, and scan-specific.
- "I have more success with expensive stocks."
- Stop = a decline back below the green dot itself.
- Prior successes on the same stock are counted but explicitly do not guarantee the next one.
- Confluence: the green dot plus a rising-50-day bounce plus the lower Bollinger Band.
