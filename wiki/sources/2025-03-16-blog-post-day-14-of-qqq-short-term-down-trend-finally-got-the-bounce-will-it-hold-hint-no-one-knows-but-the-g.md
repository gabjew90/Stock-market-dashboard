## What it covers

With the GMI Red and 14 days into a down-trend: "very few stocks are reaching all-time highs, so the odds are against my profiting from buying such break-outs now… There were only 8 such US stocks, out of 6100, on Friday. **I always look at the list each day, however, because when the market does turn, new leaders appear among the stocks breaking out to ATHs.** That is how in 2009, after the market debacle, I found GMCR making an ATH, a green line break-out, GLB, which then became a 13 bagger." And on the media: "the current decline is small and yet the omniscient media cry out that a market crash has occurred. No one knows how long the current decline will last… I have to wait until Mr. Market signals the beginning of a new significant up-trend."

## Key claims

- The daily new-ATH list is read *especially* in declines — that is where the next cycle's leaders first appear.
- GMCR from the 2009 debacle cited as a 13-bagger (the same trade he elsewhere measures as ~900% to its 2011 peak).
- 8 of 6,100 at an ATH as a quantitative read of how hostile the tape is.
