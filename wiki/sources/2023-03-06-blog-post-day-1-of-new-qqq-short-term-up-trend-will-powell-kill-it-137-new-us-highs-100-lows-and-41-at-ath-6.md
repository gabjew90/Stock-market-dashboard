## What it covers

The compact spec: "**Green bar scan = ATH in past 3 months, rising pattern of 4wk>10wk>30wk, currently bouncing rising 4wk avg and RS at 20 week high.**" Results are sorted by close ÷ minimum low of the last 250 days (VIST and INTA had tripled from their yearly lows), and all the hits were already on his IBD/MarketSmith watchlist.

## Key claims

- Green bar scan in four conditions: ATH within 3 months; 4wk > 10wk > 30wk rising; bouncing off the rising 4-week; relative strength at a 20-week high.
- Ranked by close ÷ 250-day minimum low.
- Run against the IBD/MarketSmith watchlist rather than the whole market.
