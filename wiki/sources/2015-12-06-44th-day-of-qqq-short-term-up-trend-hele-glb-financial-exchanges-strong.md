# WW 2015-12-06 — Bouncing off the green line — the retest entry, risk quantified

**Source:** [44th day of $QQQ short term up-trend; $HELE–GLB; Financial exchanges strong](../../raw/posts/2015-12-06-44th-day-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-hele-glb-financial-exchanges-strong.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2015/12/44th-day-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-hele-glb-financial-exchanges-strong/) · tier: trade_example

## What it covers

The entry the written GLB rules mention in half a clause — "or wait for a re-test" — worked out with numbers.
HELE "gapped up and broke above its green line top to an all-time high **on huge volume** on October 9. It has
retested its GLB several times since… Last week, HELE again **bounced off of its green line top** and *may* be
getting ready to begin a new advance. **The way I would play such a pattern would be to buy it and place a sell
stop around \$99.90. If I am wrong I am risking about a 4-5% loss. If I am right, I might catch a ride on a
rocket!**" So the retest entry is: buy the bounce off the green line, stop just beneath it, risk sized at 4–5%
— the green line doing double duty as support and as the invalidation level. **The scan that found it**: a
TC2000 rocket scan that "identified **15 stocks out of about 5500** that had my desired pattern of a rocket stock
(RWB) and bouncing off of support," with the fourteen others listed and the caveat attached ("these need to be
researched to see if any are worthy of purchase"). **And an unusual application of it**: "just for fun, I ran my
scan for RWB stocks bouncing off of support focusing only on **industry indexes**. I found that only one
industry, financial exchanges, came up!" — the stock scan repurposed as a sector screen, returning one hit out of
the whole industry list. He reads the monthly GMMA of that index as RWB for nearly three years and the weekly as
emerging from a year-long consolidation, then reasons about *why* (rising rates and volatile commodities meaning
more exchange volume and margin interest). One dating note: "**I am just discovering the benefit of a *monthly*
GMMA chart that shows the longer term trends**" — although he had published monthly Guppy charts of SPY's 2000
and 2007 tops eighteen months earlier.

## Key claims

- **The green-line retest entry, quantified**: buy the bounce off the green line with a stop just below it, risking 4–5%. The line is support and invalidation at once.
- A GLB that gaps up on huge volume and then retests its line several times is treated as a *feature* — each retest is a fresh entry with a tight stop.
- **Scan yield stated**: 15 names from ~5,500 for RWB-plus-bouncing-off-support.
- **The scan run over industry indexes instead of stocks** turns a stock screen into a sector screen — one industry qualified.
- Fundamental reasoning is used to explain a technical hit, after the fact and explicitly as speculation.
- He dates his own adoption of the monthly GMMA to December 2015, which sits oddly against his June 2014 monthly-Guppy top study — worth reading as when it entered *routine* use.
