# WW 2022-08-01 — MACD hourly histograms reveal weakening in QQQ, SPY, DIA

**Source:** [Blog Post: Day 11 of $QQQ short term up-trend; MACD hourly histograms reveal weakening in $QQQ, $SPY and $DIA](../../raw/posts/2022-08-01-blog-post-day-11-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-macd-hourly-histograms-reveal-weakening-in-qqq-spy-and-dia.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2022/08/blog-post-day-11-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-macd-hourly-histograms-reveal-weakening-in-qqq-spy-and-dia/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The hourly 12/26/9 MACD histogram as a very-short-term momentum read: when the bars decline and turn red while price is still climbing, that negative divergence "can signal weakness"; rising red bars had foreshadowed the prior bounce. The same divergence was visible in SPY and DIA. Explicitly a small-move indicator layered under the daily count (Day 11 of the QQQ up-trend).

## Key claims

- "One of the ways I use the 12/26/9 MACD indicator is to focus on the hourly histograms… When the histograms decline and turn RED, it can signal weakness."
- "The hourly histogram is a very short term indicator and signals small moves."
- Negative divergence — momentum weakening as price climbs — as the read; rising bars foreshadow bounces.
