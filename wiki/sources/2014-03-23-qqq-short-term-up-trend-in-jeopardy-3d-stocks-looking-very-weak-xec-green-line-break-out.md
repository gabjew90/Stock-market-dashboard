# WW 2014-03-23 — The down-trend duration study, with its n and its definition

**Source:** [$QQQ short term up-trend in jeopardy; 3D stocks looking  weak; $XEC, $NGVC, $ASGN–green line break-out](../../raw/posts/2014-03-23-qqq-short-term-up-trend-in-jeopardy-3d-stocks-looking-very-weak-xec-green-line-break-out.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2014/03/qqq-short-term-up-trend-in-jeopardy-3d-stocks-looking-very-weak-xec-green-line-break-out/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The down-trend duration study with its sample size and a definitional caveat**: "**41% of the 51 down-trends
between 2006 and December, 2013 (as I define them) lasted 5 days or less.**" The parenthesis matters — the count
depends on his own trend definition, so the base rate is conditional on it and not transferable to another
timing rule. The operational consequence: "**if a short term down-trend begins, I will be more sure of it once it
passes day 5 and add more SQQQ then.**" He was holding **both sides at once** while the trend was ambiguous — "a
small position in SQQQ and a somewhat larger position in TQQQ. I will adjust these holdings when the short term
trend becomes clearer to me" — with window dressing expected to distort the week. **A sector top diagnosed**: the
3D printing stocks (XONE, ONVO, DDD, SSYS) after "a huge bull market run," with SSYS showing a weekly
head-and-shoulders, "**large red volume spikes, suggesting selling by mutual funds**," a penetrated neckline, and
"another danger sign… **the 30 week average is now starting to level off**." His summary: "**even 3D stocks do
not advance forever.**" And the strength screen restated: "**any stock that can sprint to an all-time high in a
weak market and hold it is worth watching**" — XEC, NGVC (a 2012 IPO) and ASGN (breaking its 2000 peak), each
quoted with its IBD composite rating (94, 90, 95).

## Key claims

- **41% of 51 down-trends (2006–2013) lasted 5 days or less** — the study behind the Day-5 rule, with n stated.
- **"As I define them"** — the base rate is conditional on his own trend definition and does not transfer to another one.
- **Both leveraged ETFs held simultaneously** during an ambiguous trend, sized by conviction rather than picked.
- **Volume colour read as institutional behaviour**: large red weekly spikes as fund selling, alongside the pattern.
- A flattening 30-week average listed as a *separate* danger sign from the head-and-shoulders — pattern and stage read together.
- IBD composite ratings quoted as the fundamental gate on GLB candidates (94, 90, 95).
