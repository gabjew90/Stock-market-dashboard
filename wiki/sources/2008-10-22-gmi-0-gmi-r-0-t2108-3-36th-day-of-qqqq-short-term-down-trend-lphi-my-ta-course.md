# WW 2008-10-22 — Down-trend duration as a base rate; hunting rockets inside the crash

**Source:** [GMI: 0; GMI-R: 0; T2108: 3%; 36th day of QQQQ short term down-trend; LPHI; My TA course](../../raw/posts/2008-10-22-gmi-0-gmi-r-0-t2108-3-36th-day-of-qqqq-short-term-down-trend-lphi-my-ta-course.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2008/10/gmi-0-gmi-r-0-t2108-3-36th-day-of-qqqq-short-term-down-trend-lphi-my-ta-course/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Mid-crash working method: how he estimates *how much longer* a down-trend might run, and what he
does with the watch list while waiting. The earliest clean example of the duration-base-rate
reasoning that later becomes the published QQQ trend-duration statistics.

## Key claims

- **Duration as a base rate, computed live:** Day 36 of the down-trend. "The short term
  down-trend in the QQQQ that ended on March 24, lasted **55 days**, and the one that ended on
  August 5, lasted **39 days**. So, **we may have a ways to go** before we get a reversal." The
  year's own prior down-trends are the reference class.
- **A rally inside a bear is still tradeable:** "Such a reversal would occur within a longer
  term down-trend but **could still be a trade-able rally**." The short/long distinction doing
  work mid-crisis.
- **The bear-market watch list doctrine, applied:** "I remain in cash and a little short **and
  keep a watch list of promising stocks to buy when the market turns**." LPHI: new high on
  Tuesday, within 5% of its ATH, IBD100, "PE below its earnings growth rate" — with a
  pre-planned trigger ("if it closes above its peak price of 42.32"). **"Stocks that can survive
  this market near their all-time highs are potential rockets."**
- Breadth on the day: 6 new highs, 109 new lows; T2108 at 3%; GMI and GMI-R at zero.
