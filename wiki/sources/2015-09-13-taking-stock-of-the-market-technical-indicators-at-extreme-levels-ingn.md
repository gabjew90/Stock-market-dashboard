# WW 2015-09-13 — Taking stock: technical indicators at extreme levels; INGN

**Source:** [Taking stock of the market–  technical indicators at extreme levels; $INGN](../../raw/posts/2015-09-13-taking-stock-of-the-market-technical-indicators-at-extreme-levels-ingn.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2015/09/taking-stock-of-the-market-technical-indicators-at-extreme-levels-ingn/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Three contrarian indicators say a bottom may be in — the Investors Intelligence poll shows more bears than bulls ("a very rare occurrence"), the put/call ratio hit 1.42 in August, and T2108 went to single digits ("back up the truck and buy a large market index ETF like SPY… psychologically very difficult") — while the weekly GMMA of DIA is at the *beginning* of a bearish BWR pattern, the pattern on which he tends to exit, and "one never knows in advance when a market decline, once started, will end." Meanwhile he uses the weak tape to find leaders: only 37 new highs on Friday, 17 kept on a watchlist, most above a green-line top; that is how he found GMCR in early 2009, a 10-bagger. INGN, flagged in December 2014 at $30.25, is at $54.58.

## Key claims

- Contrarian trio at extremes: II bears > bulls; put/call 1.42; T2108 single digits.
- "I love weak markets when few stocks hit new yearly highs. It is easier to see the leaders. A stock that can come through a large decline at new highs is evidencing unusual buying interest."
- "I, of course, will not buy anything until I see a new market up-trend and have the wind at my back."
- Weekly BWR just beginning in DIA — "I tend to exit the market when this bearish pattern occurs in the major averages"; the 2011-vs-2008 duration question is unanswerable in advance.
