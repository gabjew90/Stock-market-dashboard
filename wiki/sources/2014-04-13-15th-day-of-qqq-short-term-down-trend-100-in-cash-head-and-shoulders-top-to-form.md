# WW 2014-04-13 — QQQ closes the week below its 30-week; 100% cash in trading; the pension waits

**Source:** [15th day of $QQQ short term down-trend; 100% in cash; head and shoulders top to form???](../../raw/posts/2014-04-13-15th-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-100-in-cash-head-and-shoulders-top-to-form.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2014/04/15th-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-100-in-cash-head-and-shoulders-top-to-form/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

First weekly close under the 30-week since December 2012 — "if the QQQ remains below its 30 week average so that the average itself turns down, it will signal to me the beginning of the end of the Stage 2 up-trend and possibly lead to a Stage 4 market decline… A trend follower must wait for the top to be in and for a significant decline to begin. I cannot therefore avoid giving back some gains." Four prior multi-week dips below the 30-week since 2009 all resolved upward. GMI on a Sell, D-15 → all funds out of the speculative accounts; the 401(k) stays 100% in funds "because of strict trading limits imposed by the fund group" — he would move some on a rally to ~89 (a possible head-and-shoulders right shoulder). Put/call 1.15 ("at least 1.2 usually indicates extreme bearishness"); daily indicators oversold but T2108 only 41% and the weekly stochastic far from oversold — a bounce would not hold. SPY finally under its 10-week; QQQ leading down.

## Key claims

- Weekly close under the 30-week = watch; the average turning down = Stage 2 ending / Stage 4 possible.
- Trend followers give back some gains by construction.
- Pension movement paced by fund limits; a rally into resistance as the chance to lighten.
- Put/call 1.2 threshold; T2108 41% and weekly stochastic not oversold → bounce won't hold.
