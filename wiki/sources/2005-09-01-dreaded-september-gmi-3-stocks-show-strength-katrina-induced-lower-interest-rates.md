# WW 2005-09-01 — The chart as a check on the news story; 'the idea is to make $$, not to be right'

**Source:** [Dreaded September?  GMI: +3;  Stocks show strength; Katrina-induced lower interest rates?](../../raw/posts/2005-09-01-dreaded-september-gmi-3-stocks-show-strength-katrina-induced-lower-interest-rates.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/09/dreaded-september-gmi-3-stocks-show-strength-katrina-induced-lower-interest-rates/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**A chart used to refute a causal story** — the best example of it in the corpus. The pundits attributed the
bond rally to Hurricane Katrina; he checked the dates: "the following chart of an ETF (**TLT**) that tracks the
20 year bonds shows that **long term bonds were rising, and rates were falling, since August 9, long before the
hurricane. Unless we believe that prescient traders foresaw the hurricane debacle weeks before it hit, we must
conclude that the economy was weakening and rates were falling long before the hurricane, and that Katrina's
damage merely intensified the pre-existing trend.** ***Identical*** patterns can be found in ETF's that track
shorter term bonds (SHY and IEF)." The argument is entirely about *when* the move started, and the confirmation
across three maturities is what rules out coincidence. **The bad-news tell as doctrine**: "**Declines do not
begin when the news headlines shriek of calamity, as they do now. Did you notice that the market is holding in
the face of all of this bad news? I think that means the selling is over, for now.**" **And the flattest
statement of ego-freedom he has written**: "**I am not embarrassed to change from bearish to cautiously bullish.
Each day provides new data, and when the indicators change, I must turn on a dime — no pride is involved. The
idea is to make \$\$, not to be right. A big ego is lethal in this business — in all business.**" He sold his
puts and prepared to buy, but staged it: "I will only begin to wade in and **will wait for the GMI to give a
definite buy signal**." Also: the QQQ and SPY back above their 10-day averages read as "**the first sign of a
possible reversal**."

## Key claims

- **TLT is on his charts from September 2005** — a decade before the 2016 post that names it as his long-rate instrument.
- **A price chart can falsify a causal narrative**: if the move began before the cause, the cause is not the cause. Corroborated across SHY, IEF and TLT.
- **"Declines do not begin when the news headlines shriek of calamity"** — the market's non-reaction to bad news is the signal.
- "**The idea is to make \$\$, not to be right. A big ego is lethal in this business**" — the reversal is framed as data arriving, not as a verdict on the prior view.
- Reversing the *view* and reversing the *position* are separated: puts sold now, buying staged and gated on a definite GMI signal.
- The 10-day average as the earliest reversal marker, consistent with its yellow-light role in the other direction.
