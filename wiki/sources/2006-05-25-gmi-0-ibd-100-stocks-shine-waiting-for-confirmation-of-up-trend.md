# WW 2006-05-25 — Reader mail as a sentiment sample

**Source:** [GMI: 0; IBD 100 stocks shine; Waiting for confirmation of up-trend](../../raw/posts/2006-05-25-gmi-0-ibd-100-stocks-shine-waiting-for-confirmation-of-up-trend.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2006/05/gmi-0-ibd-100-stocks-shine-waiting-for-confirmation-of-up-trend/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**His own readership used as a sentiment sample** — an instrument that appears nowhere else. Declining to add
to his puts on a bounce day: "I am reluctant to buy more puts now. This is because the **put/call ratios *and
letters from readers* suggest a lot of retail interest in put options. When that happens we usually get at least
a bounce — the market does not usually accommodate the bets of option players.**" The reader mail is treated as
the same kind of evidence as the ratio: a direct read on what retail is doing, available to him because he
publishes. **Component 6 in the negative, in its most memorable phrasing**: with the IBD growth mutual fund index
below its 50-day average, "**when these super managers can't make money in growth stocks, neither can I.**"
**Both mirror statistics on the same day**: only 9% of the 209 stocks that hit a new high ten days earlier closed
higher — "buying stocks at new highs has, thus, been a losing proposition" — while "shorting stocks at new lows
10 days ago would have been profitable in **58%** of the cases." And the scan as a one-number summary: 160
submarines to 10 rockets, with only 8% of the universe in a short-term up-trend. The IBD 100 detail worth
keeping: 95% of the 5/15 list advanced on the bounce after having been "among those most battered during this
decline" — the same beaten-down-then-snaps-back pattern as the group's behaviour throughout.

## Key claims

- **Reader correspondence as a retail-sentiment gauge**, used alongside the put/call ratio and read the same way.
- "**When these super managers can't make money in growth stocks, neither can I**" — component 6's clearest negative statement.
- **Both component-1 forms quoted together** (9% successful new highs against 58% successful new lows) — the clearest single-day demonstration of which side the tape favoured.
- A bounce with 74–90% of index components advancing is explicitly *not* treated as a trend change: "it will take a few more days… whether this is a dead cat bounce."
- The rocket-to-submarine ratio (10 vs 160) as the one-line summary of the tape.
