# WW 2023-01-16 — How I trade a GLB: the close-below rule shown intraday

**Source:** [GMI turns GREEN and Day 2 of $QQQ short term up-trend; How I trade a GLB (green line breakout), 2 examples](../../raw/posts/2023-01-16-blog-post-gmi-turns-green-and-day-2-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-how-i-trade-a-glb-green-line-breakout-2-exampl.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2023/01/blog-post-gmi-turns-green-and-day-2-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-how-i-trade-a-glb-green-line-breakout-2-exampl/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A compact GLB trading tutorial written on the day the GMI turned Green, whose ACLX example
shows *why* the close-below rule exists — the stock violated the line intraday the day after
its GLB and recovered by the close. Also states, six months before the CAVA doctrine post,
that the OSB is a built-in alternative entry to the GLB day.

## Key claims

- **The rule, with the case that justifies it:** ACLX's GLB was December 9 (green line $26.91,
  gap + huge volume). "The next day it **traded below the green line but closed the day above
  it**. That is why I sell a GLB only if the stock ***closes*** a day back below the green
  line." An intraday violation is noise; the close is the signal.
- **Buying the GLB day is optional, and the alternative is named:** "one does not have to buy
  the stock on the day of the GLB. One alternative is to buy it after the GLB **when it becomes
  oversold**... The OSB is signified by the black dot. If one bought on the day of the bounce
  s/he could place a stop loss order **right below the low of the bounce day**." The GLB+OSB
  pairing taught as standard practice in January 2023.
- **Staged confidence on the market side:** GMI Green on its second day above 3 → "I have
  started to accumulate some TQQQ in my trading IRA" — but "I am much more confident of the
  market's new up-trend **when the GMI=6**." Position size scales with the score, not just the
  signal.
- **A contrarian media read:** "Now that the media pundits are talking up dividend stocks, it
  may be time to go back into growth stocks?"
- The GLB restated with the Darvas lineage: green line at the highest monthly bar ever, after
  ≥3 months (3 bars) unexceeded.
