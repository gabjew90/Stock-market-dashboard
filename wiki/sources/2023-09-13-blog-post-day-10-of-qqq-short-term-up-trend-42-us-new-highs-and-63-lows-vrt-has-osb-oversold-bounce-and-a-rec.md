## What it covers

The cleanest worked example of the **GLB + OSB pairing**: the green line breakout identifies the stock, the oversold bounce times the entry. VRT is traced from its August breakout through its September pullback and black dot, with the monthly and daily charts doing separate jobs.

## Key claims

- The sequence: VRT "had a green line break-out to an ATH on August 2nd with volume way above average, after reporting earnings"; then, on 2023-09-13, "VRT bounced from oversold" — the black dot.
- The stop is again the bounce-day low, and interest is conditional on it holding: "If it holds Wednesday's low of 36.22 I would become interested."
- The candidate arrived via the scan, not by watching: "VRT came up on my oversold-bounce scan."
- Fundamentals checked after the technical trigger: 2023 earnings expected +203%, composite rating 98, RS 99.
- Two timeframes, two jobs — the monthly chart carries the green line, the daily chart carries the dot.
