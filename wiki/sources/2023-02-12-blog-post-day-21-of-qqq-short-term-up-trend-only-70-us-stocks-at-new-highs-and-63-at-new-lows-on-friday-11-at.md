## What it covers

"One of my favorite scans I created with TC2000 searches for a stock **in a strong up-trend, at a 20 week high in relative strength vs. SPY, that reached an ATH in the past 3 months and that has bounced up off of its rising 4 week average.**" The stop travels with it: "if I buy one of these stocks I place my sell stop at the bottom of the weekly green bar." 29 of nearly 7,000 stocks passed; he sorts by close ÷ 250-day low (`c/minl250`) and takes the top 20 — seven had at least doubled off their low. ACLS is the worked case, having held above its rising 4-week for five weeks. He colours the weekly bar green for *any* stock bouncing off its 4-week average, whether or not it passes the rest of the scan.

## Key claims

- Scan: strong up-trend + weekly RS vs SPY at a 20-week high + ATH within 3 months + bounce off the rising 4-week average.
- Stop = the low of the weekly green bar.
- Ranking by close ÷ 250-day low, as with the OSB scan.
- The green weekly bar is a chart annotation (bounce off the 4-week), broader than the scan itself.
