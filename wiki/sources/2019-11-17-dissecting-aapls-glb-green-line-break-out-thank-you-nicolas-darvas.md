# WW 2019-11-17 — The GLB worked end to end on AAPL, with dates

**Source:** [Dissecting $AAPL’s GLB (Green line break-out); thank you Nicolas Darvas](../../raw/posts/2019-11-17-dissecting-aapls-glb-green-line-break-out-thank-you-nicolas-darvas.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2019/11/dissecting-aapls-glb-green-line-break-out-thank-you-nicolas-darvas/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The cleanest full-cycle GLB in the corpus, every step dated.** "AAPL hit an all-time high of **\$233.47 on
October 3, 2018**. **After that high was not exceeded for 3 months, I drew in a green line**, designating a green
line top. **I could then set an alert on TC2000 to alert me when AAPL traded above the green line. It had a green
line break-out (GLB) one year later on October 11, 2019. Note the above average trading volume that day
confirming the break-out. AAPL has now advanced +12.5% since the GLB.**" Then the behaviour that trips people up,
named as normal: "**after the GLB, AAPL re-tested the green line for a few days, a common occurrence. If it had
CLOSED back below the green line, I would have sold my position.**" So the sequence is: high → three months
unexceeded → line drawn → alert set → breakout on volume → retest expected → close below invalidates. Every
element of the method documented separately across this wiki appears here in one stock, in order, with a
thirteen-month wait between the line being drawn and the signal firing.

## Key claims

- **The full sequence with dates**: ATH 2018-10-03, three months unexceeded, line drawn, TC2000 alert set, GLB 2019-10-11 on above-average volume.
- **Thirteen months between drawing the line and the signal** — the watchlist is a long-lived object, not a weekly refresh.
- **A retest of the line after the breakout is "a common occurrence"** and is not a failure; only a *close* below invalidates.
- The title credits Darvas — the whole construct is presented as an implementation of his idea.
- +12.5% from the GLB at time of writing, on the largest company in the index.
