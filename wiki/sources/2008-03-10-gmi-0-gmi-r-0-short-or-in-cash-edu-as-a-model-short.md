# WW 2008-03-10 — The submarine trade fully specified; T2108's bottom band calibrated

**Source:** [GMI: 0; GMI-R: 0; Short or in cash; EDU as a model short](../../raw/posts/2008-03-10-gmi-0-gmi-r-0-short-or-in-cash-edu-as-a-model-short.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2008/03/gmi-0-gmi-r-0-short-or-in-cash-edu-as-a-model-short/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**A short trade specified end to end, and T2108's bottom band calibrated against history.** The setup: EDU had
"tripled, formed a top and then **broke down on huge volume** (when the company talked of tougher earnings
comparisons in future quarters). After breaking below its **30 week average**, EDU rebounded and **failed last
week to hold that line**. Furthermore, its **10 week average is now below its 30 week average**." The trade and
its invalidation: "this is the type of stock I might short by **buying a put in my IRA**. **I would close the
trade with a loss if the stock trades again above last week's high of 67.40.**" A price-to-sales ratio over 13 is
noted as the fundamental half. **The bands**: T2108 "now at 24%, **still above the extreme level at which the
market tends to bottom. At the January bottom, it registered 18%. At the bottom in 1987, it registered an amazing
less than 1%. Most recent bottoms/bounces occur in the area around 14%.**" **And the lesson drawn from the
decline's own arithmetic**: since the down-trend began on 2 January the QQQQ had fallen 16.6% "and **80% of its
component stocks have declined — 30% have fallen 20% or more**… **The lesson to be learned is that one should not
hold stocks in a general market down-trend. Why not be in cash or short in a market decline?**" The tape summary:
55 rockets against over 500 submarines — "**the odds of success favor going short on a submarine like this rather
than betting on the rare rocket on the long side. Alternatively, I could wait safely on the sideline, in cash.**" 

## Key claims

- **A failed retest of the 30-week average from below is the short entry** — the rebound that cannot reclaim the line, with the 10-week already beneath it.
- **The short's stop is a price, stated at entry**: a trade back above the prior week's high closes it for a loss.
- **T2108's bottom band calibrated by instance**: ~14% for most recent bottoms, 18% at the January 2008 low, under 1% in 1987 — a range built from cases, not a rule.
- The gate argued from participation again: 80% of components down, 30% of them by 20%+, over one 16.6% index decline.
- **Rockets vs submarines as the one-number tape summary**: 55 against 500+.
- Cash is offered as the equal alternative to the short, in the same sentence.
