# WW 2022-01-09 — Approaching the 30-week — 'a weekly close below that level would get me out of everything'

**Source:** [Blog post: The weekly charts of $QQQ, $DIA and $SPY show each having three heavy volume down weeks, suggesting distribution; And the GMI could turn Red by Monday’s close; These greatest traders “sell too soon.”](../../raw/posts/2022-01-09-blog-post-the-weekly-charts-of-qqq-dia-and-spy-show-each-having-three-heavy-volume-down-weeks-suggesting-dist.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2022/01/blog-post-the-weekly-charts-of-qqq-dia-and-spy-show-each-having-three-heavy-volume-down-weeks-suggesting-distribution-and-the-gmi-could-turn-red-by-mondays-close-these-greatest-traders-sell/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The January 2022 state of his accounts, and the traders he quotes for company. QQQ approaching its 30-week at 375.59: "**a weekly close below that level would get me out of everything, even in my university pension, which is already reduced to about 20% invested in mutual funds.**" The corroborating evidence: "the three huge volume down weeks which I have annotated in all three major indexes suggest to me that **institutions are hitting the exits**." And the track record of the rule: "**penetration of the 30 week average saved me from the market debacles in 2000, 2008 and 2020.**" On selling early, three quotations in a row — Rothschild: "I never buy at the bottom and I always sell too soon"; Baruch: "I made my money by *selling too soon*"; Livermore: "Never buy at the bottom, and *always sell too soon*." "So I guess I am in good company… And I may be selling too soon……"

## Key claims

- **Pension already down to ~20% invested by 2022-01-09** — corroborating the November 2021 early exit.
- "Penetration of the 30 week average saved me from the market debacles in 2000, 2008 and 2020."
- Three huge-volume down weeks across all three indexes read as institutional distribution.
- Selling too soon defended with Rothschild, Baruch and Livermore — a deliberate bias, not an error.
- "I know I am more of a chicken now because I need to conserve my funds for retirement" — age stated as the reason.
