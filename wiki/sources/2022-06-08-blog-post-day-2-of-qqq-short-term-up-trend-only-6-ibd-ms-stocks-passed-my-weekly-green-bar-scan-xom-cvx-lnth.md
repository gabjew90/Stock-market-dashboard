# WW 2022-06-08 — The weekly green bar scan, every condition listed

**Source:** [Blog Post: Day 2 of $QQQ short term up-trend; Only 6 IBD/MS stocks passed my weekly green bar scan: $XOM, $CVX, $LNTH, $AZPN, $AIR, $CEIX, see charts](../../raw/posts/2022-06-08-blog-post-day-2-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-only-6-ibd-ms-stocks-passed-my-weekly-green-bar-scan-xom-cvx-lnth.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2022/06/blog-post-day-2-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-only-6-ibd-ms-stocks-passed-my-weekly-green-bar-scan-xom-cvx-lnth-azpn-air-ceix-see-charts/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The scan in full, with its yield.** "My weekly green bar scan finds stocks that have **recently reached an
ATH**, have **4wk > 10wk > 30wk averages** and **4 wk avg is rising** and **stock bounced up off of the 4 wk avg
this week** and **closed up today**. **Their relative strength vs SPY also must be at a 20 week high. And they
must be > \$30.** I ran this scan on my watchlist of stocks recently listed on IBD50 or MS growth250 lists. **I
found only 6 stocks out of 782 passed the screen.**" Seven conditions, and two of them are worth separating from
the pattern itself: **relative strength against SPY at a 20-week high** — the stock must be outperforming the
market, not merely rising — and a **\$30 price floor**, a softer version of the price-level preference. The
output is ranked "by close today / close 250 days ago," the same one-year relative sort he uses on ATH lists, and
two names had tripled. A yield of 6 from 782 on day 2 of a new up-trend is itself the market reading.

## Key claims

- **Seven conditions**: recent ATH, 4wk>10wk>30wk, rising 4-week, a bounce off it this week, an up close, RS vs SPY at a 20-week high, and price > $30.
- **Relative strength against SPY is a separate requirement** from the price pattern — the stock must beat the market, not just rise.
- Run over a curated IBD50 / MarketSmith Growth 250 watchlist rather than the full universe.
- **Yield: 6 of 782** on day 2 of a new up-trend — the count doubles as a breadth reading.
- Output ranked by one-year relative performance, the same sort as the ATH lists.
