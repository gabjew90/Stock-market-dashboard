## What it covers

The earliest post in the corpus to use "OSB" as a named setup in the title. Dr. Wish flags four strong stocks having an oversold bounce and states the two filters and the exit rule in one sentence each. Notable for the market context: this was Day 2 of a QQQ short-term down-trend, yet 378 US stocks hit new highs — his point being that non-QQQ-type stocks were still rising, so the setup was still worth scanning for.

## Key claims

- The candidates are "above their green line tops and near their ATHs" — the recent-ATH prerequisite and the overhead-supply filter, both present from the start.
- "If I buy an OSB stock I sell immediately if the bounce fails." The failure rule is stated as absolute and immediate.
- OSB candidates are published even during a QQQ short-term down-trend, when breadth outside the Nasdaq 100 is holding up.
