# WW 2014-03-30 — The 3:45 PM rule, six months earlier than recorded; a 52-week-high + earnings scan

**Source:** [5th day of $QQQ short term down-trend; $FANG shows strength](../../raw/posts/2014-03-30-5th-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-fang-shows-strength.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2014/03/5th-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-fang-shows-strength/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"Monday is a critical day for the market. The QQQ is over-sold and it will either bounce or begin a significant decline. **The key is to wait for the end of the trading day, around 3:45 PM, to see where the market will settle**" — the intraday-patience rule, appearing here in **March 2014**, six months before the September 2014 post the wiki had as its origin. The scan run that weekend: stocks at a 52-week high on Friday **with good recent quarterly earnings** — only 17 came up, and "almost one half" were energy, three of them (EOG, WGP, FANG) from the IBD 50 or New America. FANG is the pick: a 30% rise in February, a five-week consolidation, a fresh breakout, and "expanding Bollinger bands." But the gate holds: "with the GMI on a recent Sell signal, **I will keep my powder dry and wait for a change in trend to go long again.**" QQQ back below its 10-week while SPY holds above — "this decline has targeted biotech and growth stocks."

## Key claims

- **The 3:45 PM rule appears in March 2014**, predating the September 2014 statement the wiki recorded as its origin.
- Scan: 52-week high + good recent quarterly earnings; 17 hits, half of them energy.
- Expanding Bollinger bands after a consolidation as a breakout confirmation.
- Sector concentration among scan hits read as information.
- A named candidate is still not bought while the GMI is on a Sell — "keep my powder dry."
