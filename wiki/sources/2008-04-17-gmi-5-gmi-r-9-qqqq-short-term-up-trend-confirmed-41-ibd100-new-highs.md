# WW 2008-04-17 — The lesson relearned — hedging through an up-trend cost him

**Source:** [GMI: 5; GMI-R: 9; QQQQ short term up-trend confirmed!; 41 IBD100 new highs](../../raw/posts/2008-04-17-gmi-5-gmi-r-9-qqqq-short-term-up-trend-confirmed-41-ibd100-new-highs.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2008/04/gmi-5-gmi-r-9-qqqq-short-term-up-trend-confirmed-41-ibd100-new-highs/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A self-audit after a successful test of the up-trend (94% of Nasdaq-100 stocks up; more than 100 new highs for the first time since February 26; the GMI above 4 for the first time since December 26). "**Again, I had to learn the lesson not to *anticipate* a change in trend and kept a lot of my longs through today, although I was hedged and made less money than if I had just stayed 100% long with the up-trend.**" The conclusion: "it is now time for me to accumulate QLD (the Ultra long QQQQ ETF) and other strong IBD 100 stocks." 41 of the 523 stocks across a year of IBD 100 lists made new highs that day.

## Key claims

- Hedging inside an intact up-trend is itself a form of anticipating the turn — and it cost him.
- The lesson is marked as *relearned* ("again"), not newly discovered.
- GMI back above 4 → accumulate the ultra long ETF and strong IBD 100 stocks.
- The QQQQ "never violated its short term up-trend" despite testing it — tests are not breaks.
