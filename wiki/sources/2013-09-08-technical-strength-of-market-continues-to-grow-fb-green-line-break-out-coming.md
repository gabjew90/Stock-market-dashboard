# WW 2013-09-08 — Livermore's 1940 pivotal-point rule as the green line's ancestor; FB at $45

**Source:** [Technical strength of market continues to grow; FB green line break-out coming?](../../raw/posts/2013-09-08-technical-strength-of-market-continues-to-grow-fb-green-line-break-out-coming.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2013/09/technical-strength-of-market-continues-to-grow-fb-green-line-break-out-coming/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"The market is usually right, opinions are often wrong." Then the lineage: Livermore, *How to Trade in Stocks* (1940) — for a stock listed in the last two or three years whose high was made two or three years ago, "usually it is a safe play to buy the minute it touches a brand new high"; wait for the break, do not anticipate it; if the stock does not act well after the new high, get out. "Livermore was describing my concept of a green line break-out." FB: public a year, capped at $45 for many months, now $44.61 on volume — "if FB can *close* above $45, I will begin to accumulate and hold it as long as it stays above $45." Why it works: 2012 buyers at $40 who rode it under $20 "have been waiting to sell so as to get their money back"; clearing that supply "reflects a huge amount of demand." A failed GLB "is a sign to get out quickly" — AAPL, October 2012. GMI2 all eight positive; U-2; T2108 back to 33% from 23%.

## Key claims

- Livermore 1940 pivotal points = the green line, and the IPO GLB, in his own attribution.
- Entry on a *close* above the line; hold while above; failure = out quickly (AAPL 10/2012).
- Overhead supply mechanism spelled out on FB.
- "The market is usually right, opinions are often wrong."
