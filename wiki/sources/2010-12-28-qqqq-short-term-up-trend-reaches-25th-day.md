# WW 2010-12-28 — Investors Intelligence at both poles, anchored to his own re-entry

**Source:** [QQQQ short term up-trend reaches 25th day; RWB stock ODFL](../../raw/posts/2010-12-28-qqqq-short-term-up-trend-reaches-25th-day.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2010/12/qqqq-short-term-up-trend-reaches-25th-day/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The sentiment gauge with both ends and a personal reference point.** "The Investors Intelligence poll now
shows **58.8% bulls and only 20.6% bears. Readings close to 60% bullish often occur near market tops. When I got
back into the market last September, there were less than 30% bulls, and this statistic made me confident of my
wading back in on the long side.** So, we are probably close to at least a minor top." The top threshold (≈60%
bulls) is the one documented elsewhere; what this adds is the **bottom** anchor — under 30% bulls — and the fact
that he used it prospectively to gain confidence in a re-entry three months earlier rather than only reading it
in hindsight. The discipline attached is the standing one: "**the key is to wait for a definitive signal.**" So
the sentiment reading raises or lowers confidence in a position the trend indicators still have to authorise —
which is exactly how every contrarian gauge on this wiki is supposed to work.

## Key claims

- **Both poles with numbers**: ≈60% bulls near tops, under 30% bulls at the September 2010 bottom.
- **He used the low reading prospectively** — it "made me confident of my wading back in" at the time, not in retrospect.
- Sentiment adjusts confidence in a position; "the key is to wait for a definitive signal" still governs the entry.
- Corroborates the 60% top threshold stated independently in 2021 and 2010.
