# WW 2023-09-10 — What to do when you miss a GLB

**Source:** [Blog Post: Day 7 of $QQQ short term up-trend; 69 US new highs and 81 lows; $SPY is back below its 10 week average and $QQQ is just above its; Big week for $ORCL coming up, see weekly chart](../../raw/posts/2023-09-10-blog-post-day-7-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-69-us-new-highs-and-81-lows-spy-is-back-below-its-10-week-average.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2023/09/blog-post-day-7-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-69-us-new-highs-and-81-lows-spy-is-back-below-its-10-week-average-and-qqq-is-just-above-its-big-week-for-orcl-coming-up-see-weekly-chart/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The recovery entry for a breakout you did not catch**: "**When I miss a GLB, I may look to buy after a bounce
up off of the 10 week or 4wk average.**" That is the answer to the problem the
[missed alerts](../methodology/glb-in-practice.md) create — a GLB you did not take is not gone, because a stock
in a Stage 2 advance keeps offering bounces off its fast weekly averages, and each one is an entry with a nearby
stop. ORCL is the case: a GLB to an all-time high in June "on above average volume," then a **12-week
consolidation**, with "**its 4wk avg > 10 wk avg > 30 wk avg, a sign of a powerful Stage 2 up-trend**" and a
bounce off the 10-week two weeks earlier. The trigger he was watching for was a specific number plus a condition:
"a rise above **127.54** with **above average volume** to an ATH would be worth monitoring." Fundamental
corroboration from MarketSmith: composite rating 95, RS 96, and the stock already double its yearly low.

## Key claims

- **A missed GLB is recoverable**: buy a later bounce off the 4-week or 10-week average instead.
- This is why the alert failing is costly but not fatal — a Stage 2 stock keeps generating entries.
- A **12-week consolidation after a GLB** is treated as healthy, not as failure.
- The re-entry trigger is quoted as a price *and* a volume condition, set in advance.
- MarketSmith ratings (comp 95, RS 96) used as the fundamental gate.
