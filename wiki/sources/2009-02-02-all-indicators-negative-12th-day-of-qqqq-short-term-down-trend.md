# WW 2009-02-02 — Why component 1 can stay negative for months

**Source:** [All indicators negative; 12th day of QQQQ short term down-trend](../../raw/posts/2009-02-02-all-indicators-negative-12th-day-of-qqqq-short-term-down-trend.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2009/02/all-indicators-negative-12th-day-of-qqqq-short-term-down-trend/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**Why component 1 can stay negative for months, explained by its own denominator floor**: "This indicator has
been negative since late October, **largely because we rarely saw the required 20 new highs in a day**." The
February 2008 floor, working as designed and producing a persistent negative — informative about the market, but
worth knowing before reading a long run of zeros as independent evidence. **The mirror statistic quoted at an
extreme**: "I also calculate a similar indicator for 'successful' new lows, **which I do not post**… that
indicator has been **above 70% for the past four days**, indicating that shorting stocks at new lows 2 weeks ago
was likely to have been profitable. Clearly, this market has been inhospitable to the strategy of buying growth
stocks breaking to new highs." **Whipsaw avoidance stated as a reason not to trade**: "I have considered buying
some QID, but **with my short term moving averages being flat, I will probably get repeatedly whipsawed** as the
indexes move above and below their averages. However, at some point a real tradable trend will develop." — the
2006 trendless-market epiphany applied to a decision not to take a position. **And T2108's thresholds qualified
by regime**: at 38%, "a reading below 25% is where markets in the past have tended to bounce. **But this bear has
bounced at much lower readings, sometimes below 10%.**" IBD 100: 16% of the January 5 list higher. On the long
side he notes GLD and NFLX — the latter sourced from his son's college friends using the new streaming service.

## Key claims

- **A component can be pinned negative by its own guard condition** — component 1 stayed negative for months because fewer than 20 stocks made new highs each day.
- **The mirror statistic above 70% for four days** — the strongest low-side reading documented, and he states plainly that it is unpublished.
- **Flat moving averages are a reason to take no position at all**, on either side: the signal will whipsaw, so wait for a trend rather than trade a range.
- **T2108's bounce threshold is regime-dependent**: below 25% historically, but "this bear has bounced at much lower readings, sometimes below 10%."
- Scuttlebutt as a source: NFLX noticed because his son's fellow students were using the streaming service.
