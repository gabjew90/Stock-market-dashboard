# WW 2008-04-21 — The 2000 pension exit in dollars; GMI-S at 100% inside a bear

**Source:** [GMI: 5; GMI-R: 9; My General Market Index catches trend changes again!; more Judy's picks: ITRI and IMAX](../../raw/posts/2008-04-21-gmi-5-gmi-r-9-my-general-market-index-gmi-catches-trend-changes-again-more-judys-picks-itri-and-imax.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2008/04/gmi-5-gmi-r-9-my-general-market-index-gmi-catches-trend-changes-again-more-judys-picks-itri-and-imax/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Written during the April 2008 bear-market rally, and it is the richest single snapshot of the
multi-composite dashboard in action: GMI 5, GMI-R 9, **GMI-S 100%**, GMI-L 50% — every short-term
indicator positive while the long-term ones said the bear was intact. Also puts **dollar figures
on the 2000 pension exit** for the only time in the ingested corpus, and states the tax-deferred
precondition for the whole trading style.

## Key claims

- **The dashboard in a bear rally:** "The only indicator that is not positive is the Weekly QQQQ
  Index which indicates that the major longer term trend is still down... The GMI-S is at
  **100%**, indicating that all 16 short term indicators for four key index ETF's are positive.
  The GMI-L is still at **50%**." A textbook case of the fast/slow split — and read with
  hindsight, the long-term indicators were right: the 2008 collapse followed.
- **The 2000 exit, in dollars:** "in 2000, I got out of my university pension growth fund around
  **$103** and by 2002 it was trading **in the 30's**." Around a −70% avoided drawdown — the
  hardest number attached to the 2000 call anywhere in the ingested corpus.
- **The full self-stated record:** margin account closed October 2000; IRA up more than 50% in
  the 2003 recovery; pension in cash 2000–2002; pension in cash again "since December" 2007 —
  "and will remain in cash **until the GMI rises to 6**." (Note: a stricter pension re-entry bar
  than the ≥4 trading threshold.)
- **On buy-and-hold:** "This advice is garbage and many people were hurt. Once the market turns
  it rises for months and there is always plenty of time to get on board."
- **The train metaphor:** "When the train is moving in the right direction, one should hop on
  and **not argue with others regarding whether it should be moving or how far it will go.** If
  the train reverses I can always hop off and wait for it to come my way again."
- **The tax-deferred precondition:** "The key is to use **tax deferred accounts** where there are
  no tax consequences from getting in and out of positions." The in-and-out style is viable
  *because* it is untaxed per trade — a structural fact the wiki had nowhere.
- Judy's concept-stock picks continue (CPST +50% since mentioned; ITRI, IMAX watched) — "I tend
  to wait for the right technical signal to buy them, usually at higher prices than she does."
