# WW 2017-02-12 — A breakout scan that fires only on the first day — and is run in the last hour

**Source:** [New TC2000 Scan finds GLB and possible cup with handle break-out $PJC;  next post Tuesday night](../../raw/posts/2017-02-12-new-tc2000-scan-finds-glb-and-possible-cup-with-handle-break-out-pjc-next-post-tuesday-night.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2017/02/new-tc2000-scan-finds-glb-and-possible-cup-with-handle-break-out-pjc-next-post-tuesday-night/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A TC2000 scan with two design choices worth copying. The criteria: a stock hitting a **100-day high that did not hit a 100-day high the prior day** ("I want a stock to be just breaking out"), up significantly from a year ago, on above-average volume — 19 hits, PJC the example (IBD Composite 95). The timing: "**I like to run this scan during the last hour of the trading day** (I do not want a stock that breaks out early only to reverse by the end of the day)." And the framing: "I use the results of my scans as a *starting point* to identify strong stocks that I must then research for other technical and fundamental attributes." Entry: an initial position with a sell stop below the day's low. Published to his TC2000 Club and described in the blog glossary.

## Key claims

- The not-yesterday condition isolates the first day of a breakout.
- Run intraday scans in the final hour so early breakouts that fade are excluded.
- Scans are a starting point for research, never a buy list.
- Entry: initial position, stop below the day's low.
