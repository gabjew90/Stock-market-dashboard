# WW 2022-07-07 — Where the green line is drawn — the top of the highest monthly bar

**Source:** [Blog post: Friday could be first day of new $QQQ short term up-trend; why this $QQQ rally may be different; how I draw green line for $HALO](../../raw/posts/2022-07-07-blog-post-friday-could-be-first-day-of-new-qqq-short-term-up-trend-why-this-qqq-rally-may-be-different-how-i.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2022/07/blog-post-friday-could-be-first-day-of-new-qqq-short-term-up-trend-why-this-qqq-rally-may-be-different-how-i-draw-green-line-for-halo/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A reader question resolves an ambiguity the definition alone leaves open. On HALO: "I drew it at **the top of the highest monthly bar even though the stock retreated from the high that month**. Some people think it should be drawn at 51.57, the peak reached two months later. I adhere" to the former. So the line marks the **intramonth high of the highest monthly bar**, not the highest close, and not a later lower peak. Also a short-term structure read: marking tops (T) and bottoms (B), "in a declining trend we see lower tops and bottoms. However the most recent bottom is higher than the previous one. We did not get the expected lower bottom" — a possible higher top would be short-term bullish, with bearish sentiment supporting the case.

## Key claims

- The green line is drawn at the **top of the highest monthly bar** — the intramonth high, even if the month closed well below it.
- A later, lower peak does not move the line.
- Higher-low structure inside a down-trend as an early turn tell.
