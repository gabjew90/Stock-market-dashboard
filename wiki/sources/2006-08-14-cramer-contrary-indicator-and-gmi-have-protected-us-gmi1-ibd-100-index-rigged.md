# WW 2006-08-14 — Cramer as contrary indicator; the GMI's May–August 2006 record; is the IBD 100 Index rigged?

**Source:** [Cramer Contrary Indicator and GMI have protected us; GMI:1; IBD 100 Index rigged?](../../raw/posts/2006-08-14-cramer-contrary-indicator-and-gmi-have-protected-us-gmi1-ibd-100-index-rigged.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2006/08/cramer-contrary-indicator-and-gmi-have-protected-us-gmi1-ibd-100-index-rigged/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Quotes his own May 12, 2006 post — Cramer on the Today Show comparing the bull to the 1920s as a topping sign; "when a major index is below its 30 week average I get very defensive in the stocks represented by that indicator" — and audits what followed: the GMI fell from 4 to 1 after that post and stayed 0–2 through June, briefly 3–4 in early July, then 0–2 again; "I have therefore been in a defensive mode all of this time. Most of my short trades since May have been profitable… Patience is the sine qua non of successful trading." Then the IBD 100 Index: +127.8% vs the S&P's +37.6% since 2003, but poor performers are removed weekly as the index is recalculated — his own tracking of the 5/15 list showed the survivorship gap; "we readers deserve a more accurate system for tracking the performance of the IBD 100 selections."

## Key claims

- Below the 30-week → very defensive in that index's stocks (2006 statement).
- GMI regime record May–August 2006 audited in the post.
- "Patience is the sine qua non of successful trading."
- IBD 100 Index survivorship: weekly removal of losers inflates the published index (2006 — a year before the 2007 audit).
