## What it covers

A routine OSB scan publication that nonetheless pins down two rules precisely: the stop is placed **immediately** on entry, and earnings dates are treated as a distinct hazard the setup does not protect against. Includes AMPH as a recent success and NVDA at the top of the ranked list.

## Key claims

- The stop rule, unconditional: "**If I buy any OSB, I place an immediate stop below the bounce.**"
- The list is ranked by close ÷ lowest price of the past year — the same sort as [WW 2023-07-09](2023-07-09-blog-post-day-48-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-my-favorite-set-up-oversold-bounce-black-dot-after-a-recent-yearl.md). NVDA sat at the top.
- **Earnings risk is called out as separate from the setup:** "Note the earnings dates. Holding through earnings has been treacherous lately, see QCOM and SDGR." The bounce-low stop does not defend against an earnings gap.
- Entry condition restated: the stocks "reached an ATH recently and have had an oversold bounce, indicated by a black dot."
- AMPH is offered as a recent case where "My OSB setup worked."
