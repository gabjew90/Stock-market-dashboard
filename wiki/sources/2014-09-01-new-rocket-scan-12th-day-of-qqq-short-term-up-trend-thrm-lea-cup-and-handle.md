# WW 2014-09-01 — A new rocket scan: a bounce off the 10-week; the cup-and-handle's three components

**Source:** [New Rocket Scan; 12th day of $QQQ short term up-trend; $THRM, $LEA-cup and handle](../../raw/posts/2014-09-01-new-rocket-scan-12th-day-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-thrm-lea-cup-and-handle.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2014/09/new-rocket-scan-12th-day-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-thrm-lea-cup-and-handle/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"One of the best areas of support for a rising stock is its 10 week moving average. A lot of rocket stocks rest there and then resume their rise." The scan: over 6,000 US stocks screened for a strong rocket pattern **and** a bounce up off the stock's 10-week average — ten hits that weekend, two carrying his IBD flag. And the cup-with-handle taught in three parts on THRM: "first, there must be a prior up-trend because this is a continuation pattern. Second, there must be a pattern that looks like a side view of a cup and handle. Then there must be a break out above the top of the handle on above average volume." The gate as always: "whether this break-out continues will depend on the general market's trend."

## Key claims

- A weekly-support scan: rocket pattern + bounce off the 10-week average.
- Cup-and-handle in three components: prior up-trend (it is a continuation pattern), the shape, the handle-top breakout on volume.
- The 10-week average as the preferred resting place of an advancing rocket.
