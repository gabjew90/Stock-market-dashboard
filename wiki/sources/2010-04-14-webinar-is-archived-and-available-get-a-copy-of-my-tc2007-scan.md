# WW 2010-04-14 — The Worden webinar scan — an oversold stochastic plus a 30-day bounce (2010)

**Source:** [Webinar is archived and available; Get a copy of my TC2007 scan](../../raw/posts/2010-04-14-webinar-is-archived-and-available-get-a-copy-of-my-tc2007-scan.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2010/04/webinar-is-archived-and-available-get-a-copy-of-my-tc2007-scan/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

After his webinar with Julia Ormond of Worden he published the scan he had demonstrated: "a scan that I use to find a stock rebounding from an oversold condition. It employs **an oversold stochastic together with a bounce from, or break out above, the 30 day moving average**," offered as a downloadable PDF. It is the earliest oversold-bounce *scan* on the blog — the same shape as BOS (2016) and the dots (2018–2025), six years before the family got its names.

## Key claims

- An oversold-stochastic + 30-day-average bounce scan, published April 2010.
- The 30-day average as the reference the bounce is measured against (later the 21-EMA / 30-SMA pair).
- The oversold-bounce idea is running as a scan in 2010, well before BOS or the dots.
