# WW 2005-05-30 — The orthodox agnostic; window dressing explained; the MW plan with re-entry pre-written

**Source:** [GOOG, MW, Other rockets, fabricated fantasy revisited, GMI +5](../../raw/posts/2005-05-30-goog-mw-other-rockets-fabricated-fantasy-revisited-gmi-5.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/05/goog-mw-other-rockets-fabricated-fantasy-revisited-gmi-5/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Three durable pieces: his self-description as an **"orthodox agnostic"** about rationales for
market moves; the fullest explanation of the **window-dressing** mechanism in the corpus (131
mentions, previously unexplained in the wiki); and a complete worked entry plan for an extended
stock — including the re-entry pre-written at the moment of entry.

## Key claims

- **The orthodox agnostic:** "Why do so many people need a reason for the market to rally. If
  there are good reasons, **they almost always come out after the move**. If the train or rocket
  is moving, why should we fixate on an explanation — just jump on board. I guess I am an
  **orthodox agnostic** when it comes to believing in a rationale for stock movements."
- **Window dressing, the mechanism:** quarter-end brings "that famous 'window dressing' period
  when mutual fund managers are rumored to load up on the current quarter's winners **so that
  they can look smart** when they publish their quarterly reports listing their holdings." With
  a sceptic's footnote — John Bogle told him personally it doesn't happen — and a reform
  proposal: "why doesn't the SEC require that all funds list the **date and price** of all
  stocks purchased in the reporting quarter?" Applied as a GOOG hunch (fund managers who missed
  the Dutch auction stampeding in by quarter-end), offered *as* a scenario while disavowing
  scenarios — labelled a hunch, not a signal.
- **Reading the Successful 10-Day as a ratio when counts are thin:** the index read only 16,
  "nevertheless, most (16/26) of the stocks that hit new highs 10 days ago did close higher" —
  against 21 of 117 for new lows. The *ratio* logic that formally replaced the count in 2014,
  visible nine years early.
- **The MW plan — entry, stop, and re-entry all pre-written:** a Lynch-style observation
  candidate (his own menswear store), gapped up on earnings, A+ IBD rating, but extended. The
  plan: "buy a few shares on Tuesday and place an immediate sell stop below the last mini
  reaction low of 49.40... **If I got stopped out, I would buy it again if it bounces near its
  50 day average.**" The re-entry condition specified before the first share is bought.
- **The disclaimer era begins:** the post opens with the "I am only one trader, not a guru, and
  not a financial advisor" language that becomes the blog's standing footer.
