# WW 2013-08-25 — GMI2 revised from 6 to 8 components — the dated moment

**Source:** [QQQ remains in Stage 2 up-trend; GMI2=8 (of 8)](../../raw/posts/2013-08-25-qqq-remains-in-stage-2-up-trend-gmi28-of-8.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2013/08/qqq-remains-in-stage-2-up-trend-gmi28-of-8/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The GMI2's expansion, dated. "**I have revised the GMI2 by adding two new indicators (7 and 8)** that I want to monitor. **I use the GMI2 to keep me focused on this set of 8 very technical indicators and not for specific buy/sell signals.** Note that the GMI2 = 8, which means that all of its indicators are positive." Also the pension rule in its clearest short form: the weekly chart "usually provides me with a better picture of the market's main trend"; the QQQ is well above its rising 30-week in a Stage 2 up-trend, and "**as long as the major indexes show this pattern, I remain 100% invested in my primary university pension accounts**" — QQQ, DIA and SPY all in Stage 2. GMI on a Buy since July 5.

## Key claims

- **GMI2 grows from 6 to 8 components on 2013-08-25** — previously the wiki dated the 8-component form only to a 2020 table image.
- "I use the GMI2 to keep me focused on this set of 8 very technical indicators and **not for specific buy/sell signals**" — the no-decision-rules statement, restated.
- The pension rule in one line: all major indexes in Stage 2 ⇒ 100% invested.
- The weekly chart as the better picture of the main trend.
