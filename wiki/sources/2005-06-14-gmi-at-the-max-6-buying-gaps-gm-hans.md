# WW 2005-06-14 — GMI at the max; buying gaps; the 70% rule measured

**Source:** [GMI at the max:  +6; Buying Gaps; GM, HANS](../../raw/posts/2005-06-14-gmi-at-the-max-6-buying-gaps-gm-hans.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/06/gmi-at-the-max-6-buying-gaps-gm-hans/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"Since the beginning of the up trend on May 6, the QQQQ has risen 4.8% and 75% of the Nasdaq 100 stocks have increased. (This is an example of the wisdom of the saying that about 70% of stocks follow the general market averages.)" Median gainer +8.5%; and a note on stocks gapping up that day (GM, HANS) — the gap-up as an entry once it holds.

## Key claims

- The 70% rule measured on a live up-trend: 75% of Nasdaq-100 up.
- Gap-ups as entries.
