# WW 2008-03-31 — Twelve new highs out of 4,000 — when the strategy itself cannot work

**Source:** [GMI-1; GMI-R: 3; RICK- a stock for a “bare” market?](../../raw/posts/2008-03-31-gmi-1-gmi-r-3-rick-a-stock-for-a-bare-market.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2008/03/gmi-1-gmi-r-3-rick-a-stock-for-a-bare-market/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The clearest statement of when his method is simply inapplicable: "there were only 12 new highs and 42 new lows in my universe of 4,000 stocks on Friday. **A growth stock strategy of buying stocks at new highs will not work in an environment when only 12 stocks out of 4,000 can hit a new high in a day.**" With the QQQQ below its 10-week for most of 19 weeks and SPY for 20: "**I have learned that my strategy of buying rockets going to new highs works best when the averages are consistently above their 10 week averages.**" He names his exit level in advance (the QQQQ 0.21 above key support; a failure to close above it means going back to cash) and the reason: "my indicators get me out of declining markets fast… **the key is to conserve capital**."

## Key claims

- A new-high count that low means the strategy has no raw material — not that he should trade it differently.
- "My strategy of buying rockets going to new highs works best when the averages are consistently above their 10 week averages."
- The exit level is named before the day it might be hit.
- Capital conservation as the purpose of the fast exit.
