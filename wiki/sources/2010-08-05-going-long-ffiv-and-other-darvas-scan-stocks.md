# WW 2010-08-05 — Going long FFIV — the Darvas scan's criteria, and the one scan he will not publish

**Source:** [Going long—FFIV and other Darvas Scan stocks](../../raw/posts/2010-08-05-going-long-ffiv-and-other-darvas-scan-stocks.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2010/08/going-long-ffiv-and-other-darvas-scan-stocks/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The selection filter stated as a checklist: "I prefer to buy stocks that **have already doubled in the past year** and that are **trading at or near their all-time highs**. I also want the stock to have **appeared on the IBD New America or IBD100 list**." FFIV met all three and also surfaced on his **Darvas Scan**, "that identifies stocks that have many of the attributes he liked" — its monthly chart showing a burst through the all-time high reached in late 1999. Entry and add rule: "I purchased a little of FFIV and **will add to it if it keeps rising**." And a boundary he draws around his own material: "**Please do not ask me to post my Darvas Scan.**" — unlike the TC2000-club scans, this one stays private.

## Key claims

- Three-part selection filter: already doubled in the past year, at or near an ATH, and on an IBD New America / IBD100 list.
- Pilot buy plus add-only-if-it-rises, restated.
- The Darvas scan is deliberately unpublished, unlike the scans in his TC2000 club.
- A stock can be extended on the daily while the weekly still qualifies — he bought anyway, small.
