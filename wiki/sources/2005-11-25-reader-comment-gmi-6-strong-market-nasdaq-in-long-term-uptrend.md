# WW 2005-11-25 — A reader on using the GMI as the 'tie breaker' — and his own use of it

**Source:** [Reader comment; GMI: +6:  Strong market; Nasdaq in long term uptrend?](../../raw/posts/2005-11-25-reader-comment-gmi-6-strong-market-nasdaq-in-long-term-uptrend.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/11/reader-comment-gmi-6-strong-market-nasdaq-in-long-term-uptrend/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A reader letter he reprints because it describes his own practice: "The CANSLIM method depends on the direction of the market. Your indicator is something that I look for in order to determine whether or not I am in the market. I use a number of indicators and yours is the '**tie breaker**'." His reply states the rule in one line: "I use the GMI the same way as the above reader — to determine whether to be in the market on the long or short side. **I always trade consistent with the GMI.**" The tape that day: GMI +6, 111 successful 10-day new highs, 351 new yearly highs, and **82% of the stocks that doubled in the past year above their 30-day averages** — the doubler indicator reading a healthy leadership.

## Key claims

- The GMI answers one question only: long side or short side.
- "I always trade consistent with the GMI."
- A reader's framing he endorses — the GMI as the tie breaker among several indicators, tied to CAN SLIM's 'M'.
- Doubler indicator at 82% as a leadership-health read.
