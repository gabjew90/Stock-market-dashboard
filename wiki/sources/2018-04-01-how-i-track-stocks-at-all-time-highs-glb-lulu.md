# WW 2018-04-01 — The ATH watchlist workflow; TC2000's survivorship trap

**Source:** [How I track stocks at all time highs; GLB: $LULU](../../raw/posts/2018-04-01-how-i-track-stocks-at-all-time-highs-glb-lulu.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2018/04/how-i-track-stocks-at-all-time-highs-glb-lulu/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The step-by-step construction of the ~800-stock ATH watchlist that several later scans run
against — including a tooling limitation in TC2000 that he engineers around, and independent
corroboration that green lines are drawn *manually*.

## Key claims

- **Why mine ATHs during weak markets:** "A stock that can come through market turmoil and
  still hit an all time high is a stock that **might take off when the market strengthens**."
  His example: finding GMCR in April 2009, at the dawn of the new bull.
- **TC2000 has a survivorship trap, and he knows it:** "TC2000... returns a **null result** on a
  filter if the stock did not exist in the time period examined... any stock that was not
  trading for the full 10 years would drop out. This is a major problem for stocks that came
  public in the past few years." The same class of bias our own breadth reconstruction
  documents — identified here in his charting tool, by him.
- **The workaround pipeline:** barchart.com → Stocks → New highs and lows → switch the dropdown
  to *all-time high* → copy → Excel → paste symbols into a TC2000 watchlist. "I repeat this
  process periodically creating a larger watchlist."
- **The curation pass is manual, twice over:** delete false ATHs from the barchart list, then
  "**draw a horizontal green line at a monthly high that has not been surpassed for three
  months (3 bars)**." Corroborates the comment-thread finding that no GLB scan exists — the
  lines are hand-drawn on the monthly chart.
- **The LULU example:** from that watchlist, a GLB "on above average volume" Wednesday plus a
  green dot Thursday — two of his signals stacking on one candidate.
- **The Red-signal caveat:** GMI Red, day D-5 — "if I buy anything, I will set a **very close
  stop loss**." Buying against the gate is allowed but with compressed risk.
