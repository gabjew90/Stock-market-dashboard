# WW 2006-07-18 — Cap-weighted indexes masking a weakening market — the 2006 instance

**Source:** [GMI: 0; Indicators deteriorate; On biased market indexes](../../raw/posts/2006-07-18-gmi-0-indicators-deteriorate-on-biased-market-indexes.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2006/07/gmi-0-indicators-deteriorate-on-biased-market-indexes/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The same divergence he would describe again in 2021, fifteen years earlier: "the markets weakened a lot more than was reflected in the major indexes. **50-54% of the stocks in the Nasdaq 100, S&P 500 and Dow 30 indexes advanced on Monday, but only 36% of my universe of 4,000 stocks rose**, along with just 23% of the stocks in the IBD 100 list… Thus, **it was the large cap stocks reflected in the big indexes that were most likely to rise**." With 16 new highs against 195 new lows, and component 1 at 19% — "buying stocks at new highs has not been profitable." Only 23% of his universe and 29% of the IBD 100 remained in a short-term up-trend.

## Key claims

- Index-level breadth (50–54% up) against universe-level breadth (36%) as the divergence measure.
- The IBD 100 *underperforming* the universe (23%) — growth stocks leading down, as they do in declines.
- Component 1 at 19% read directly: "buying stocks at new highs has not been profitable."
- The same mechanism as the November 2021 exit, observed in 2006.
