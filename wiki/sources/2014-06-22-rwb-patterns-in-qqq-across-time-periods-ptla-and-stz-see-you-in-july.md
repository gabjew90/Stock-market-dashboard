# WW 2014-06-22 — RWB patterns in QQQ across four timeframes; which ones he trades from

**Source:** [RWB patterns in $QQQ across time periods; $PTLA and $STZ; See you in July?](../../raw/posts/2014-06-22-rwb-patterns-in-qqq-across-time-periods-ptla-and-stz-see-you-in-july.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2014/06/rwb-patterns-in-qqq-across-time-periods-ptla-and-stz-see-you-in-july/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The same modified GMMA on the QQQ at four resolutions — weekly (classic RWB emerging from a tight red-line consolidation), daily (RWB, out of a base at end-May), hourly (recent congestion, red lines back above blue and turning up; needs 93 to clear), 5-minute (end-of-day strength) — and the sentence that assigns roles: "As a longer term swing trader I focus on the weekly and daily charts. The hourly and 5 minute charts are useful only if I want to pinpoint areas for entries or where to put sell stops." TC2000 email alerts on index crosses of key averages. Also PTLA (Judy's early buy; "an important technical signal when a recent IPO forms a multi-month base and then breaks to a new all-time high"; green line ~$31), STZ cup-and-handle on volume, and the AAII covered-call workshop with Alan Ellman.

## Key claims

- Four-timeframe RWB read on one index; consolidation = red lines converging well above the blue.
- "As a longer term swing trader I focus on the weekly and daily charts. The hourly and 5 minute charts are useful only if I want to pinpoint areas for entries or where to put sell stops."
- TC2000 alerts on moving-average crosses as the monitoring mechanism.
- IPO base → ATH as an important signal (PTLA, 2014) — the IPO GLB before it was named.
