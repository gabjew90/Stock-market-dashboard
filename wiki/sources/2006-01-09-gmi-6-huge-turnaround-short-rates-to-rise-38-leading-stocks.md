# WW 2006-01-09 — The same breadth reading means different things at different points in a trend

**Source:** [GMI: +6; Huge turnaround; short rates to rise?  38 leading stocks](../../raw/posts/2006-01-09-gmi-6-huge-turnaround-short-rates-to-rise-38-leading-stocks.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2006/01/gmi-6-huge-turnaround-short-rates-to-rise-38-leading-stocks/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A lesson in reading a breadth extreme in context rather than off a scale. There were "an amazing **505 new
yearly highs**" — and instead of celebrating he goes to the last comparable reading: "The last time we had over
500 new highs was on July 28 (**522 highs**), **just before the August 2nd top in the QQQQ**." The same number,
five months earlier, marked a top. What separates them is where each sat inside its trend: "At that time,
however, 81% of stocks were above their 10 week averages, compared with 76% on Friday, and 57% were in a short
term up trend compared with 49% on Friday. Moreover, **last July the QQQQ had been rallying for about a month,
while the QQQQ short term trend just broke out again last week.**" A high new-high count late in an advance is
exhaustion; the same count on day 2 of a new up-trend is initiation. The doubler indicator reads healthy — "80%
of the stocks that have doubled in the past year closed above their 30 day averages and 31% hit a new high" —
and the screen he publishes tightens the earlier one: 38 stocks that "hit a new high on Friday, that **doubled in
the past year** and that had **triple digit earnings gains the most recent quarter**." The standing reservation
is the rate indicator, which "has again broken out to the up side… **Why is the smart money betting on still
higher rates?**"

## Key claims

- **A breadth extreme is read against its own history, not against a threshold.** 505 new highs meant a top in July and an initiation in January — the difference is the trend's age, not the number.
- Two supporting comparisons make the point quantitative: % above the 10-week (81 → 76) and % in a short-term up-trend (57 → 49) were both *lower* at the higher reading.
- **The doubler screen tightened**: new high today + doubled in the past year + **triple-digit EPS growth in the most recent quarter**, against "strong recent quarterly earnings" and ≥ 60% two months earlier.
- The doubler indicator used as a leader-health gauge: 80% above their 30-day averages, 31% at new highs.
- The rate indicator breaking out again while the rally was predicated on the Fed pausing — the divergence noted, not traded.
