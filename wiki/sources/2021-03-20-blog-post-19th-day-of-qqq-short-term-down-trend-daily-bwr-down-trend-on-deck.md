# WW 2021-03-20 — The market discounts the future — why good news can accompany a decline

**Source:** [Blog post: 19th day of $QQQ short term down-trend; daily BWR down-trend on deck?](../../raw/posts/2021-03-20-blog-post-19th-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-daily-bwr-down-trend-on-deck.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2021/03/blog-post-19th-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-daily-bwr-down-trend-on-deck/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The discounting mechanism stated plainly, with a worked example from the year just past.** "**The market
discounts the future. It rose during the pandemic as it focused on the coming recovery. It would not be unusual
for the market to decline during the economic recovery as it foresees inflation, higher rates and an exploding
budget deficit.**" That resolves the puzzle he keeps encountering in the corpus — a market rising into terrible
news and falling into good news — without appealing to irrationality: price reflects expectations about a future
state, so it moves *before* the state arrives and can move *against* it once it does. It is also why he treats
the news as uninformative about direction and the price as the only readable thing. On the rotation debate of the
moment: "the pundits are talking about rotation from tech to value stocks. **But in my experience, tech has
always led. It could be we are seeing a top in tech to be followed by a top in the other stocks after the
laggards rise a little.**" A specific, falsifiable claim about sequencing.

## Key claims

- **"The market discounts the future"** — the mechanism that explains rising into bad news and falling into good, without appealing to irrationality.
- The 2020–21 case worked both ways: up during the pandemic on the coming recovery, then potentially down *during* the recovery on inflation and rates.
- **"In my experience, tech has always led"** — so a rotation into value is read as laggards catching up before a broader top, not as a healthy handoff.
- Consistent with treating news as uninformative about direction: the reaction has already happened.
