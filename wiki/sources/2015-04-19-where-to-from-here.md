# WW 2015-04-19 — Three indicators read together for a bounce

**Source:** [Where to from here?](../../raw/posts/2015-04-19-where-to-from-here.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2015/04/where-to-from-here/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The bounce read stated as a checklist of three, used together rather than in sequence.** "There are three
things I look for in this daily chart. **First, I look for the QQQ to bounce back and forth between the channel
lines until an up or down-side break-out. Second, I look at the Bollinger Bands to guide my interpretation. The
QQQ often finds support at the lower daily 15.2 BB. And third, the stochastics (daily 10.4) provides further
indication of a possible oversold bounce. I look at all 3 of these indicators to guide my trading.**" The
instrument for the trade is the leveraged ETF — "I sometimes trade the 3x leveraged bullish ETF, TQQQ, to ride
the bounce up" — and the risk control is decided before entry: "**I reduce risk by determining in advance a price
which may signal that the bounce has failed and where I intend to sell out my position for a small loss.**" Then
the regime qualifier that governs all of it: "**I find it much safer to trade a bounce off of support in an
up-trend**" — the same setup is a different proposition inside a decline.

## Key claims

- **Three instruments read together for one decision**: the channel, the lower 15.2 Bollinger Band, and the daily 10.4 stochastic.
- **The failure price is chosen before the entry**, not after — "determining in advance a price which may signal that the bounce has failed."
- **"Much safer to trade a bounce off of support in an up-trend"** — the bounce setup is regime-conditional like everything else.
- TQQQ used as the vehicle for a bounce trade, not only for a confirmed trend.
- "Never one indicator" demonstrated rather than asserted.
