# WW 2020-07-23 — TC2000 alerted me the moment AMD crossed the green line

**Source:** [Blog post: TC2000 alerted me to a high volume GLB yesterday: $AMD–learn how to set GLB alerts](../../raw/posts/2020-07-23-blog-post-tc2000-alerted-me-to-a-high-volume-glb-yesterday-amd-learn-how-to-set-glb-alerts.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2020/07/blog-post-tc2000-alerted-me-to-a-high-volume-glb-yesterday-amd-learn-how-to-set-glb-alerts/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The automation that makes the GLB tradeable for someone not watching the screen: "TC2000 sent me a **text** yesterday immediately when AMD crossed the green line. **I had created the alert on June 19**" — five weeks before it fired — "note the nice volume of trading yesterday on the break-out to an ATH." He notes the Gold option is what enables it and promises a walk-through of building GLB alerts.

## Key claims

- A GLB alert is set on the green line weeks in advance and delivered by text when price crosses.
- This is the monitoring counterpart of the Darvas GTC buy-stop: alert first, decide, then buy.
- Volume on the breakout day still checked after the alert fires.
