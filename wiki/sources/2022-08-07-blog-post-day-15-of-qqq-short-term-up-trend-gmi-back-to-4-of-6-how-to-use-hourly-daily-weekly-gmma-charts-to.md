# WW 2022-08-07 — The hourly GMMA

**Source:** [Day 15 of $QQQ short term up-trend; GMI back to 4 (of 6); How to use hourly/daily/weekly GMMA charts](../../raw/posts/2022-08-07-blog-post-day-15-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-gmi-back-to-4-of-6-how-to-use-hourly-daily-weekly-gmma-charts-to.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2022/08/blog-post-day-15-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-gmi-back-to-4-of-6-how-to-use-hourly-daily-weekly-gmma-charts-to-assess-trends/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The **hourly** timeframe finally documented — flagged as an undocumented layer in the first
coverage review. Also the precise construction of his *adapted* GMMA, which differs from stock
Guppy in one detail the wiki had never recorded: a 13th "average" that plots price itself.

## Key claims

- **The adapted GMMA, precisely:** "12 exponential moving averages with different periods **and
  a 13th average equal to 1 which shows the current price**" — the dotted line. "In an up trend
  one wants to see the closing price (dotted line) **leading all of the other averages
  higher**." Red = 6 shorter, blue = 6 longer, white background; RWB and BWR as usual.
- **Bases are visible as red-line convergence:** "These charts also reveal bases when all of the
  red lines come together, thus showing no price differences over the 6 shorter time periods."
  Convergence = equilibrium at that timescale, at *any* timeframe.
- **The hourly layer's job:** the hourly QQQ was "in an hourly RWB uptrend that is **forming a
  small base** with the red lines converging. I am watching for a move up or down out of the
  base this week." The hourly resolves *where inside the daily trend you are* — resting or
  resuming — without predicting the break's direction.
- **The stack:** hourly (base/resumption inside the trend) → daily (the operative RWB trend,
  with April 2022's failure as the cautionary precedent) → weekly (the regime). With the
  monthly template from 2014, the full ladder is now hourly → daily → weekly → monthly, each
  with a distinct job.
