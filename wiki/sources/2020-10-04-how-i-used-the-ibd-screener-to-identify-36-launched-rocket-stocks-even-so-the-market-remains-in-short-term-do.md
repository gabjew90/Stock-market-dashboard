# WW 2020-10-04 — The IBD screener workflow; the all-12-GMMA exit line

**Source:** [How I used the IBD screener to identify 36 launched rocket stocks](../../raw/posts/2020-10-04-how-i-used-the-ibd-screener-to-identify-36-launched-rocket-stocks-even-so-the-market-remains-in-short-term-do.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2020/10/how-i-used-the-ibd-screener-to-identify-36-launched-rocket-stocks-even-so-the-market-remains-in-short-term-down-trend-and-daily-rwb-pattern-did-not-form-for-qqq/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

A complete candidate-sourcing workflow using IBD's screener, with the exact criteria; a
precise index-level exit line stated in GMMA terms; and the O'Neil origin credit. Also documents
the student channel: the tutorial he points readers to is by his own student, Richard Moglen.

## Key claims

- **The screen, exactly:** "RS 90-99, ACC/DIS = A or B, Price >30, Next Quarter EPS est >100%"
  → **36 stocks out of 7,000+**, exported to Excel → TC2000 watchlist with alerts on his
  set-ups. Survivors included CRWD, DDOG, ETSY, PTON, ZM, ZS — "many of the stocks I have been
  following came up in my first screen."
- **A hard exit line in GMMA terms:** "If QQQ (dotted line) declines below **all 12 GMMA
  averages**, lowest is currently at 269.55, then **it will be time to exit all positions.**"
  A total-exit trigger stated as a single price, from the weekly Guppy chart.
- **The earnings engine:** "William O'Neil and David Ryan teach people to buy great stocks with
  proven or expected large earnings increases. **Great earnings propel stocks higher.**"
- **The O'Neil origin credit:** O'Neil's book "is required reading for my students. **I began to
  make money in the market after reading it** and getting O'Neil's newspaper, IBD."
- **The student channel:** the how-to video is by "my talented student, Richard Moglen
  (@RichardMoglen)" — and the screener exercise was assigned to his class of 90+ undergraduates
  for the virtual trading exercise.
- Market context kept separate from stock strength: rockets held (PTON, PINS, REGI above their
  green line tops) while "the GMI remains on a cautious Red signal" and the daily RWB pattern
  failed to form — stock-level strength does not override the market gate for *new* buying.
