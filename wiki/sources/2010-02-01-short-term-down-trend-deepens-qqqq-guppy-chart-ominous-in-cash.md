# WW 2010-02-01 — The GMI explained for students; T2108 30%; the daily Guppy chart turns bearish

**Source:** [Short term down-trend deepens; QQQQ Guppy chart ominous; In cash](../../raw/posts/2010-02-01-short-term-down-trend-deepens-qqqq-guppy-chart-ominous-in-cash.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2010/02/short-term-down-trend-deepens-qqqq-guppy-chart-ominous-in-cash/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

For a new class: the GMI "focuses largely on the NASDAQ 100 stocks"; the Successful New High Index; "at least 100 new highs in my stock universe of roughly 4,000 actively traded stocks above $5"; daily QQQQ/SPY indicators = short-term, weekly QQQQ = longer-term ("weekly charts provide me with a more interpretable and reliable picture"). D-6 after a 51-day up-trend; QQQQ/SPY under their 10-week two weeks; 7% of Nasdaq-100 with MACD above signal. Shorting in a long-term up-trend risks being caught "with my shorts down." T2108 at 30%: "beginning to get into oversold levels where a bottom is possible. If we get below 20%, I would start to expect some type of rebound." The daily Guppy chart (black short / red long, no price bars): shorts projecting below longs for the first time since the March 2009 bottom.

## Key claims

- GMI construction restated for students (2010).
- T2108: 30% = beginning oversold; < 20% = expect a rebound.
- Daily Guppy short-below-long as a cash/short reason.
- Countertrend shorting inside a long-term up-trend is the risky version.
