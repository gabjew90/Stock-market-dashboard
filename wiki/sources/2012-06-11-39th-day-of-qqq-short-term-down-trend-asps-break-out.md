# WW 2012-06-11 — The new-high list is where the next leaders are — ASPS in a 39-day down-trend

**Source:** [39th day of QQQ short term down-trend; ASPS break-out?](../../raw/posts/2012-06-11-39th-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-asps-break-out.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2012/06/39th-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-asps-break-out/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Day 39 of a QQQ down-trend, GMI 1, both indexes six weeks below their 10-week: "**I cannot consistently make money trading on the long side when these indexes are below their 10 week averages.**" Only 98 stocks of almost 6,000 made a 52-week high — and that is exactly why he reads the list: "**It is in the new high list that I look for the leaders of the next move up. Any stock that can reach a new high, and especially an all-time high, during protracted market weakness is likely to be reflecting some serious buying pressure.**" ASPS is the instance, followed from a cup-with-handle breakout at $43.50 the previous November to ~$65.

## Key claims

- "It is in the new high list that I look for the leaders of the next move up."
- A new high during protracted weakness signals serious buying pressure.
- Reading and following the list is compatible with not buying — the market gate still binds.
- Weekly charts preferred for following such a stock over months.
