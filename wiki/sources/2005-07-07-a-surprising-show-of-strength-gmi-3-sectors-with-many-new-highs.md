# WW 2005-07-07 — A market that will not fall on terrible news; sectors read off the new-high list

**Source:** [A surprising show of strength; GMI: +3; Sectors with many new highs](../../raw/posts/2005-07-07-a-surprising-show-of-strength-gmi-3-sectors-with-many-new-highs.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/07/a-surprising-show-of-strength-gmi-3-sectors-with-many-new-highs/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Written the day of the London bombings, and it produces the fourth corner of his news-reaction diagnostic.
"Who would ever have believed that a market in a confirmed down trend could **withstand a terrorist attack and
actually hold its own**?" — 50–56% of the three major indexes' components advanced, against 7% of the Dow 30 the
day before, with 173 new highs to 28 new lows on D-9 of the QQQQ decline. His conclusion is the tell: "**If
stocks were going to decline more near term, I think the market had the perfect excuse to do so today — and it
did not.**" **And the earliest sector read off the new-high list**: rather than count the 173 new highs he groups
them by industry — apparel (7), independent oil and gas (13), oil and gas equipment services (11), investment
brokerage (5) — and draws the inference: "**it would seem to me that the sectors that have a lot of new highs on
a day like this are sending signals of future strength.**" He added to positions that rose, inside a confirmed
down-trend, on the reasoning that "the stocks that will report good earnings will buck the trend, at least until
their earnings come out."

## Key claims

- **A market that refuses to fall on terrible news is strong** — the mirror of leaders that cannot rise on good news, and the same instrument read at the index level.
- "The perfect excuse to [decline] — and it did not" is the test: the news supplies the occasion, the response supplies the information.
- **The new-high list grouped by industry as a sector-strength read** — the earliest instance of a technique he still runs in 2015 and 2022.
- Breadth beneath a declining index tracked separately from the index: 55% of QQQQ components advanced on D-9.
- Adding to winners inside a down-trend, justified by the earnings calendar rather than by the trend.
