**Source:** [Blog post: Day 14 of $QQQ short term up-trend; $TQQQ strategy beats most stocks again; Since 10/18,  TQQQ + 21.7%, beating 98% of Nasdaq100 stocks,  and 99% of S&P500 stocks](../../raw/posts/2021-11-04-blog-post-day-14-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-tqqq-strategy-beats-most-stocks-again-since-10-18-tqqq-21-7-beati.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2021/11/blog-post-day-14-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-tqqq-strategy-beats-most-stocks-again-since-10-18-tqqq-21-7-beating-98-of-nasdaq100-stocks-and-99-of-sp500-stocks/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The risk statement that belongs with every leveraged-ETF recommendation on this wiki, in his own words.** "I
have demonstrated repeatedly that during a QQQ short term up-trend, its triple leveraged ETF outperforms almost
all individual stocks. Why search for the rare stock that can beat TQQQ? **If we are in it to make money rather
than satisfying our egos, we should just buy TQQQ. But TQQQ also falls 3x faster than QQQ and one must have solid
rules for exiting. I start buying TQQQ on the first day of a new QQQ short term up[-trend]**." Three things in
sequence: the case, the ego observation, and then the condition that makes the case survivable. **The leverage is
symmetric and the exit rules are what make it usable** — which is why the instrument sits downstream of the trend
count rather than replacing it. The ego line is worth keeping too: stock-picking is characterised as partly a
self-image activity, and the leveraged index as the unglamorous alternative that pays better.

## Key claims

- **"TQQQ also falls 3x faster than QQQ and one must have solid rules for exiting"** — the symmetry stated as a precondition, not a footnote.
- The instrument is downstream of the trend count: it amplifies a signal, it does not supply one.
- **"If we are in it to make money rather than satisfying our egos"** — stock-picking named as partly a self-image activity.
- Entry from day 1 of the new up-trend, consistent with the scaling practice elsewhere.
