# WW 2011-03-10 — The leader watchlist, named

**Source:** [Short term trend still in doubt; in cash and gold](../../raw/posts/2011-03-10-short-term-trend-still-in-doubt-in-cash-and-gold.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2011/03/short-term-trend-still-in-doubt-in-cash-and-gold/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The leader watchlist, named in full** — asked for by readers and answered directly: "a number of people have
asked me **what stocks I monitor for an indication of how the market leaders are doing. They are: PCLN, AZO,
AAPL, BIDU, CMG, AMZN, GOOG, NFLX, FFIV.**" That is the concrete instrument behind every "the leaders are
weakening" statement in the corpus — a fixed, small, named list rather than an impression. And the test applied
to it is the ordinary one: "**the last 4 are showing weakness, closing below their 10 week averages.** I am
cautious now, given the signs of short term weakness **in the leaders and in my other indicators**." The trend
read that day: D-2 by his own definition, "**I will be more confident of the new down-trend if it persists for 5
days**"; the QQQQ finding support at its **50-day average**, with "a *close* below this important average
[signalling] important short term weakness in tech and growth stocks." Positioning shows the two speeds diverging
as usual — "**while I remain fully invested in mutual funds in my university pension, I am largely in cash and in
a little gold (DGP) in my IRA account.**"

## Key claims

- **The leader watchlist is a named list of nine**: PCLN, AZO, AAPL, BIDU, CMG, AMZN, GOOG, NFLX, FFIV.
- **The leaders are scored by the same 10-week test as everything else** — four of nine below theirs is what "the leaders are weakening" meant that day.
- The 50-day average used as short-term support on the index, with a close below it as the weakness trigger.
- Day-5 confirmation restated on day 2, before the fact.
- Pension fully invested while the trading account is largely cash — the two speeds, on the same evening.
