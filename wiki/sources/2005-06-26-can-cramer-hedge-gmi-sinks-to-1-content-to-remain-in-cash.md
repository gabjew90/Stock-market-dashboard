# WW 2005-06-26 — Can Cramer hedge? GMI sinks to +1 — the first documented pension exit

**Source:** [Can Cramer hedge? GMI sinks to +1; Content to remain in cash](../../raw/posts/2005-06-26-can-cramer-hedge-gmi-sinks-to-1-content-to-remain-in-cash.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/06/can-cramer-hedge-gmi-sinks-to-1-content-to-remain-in-cash/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

"I went to 95% cash in my IRA and 100% money market funds in my university pension. While GOOG was strong, I sold that too." On the strong-stock paradox: a popular stock "can often resist a general market decline and then decline when the market strengthens." And the account logic that makes early exits cheap: "Because I trade in tax deferred accounts, I do not care if I get out too soon."

## Key claims

- June 2005: IRA to 95% cash, pension to 100% money market at GMI +1 — the earliest pension move on the blog.
- Tax-deferred accounts remove the cost of exiting early.
