# WW 2018-10-21 — A TC2000 condition set for finding all-time highs intraday

**Source:** [TC2000 workshop gems; $QQQ $SPY in daily BWR down-trends](../../raw/posts/2018-10-21-tc2000-workshop-gems-qqq-spy-in-daily-bwr-down-trends.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2018/10/tc2000-workshop-gems-qqq-spy-in-daily-bwr-down-trends/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

From a TC2000 workshop with educator Michael Thompson: how to build a condition that finds stocks at an **all-time high during the trading day**, using built-in TC2000 variables. His own filters on top — "**my requirements of price > $10 and daily volume > 50,000 are my specs and differ from those used by Michael**" — and the point of it: "one can create this condition set in a column (or scan) and use it to find all stocks in a watchlist that are hitting all-time highs during the trading day. **One can then plot the stocks on a monthly chart, draw in the green line tops (an all-time high not surpassed for at least 3 months) and find stocks having a green line break-out (GLB) during the current day.**" A GLB "with a high volume buzz" is what he is after. He also notes TC2000 was about to run natively on Macs and iPads.

## Key claims

- An intraday ATH condition set — the mechanism behind catching a GLB *on the day* rather than after the close.
- His universe filters: price > $10 and daily volume > 50,000, explicitly his own rather than the instructor's.
- The manual step survives automation: green lines are still drawn by hand on the monthly chart.
- "High volume buzz" as the confirming statistic on the breakout day.
