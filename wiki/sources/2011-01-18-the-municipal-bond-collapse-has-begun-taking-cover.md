# WW 2011-01-18 — The municipal bond collapse has begun — an industry-sorted new-lows alarm

**Source:** [The municipal bond collapse has begun–taking cover](../../raw/posts/2011-01-18-the-municipal-bond-collapse-has-begun-taking-cover.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2011/01/the-municipal-bond-collapse-has-begun-taking-cover/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

116 new 52-week lows on an *up* day, the most since August 2010; sorted by industry, almost all municipal-bond funds; their weekly charts "a uniform pattern similar to that shown by the bank stocks in 2008, as they *began* their horrific descent" (his June 8, 2008 BAC post at $30.50; BAC bottomed at $2.53). TFI as the exemplar: a classic Weinstein Stage 4 with heavy red volume (institutional selling); the weekly GMMA in BWR — "more like what I call a submarine pattern." "As a technical analyst, I act on what the market behavior tells me, not what people opine" — Meredith Whitney's warning noted, not relied on. "It may be time for me to take most of my profits off of the table" while the GMI and GMI-R are at maximum: "these indicators only detect a change in trend *after* it has occurred. Such is the fate of trend following indicators." Investors Intelligence bears at 19.1% — a top warning.

## Key claims

- Sorting the day's new lows by industry as a diagnostic; a sector in Stage 4 with institutional volume as an early alarm.
- The 2008 bank template applied to munis; "can the rest of the market be far behind?"
- GMI at maximum ≠ safety: trend followers detect after the fact.
- II bears 19.1% as a top tell.
