# WW 2008-02-25 — Nibbling in a down-trend — with close stops and written calls

**Source:** [GMI: 0; GMI-R: 0; Nibbling a little at rocket stocks](../../raw/posts/2008-02-25-gmi-0-gmi-r-0-nibbling-a-little-at-rocket-stocks.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2008/02/gmi-0-gmi-r-0-nibbling-a-little-at-rocket-stocks/) · tier: trade_example

## What it covers

Buying inside a down-trend, deliberately and small. With the GMI and GMI-R both at zero — "this means that most
stocks are in a down-trend. I have therefore been on the sideline for most of the decline" — he notes that "a
small group of sectors and stocks have been rocketing up. **It seems to me it may be prudent to nibble a little
at these strong performers.**" The screen: IBD 100 names from the past year's lists that "hit a new high during
the past week and meet **my strict technical criteria for rocket stocks**" — MOS, MTL, CF, SID, POT, PCLN, CMED,
RRC, OIL, SWN, PRXL, mostly fertiliser, coal and energy. Off-list, FDG on a monthly chart showing "a **high volume
break-out to a new all-time high**, breaking its peak from September 2005… **this monthly chart of FDG looks like
a three year cup-with-handle formation.**" The risk control is size plus stops plus an income overlay: "**I may
nibble at some of these stocks and place close stops in case they reverse. Many of these stocks have rich
premiums on their March call options. I may buy some shares and immediately write March calls.**" A **buy-write**
into a hostile tape — the premium pays for some of the risk the market is imposing. T2108 at 41%, "in the neutral
range"; Judy is interested in RIMM, "bounced off of its 30 week average. (She, of course, started buying it in
the low 80's!)" 

## Key claims

- **Nibbling is a defined mode**, not a lapse: small size, close stops, and only at names making new highs against the tape.
- **A buy-write as the down-trend overlay**: rich call premiums on strong names turn a risky long into a partially funded one.
- A **multi-year cup-with-handle on a monthly chart** (FDG, three years) treated as the same pattern at a slower scale.
- Strength during a decline concentrated by sector — fertiliser, coal, energy — which is the sector read doing its work.
- Judy credited again, including for being earlier than him.
