# WW 2021-08-05 — The climax top — a large gain on the highest volume in many days

**Source:** [Blog post: Day 49 of $QQQ short term up-trend; $BNTX signals climax top?](../../raw/posts/2021-08-05-blog-post-day-49-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-bntx-signals-climax-top.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2021/08/blog-post-day-49-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-bntx-signals-climax-top/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**A top signal for an individual stock, defined by the combination rather than by either element.** "**When a
stock that has been on a tear has a large gain on the highest volume in many days it can be a sign of a top.**
Note that it is **extended outside the upper 15.2 daily Bollinger band.**" Three conditions stacking: an advance
already well established, an unusually large single-day gain, and the **highest volume in many days** — with the
band excursion confirming the extension. The logic is the inverse of a breakout: on a breakout, high volume means
demand arriving; at the end of a long advance, high volume on a large gain means the last buyers arriving, with
nobody left behind them. That is why the same volume reading means opposite things depending on where in the move
it occurs — the clearest instance in the corpus of a signal whose meaning is positional. BNTX is the case, with
the earlier oversold-bounce entry ("N") marked on the same chart after a VCP.

## Key claims

- **A climax top = a large gain on the highest volume in many days, in a stock already on a tear** — the combination, not any single element.
- Extension outside the upper 15.2 Bollinger Band is the confirming condition.
- **The same volume reading means opposite things by position**: demand arriving on a breakout, the last buyers arriving at the end of an advance.
- Charted alongside the earlier OSB entry in the same stock, so the full round trip is visible.
