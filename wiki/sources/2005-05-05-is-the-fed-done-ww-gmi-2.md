# WW 2005-05-05 — Is the Fed done? — Loeb on pyramiding; the pilot buy's root

**Source:** [Is the Fed Done?  WW-GMI: +2](../../raw/posts/2005-05-05-is-the-fed-done-ww-gmi-2.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/05/is-the-fed-done-ww-gmi-2/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Gerald Loeb, *The Battle for Investment Survival* (1965): buy 100 shares, sell if it does not act right, buy 100 of the next, add 200 as it advances, "first thing you know, if it's good I am long a big line of the right stock with a small initial risk… the risk in the stock I bought on the way up was mainly the risk of my paper profits." "All of the great stock traders speak of pyramiding their purchases. Don't plunge into a stock and risk a lot of money. The idea is to make a small pilot buy, test the waters, and then add more."

## Key claims

- Loeb's pyramiding as the source of the pilot buy.
- "Battle for survival" — the framing.
