# WW 2005-06-13 — GMI back to +5; the first chart of GMI performance; a buy-stop entry on BTU

**Source:** [GMI back to +5; Chart of GMI performance; BTU](../../raw/posts/2005-06-13-gmi-back-to-5-chart-of-gmi-performance-btu.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2005/06/gmi-back-to-5-chart-of-gmi-performance-btu/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The first published chart of the GMI against the QQQQ (April–June 2005): steady at zero as the QQQQ bottomed in late April, 1 on May 4, 1–3 for a few days, then "consistently registered 3 or above" since May 18. And an entry technique in full: BTU gapped to a new high Friday at 52.71; "I placed a stop order Sunday night to buy the stock if it traded above Friday's intraday high price of 52.91. After the buy stop was executed around 53.10 on Monday, I placed a sell stop in just below Friday's" low.

## Key claims

- The first GMI performance chart, June 2005.
- Entry via a buy-stop above the prior day's high, with a sell-stop under the prior day's low placed immediately.
