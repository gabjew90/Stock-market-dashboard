# WW 2017-01-08 — Why component 1 exists, in one sentence; SHOP's GLB and a missed alert

**Source:** [$QQQ short term up-trend reaches 20th day; GLB: $SHOP; bull markets like sex](../../raw/posts/2017-01-08-qqq-short-term-up-trend-reaches-20th-day-glb-shop-bull-markets-like-sex.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2017/01/qqq-short-term-up-trend-reaches-20th-day-glb-shop-bull-markets-like-sex/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

Two contributions. **The plainest statement of what GMI component 1 is for**: the GMI was at 5 of 6 because
"my 10 day successful new high indicator was negative. Only one third of the stocks that hit a new high 10 days
ago closed higher than they did 10 days ago. **When stocks that hit a new high do not continue to rise, it is
one sign of potential weakness I track.**" And **a breadth divergence at an index all-time high**: the QQQ had
just broken above its April 2000 high of 120.50, closing the week at 121.93, yet "only 138 of almost 5,000
stocks hit a new high on Friday… On December 5-12, the number of stocks hitting new highs each day ranged from
430-908. The last 5 days the range was 88-306… only 39% of all stocks rose, compared to about two thirds of the
Nasdaq 100 stocks… **there is tremendous heterogeneity in the way stocks are performing.**" His response is not
to act on it: "**Regardless, I must ride the trend until it ends. You know better than to ask me (or anyone
else) to predict when that will be.** As they say, bull markets are like sex, they feel best just before they
end." The trade is SHOP, which "convincingly broke above its green line (GLB) to an all-time high, **after
consolidating about 4 months around its green line**" on "a wonderful high volume break-out" — with the exit
attached in the same breath: "**If I bought it I would exit if it returns below its green line.**" He had a
TC2000 alert set and missed it: "**a TC2000 alert I had set went off but I did not notice it until this
weekend.**" On the 16-year round trip in the QQQ he is pointed: those "who say they never take a loss because
the market eventually comes back are vindicated — if they held on for 16 years!"

## Key claims

- **Component 1, stated as a purpose rather than a formula**: new highs that do not follow through are the weakness he is watching for.
- A **thinning new-high count while the index prints an all-time high** is a documented divergence — but one he explicitly declines to trade against.
- The GLB setup is improved, not invalidated, by a long consolidation *around* the green line — four months here.
- The exit rule is stated at the moment of entry, every time.
- **A missed TC2000 alert in 2017**, six years before the WING miss he wrote up in 2023 — the same failure mode, twice documented.
- The 16-year QQQ round trip as the argument against "it always comes back": vindication requires surviving an 84% drawdown first.
