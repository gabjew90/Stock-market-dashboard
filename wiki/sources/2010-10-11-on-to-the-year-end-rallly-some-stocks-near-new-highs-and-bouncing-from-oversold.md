# WW 2010-10-11 — The ultra-ETF measurement repeated; a bounce scan built for students

**Source:** [On to the year end rally; some stocks near new highs and bouncing from oversold](../../raw/posts/2010-10-11-on-to-the-year-end-rallly-some-stocks-near-new-highs-and-bouncing-from-oversold.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2010/10/on-to-the-year-end-rallly-some-stocks-near-new-highs-and-bouncing-from-oversold/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The ultra-ETF measurement run again mid-trend, and a scan built for a classroom. **The measurement**: 24 days
into a QQQQ up-trend, "the QQQQ has risen +8.9%, the SPY +6.3%, and the DIA +6.4%. On the other hand the ultra
long index ETF's have done much better, **QLD +18.5% and the TYH +26.7%. So, we see again the virtue of buying
the ultra long index ETF's at the beginning of a QQQQ short term up-trend.**" **The duration base rate**: "**Eight
QQQQ short term up-trends have lasted between 31-86 days in the past couple of years. So the current up-trend may
have a ways to go**" — the day count used to size confidence rather than to exit. **The scan**, run for his
undergraduates' trading competition over his IBD 100 and New America watchlist: "stocks that have **recently hit
a new high then became oversold and just bounced off of or broke above their 30 day averages**," filtered on
revenue growth, displayed with a fundamental column set (EPS change for the most recent quarter, prior quarter
and annual; PE; price against price a year ago; % change in revenue last quarter; P/S; ROE) and **sorted by
ROE**. Two teaching conditions attached: the list is "a starting point *for them to research*," and "**All
students must have an objective set of trading rules approved by me before they can trade.**" The stop is
specified: "a GTC stop loss **somewhere below the stock's 30 day average**." Breadth footnote — 44% of the
Nasdaq-100 with MACD above its signal line, down from 63% a week earlier.

## Key claims

- **The ultra-ETF case restated as an in-trend measurement**, not a backtest: 24 days in, QLD had roughly doubled the index's gain and TYH tripled it.
- **Eight up-trends of 31–86 days** is the base rate he was working from in 2010 — used to argue the trend had room, not to time an exit.
- **A bounce-after-a-new-high scan**, run over a curated fundamental watchlist rather than the full universe.
- **The stop is part of the scan's output**: GTC, below the 30-day average, stated with the list.
- Pedagogy as method: a scan is a research starting point, and pre-approved written rules are a precondition for trading at all.
- MACD breadth (share of Nasdaq-100 above their signal line) tracked week over week as a short-term weakness gauge.
