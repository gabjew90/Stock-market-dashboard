# WW 2006-06-12 — GMI 0; 185 submarines, 8 rockets; brainwashed against shorting

**Source:** [GMI: 0;  185 Submarines, 8 Rockets; Brainwashed against shorting](../../raw/posts/2006-06-12-gmi-0-185-submarines-8-rockets-brainwashed-against-shorting.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2006/06/gmi-0-185-submarines-8-rockets-brainwashed-against-shorting/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

All six GMI components negative; "I cannot profitably trade growth stocks when the IBD Mutual Fund Index is below its 50 day average" — and it is now under its 200-day; GMI-S 0, GMI-L 19 ("significant market weakness that could persist for quite a while"); D-21; a new Bullish Stage 2 statistic in the IBD100 table (68% of IBD100 in Stage 2 vs 38% of the universe). "I have been 100% cash in my pension for several weeks… I am a chicken and… go to cash at the first sign of market weakness. In this way, I have avoided all significant market declines since 1995, but have not missed out on the subsequent rises." The TC2005 scan: 185 submarines, 8 rockets — "too bad that we have all been brainwashed into thinking that shorting stocks is too risky. It is much more risky to buy stocks in a market down-trend."

## Key claims

- Component 6 in 2006 words: growth stocks are untradeable with the IBD MF index under its 50-day.
- GMI-L at 19: low readings in the long averages imply persistent weakness.
- "There is plenty of time to jump back on board once the market has confirmed a new up-trend."
- Submarine/rocket census as a market read (185 : 8).
- "Brainwashed against shorting": buying in a down-trend is the riskier act.
