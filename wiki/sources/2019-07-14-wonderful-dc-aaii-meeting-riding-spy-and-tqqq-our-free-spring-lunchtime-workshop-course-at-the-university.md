## What it covers

After presenting the undergraduate course's slides to the DC AAII chapter with a focus on index ETFs rather than growth stocks ("most appropriate for a largely gray haired audience, like myself"), he states what he is doing: riding SPY and TQQQ while the daily RWB pattern in QQQ and SPY is "just too strong to ignore." "I do not argue with the market or listen to media pundits. I simply follow the general market's trend until it ends." Also links his 2012 Houston TC2000 webinar.

## Key claims

- "I am riding SPY and TQQQ during this market up-trend. The daily RWB pattern is just too strong to ignore."
- "I do not argue with the market or listen to media pundits. I simply follow the general market's trend until it ends."
- Index-ETF investing presented as the conservative version of the method for older investors.
