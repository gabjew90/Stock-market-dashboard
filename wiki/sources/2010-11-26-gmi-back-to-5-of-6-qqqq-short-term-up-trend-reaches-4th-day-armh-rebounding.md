# WW 2010-11-26 — Monthly, weekly AND daily — the three-timeframe requirement

**Source:** [GMI back to 5 (of 6); QQQQ short term up-trend reaches 4th day; ARMH rebounding?](../../raw/posts/2010-11-26-gmi-back-to-5-of-6-qqqq-short-term-up-trend-reaches-4th-day-armh-rebounding.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2010/11/gmi-back-to-5-of-6-qqqq-short-term-up-trend-reaches-4th-day-armh-rebounding/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

**The three-timeframe requirement in one sentence**: "**I only buy stocks that have monthly, weekly AND daily
up-trends in place.**" That is the conjunction behind the three-chart setup procedure documented elsewhere,
stated as a purchase precondition rather than as a workflow. ARMH is the worked example, and it is read on both
scales: on the daily, it "broke above its 50 day average and its 30 day average, and **the 10 day average is
rising above the 30 day**"; on the weekly GMMA, "the **RWB pattern typical of rocket stocks**. The short term
averages (red) are well above the rising long term averages (blue)." **And the anticipation lesson, relearned in
public**: "**I have to relearn the lesson from time-to-time that I should not try to anticipate a change in
trend, but to wait for the signal.** Just when it looked like the new QQQQ short term up-trend would expire, it
reasserted itself." With the Day-5 rule stated in its positive form: "**generally, if a new up-trend can reach 5
days, it has legs to continue.**"

## Key claims

- **"I only buy stocks that have monthly, weekly AND daily up-trends in place"** — the three-chart procedure stated as a conjunction, not a sequence.
- The daily condition worked out concretely: above the 50- and 30-day averages, with the 10-day rising above the 30-day.
- **The anticipation error is named as recurring** — he nearly wrote off an up-trend that then reasserted itself.
- The Day-5 rule in its up-trend form: reaching 5 days means "it has legs to continue."
- The leaders are listed as the confirming evidence that the trend resumed (AAPL, AMZN, APKT, CAVM, CTXS, RVBD, FFIV).
