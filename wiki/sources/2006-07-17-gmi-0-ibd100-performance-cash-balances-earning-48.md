# WW 2006-07-17 — Why he tracks a fixed IBD 100 list; what cash was paying

**Source:** [GMI: 0; IBD100 performance; Cash balances earning 4.8%?](../../raw/posts/2006-07-17-gmi-0-ibd100-performance-cash-balances-earning-48.md) · [original](https://wishingwealthblog.com/2006/07/gmi-0-ibd100-performance-cash-balances-earning-48/) · tier: teaching

## What it covers

The methodological objection behind every IBD 100 audit on the wiki, stated plainly: "**I think that the way
IBD tracks the performance of the IBD 100 is very misleading because they keep replacing underperforming stocks
with better performing stocks. When you look at the performance of the IBD 100 list published on a particular
date (5/15) we get a more accurate indication of how these types of stocks have performed.**" That is why his
audits always name a publication date and follow that frozen list. The result on this occasion: since 5/15 only
19% of the IBD 100 had advanced against 27% of his 4,000-stock universe; none of the IBD 100 and under 1% of the
universe hit a new high that day. Component 6 read at two horizons — the IBD mutual fund index "below both its
50 day and 200 day moving averages and the 50 day is close to crossing below the 200 day. Thus, **the
professional growth fund managers are suffering in this market, another indication to stay away from growth
stocks now**." And a detail about cash that the wiki has not recorded anywhere: "With **Fidelity Cash Reserves
paying me about 4.8%** for the cash balance in my account, it is nice to be on the sidelines. **What is your
brokerage firm paying you for your cash balances?** You may be surprised/disappointed." Also: extreme-low
calibration in progress (300 new lows against 16 new highs, but "many of my indicators, while low, are not at
extreme lows" — 14% of the Nasdaq-100 above their 30-day average against 9% a month earlier), a TC2005 scan
returning 7 rockets to 422 submarines, and deep in-the-money puts in the IRA described as "a wonderful strategy
for this market," with O'Neil's book on shorting recommended.

## Key claims

- **The fixed-list method has a stated justification**: a continuously re-constituted index cannot measure how its selections performed, so he freezes a published list and follows it.
- **Cash yield is part of the cash decision.** Sitting out at 4.8% is a different proposition from sitting out at zero — the only place he has quantified it.
- Component 6 checked against the 200-day as well as the 50-day, with the 50/200 cross approaching read as confirmation.
- **A low reading is not an extreme reading**, and he calibrates against the prior trough rather than against the scale.
- The rocket-to-submarine ratio (7 vs 422) used as a one-number summary of the tape.
