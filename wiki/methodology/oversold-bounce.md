# The oversold bounce (OSB / ATHOSB)

**OSB** — oversold bounce — is the entry setup Dr. Wish states he *prefers to the breakout itself*. A strong stock that has recently made an all-time high pulls back until it is oversold, then turns up; he buys the turn and puts the stop just under the bounce low. The whole point is the stop: at a [GLB](green-line-breakouts.md) the correct stop location is genuinely hard to name in advance, and at a bounce it is obvious.

When he wants to stress the all-time-high prerequisite he writes it **ATHOSB** (ATH + OSB) — "one of my favorite set-ups." ([WW 2022-06-15](../../raw/posts/2022-06-15-blog-post-one-of-my-favorite-set-ups-athosb-found-one-stock-sqm-check-it-out-and-see-the-tutorial.md))

## Why he prefers it to a breakout

The clearest statement of the doctrine came after a failed CAVA breakout in July 2023:

> "I find it more difficult to control my loss with a failed break-out. Where should one place their stop loss? It is not always easy to designate that in advance. However, with the OSB (oversold bounce) set-up it is much easier to determine the price where the set-up has failed. Place the stop just below the low of the bounce, the place where the black dot appears." ([WW 2023-07-13](../../raw/posts/2023-07-13-blog-post-cavas-break-out-fails-why-i-prefer-my-osb-set-up-over-break-outs-see-daily-charts-of-cava-and-mlm.md))

CAVA opened above the prior day's high and closed back below its breakout line; a buyer at that open carried a large loss with no pre-defined exit. The same argument, put positively: "The beauty of buying a strong stock that may be bouncing is that if the bounce fails it is immediately apparent and I can exit promptly with a small loss." ([WW 2022-06-15](../../raw/posts/2022-06-15-blog-post-one-of-my-favorite-set-ups-athosb-found-one-stock-sqm-check-it-out-and-see-the-tutorial.md))

**He had said the same thing five years earlier**, in the post defining the green dot: "I have learned over the years that I have more success trading strong up-trending stocks that are turning up after they have had a small decline **than buying break-outs**... if I think a stock has bottomed and turned up, **I know exactly where to get out if I am wrong**." ([WW 2018-03-11](../../raw/posts/2018-03-11-green-dot-strategy-defined.md)) So the 2023 post is the sharpest statement of the preference, not its origin — the reasoning is stable from at least 2016 (BOS) through 2018 to 2023.

This is the same risk logic he had already given for [BOS](stock-selection.md) in 2016 — the bounce entry narrows the entry-to-stop distance — now stated as a preference over the GLB moment rather than an alternative to it. **OSB is not a new idea in 2021 so much as a renamed and tightened BOS:** BOS (2016) triggered off the lower 15.2 Bollinger Band; OSB adds an explicit recent-ATH prerequisite and a stochastic-based trigger. Both remain in use, and he still cites the 15.2 band as a supporting condition on OSB charts ([WW 2023-07-20](../../raw/posts/2023-07-20-blog-post-day-57-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-list-of-4-growth-stocks-with-black-dot-osb-set-up-see-daily-chart.md)).

## The setup

1. **Recent all-time high (or recent yearly high).** The stock must have been strong enough to reach a high before pulling back. "I am looking for a stock that has been strong enough to reach a high and then becomes oversold and starts to move up." ([WW 2023-07-09](../../raw/posts/2023-07-09-blog-post-day-48-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-my-favorite-set-up-oversold-bounce-black-dot-after-a-recent-yearl.md))
2. **Above its last green line top.** Named as a filter on essentially every worked OSB example ([WW 2021-05-06](../../raw/posts/2021-05-06-blog-post-day-2-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-but-378-new-highs-among-us-stocks-non-qqq-type-stocks-still-risi.md), [WW 2023-07-20](../../raw/posts/2023-07-20-blog-post-day-57-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-list-of-4-growth-stocks-with-black-dot-osb-set-up-see-daily-chart.md)) — the same overhead-supply logic as the GLB.
3. **Oversold, then turning up.** The oversold measure is "based on the daily 10.1, 10.4 and 10.4.4 stochastics indicators," rendered on his chart as the black and green dots. ([WW 2022-06-15](../../raw/posts/2022-06-15-blog-post-one-of-my-favorite-set-ups-athosb-found-one-stock-sqm-check-it-out-and-see-the-tutorial.md)) He has never published the exact composite rule for the black dot in this form — he invites readers to "see if you can figure out the signals" — but the individual dot definitions are in the [glossary](glossary.md).
4. **Price > $200.** Added as a scan filter in August 2021 — see [stock selection](stock-selection.md#price-level-preference). ([WW 2021-08-11](../../raw/posts/2021-08-11-blog-post-why-buying-stocks-over-200-works-better-revisited-examples-bntx-and-gs.md))
5. **Fundamentals checked before buying.** The scan runs against his ~700-stock IBD/MarketSmith watchlist; IBD composite rating, RS, expected earnings growth and the **earnings date** are checked before committing. ([WW 2022-06-15](../../raw/posts/2022-06-15-blog-post-one-of-my-favorite-set-ups-athosb-found-one-stock-sqm-check-it-out-and-see-the-tutorial.md), [WW 2023-08-03](../../raw/posts/2023-08-03-blog-post-day-67-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-115-new-us-highs-and-44-lows-11-stocks-have-osb-after-recent-ath.md))

## Entry and stop

- **Entry:** buy the bounce day, or shortly after, once the dot has printed. He has bought in the after-market on the signal day ([WW 2023-07-20](../../raw/posts/2023-07-20-blog-post-day-57-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-list-of-4-growth-stocks-with-black-dot-osb-set-up-see-daily-chart.md)).
- **Stop:** immediately, below the low of the bounce day — the day the black dot appears. "If I buy any OSB, I place an immediate stop below the bounce." ([WW 2023-08-03](../../raw/posts/2023-08-03-blog-post-day-67-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-115-new-us-highs-and-44-lows-11-stocks-have-osb-after-recent-ath.md)) Worked numerically on WWE: "I bought a little in the after market and will exit if it trades below Thursday's low (104.34)." ([WW 2023-07-20](../../raw/posts/2023-07-20-blog-post-day-57-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-list-of-4-growth-stocks-with-black-dot-osb-set-up-see-daily-chart.md))
- **Failure is immediate and cheap** — the defining property of the setup. "If I buy an OSB stock I sell immediately if the bounce fails." ([WW 2021-05-06](../../raw/posts/2021-05-06-blog-post-day-2-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-but-378-new-highs-among-us-stocks-non-qqq-type-stocks-still-risi.md))
- **Earnings are a separate risk.** "Holding through earnings has been treacherous lately." ([WW 2023-08-03](../../raw/posts/2023-08-03-blog-post-day-67-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-115-new-us-highs-and-44-lows-11-stocks-have-osb-after-recent-ath.md))

## Market-state dependence

The setup is explicitly conditional on the market environment, in the same way [stock selection](stock-selection.md) is: "In a weak market most set-ups fail, so it will be interesting to see if SQM works out. In a strong up-trending market this is a powerful set-up." ([WW 2022-06-15](../../raw/posts/2022-06-15-blog-post-one-of-my-favorite-set-ups-athosb-found-one-stock-sqm-check-it-out-and-see-the-tutorial.md)) He does publish OSB candidates during QQQ short-term down-trends ([WW 2021-05-06](../../raw/posts/2021-05-06-blog-post-day-2-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-but-378-new-highs-among-us-stocks-non-qqq-type-stocks-still-risi.md), [WW 2021-05-23](../../raw/posts/2021-05-23-blog-post-13th-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-revised-osb-oversold-bounce-scan-yields-gs-and-bgfv.md)), but with the caveat attached — in May 2021, with the GMI on a Red signal, he noted it was "a very difficult time to be in the market profitably." See the [market-state playbook](../playbooks/market-state.md).

## Reading the scan output

He sorts OSB scan results by **close ÷ lowest price of the past 250 days** — a crude "how far has this already run" ranking, putting the strongest movers at the top. BELFB at 3.3× its low; NVDA topped the August 2023 list on the same measure. ([WW 2023-07-09](../../raw/posts/2023-07-09-blog-post-day-48-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-my-favorite-set-up-oversold-bounce-black-dot-after-a-recent-yearl.md), [WW 2023-08-03](../../raw/posts/2023-08-03-blog-post-day-67-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-115-new-us-highs-and-44-lows-11-stocks-have-osb-after-recent-ath.md))

**Industry clustering is a second-order signal.** When 6 of 24 stocks passing the setup on one day were all residential-construction names, he read it as institutional intent: "The fact that so many stocks in the same industry had oversold bounces (OSB) after a recent yearly high suggests to me big buying interest." ([WW 2023-07-10](../../raw/posts/2023-07-10-blog-post-day-49-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-6-of-24-growth-stocks-came-up-in-the-black-dot-set-up-discussed-y.md))

## Combining OSB with a GLB

The two are complements, not rivals — the GLB identifies the stock, the OSB times the entry. VRT is the worked case: a green line breakout to an ATH on 2023-08-02 on volume "way above average," then a pullback, then a black-dot OSB on 2023-09-13. "If it holds Wednesday's low of 36.22 I would become interested." ([WW 2023-09-13](../../raw/posts/2023-09-13-blog-post-day-10-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-42-us-new-highs-and-63-lows-vrt-has-osb-oversold-bounce-and-a-rec.md))

The pattern recurs with the later blue-dot variant: SHAK, a recent GLB, fell back below its green line, printed a Blue Dot OSB, bounced off its 8-day EMA and retook the green line. ([WW 2025-07-09](../../raw/posts/2025-07-09-blog-post-day-51-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-recent-glb-shak-has-retaken-green-line-after-a-recent-blue-dot-os.md))
