This is a very volatile market.   Time to be cautious even though the QQQ remains in a short and longer term up-trend. This is not the time to make large bets.

This weekly Guppy [GMMA](http://www.guppytraders.com/gup329.shtml) chart of the SPY shows a [bullish RWB pattern](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2010/09/introducing-red-white-and-blue-rwb-stocks-the-pattern-of-rockets/).   The large cap nontech stocks represented in this index ETF (S&P 500) are stronger than the growth stocks represented in the QQQ. It looks like the SPY is moving up after a period of consolidation.   All of the shorter term averages (red) are rising above the longer term averages (blue).
