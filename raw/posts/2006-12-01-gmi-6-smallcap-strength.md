The GMI is still 6, as 50-55% of the stocks in the Nasdaq 100, S&P 500 and Dow 30 indexes advanced on Thursday. [![Gmi1130_1](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/gmi1130_1.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/gmi1130_1.gif) There were 327 new yearly highs in my universe of 4,000 stocks.  Interestingly, [IJR](http://finance.yahoo.com/q?s=ijr) (S&P 600 SmallCap ETF) showed a little strength on Thursday, causing the GMI-S to climb to 63.  As you probably know, small cap stocks are said to show strength around January.  Thursday was the 73rd day in the current QQQQ up-trend.
