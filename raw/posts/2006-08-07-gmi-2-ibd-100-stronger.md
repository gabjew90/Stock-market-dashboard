The GMI fell back to +2; there were only 53 new highs (82 lows) in my universe of 4,000 stocks.  On Monday, 24% of the Nasdaq 100 stocks advanced, along with 33% of the S&P 500 and Dow 30 stocks.  The GMI-S fell to 69, with more weakness in the short term indicators for the QQQQ and IJR ETF’s.  [![Gmi0807](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/gmi0807.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/gmi0807.gif) In contrast, the SPY and DIA indicators remain strong.  Monday was the 23rd day in the current short term decline in the QQQQ.  However, the IBD 100 stocks of 5/15 outperformed the general market on Monday.  54% of the IBD 100 stocks from 5/15 advanced while only 32% of the 4,000 stocks in my universe advanced.  I do not yet know if the IBD Mutual Fund Index has turned positive.  My TC2005 scan of the market yielded 282 submarines and nine rockets.  With the GMI at +2, it is a good time to sit back in cash. The growing strength in the IBD 100 growth stocks suggests that there is interest in acquiring stocks with good fundamentals.
