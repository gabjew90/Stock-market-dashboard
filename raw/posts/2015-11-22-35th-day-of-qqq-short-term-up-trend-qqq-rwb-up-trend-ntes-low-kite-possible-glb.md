Curiously, there were still more now lows than highs on Friday.   This may be because the SPY and DIA are not as strong as the QQQ, which is composed mainly of nonfinancial tech stocks.   The QQQ is clearly in the beginning of a [RWB up-trend](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2010/09/introducing-red-white-and-blue-rwb-stocks-the-pattern-of-rockets/) and heading to new highs.
