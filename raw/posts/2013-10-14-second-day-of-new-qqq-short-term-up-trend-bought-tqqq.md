I nibbled a little at TQQQ; I couldn’t resist! If we get to a 5th day of the short term   up-trend I will buy more. [TQQQ is a leveraged bullish ETF](http://finance.yahoo.com/q/pr?s=TQQQ+Profile) that tracks 3X the movement of the QQQ. Other less risky alternatives are [QLD (2X)](http://finance.yahoo.com/q/pr?s=qld&ql=1) or just the [QQQ](http://www.invescopowershares.com/products/overview.aspx?ticker=QQQ) itself. TQQQ advanced +2.07% on Monday, compared with +.71% for QQQ and +1.4% for QLD.   Pick your poison!   (Click on chart to enlarge.)
