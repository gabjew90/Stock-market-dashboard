We completed the 6th day of the new QQQ down-trend on Wednesday.   The [Worden T2108](http://www.yachtsmanretirementinvesting.com/id153.html) indicator is at 10%, up a little.   I wish I had the courage to buy a little of QLD.   The Worden T2108 indicator rarely goes into single digits and is a sign of an extremely oversold market. I saw this [interesting article](http://finance.yahoo.com/news/Chartbased-trading-behind-big-apf-1691830324.html?x=0&sec=topStories&pos=5&asset=&ccode=) about technical analysis this morning.   Technical analysis always works better than fundamental analysis when markets decline a lot.   Technical analysis tracks human psychology and behavior. Fundamental analysis is based on company “facts” and subjective interpretations   about their business.
