The GMI  and GMI-S are all at their maximum values.  65% of the Nasdaq 100 stocks closed above their 30 day averages.  64% of the IBD 100 list from 2/5 rose on Friday and 20% of them hit new highs.  There were 256 new highs in my universe of 4,000 stocks on Friday.  [![Gmi0216_1](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/gmi0216_1.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/gmi0216_1.gif) Friday was the third day in the current QQQQ up-trend.   The SPY has closed above its 10 week average for 29 straight weeks.  With so many people skeptical of this rise, this up-trend may have further to go.  CEPH still looks like a cup with handle  in the making.  GLD (pegged to the price of gold) also appears to be in an up-trend. NUAN, a cup with handle break-out, also looks strong.
