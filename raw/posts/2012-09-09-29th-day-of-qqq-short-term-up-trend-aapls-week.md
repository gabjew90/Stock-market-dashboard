Both the GMI and GMI2 are at their maximum readings.  [![](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/GMI09072012-300x292.jpg)](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/GMI09072012.jpg)   The GMI buy signal of July 30 remains in effect. 50% of the Nasdaq 100 stocks have now closed with their MACD above its signal line, a sign of short term strength. However, the Worden T2108 indicator is now at 78%, very close to extreme overbought territory. This week AAPL announces the new iphone 5.   The reaction to the announcement will be very interesting to watch and may provide an indication of the health of the market, at least with regard to tech stocks. This *weekly* chart of AAPL shows how nicely AAPL has progressed since breaking a green line top to an all-time high a few weeks ago. Click on chart to enlarge.
