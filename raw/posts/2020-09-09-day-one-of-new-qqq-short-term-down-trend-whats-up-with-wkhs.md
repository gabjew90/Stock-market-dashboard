The up-trend that just ended lasted a record (since 2006) 107 days. About 40% of new QQQ short term down-trends end within 5 days. It does not mean a new bear market. But if it lasts for more than 5 days it could lead to a significant decline and I would accumulate SQQQ.

If a stock can hold up after the market’s recent steep decline and break to new all-time highs there must be something going for it. Check out this weekly chart of WKHS, which manufactures electric powered trucks. The stock went vertical, up 7x to a GLB  and consolidated for nine weeks and is now moving up again. Flag like patterns like this can be explosive. The move out of the flag can be as large as the preceding flag pole (here about $17).
