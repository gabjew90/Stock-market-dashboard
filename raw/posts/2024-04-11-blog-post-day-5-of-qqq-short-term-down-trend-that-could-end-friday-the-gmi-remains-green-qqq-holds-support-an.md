Thus far, QQQ has held above last week’s low of 435.11 and has reclaimed its 4 and 10 week averages. My adapted weekly [GMMA](https://school.stockcharts.com/doku.php?id=technical_indicators:ma_ribbon:guppy-multiple-moving-average) chart shows QQQ to remain in a sustained RWB up-trend. The dotted line represents each weekly close. The purple dots are the weekly lows. We want all the short term  (red) averages to rise above the longer term averages (blue) with a large white band separating them, hence the RWB pattern.
