The GMI held at 6, but only 14-20% of the stocks in the Nasdaq 100, S&P 500 and Dow 30 indexes advanced on Friday. [![Gmi1027_1](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/gmi1027_1.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/gmi1027_1.gif) There were 258 new highs in my universe of 4,000 stocks.  The GMI-S fell to 94, with the weakening of one of the short term indicators for the QQQQ.  Still, Friday was the 50th day in the current QQQQ up-trend and the 11th week that it has closed above its 10 week average.

The IBD 100 stock lists that I have been following did a little better than the Nasdaq 100 stocks on Friday.  [![Ibdperf1027](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/ibdperf1027.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/ibdperf1027.gif)22-33% of the stocks in the four lists below advanced on Friday.  The list from 10/16 had 14 new highs, but only 43% of these 100 stocks closed higher than they closed on 10/16 when the list was published.  The 14 stocks from the 10/16 list that hit new highs on Friday were:  HXM,CELG,DRIV,JLL,GRMN,ROG,WCG,TV,SIM,

CTSH,BLUD,WRLD,HCSG, CSH.

Monday’s IBD says that the put/call ratio from Friday was .94.  As long as there remains such a high amount of pessimism exhibited by the options players, this rally probably has further to go.  Put/call ratios near one are rare and typically occur only after a sustained decline.  This is a rally that traders love to hate…..
