My adapted daily GUPPY, [GMMA](https://school.stockcharts.com/doku.php?id=technical_indicators:ma_ribbon:guppy-multiple-moving-average) charts tell me when an up-trend has ended. My former student, Richard Moglen, tells how to build them using [TC2000](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yvmRQiTMZFw). As long as the shorter term (red) averages are rising above the longer term (blue) averages with a white space separating them, RWB, the up-trend is intact. When the white space disappears it is time for me to raise stops and/or exit. When the pattern reverses to BWR, I am out or short, see TSLA. The dotted line is the daily close.
