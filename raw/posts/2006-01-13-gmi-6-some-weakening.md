While the GMI is still at +6, the market weakened a little on Thursday.  Only 20-26% of the stocks in the Nasdaq 100, S&P 500 and Dow 30 indexes advanced.  [![Gmi011206](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/gmi011206.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/gmi011206.gif) There were only 291 new highs in my universe of 4,000 stocks.  Thursday was the sixth day of the QQQQ up trend (U-6).  Most of the market indicators declined on Thursday.
