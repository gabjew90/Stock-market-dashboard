---

Another up or flat day on Wednesday will change my QQQ short term trend count to an up-trend.   Tuesday was the 20th day of the current short term down-trend.   The longer term trend remains down.   [September is typically the worst month](http://www.marketwatch.com/story/september-is-the-cruelest-month-2011-08-31?link=MW_story_investinginsight) of the year for the market, so I would not be surprised to see a decline start soon. By the way, I am not concerned that the market is rising on sluggish volume.   The multi-month rally that began last September also came on below average trading volume.
