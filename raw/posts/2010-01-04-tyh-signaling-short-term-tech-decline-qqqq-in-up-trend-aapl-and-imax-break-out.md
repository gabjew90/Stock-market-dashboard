I thought you might like to see why we may be at the beginning of a short term decline in the tech stocks.   TYH is the [3X ultra long tech ETF](http://moneycentral.msn.com/investor/partsub/funds/holdings.asp?ETF=true&Symbol=TYH) and it appears to be in a rising channel.   TYH   recently hit the top of the channel, which   has been followed by a decline four times in the recent past. Nevertheless, the tech stocks remain in a longer term up-trend. The question is whether to sell a little now, or just hold on to see if the channel holds.   Regardless, it seems to me that this may not be the time to take on new tech positions.   Much better to buy closer to the bottom of the channel, if it holds.

[![TYHchannel](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/TYHchannel.jpg)](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/TYHchannel.jpg)
