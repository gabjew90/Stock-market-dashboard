In response to a comment left by a reader who questioned the value of the GMI, I am posting a visual of how the QQQ behaved since 2006 while the GMI was on a Sell (red) or Buy (green) signal.   You will see that the GMI got me out of the major declines.   The cost was that sometimes the GMI gave a false signal and got me out at the end of a short decline.   It then got me back in. I think this is a fine trade-off to make in order to avoid the pain of a serious decline. I am a chicken when it comes to trading. (Click on chart to enlarge.)[![GMIperf10272014](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/GMIperf10272014-300x168.jpg)](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/GMIperf10272014.jpg)The GMI is now back to a Buy signal as of Monday’s close. Two consecutive closes above 3 yield a Buy signal. Monday was also the second day of the new QQQ short term up-trend.

[On September 10, I wrote](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2014/09/19th-day-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-7/) that RCPT had had a [green line break-out (GLB)](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2012/07/24th-day-of-qqq-short-term-up-trend-stage-analysis-and-green-line-charts/).   The stock exploded up after hours on Monday and is trading up about 25%.   Check it out on Tuesday. After the close RCPT [announced good results](http://finance.yahoo.com/news/receptos-reports-positive-phase-2-200100778.html) on their colitis treatment. This is why technical analysis has value.   Somebody apparently anticipated this news and bought into the company huge over a month ago! Their buying was evident in the chart. Here is its hourly chart after Monday’s close. Expect RCPT to open way up on Tuesday.
