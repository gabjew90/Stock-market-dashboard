The GMI rose to +2 on Monday.  [![Gmi1031](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/gmi1031.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/gmi1031.gif) There were five times more new highs than lows (165/31) in my universe of 4,000 stocks, a promising sign. 86% of the Nasdaq 100 stocks advanced Monday, along with 80% of the S&P 500 stocks, but only 57% of the Dow 30 stocks.  The daily QQQQ and SPY indicators are too close to call.  An advance today will turn them positive.  A number of my internal indicators improved yesterday.  36% of stocks are now above their 10 week averages  and 25% are in a short term up-trend.  Almost two-thirds (64%) of the 177 stocks that have doubled in the past year are now above their 30 day averages– the strongest stocks are recovering.  And there are more stocks within 5% of their yearly highs than lows (15% vs. 11%). Monday was day three of the current QQQQ downtrend (D-3).
