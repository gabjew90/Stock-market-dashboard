---

A good way to find winners is to look at stocks that can go up when the market is in a steep decline. I happened to find GME, which broke out of a cup and handle base on above average volume on Thursday.   See [this article](http://bits.blogs.nytimes.com/2013/06/20/xbox-reversal-wont-stop-the-inevitable/?partner=yahoofinance&_r=0) about the GME advance on Thursday. This stock is probably worth watching to see if the break-out can hold. I do not like to go long, however, when the market indexes are weakening.

[![GMIcupandhandle](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/GMIcupandhandle-300x150.jpg)](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/GMIcupandhandle.jpg)

The market is getting very over-sold. Three of the 4   oversold indicators I watch are negative.   The put/call ratio is at 1.29, one of the highest readings of bearish sentiment I have ever seen.   The Worden T2108 is at 21%, in an area where the market could bottom. While I expect a bounce soon, another day with the GMI below 3 will cause a Sell signal to be issued.
