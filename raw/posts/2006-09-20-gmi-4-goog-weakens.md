The GMI fell to 4.  There were only 78 new highs in my universe of 4,000 stocks and a reduction in the number of successful 10 day new highs.  Only 32% of the Nasdaq 100 stocks rose on Tuesday, along with 40% of the S&P 500 stocks and 50% of the Dow 30 stocks.  [![Gmi0919](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/gmi0919.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/gmi0919.gif) The QQQQ up-trend is intact and in its 22nd day…..

GOOG weakened on huge volume, after Yahoo’s news of a reduced sales  forecast.  GOOG is back below its declining trendline but is still in an up-trend. Wednesday’s action should provide clues to whether GOOG’s decline is just a temporary setback or the start of a meaningful decline.
