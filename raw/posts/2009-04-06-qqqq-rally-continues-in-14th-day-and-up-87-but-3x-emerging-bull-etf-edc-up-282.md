There were 11 new highs and 4 new lows in my universe of 4,000 stocks on Monday.   Five of the 11 new highs were on the IBD100 list from March 30:   TNDN, NFLX, GMCR, NTES and CPSI.   All of these but CPSI hit all-time highs, an extraordinary accomplishment, given the market’s decline over the past year.   Any stock trading today at an all-time high is probably worthy of   attention.   I own GMCR and NFLX.   One other stock on the new high list Monday is TSYS, which I wrote about months ago.   It has huge recent earnings increases and has been in a steady up-trend.

I am putting more and more funds back into the market as this up-trend continues. By my indicators, the QQQQ   just completed the 14th day of its short term up-trend.   During that time, the QQQQ has advanced 8.7%, while the ultra long QQQQ ETF, QLD,   has advanced 17.6%.   During QQQQ up-trends I buy QLD.   During this same time period, the emerging markets bull [3X ETF](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2009/02/the-new-3x-etfs-triple-your-pleasure-or-pain/), EDC, has advanced 28.2%.   Those who say the [3X ETF’s](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2009/02/the-new-3x-etfs-triple-your-pleasure-or-pain/) are worthless as trading vehicles (as in J. Cramer) should look at these data.
