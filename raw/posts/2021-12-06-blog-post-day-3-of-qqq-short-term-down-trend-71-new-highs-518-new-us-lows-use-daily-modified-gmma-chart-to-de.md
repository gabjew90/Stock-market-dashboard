This modified [GMMA](https://www.barchart.com/education/technical-indicators/guppy_multiple_moving_average) daily chart shows 6 shorter term exponential moving averages (red lines) and 6 longer term averages (blue lines). I have added a dotted line that shows each daily close.  After a large decline, a close back above all of the averages (shown by arrows) can signal a new up-trend. Right now we would need a close of the QQQ above 391.68.
