Weekly chart shows QQQ still below its critical 30 week average (red line). This is critical level to get through. Note 10 week average (purple dotted line) is crossing below 30 week. A close above both of these averages would indicate to me a likely change in trend. However, prior bear markets have sometimes shown a rebound to the 30 week average and then a failure to hold,  leading to the real major decline.
