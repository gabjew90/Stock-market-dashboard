Things turned weak on Tuesday, and the GMI declined one, to +5.  While there were 300 new yearly highs in my universe of 4,000 stocks, only 32% of the Nasdaq 100 stocks, 24% of the S&P 500 stocks and 17% of the Dow 30 stocks advanced. [![Gmi1004_1](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/gmi1004_1.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/gmi1004_1.gif) Significantly, the DIA and SPY indexes closed below their 30 day averages.  The QQQQ held.  Even worse, the DIA closed below its 30 week average, a major sign of weakness.  The market is not exhibiting any sustained trend, with the indexes closing above, then below their key moving averages.  Housing stocks remain in a down-trend, with interest rates rising.  With earnings season approaching, selected stocks are rising, hinting at good earnings to come.  Nevertheless, this is probably a good time to be mainly in cash on the sidelines.
