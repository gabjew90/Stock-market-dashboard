You can learn about the green dot indicator in my [prior posts](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2018/09/waiting-for-qqq-to-show-a-green-dot/). It is a daily 10.4 stochastic cross and indicated by a green dot on the price chart and the arrow on the stochastic chart below. When a stock or index ETF is in a sustained up-trend (QQQ is not) buying on the green dot signal often works for me. During a down-trend one can sometimes also get a tradable bounce. You can access at my [TC2000 Club](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/club) a video showing how to add the green dots to the price chart.
