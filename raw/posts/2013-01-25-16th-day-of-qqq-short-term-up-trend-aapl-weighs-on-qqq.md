With the large AAPL post-earnings decline (-12.4%), the QQQ has weakened (see daily chart below) and the more sensitive GMI-2 is now at 3. The QQQ has been flat since the New Year pop. With the T2108 at 84, I am a little more cautious than I would be with the GMI still at 6 (of 6). Some of the air came out of ONVO on Thursday and I bought back some of my shares at a lower price than I sold them earlier in the week. AAPL remains in a Weinstein Stage 4 down-trend and should have been sold long ago, [as I had written](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2012/10/4th-day-of-qqq-short-term-down-trend-aapl-weak/) months ago.
