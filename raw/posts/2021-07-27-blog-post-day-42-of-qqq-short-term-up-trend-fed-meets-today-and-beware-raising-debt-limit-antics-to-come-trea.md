In my experience, the end of the month is strong, followed by weakness in the beginning of the next month. Put this together with the end of the debt limit [agreement](https://www.cbo.gov/publication/57152)  that could lead to a government shut-down, and we have a prescription for volatility. Treasury bonds (TLT) may have peaked.
