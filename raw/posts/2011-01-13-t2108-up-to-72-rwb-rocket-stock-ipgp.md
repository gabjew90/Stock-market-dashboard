The Worden T2108 Indicator registered 72%, indicating that 72% of NYSE stocks closed above their simple 40 day moving average.   Readings above 80% typically signal at least a short term top. ARMH, which I wrote about when it broke out from a cup with handle pattern has been very strong.   Check out the weekly [GMMA](http://www.guppytraders.com/gup329.shtml) chart for [IPGP](http://finance.yahoo.com/q/pr?s=IPGP+Profile).   It clearly has an [RWB pattern](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2010/09/introducing-red-white-and-blue-rwb-stocks-the-pattern-of-rockets/) with all of the short term averages (red lines) well above the rising longer term averages (blue).
