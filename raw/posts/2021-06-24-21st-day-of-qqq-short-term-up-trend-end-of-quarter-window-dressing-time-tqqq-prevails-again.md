With the end of the 2nd quarter, mutual funds are dressing up their quarterly reports by buying the strongest stocks. Hence the rally we have seen in the tech stocks as evidenced by QQQ. Since the beginning of the QQQ short term up-trend on 5/26, QQQ has advanced +4.7% while TQQQ has advanced +14.7%. I like to ride TQQQ. Only nine Nasdaq100 stocks have advanced more than TQQQ.
