With the GMI stuck at a middle 3 reading (see table below), I thought it might be useful to analyze the Dow-30 stocks according to the patterns I have been writing about that use a modified [GMMA](http://www.guppytraders.com/gup329.shtml) chart. [![Dow30GMMA024022015](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/Dow30GMMA024022015-275x300.jpg)](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/Dow30GMMA024022015.jpg)It is noteworthy that a little less than one half (14) of the Dow 30 stocks remain in a strong [RWB](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2015/02/red-white-and-blue-rwb-the-rocket-pattern-and-gldaapl-and-the-dow-30-nyse-bear/) up-trend.   Ten stocks are too close to call with their 6 red short term exponential averages overlapping with their 6 blue longer term averages such that there is no white space between the two sets of averages. Finally, six stocks are in solid BWR down-trends with all of their shorter term averages well below their longer term averages. I only want to own stocks showing RWB patterns because they are in strong up-trends. BWR stocks are for shorting, and I would never hold any of them long. The fact that about one third of the Dow 30 stocks are   in No-W patterns (no clear up or down-trends) could signify a market in transition. I provide an example of each pattern below.

No-W, indeterminate trend: JNJ

Only time will tell…………
