The GMI fell to 5 on Wednesday.  [![Gmi0117](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/gmi0117.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/gmi0117.gif) Only 39% of the Nasdaq 100 stocks rose, compared with 50-53% of the S&P 500 and Dow 30 stocks.  Thursday was the sixth day of the current QQQQ up-trend.  Both AAPL and GOOG have been weak, not a good sign.  But the trend of the QQQQ stocks remains up for now.
