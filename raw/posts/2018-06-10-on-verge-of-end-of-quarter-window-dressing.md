title: On verge of end of quarter window dressing
---

I suspect any weakness this week will set up a strong rebound by the end of June as we reach the end of quarter mutual fund [window dressing period](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/w/windowdressing.asp). Watch for the mutual funds to gobble up the strongest stocks so they look brilliant when their quarterly reports are sent to their customers. Meanwhile, the GMI remains at 6 (of 6) and Green.
