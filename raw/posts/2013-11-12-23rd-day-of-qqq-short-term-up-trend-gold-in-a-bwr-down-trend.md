Here is   a weekly [GMMA](http://www.guppytraders.com/gup329.shtml) chart of the gold ETF, GLD.   Gold is in a BWR down-trend with all of the shorter term averages (red) declining below the longer term averages (blue).   This is the opposite of the [RWB rocket stock pattern](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2010/09/introducing-red-white-and-blue-rwb-stocks-the-pattern-of-rockets/) I have discussed before. Note GLD’s RWB pattern during the up-trend through mid-2011. (The green lines are prior multi-month bases at all-time highs.)
