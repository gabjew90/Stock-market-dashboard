The GMI declined on Tuesday.  Break out stocks are failing.  Only 41% of the stocks that hit a new high ten days ago closed higher Tuesday than they did ten days earlier.  [![Gmi0411](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/gmi0411.GIF)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/gmi0411.GIF) The Daily SPY index turned negative and the GMI-S fell to 38.  Between 13-23% of the stocks in the QQQQ, DIA and SPY rose on Tuesday.  Only the majority (75%) of the short term indicators for the QQQQ remain positive.  While the short term up trend in the QQQQ is still intact, I am raising stops and becoming defensive………
