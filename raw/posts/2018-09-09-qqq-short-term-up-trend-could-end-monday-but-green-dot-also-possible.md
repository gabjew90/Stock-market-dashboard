The current QQQ short term up-trend has lasted 44 days and could end on Monday. If it does, new QQQ short term down-trends often (41% of time) last less than 5 days. The QQQ daily 10.4 stochastic = 38.56 and is also low enough to bounce and could  flash a [green dot signal](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2018/09/waiting-for-qqq-to-show-a-green-dot/). But I would prefer that the stochastic reach a very oversold level, around 20, first. Must be patient and react after it happens and not anticipate the signal, however.
