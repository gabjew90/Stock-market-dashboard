The GMI remains at 6 and the QQQQ is in its 52nd day of its up-trend.  Furthermore, the short term interest rate indicator I have been writing about plunged on Friday. [![Irxx0615](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/2007/06/17/irxx0615.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/2007/06/17/irxx0615.gif)  The sustained down trend in this indicator suggests to me that the next move by the Fed will be to lower rates.
