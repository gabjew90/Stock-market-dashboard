I have written before that an [RWB pattern](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2010/09/introducing-red-white-and-blue-rwb-stocks-the-pattern-of-rockets/), where short term averages (red lines) are well above rising longer term averages (blue lines) such that there is a white space in between them, is typical of rocket stocks.   I decided to create these modified [GMMA](http://www.guppytraders.com/gup329.shtml) charts for four time periods to get an idea of how the QQQ is likely to behave.

The first chart shows the longer term weekly chart, which is exhibiting a classic RWB pattern. It looks like the QQQ has emerged from a nice consolidation period where the red lines converged into a tight pattern (indicating little price change) well above the blue lines: (click on charts to enlarge)

[![QQQGMMAwkly06202014](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/QQQGMMAwkly06202014-300x269.jpg)](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/QQQGMMAwkly06202014.jpg)

The daily chart shows a similar RWB pattern and the emergence of QQQ from a base at the end of May:

[![GMMAQQQdaily06202014](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/GMMAQQQdaily06202014-300x270.jpg)](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/GMMAQQQdaily06202014.jpg)The hourly chart shows very recent congestion, but it looks like the red lines are back above the blue lines and are turning up.   Thus the QQQ ended Friday with a small show of strength, but the QQQ needs to break the 93 level to come out of this short term consolidation.

[![QQQGMMAhourly06202014](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/QQQGMMAhourly06202014-300x272.jpg)](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/QQQGMMAhourly06202014.jpg)Finally, the 5 minute chart shows more clearly the end of day strength on Friday, with the red lines breaking above the blue lines in a RWB pattern.

[![GMMAQQQ5minute06202014](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/GMMAQQQ5minute06202014-300x273.jpg)](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/GMMAQQQ5minute06202014.jpg)

So what does all of this mean to me?   Consistent with the GMI and its Buy signal from April 22nd, the market, as measured by QQQ, looks like it remains in a strong up-trend.   As a longer term swing trader I   focus on the weekly and daily charts.   The hourly and 5 minute charts are useful only if I want to pinpoint areas for   entries or where to put sell stops.   I sometimes have [TC2000](http://www.worden.com/) send me an automatic email alert when the price of the index I am following crosses below or above one of these key moving averages…..
