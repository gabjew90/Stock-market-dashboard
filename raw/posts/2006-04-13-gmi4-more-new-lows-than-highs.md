The GMI remains at +4 and in danger of falling again today if we have fewer than 100 successful ten day break-outs.  [![Gmi0412](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/gmi0412.GIF)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/gmi0412.GIF) Between 55-57% of stocks in the QQQQ, SPY and DIA rose on Wednesday.  There were 55 new yearly lows and 46 new highs, an ominous sign.  Only 35% of the 4,000 stocks in the universe I follow are in a short term up trend, the lowest since March 10.  Wednesday was the twelfth day (U-12) in the current short term up trend in the QQQQ.
