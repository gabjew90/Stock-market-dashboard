If you watched [60 Minutes on Sunday night](http://www.businessinsider.com/lumber-liquidators-60-minutes-report-2015-3) you know why LL fell out of bed on Monday, down -25%. But should anyone have been holding LL stock recently?   My students would have probably been out of LL simply by looking at a weekly chart and applying [Weinstein’s Stage Analysis](http://www.nextbigtrade.com/stage-analysis/) technique. LL was below its 30 week moving average, which had topped out a year ago. LL closed below its 30 week average last week after spending a few weeks above it in January and February.   This could have caused a head-fake as a possible beginning Stage 2 advance, but note that LL was below its final [green line top](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2013/03/some-more-green-line-break-out-stocks-dfs-wat-cab-nee-dow-theory-buy-signal/) at around $120. I tell students only to hold stocks above a stock’s last green line top. The solid red line in this weekly chart is the critical 30 week moving average. Notice the strong Stage 2 up-trend throughout all of 2013.   I only own stocks that are in Stage 2 up-trends.

[![LLweekly03022015](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/LLweekly03022015-300x255.jpg)](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/LLweekly03022015.jpg)I decided to also look at the weekly modified [GMMA chart](http://www.guppytraders.com/gup329.shtml) for LL. I was struck by the fact that this chart did not show the recent head-fake in January and February.   The [RWB pattern](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2010/09/introducing-red-white-and-blue-rwb-stocks-the-pattern-of-rockets/) had clearly ended in 2013 and we never got a RWB pattern since. The GMMA chart clearly provided the better indication that one should not have been in LL since at least June, 2014, when a bearish BWR pattern became evident. Beware the whites of their charts!
