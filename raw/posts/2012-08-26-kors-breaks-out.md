Anyway, I had drawn a green line chart of KORS weeks ago and was ready to buy it if it broke to an all-time high.   See the monthly green line chart below.   The green line shows it all-time high. KORS came public and then doubled over the next 13 weeks.   It then consolidated for about 6 months and broke out last week.   The stock has the highest composite rating from IBD, 99th percentile, and is in my IBD 50 watch-list from August.
