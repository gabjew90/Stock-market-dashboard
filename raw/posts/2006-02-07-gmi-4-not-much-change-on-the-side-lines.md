The GMI remained at +4 after Monday’s trading.  There were 167 new highs in my universe of 4,000 stocks.  [![Gmi0206](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/gmi0206.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/gmi0206.gif) 48% of the Nasdaq 100 stocks rose along with 57% of the S&P 500 stocks and 43% of the Dow 30 stocks.  The GMI-L and GMI-S were unchanged. Monday was the second day (D-2) of the short term QQQQ decline.  Still 68% of stocks closed above their 10 week averages, but only 52% were in a short term up trend.  The market remains in a short term decline with the longer term indicators still pointing up.  This is the  time for me to be on the side lines in my trading account………………
