title: $TLT hits the skids as interest rates rise–will higher rates help the stock
  market?
---

TLT, the 20 yr treasury ETF, sold off on above average volume on Tuesday. As TLT declines, rates rise. [Bill Miller](https://www.cnbc.com/2018/01/09/legendary-investor-bill-miller-market-could-be-headed-for-a-melt-up-of-30-percent.html) suggests that falling bonds will push people into stocks. That may be true initially, up to a point. But if yields rise enough it will suck the money out of stocks–it always has.
