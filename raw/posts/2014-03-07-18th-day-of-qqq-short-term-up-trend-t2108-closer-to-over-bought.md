All of my GMI and GMI-2 indicators are now positive. One concern, however,   is that the [T2108 Worden indicator](http://www.drduru.com/money/T2108.htm) is now at 77%.   This means that 77% of the NYSE stocks closed above their average price over the last 40 days.     The closer T2108 is to 90% the more over bought the market tends to be. Check out this monthly chart of T2108, available in the Worden [TC2000](http://www.worden.com/products/tc2000/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_term=%2Btc2000%20%2Bworden&utm_content=33371341566&mktid=68&gclid=CILcxaKGgL0CFYMcOgodZAsAXw) software.
