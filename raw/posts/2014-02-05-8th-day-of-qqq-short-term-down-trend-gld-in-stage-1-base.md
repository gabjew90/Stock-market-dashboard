GMI-2 falls to zero, with all indicators negative. I am mainly in cash. [This is an unusually good description](http://www.usatoday.com/story/money/markets/2014/02/05/correction-stocks-already-here/5232811/) of how bad this market decline is.

It looks like gold is forming a Stage 1 base.   The gold ETF, GLD, formed a double bottom around 114.50 and appears to be forming a base.   Gone are the pundits talking about gold reaching $2,000 or $3,000 per ounce. As they now proclaim that [gold has more to fall](http://blogs.barrons.com/focusonfunds/2014/01/07/bob-doll-gold-commodities-to-fall-again-in-2014/), it probably will start to rise.   I am nibbling at GLD.   Here is its weekly chart. As always, watch the chart, not what the pundits say.
