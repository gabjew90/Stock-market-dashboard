title: TDSC, a Judy’s Pick, shines on Thursday
---

For several months now I have been posting in the “Stocks I’m Watching” column to the lower right, TDSC. On [December 10](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2010/12/14th-day-of-qqqq-short-term-up-trend-a-judy-pick-tdsc/), I wrote about TDSC as an RWB stock when it was at $31.   Judy brought this stock to my attention because she loved the concept.   The company makes 3D modeling equipment that she found very promising.   I had bought TDSC, but got shaken out in January when the stock dipped. The stock gave me another technical buy signal around $31 in early February, but I failed to act on it, even as Judy bought more.   Well, the market woke up to TDSC on Thursday, when it reported earnings and shot up about 40%.   I am going to start a watchlist of “Judy’s Picks” and monitor it daily for technical buy signals. Way to go– yet again, Judy!
