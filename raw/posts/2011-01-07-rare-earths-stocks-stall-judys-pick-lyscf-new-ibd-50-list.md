The [rare earths stocks stalled](http://finance.yahoo.com/news/Rare-Earths-Take-Breather-indie-2855394130.html?x=0&.v=1) on Thursday.   Judy had told me that the [lock-up period for MCP,](http://seekingalpha.com/article/244568-2-standout-companies-among-junior-rare-earth-miners) when insiders   from an IPO cannot sell their shares, expires this month.   After the stock recovers from the anticipated selling, MCP may be a good buy again.   Meanwhile, Judy told me about [LYSCF](http://finance.yahoo.com/q/pr?s=LYSCF.PK+Profile), a small company developing a new mine for rare earths. I bought a few shares just to keep it in mine (pun intended)….

With the new year, IBD has apparently shrunk the IBD 100 list down to 50 stocks.   The new [IBD 50 list](http://blogs.investors.com/capitalhill/index.php/home/35-politicsinvesting/2316-the-ibd-50-50-ways-to-leave-your-losers) contains a more selective set of stocks to watch.   I will run some performance tests in the future to see if this list does in fact perform better.   Most of my buys have come from stocks on their IBD 100 and New America lists….
