ONVO   is   an almost vertical climb.   It broke above its green line top on Thursday and has closed at an all-time   high. Normally this break above a multi-month top would be a major buy signal, but this strong rise could be a climax run or reflect some important [development in their business](http://www.fool.com/investing/general/2013/11/14/why-organovo-holdings-yume-and-lightinthebox-soare.aspx)? I still have a small position in ONVO, but it remains a very speculative and fragile holding for me.
