title: University of Maryland Stock Challenge now open
---

All students, faculty,   staff and honors alumni of the University System of Maryland are invited to register for the Spring 2011 Stock Challenge.   Registration is free and everyone gets a $100,000 margin account to trade.   It is a great way to learn how to manage an account without risking real money.   There are a lot of educational links on the site.   Register at   [www.umdsmc.com](http://www.umdsmc.com).
