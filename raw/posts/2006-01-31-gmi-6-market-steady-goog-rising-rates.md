The market held steady Monday, as did the GMI, at +6.  There were only 106 successful 10 day new highs–stocks that hit a new high 10 days ago and closed higher on Monday than they did 10 days ago. [![Gmi0130](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/gmi0130.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/gmi0130.gif)  The number of new highs declined to 414.  Only 40% of the Nasdaq 100 stocks rose, along with 42% of the S&P 500 stocks, 33% of the Dow 30 stocks and 44% of the S&P 600 small cap stocks. There were no changes in the GMI-L or GMI-S.  Monday was the second day in the QQQQ short term up trend (U-2)…..

The way the market reacts to GOOG’s earnings release Tuesday  will probably tell us a lot about the market’s near term health.  [![Irx0130](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/irx0130.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/irx0130.gif) Also, the chart of the short term interest rate index shows that traders see no end in sight to the rise in rates.  The Fed will surely accommodate them on Tuesday.  I would be much more optimistic about this market if the QQQQ could take out its January 11 peak.
