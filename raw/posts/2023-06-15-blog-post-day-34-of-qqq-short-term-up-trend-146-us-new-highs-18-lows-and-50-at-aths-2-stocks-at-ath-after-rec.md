A green line break-out (GLB)  in a strong market can sometimes be the *beginning* of a big move. AMPH and ORCL broke above their green lines and have gone on to a series of ATHs (all time highs). Look for stocks that hit an ATH and then have rested for at least 3 months. When they *close* above that earlier peak (where the green line is drawn) it can be a major buy signal. A rocket-stock to the moon must continually hit new highs.
