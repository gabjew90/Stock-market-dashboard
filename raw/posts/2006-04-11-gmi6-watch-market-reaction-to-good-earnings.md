The GMI stayed at +6 as stocks tread water.  41% of the Nasdaq 100 stocks rose on Monday along with 48% of S&P stocks and 53% of Dow 30 stocks.[![Gmi0410](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/gmi0410.GIF)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/gmi0410.GIF) There were minor changes in my indicators.  Monday was the tenth day of the QQQQ short term up trend (U-10). We need to wait to see whether the current weakness is just a pause or the start of a larger decline.  How stocks react to good earnings releases should help us to divine the market trend…..
