The GMI remains at one.  Only 24-34% of the stocks in the Nasdaq 100, S&P 500 and Dow 30 indexes advanced on Thursday.  There were 54 new highs and 77 new lows in my universe of 4,000 stocks.  Only 43% of the stocks in my universe closed above their 10 week averages.  [![Gmi0301_2](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/gmi0301_2.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/gmi0301_2.gif) The real reasons behind the markets plunge may be yet to be disclosed.  Time to stay in cash.
