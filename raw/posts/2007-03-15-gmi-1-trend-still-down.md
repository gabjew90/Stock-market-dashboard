The GMI is still 1.  67-75% of the stocks in the Nasdaq 100, S&P 500 and Dow 30 indexes rose on Wednesday.  Wednesday was the 13th day in the QQQQ down-trend.  [![Gmi0314](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/2007/03/14/gmi0314.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/2007/03/14/gmi0314.gif) There were 29 new highs and 142 new lows in my universe of 4,000 stocks.  Will Wednesday’s bounce hold?  No one knows.  The trend is down until it changes.
