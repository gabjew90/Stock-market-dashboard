Since the GMI flashed a by signal on December 23rd, the QQQ has advanced +7.8%, the [QLD](http://finance.yahoo.com/q/pr?s=qld) +16% and the [TYH](http://finance.yahoo.com/q/pr?s=tyh) +24.7%.   In the same period, 30% of the Nasdaq 100 stocks have advanced 10% or more and only 5% have advanced more than 23%.   Yet again, we find that riding a [triple leveraged ETF](http://etf.stock-encyclopedia.com/category/leveraged-etfs.html) in an up-trend beats almost all other stocks.   Why do we search for the rare stock that will outperform the 3X leveraged ETF , like TYH! Another leveraged ETF that did as well as TYH during this period is [TQQQ](http://finance.yahoo.com/q/pr?s=tqqq). In fact the leveraged ETF, [SOXL](http://finance.yahoo.com/q/pr?s=SOXL+Profile), advanced +34.4%!
