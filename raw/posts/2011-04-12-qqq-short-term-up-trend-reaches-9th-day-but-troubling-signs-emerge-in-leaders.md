The short term up-trend may be in jeopardy, with pre-market futures weak this morning.   I am still very concerned about the continued weakness in AAPL’s shares.   When the leaders of a bull market weaken, it often is an early sign that the other stocks will follow.   GOOG, AAPL, NFLX and FFIV are well below their highs.   Time to be cautious again.
