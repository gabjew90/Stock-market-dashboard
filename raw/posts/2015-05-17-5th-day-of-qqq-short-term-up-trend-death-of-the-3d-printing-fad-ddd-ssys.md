All of my GMI and GMI-2 indicators are positive. This market acts like it wants to continue the longer term up-trend.

[![GMI05152015](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/GMI05152015-280x300.jpg)](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/GMI05152015.jpg)I thought you might like to see the value of the GMMA for tracking the life cycle of a growth stock. DDD remained in an [RWB up-trend](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2015/02/red-white-and-blue-rwb-the-rocket-pattern-and-gldaapl-and-the-dow-30-nyse-bear/) for most of the period from 2012-2013. In May of 2014 the RWB pattern ended and changed into a bearish BWR pattern. Every stock, ETF and mutual fund owner should draw the GMMA chart and see whether the pattern is [RWB or BWR.](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2015/02/red-white-and-blue-rwb-the-rocket-pattern-and-gldaapl-and-the-dow-30-nyse-bear/)
