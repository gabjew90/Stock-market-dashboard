I especially look for blue dot stocks that have also had a recent high volume rise, as did CLS. CLS rose Thursday on a weak day, showing relative strength. Its IBD RS=Comp=99 and its price is 5x its price a year ago. I am not a blind empiricist. As with CAN SLIM, stocks I purchase must look good from a technical and fundamental perspective.
