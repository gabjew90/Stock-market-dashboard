So many of the indicators and stocks I look at are looking very weak.   A lot of people say that copper prices are correlated with [economic growth](http://oilprice.com/Metals/Copper/Why-Copper-Is-Such-An-Important-Economic-Indicator.html).   Copper is a key material in so many products related to building and economic development. While looking at my watchlist of indicators, I happened to see this weekly chart of copper.   [![](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/Copper-300x197.jpg)](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/Copper.jpg)By my analysis, copper looks like it is getting ready to enter a major Stage 4 (See Weinstein book below for a discussion of stage analysis) down-trend.   I can’t trade futures in my IRA account and I can’t short stocks.   But I could buy some [put options on SCCO](http://finance.yahoo.com/q/op?s=SCCO+Options), whose chart tracks the price of copper quite closely. Regardless, I think that this chart of copper is an ominous sign for the world’s economy…..

Another ominous sign is that the GMI and GMI-R   are each at zero.   The markets are in short and longer term down-trends and it makes no sense to hold stocks or long positions now, except for gold (GLD).   So I am short a few index ETF’s and own GLD.[![](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/GMI06042010-300x234.jpg)](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/GMI06042010.jpg) I do not know how long this decline will last, but things do not look good right now.   Friday was the 22nd day of the current QQQQ short term down-trend.   The Worden T2108 is at 19%, which is in oversold territory but not at an extreme level.   One promising sign is that 54% of the Nasdaq 100 stocks had their MACD above their signal line, a sign of short term strength.

Anyone hanging on to long positions probably has an addiction to trading or gamboling.   One needs to have a plan to minimize market risk.   It is so much easier to make money owning stocks when the GMI is above 3.
