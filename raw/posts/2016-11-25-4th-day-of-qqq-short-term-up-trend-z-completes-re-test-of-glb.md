This weekly chart of Z  shows that Z has resumed its up-trend after a 14 week consolidation where it found support at its Green Line Break-out (GLB) ands its 30 week average (red line). The resumption of its advance came on weekly volume that was 2X greater than its average volume over the past 50 weeks.
