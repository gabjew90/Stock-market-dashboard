I see few media articles discussing a government shut down in October. [Here is an exception](https://www.marketwatch.com/story/can-democrats-raise-the-debt-ceiling-alone-and-avert-a-market-meltdown-11627672206). While a lifting of the debt limit may eventually be passed by Democrats  as part of a reconciliation bill, I would not rule out hysteria about it until then. This could add to market volatility the next few months along with  a resurgent pandemic. For all of these reasons and the “air pockets” in leading stocks last week I am getting very defensive in my trading accounts……
