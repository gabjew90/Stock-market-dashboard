The GMI remains at +6 and market internals are strong.  [![Gmi1110](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/gmi1110.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/gmi1110.gif) There were 216 new highs and only 77 new lows on Thursday in my universe of 4,000 stocks.  81% of the Nasdaq 100 stocks, 80% of the S&P 500 stocks and 90% of the Dow 30 stocks rose.  More than one half of stocks are in a short term up trend and 54% are above their 10 week averages (compared with 20% on October 18).  One in five stocks (21%) are now within 5% of their 52 week highs.  Thursday was the eighth day (U-8) of the QQQQ up-trend.
