IBD announced in Thursday’s edition that the latest sentiment poll had almost twice as many bulls than bears.   This amount of bullish advisers is not what [you would expect](http://www.cnbc.com/id/44177294) if the market were near the bottom of a decline. This weekly GMMA chart of the S&P 500 stock index shows that the shorter term averages (red) are just breaking below the longer term averages (blue).   It is still too early to determine if this down-trend will develop into a stage 4 major decline. Click on chart to enlarge.

With the futures down a lot   this morning, we need to see whether this renewed decline will hold above the recent lows.   I am in cash and safely watching from the sidelines.
