I have found that if the a new QQQ short term up-trend lasts 5 days, it often will continue for more days.   I am very slowly wading into this up-trend with a small purchase of QLD, [the leveraged bullish ETF](http://www.proshares.com/funds/qld.html) that is designed to rise (or fall) twice as much as the QQQ. If the up-trend continues, I will add to my position. If it fails, I will quickly exit. I know the longer term trend remains down, but the shorter term trend, by definition, turns up before the longer term trend. I remain in money market funds in my university pension.
