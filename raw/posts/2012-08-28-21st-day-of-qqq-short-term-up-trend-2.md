Short and longer term trends remain up.   However, note that September is typically the worst month of the year for the market. I think the stength of AAPL is masking the developing weakness in techs.   Only 31% of the Nasdaq 100 stocks rose on Monday and only 43% have their MACD above the signal line, down from over 80% in early August. So I am becoming more cautious.
