Chances are we might have some more strength in the leaders of the past year, as mutual funds close out their 4th quarter portfolios.   The funds want to hold winners and shed the losers. Thursday was the 24th day of the current QQQQ short term up-trend. As this weekly [GMMA](http://www.guppytraders.com/gup329.shtml) chart of the QQQQ shows, the Nasdaq 100 index remains in a strong [RWB](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/2010/09/introducing-red-white-and-blue-rwb-stocks-the-pattern-of-rockets/) up-trend.
