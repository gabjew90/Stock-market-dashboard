The GMI fell to 4 (of 6) and the GMI-R to 70%.  There were 98 new highs and 190 new lows in my universe of 4,000 stocks on Wednesday. [![Gmi1024](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/2007/10/24/gmi1024.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/2007/10/24/gmi1024.gif)  The QQQQ trend remains up in the face of weakness in the DIA, SPY and IJR.  Wednesday was the 39th day in the current QQQQ short term up-trend.  However, only 48% of the Nasdaq 100 stocks closed above their 30 day averages and only 32% of them rose on Wednesday.  With the GMI at 4 and the market so split, I am content to remain mainly in cash for a few more days.
