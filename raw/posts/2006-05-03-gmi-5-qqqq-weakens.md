---

The GMI declined one to +5, as the QQQQ weakened.  Tech stocks continue to underperform, with serious declines in some biotechs.  [![Gmi0502](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/migrated/wishingwealth.typepad.com/report/images/gmi0502.gif)](http://wishingwealth.typepad.com/.shared/image.html?/photos/uncategorized/gmi0502.gif) 53% of the Nasdaq 100 stocks advanced on Tuesday, compared with 65% of the S&P 500 stocks and 77% of the Dow 30 stocks. Still, 275 stocks hit new highs.  The GMI-S fell to 69, primarily because of weakness in the QQQQ’s short term indicators.  BBH and DNA continue to weaken.
