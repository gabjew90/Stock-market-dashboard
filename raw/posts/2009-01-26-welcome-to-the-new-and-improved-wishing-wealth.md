![grand opening](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2009/01/grandopening.jpg "grand opening") Hello and welcome to the new home of Dr. Wish’s Wishing Wealth Blog. I’m your host: Mr. Wish.

My dad has been slaving over this blog for a few years now and I have always felt incredibly inspired by his commitment and devotion to his field. And you’re probabaly thinking, jeez aren’t you lucky to have a dad who’s so savvy about the stock market, you’re gonna be filthy rich if you just follow his financial advice. Unfortunately for me the only advice I have ever taken from him is to follow your passion. My passion is designing web sites. Enter: the new wishing wealth blog.

I designed this site as a dedication to my dad, because he has provided me with all the support in the world to do what I love. I wanted to make sure I had the chance to return the favor. Keep up the good work, pops.

Now let’s get down to business and let me introduce you to some of the features of the new site:

This site is powered by WordPress. We moved from TypePad in order to allow for the new site to support advanced features. I will have full control of every aspect of the site to make it more usable for all of you as well as my dad. here are some of the notably new features:

**Hand Picked Quotes**  
To your right you will see quotes of the indexes and hand picked quotes from Dr. Wish. This is where you’ll find quotes from his portfolio as well as quotes that he references in his posts.

**Rated Posts**  
This is where you guys get the opportunity to rank your favorite. Once you click on the title of a post from the home page you will see a place to add comments but right above that you should see a red and green arrow that you can click to add a rating to the post. Please utilize this so that new users have an idea of what the best content is and so Dr. Wish has feedback about what to focus on in his posts.

**Recommended Reading**  
At the bottom of this sidebar you’ll see icons of the books that Dr. Wish has recommended. If you plan on buying any one of these books because of his recommendation please buy it through the link posted here. You will receive your book and and amazon will contribute a percentage of the purchase towards keeping this site up and running. All your support is much appreciated.
