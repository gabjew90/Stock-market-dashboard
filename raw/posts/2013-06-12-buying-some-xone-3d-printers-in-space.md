My stock buddy, Judy, has been talking about XONE, which she bought a while ago.   XONE was an IPO in February.   It came public at around $23.   Since then it has advanced to close at $51.91 on Tuesday.   [XONE markets 3D printers to commercial customers](http://beta.fool.com/houlijr8/2013/06/04/exones-big-expectations-arent-big-enough/35928/?source=eogyholnk0000001). And [3D printing in space](http://www.nasa.gov/home/hqnews/2013/may/HQ_13-161_Made_in_Space.html) is about to open a new frontier.   Judy told me about 3D printers and DDD a long time ago.   I wish I had acted on it!   Better late than never….

Note XONE’s short life as a trading stock and that it managed to rise to a new high even during Tuesday’s rout! Such relative strength is very interesting….
