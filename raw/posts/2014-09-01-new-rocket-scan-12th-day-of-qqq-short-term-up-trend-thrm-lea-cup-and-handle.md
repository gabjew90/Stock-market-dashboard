One of the best areas of support for a rising stock is its 10 week moving average. A lot of rocket stocks rest there and then resume their rise.   I created a new [TC2000](http://www.worden.com/products/tc2000) scan that scans over 6,000 U.S. stocks for a strong rocket pattern and a bounce up off of the stock’s ten week average.   This scan gives me a small list of stocks to research further for possible purchase. This weekend’s scan yielded 10 stocks, shown on the right. [![Rocketbounce10wkscan08312014](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Rocketbounce10wkscan08312014-186x300.jpg)](https://www.wishingwealthblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Rocketbounce10wkscan08312014.jpg)Two of them, PANW and THRM, have a blue flag on the left column, indicating they have appeared on an [IBD50](http://education.investors.com/investors-corner/605107-how-to-invest-using-the-ibd-50-list.htm) list or other IBD lists at some point.   I looked closely at THRM (which I own) and found that it appears to have broken out of a [cup and handle pattern](http://www.investopedia.com/university/charts/charts3.asp), a base that occurs in an up-trend. I have labeled this daily chart of THRM to illustrate the pattern’s primary components.   First, there must be a prior up-trend because this is a continuation pattern. Second, there must be a pattern that looks like a side view of a cup and handle.   Then there must be a break out above the top of the handle (blue solid line) on above average volume.   [IBD](http://www.investors.com/offer/splash.aspx?id=subOffersTrials&src=AURLEDW) made this pattern famous as one that can lead to a large gain.   As always, however, whether this break-out continues will depend on the general market’s trend. I typically sell quickly if a stock closes back below its 10 week average or if a break-out fails.
