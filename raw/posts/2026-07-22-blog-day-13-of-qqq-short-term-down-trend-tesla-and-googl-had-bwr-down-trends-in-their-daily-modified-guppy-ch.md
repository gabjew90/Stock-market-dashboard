Stocks often top out before their earnings do. It may be a good idea to check a daily modified Guppy chart before holding through earnings. TSLA and GOOGL declined tonight after market when their earnings were released. Look at their charts. Both have their red, shorter term, averages declining below their blue, longer term averages, with a white space separating them, hence, BWR down-trends.
