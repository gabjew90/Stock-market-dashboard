In a prior post I gave the figure for the length of new down-trends but meant to give the figure for new up-trends. I trust a change in short term trend more once it reaches beyond 5 days. This is especially true for a change to a down-trend, where 39% end within 5 days. I became defensive on February 24 when that short term  up-trend extended beyond the high of 88 days from 2006-2019 shown in this table and the up-trend subsequently ended at a record 91 days.
